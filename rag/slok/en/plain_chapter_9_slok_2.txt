Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
rājavidyā rājaguhyaṃ pavitramidamuttamam .
pratyakṣāvagamaṃ dharmyaṃ susukhaṃ kartumavyayam

Word Meanings:
राजविद्या - king of sciences
राजगुह्यं - king of secrets
पवित्रमिदमुत्तमम् - this purest and supreme
प्रत्यक्षावगमं - directly perceivable
धर्म्यं - righteous
सुसुखं - very joyful
कर्तुमव्ययम् - to practice and imperishable

Life Application:
Value this king of sciences and secrets as pure, directly realizable, righteous, easy to perform, and imperishable.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
This is the Sovereign Science, the Sovereign Secret, the supreme purifier, directly experienced, righteous, very easy to practice, and imperishable.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
This is the King of Sciences, the King of Secrets, the Supreme Purifier, realized by direct intuition, endowed with righteousness, easily performed and imperishable.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
This Self-knowledge is the King of Sciences, the King of Secrets, the supreme purifier, directly verifiable, righteous, easy to practice, and eternal.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
This Sovereign Knowledge is supreme among all sciences, most confidential, purest, directly realized, in accord with Dharma, joyful to perform, and imperishable.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
This is the King of Sciences, the King of Secrets, the supreme purifier, directly experienced, righteous, easy to practice, and imperishable.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
This is the King of Sciences, the King of Secrets, the supreme purifier, directly realizable, righteous, easy to practice, and imperishable.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
This is the King of Sciences, the King of Secrets, the supreme purifier, directly realizable, righteous, easy to practice, and imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: This Self-knowledge is luminous (Sovereign Science), deeply secret (Sovereign Secret), supreme purifier destroying all sins, and bestows direct realization.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
This is the King of Sciences, the King of Secrets, supreme purifier, directly experienced, righteous, very easy to perform, and imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: This knowledge in the form of devotion is the Science of Kings and the Secret of Kings; it is supremely pure, yields direct perception of God, and is joyfully practiced.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: 'Rajavidya' shines supreme among all sciences. This knowledge is immensely sacred, bestows direct Self-realization, and confers imperishable liberation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: 'Sovereign Science, Sovereign Secret'—it is King of Sciences because it is supreme among all wisdom, and King of Secrets being most profound. It wipes away all sins, is directly intuited, fully accordant with Dharma, joyfully practiced, and yields imperishable fruit.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The wise are luminous and sovereign like kings, hence their wisdom is called Rajavidya. It bestows direct perception of the Supreme Self and is imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: This science is the crown jewel of all practices; endowed with devotion, it provides the soul with the easy and eternal means of direct God-realization.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: This Brahma-vidya is pre-eminent among all sciences (Rajavidya), most profound among secrets (Rajaguhyam), root-destroyer of ignorance, direct realization, and righteous.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Being the wisdom of spiritual kings (jnanis), it is Rajavidya; being deeply confidential, it is Rajaguhyam; it yields direct perception and is joyfully practiced.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Conferring liberation, this science is supreme among all wisdom, kindling the purest light of direct Self-realization in the seeker's heart.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: This knowledge as Rajavidya and Rajaguhyam is ever pure and bestows direct perception, representing the supreme path of devotion.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: This science is paramount among all spiritual paths, attaining which the soul directly experiences divine bliss.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Crown jewel of all sciences, this Sovereign Knowledge bestows direct Self-experience and is immensely joyful in practice.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
This knowledge is the king of education, the most secret of all secrets. It is the purest knowledge, and because it gives direct perception of the self by realization, it is the perfection of religion. It is everlasting, and it is joyfully performed.
