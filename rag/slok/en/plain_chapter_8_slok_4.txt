Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
adhibhūtaṃ kṣaro bhāvaḥ puruṣaścādhidaivatam .
adhiyajño.ahamevātra dehe dehabhṛtāṃ vara

Word Meanings:
अधिभूतं - the material nature
क्षरो - perishable
भावः - existence
पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम् - and the divine person is Adhidaiva
अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र - I alone am Adhiyajna here
देहे - in the body
देहभृतां - of the embodied
वर - O best

Life Application:
Recognize physical nature as perishable, the divine person as cosmic spirit, and God as the inner sacrifice in the body.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Adhibhuta is the perishable entity, and Purusha is the Adhidaiva; I alone am the Adhiyajna here in this body, O best of the embodied.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Adhibhuta is the perishable entity, and Purusha is the Adhidaiva; I alone am the Adhiyajna here in this body, O best of the embodied.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O best of embodied beings, all perishable physical matter is Adhibhuta; the cosmic Purusha is Adhidaiva; and I, Lord Vasudeva residing within this body, am Adhiyajna, the enjoyer of all sacrifices.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Adhibhuta is perishable nature"—the prakriti-born perishable world is Adhibhuta; four-faced Brahma is Adhidaiva; and Supreme Lord Sri Hari Himself is Adhiyajna in all bodies.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Adhibhuta is perishable nature"—the transient world appearance is Adhibhuta; Hiranyagarbha in the sun globe is Adhidaiva; and Lord Vasudeva as enjoyer of sacrifice is Adhiyajna.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Adhibhuta is the perishable entity, and Purusha is the Adhidaiva; I alone am the Adhiyajna here in this body, O best of the embodied.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Perishable modifying objects of sound/touch constitute Adhibhuta; presiding demigods over senses are Adhidaiva; and Lord Vasudeva dwelling in the body as Master of sacrifice is Adhiyajna.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Perishable object is Adhibhuta, cosmic Purusha is Adhidaiva, and the Lord in the body is Adhiyajna. One who leaves the body at death meditating on the Lord attains His divine state without doubt.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Adhibhuta is the perishable nature"—whatever is subject to origin and dissolution is Adhibhuta. "Purusha is Adhidaiva"—Hiranyagarbha dwelling in the solar orb as the animating soul of all sense-organs is Adhidaiva. "I alone am Adhiyajna in this body"—Lord Vasudeva Himself, presiding over all Vedic sacrifices as enjoyer and rewarder, is Adhiyajna abiding within the body.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Supreme Lord Vishnu is the primary Adhidaiva and Adhiyajna; individual souls and the world function solely under His sovereign divine command.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The Lord Himself, as the Supreme Master of sacrifice Adhiyajna, dwells within the hearts of His devotees.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I alone am Adhiyajna here"—Lord Vasudeva residing in every body as the enjoyer of all sacrifices is Adhiyajna, bestowing true spiritual rewards.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Adhibhuta is the perishable entity, and Purusha is the Adhidaiva; I alone am the Adhiyajna here in this body, O best of the embodied.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative philosophical commentary elucidating Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva residing as Inner Controller in all embodied beings is the primary Adhiyajna.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna Himself is the enjoyer, Master, and Adhiyajna of all sacrificial acts.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Perishable universe is Adhibhuta, presiding Purusha over senses is Adhidaiva, and Supreme Self in body is Adhiyajna.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O best of the embodied beings, the physical nature, which is constantly changing, is called adhibhuta [the material manifestation]. The cosmic form of the Lord, which includes all the demigods, like those of the sun and moon, is called adhidaiva. And I, the Supreme Lord, represented as the Supersoul in the heart of every embodied being, am called adhiyajna [the Lord of sacrifice].
