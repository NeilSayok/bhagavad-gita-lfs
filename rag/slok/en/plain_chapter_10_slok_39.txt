Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yaccāpi sarvabhūtānāṃ bījaṃ tadahamarjuna .
na tadasti vinā yatsyānmayā bhūtaṃ carācaram

Word Meanings:
यच्चापि - and whatever is
सर्वभूतानां - of all beings
बीजं - seed
तदहमर्जुन - that I am, O Arjuna
न - not
तदस्ति - that exists
विना - without
यत्स्यान्मया - which could be from Me
भूतं - entity/being
चराचरम् - moving or unmoving

Life Application:
Know God as the seed of all beings; no moving or unmoving creature can exist without the divine presence.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
And whatever is the seed of all beings, that am I, O Arjuna; there is no entity, moving or unmoving, that can exist without Me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
And whatsoever is the seed of all beings, that am I, O Arjuna; nor is there anything, moving or unmoving, that can exist without Me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
And whatever is the seed of all beings, that am I, O Arjuna. There is no entity, moving or unmoving, that can exist without Me.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
The Supreme Lord is Himself the primal seed of all creation; there is no moving or unmoving entity in the universe that can exist apart from Him.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
And whatever is the seed of all beings, that am I, O Arjuna; nor is there anything, moving or unmoving, that can exist without Me.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
And whatever is the seed of all beings, that am I, O Arjuna; nor is there anything, moving or unmoving, that can exist without Me.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
And whatever is the seed of all beings, that am I, O Arjuna; nor is there anything, moving or unmoving, that can exist without Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: 'No entity can exist without Me'—as declared in the Mahabharata Shantiparva (Mokshadharma), Lord Vishnu pervades the entire universe of moving and unmoving entities through His infinite divine manifestations.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Supreme Lord is the primal seed of all manifestation; without Him, no moving or unmoving object can possess existence or activity.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
And whatever is the seed of all beings, that am I, O Arjuna; there is no entity, moving or unmoving, that can exist without Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: I am the ultimate seed of origin for all beings; without My governance and association, no entity, moving or unmoving, can maintain its existence.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Whatever is the seed of germination of all beings, that am I, O Arjuna. Nothing whatsoever, moving or unmoving, can exist destitute of Me; all things derive their existence solely from Me.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: 'There is no entity that can exist without Me'—the Supreme Lord, possessing infinite forms, resides within all objects. Without the all-pervading Divine, no moving or unmoving entity can exist even for a moment.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The seed of all beings is the Lord's own Form; the entire universe of moving and unmoving entities exists solely supported by Him.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: I am the universal seed for the germination of all beings; without Me, no moving or unmoving entity can possess real existence.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Whatever is the seed of all beings, that am I, O Arjuna; for nothing, moving or unmoving, can exist without Me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Being the universal seed and substratum of existence belongs uniquely to God, without whom creation is lifeless.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Being the seed-cause of the universe and associating with all moving and unmoving beings is the Lord's inherent nature.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: I am the seed of all beings; without Me no entity, moving or unmoving, exists.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The seed of origin of all beings and the fundamental substratum of existence is the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Furthermore, O Arjuna, I am the generating seed of all existences. There is no being – moving or nonmoving – that can exist without Me.
