Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
avajānanti māṃ mūḍhā mānuṣīṃ tanumāśritam .
paraṃ bhāvamajānanto mama bhūtamaheśvaram

Word Meanings:
अवजानन्ति - disregard
मां - Me
मूढा - fools
मानुषीं - human
तनुमाश्रितम् - body assumed
परं - supreme
भावमजानन्तो - not knowing My nature
मम - my
भूतमहेश्वरम् - supreme Lord of beings

Life Application:
Do not disregard the divine presence when masked in human form, failing to know God's supreme nature.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Fools deride Me when I descend in a human form, not knowing My higher nature as the Supreme Lord of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Fools disregard Me when I assume a human form, unaware of My higher nature as the Supreme Lord of all beings.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Ignorant people disrespect Me when I appear in a human form, unmindful of My supreme nature as the Supreme Lord of all creation.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Fools disregard Me when I assume a human form, unmindful of My higher nature as the Supreme Lord of all beings.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Fools disregard Me clad in human form, not knowing My higher nature as the Supreme Lord of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Fools disregard Me when I assume a human form, unaware of My higher nature as the Supreme Lord of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Fools disregard Me when I assume a human form, unaware of My higher nature as the Supreme Lord of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Ignorant people, unaware of the Lord's supreme Sachidananda nature in His human manifestation, mistakenly regard Him as an ordinary mortal and deride Him.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Fools disregard Me when I assume a human form, unaware of My higher nature as the Supreme Lord of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Out of immense compassion when I descend in a human embodiment like Krishna for saving the world, foolish ignorant people fail to recognize My supreme divine nature and deem Me an ordinary human.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Souls veiled by ignorance fail to perceive the all-pervading, ever-pure nature of the Lord within all beings, and disrespect Him when manifested in human form.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: 'Fools deride Me'—deluded ignorant people, unaware of My supreme nature as the Supreme Lord of all beings (all-pervading, unattached Truth-Consciousness-Bliss like ether), disrespect Me who have assumed a human form for divine play.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Demonic ignorant souls fail to acknowledge Krishna as direct Supreme Brahman Lord Vishnu, treating Him as a mere human; this utter delusion causes their ruin.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Souls blinded by ignorance fail to perceive Lord Krishna's blissful divine Form and disregard Him as a mortal human, indicating their deep delusion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: 'Fools deride Me'—ignorant souls, unaware of the Supreme Lord's boundless opulence and unattached nature, disrespect Him when He appears in human form.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Foolish people, ignorant of My true nature as the Supreme Controller of all beings, disregard Me when I assume a human form.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Viewing the Lord's avatara with ordinary material vision is foolishness; those who fail to see the Supreme Ruler's ever-pure nature remain fallen in Samsara.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord's human advent is solely out of grace to redeem souls; yet unfortunate ones, ignorant of His divine glory, mock Him as an ordinary mortal.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Ignorant souls, unaware of the Lord's infinite opulence and true Self, disrespect Lord Krishna manifested in human form.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Foolish souls unacquainted with the Lord's supreme Witness-Consciousness deride Him as an ordinary mortal when embodied.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Fools deride Me when I descend in the human form. They do not know My transcendental nature as the Supreme Lord of all that be.
