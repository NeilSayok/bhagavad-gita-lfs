Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
akṣarāṇāmakāro.asmi dvandvaḥ sāmāsikasya ca .
ahamevākṣayaḥ kālo dhātāhaṃ viśvatomukhaḥ

Word Meanings:
अक्षराणामकारोऽस्मि - among letters I am the letter 'A'
द्वन्द्वः - dual compound
सामासिकस्य - among compound words
च - and
अहमेवाक्षयः - I alone am eternal
कालो - eternal time (kala)
धाताहं - I am the dispenser/creator
विश्वतोमुखः - all-facing (universal form)

Life Application:
See God as the letter 'A' among alphabets, dual compound in grammar, imperishable time, and all-facing creator.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among letters I am the letter 'A'; among compound words I am the Dvandva (dual compound); I am endless Time, and I am the Dispenser/Creator facing all directions.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among letters I am the letter 'A'; among compound words I am the Dvandva; I am verily the inexhaustible Time; I am the All-faced Creator.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Among letters I am the letter 'A'; among compounds I am the Dvandva, the dual compound. I am imperishable Time, and I am the Creator whose face is turned everywhere.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among letters, the primal 'A'; among compounds, the balanced Dvandva; imperishable Time; and the All-facing Creator (Vishvatomukha Dhata) represents the Supreme Being.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.
