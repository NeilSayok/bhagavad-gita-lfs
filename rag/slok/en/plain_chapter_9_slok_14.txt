Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
satataṃ kīrtayanto māṃ yatantaśca dṛḍhavratāḥ .
namasyantaśca māṃ bhaktyā nityayuktā upāsate

Word Meanings:
सततं - constantly
कीर्तयन्तो - glorifying
मां - Me
यतन्तश्च - and striving
दृढव्रताः - of firm vows
नमस्यन्तश्च - and bowing down
मां - Me
भक्त्या - with devotion
नित्ययुक्ता - ever-steadfast
उपासते - worship

Life Application:
Glorify God constantly, striving with firm resolve, bowing down with love, ever devoted in worship.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Always glorifying Me, striving with steadfast vows, bowing down to Me in devotion, they worship Me, ever steadfast.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Always glorifying Me, striving, steadfast in vows, bowing down to Me in devotion, worshipping Me with single-mindedness.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Always chanting My glories, striving with firm resolve, and bowing down to Me with devotion, they worship Me with constant concentration.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Always glorifying Me, striving, steadfast in vows, bowing down to Me with devotion, worshipping Me, ever steadfast.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Always glorifying Me, striving, steadfast in vows, bowing down to Me in devotion, worshipping Me, ever steadfast.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Always glorifying Me, striving, steadfast in vows, bowing down to Me in devotion, worshipping Me, ever steadfast.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Always glorifying Me, striving, steadfast in vows, bowing down to Me in devotion, worshipping Me, ever steadfast.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Great souls remain absorbed in worshipping the Supreme Lord through chanting His names, sense-control endeavors, firm vows, and acts of prostration.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Always glorifying Me, striving, steadfast in vows, bowing down to Me in devotion, worshipping Me, ever steadfast.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Great souls serve Me with eternal union, chanting My names, striving with control of mind and senses, prostrating with deep devotion, and cultivating intense love.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Great souls endowed with divine nature, knowing the Supreme Lord to be the imperishable source of all beings, worship Him with undivided mind through perpetual chanting, steadfast endeavor, and devotion.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: 'Always glorifying Me, striving with firm vows'—those noble souls constantly chant My divine names, control their senses, adhere strictly to vows of non-violence, and bow down in deep humility, worshipping Me with ever-integrated mind.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Continuous chanting of the Lord's names, dedicated effort, and loving prostrations define the exclusive devotional service of great souls.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Through constant chanting of names, practice of hearing/reflecting, strict vows, and humble prostrations, great devotees worship Supreme Brahman with integrated mind.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Always glorifying Me, striving with steadfast vows, bowing down to Me in devotion, they worship Me, ever steadfast.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Chanting, striving, firm vows, and prostrations are the four cardinal limbs of exclusive devotion, through which seekers stay absorbed in God.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The devotion of great souls who worship the Supreme Lord through perpetual chanting and humble prostrations is eternal and unbroken.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Constant chanting of divine names and loving prostrations constitute the sweet essence of eternal worship by exclusive devotees.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Perpetual chanting and steadfast vows illuminate Sattvic devotion, enabling the seeker to surrender all mental modes to the Supreme.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Always chanting My glories, endeavoring with great determination, bowing down before Me, these great souls perpetually worship Me with devotion.
