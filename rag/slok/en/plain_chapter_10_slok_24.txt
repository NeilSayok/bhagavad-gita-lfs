Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
purodhasāṃ ca mukhyaṃ māṃ viddhi pārtha bṛhaspatim .
senānīnāmahaṃ skandaḥ sarasāmasmi sāgaraḥ

Word Meanings:
पुरोधसां - among priests
च - and
मुख्यं - chief
मां - Me
विद्धि - know
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
बृहस्पतिम् - Brihaspati
सेनानीनामहं - among generals I am
स्कन्दः - Kartikeya (Skanda)
सरसामस्मि - among water bodies I am
सागरः - ocean

Life Application:
Know God as Brihaspati among priests, Skanda among generals, and the vast ocean among bodies of water.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among household priests, O Arjuna, know Me to be the chief, Brihaspati; among generals I am Skanda (Kartikeya); and among reservoirs of water I am the ocean.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
And, among the household priests, O Arjuna, know Me to be the chief, Brihaspati; among generals I am Skanda; among lakes I am the ocean.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of priests, O Arjuna, know Me to be the chief, Brihaspati. Of generals I am Kartikeya, and of bodies of water I am the ocean.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among royal priests, Brihaspati (preceptor of the gods); among military commanders, Skanda (lord of war); and among standing reservoirs of water, the unfathomable Ocean symbolizes the Lord's majesty.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Know Me, O Arjuna, to be Brihaspati, the chief of priests; among military commanders I am Skanda; and among lakes I am the ocean.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Know Me, O Arjuna, to be the chief of priests, Brihaspati; among military commanders I am Skanda; among reservoirs of water I am the ocean.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Know Me, O Arjuna, to be the chief of priests, Brihaspati; among military commanders I am Skanda; among reservoirs of water I am the ocean.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Among royal priests, the omniscient preceptor Brihaspati; among generals, Skanda (commander of demigods); and among static water reservoirs, the boundless Ocean are the Lord's chief manifestations.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Of priests, O Arjuna, know Me to be the chief, Brihaspati; of generals I am Skanda, and of bodies of water I am the ocean.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among priests, the supreme preceptor Brihaspati; among generals, the divine commander Skanda; and among reservoirs of water, the profound Ocean is My divine Vibhuti.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among priests (royal preceptors), know Me to be the chief, Brihaspati. Among generals I am Skanda (commander of gods); and among reservoirs of water I am the ocean.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Brihaspati among priests, Skanda among generals, and the all-encompassing Ocean among static water reservoirs manifest the Lord's Form.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Among priests, the all-knowing Brihaspati; among commanders, Skanda (son of Rudra); and among static bodies of water, the jewel-filled Ocean is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Know Me, O Arjuna, to be the chief of priests, Brihaspati; among generals I am Skanda; and among bodies of water I am the ocean.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Brihaspati among priests, Skanda among military leaders, and the unfathomable Ocean among water bodies reveal the Lord's supreme glory.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The supreme priest Brihaspati, general Skanda, and ocean, chief of all water bodies, assume their glorious roles solely through the Lord's power.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Among priests I am Brihaspati; among generals I am Skanda; and among bodies of water I am the ocean.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Brihaspati among priests, Skanda among generals, and the Ocean among water bodies represent the Lord's divine manifestation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
