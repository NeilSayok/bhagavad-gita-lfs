Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
gatirbhartā prabhuḥ sākṣī nivāsaḥ śaraṇaṃ suhṛt .
prabhavaḥ pralayaḥ sthānaṃ nidhānaṃ bījamavyayam

Word Meanings:
गतिर्भर्ता - the goal and sustainer
प्रभुः - the Lord
साक्षी - the witness
निवासः - the abode
शरणं - the refuge
सुहृत् - the dear friend
प्रभवः - the origin
प्रलयः - the dissolution
स्थानं - the foundation
निधानं - the resting treasury
बीजमव्ययम् - imperishable seed

Life Application:
Know God as the goal, supporter, Lord, witness, abode, refuge, friend, origin, dissolution, foundation, and seed.

I am the Goal, the Supporter, the Lord, the Witness, the Abode, the Refuge, the Friend, the Origin, the Dissolution, the Substratum, the Storehouse, and the Seed Imperishable.

I am the supreme Goal, Supporter, Lord, Witness, Abode, Refuge, Friend, Source, Dissolution, Substratum, Storehouse, and Eternal Seed.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Goal"—according to the Samaveda and Shruti, Lord Vishnu alone is the supreme goal of liberation. He is the witness, refuge, abode, and imperishable seed of all.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The fruit of action, cosmic sustainer, all-witnessing observer, and unselfish friend is God Himself, abiding everywhere as the imperishable seed.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am the Goal, the Supporter, the Lord, the Witness, the Abode, the Refuge, the Friend, the Origin, the Dissolution, the Substratum, the Storehouse, and the Seed Imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The ultimate destination of salvation, maintainer, ruler, witness, abode, refuge removing distress, uncaused friend, source, dissolution, and imperishable seed is the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The ritual, offering, medicine, mantra, oblation, fire, parents and sustainer of the cosmos, sacred Omkara, Vedas, refuge, dissolution, immortality, death, and being and non-being—all this is solely the divine expansion of the All-Facing Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Goal, Supporter, Lord, Witness, Abode, Refuge, Friend"—I am the fruit of actions, sustainer, supreme ruler, witness of all deeds, cosmic abode, protector of the afflicted, unselfish well-wisher, origin, dissolution, and imperishable seed.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Goal" denotes not merely reward of works, but the ultimate supreme objective of seekers—Lord Vishnu, who is sustainer, master, friend, and storehouse of all.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The destination, maintainer, lord, and friend are all directly Lord Krishna Himself; He is the supreme refuge and imperishable seed of His devotees.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Supreme goal, maintainer, lord, all-witness, abode, refuge, uncaused friend, and imperishable seed—all this is the non-different nature of Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am the Goal, the Supporter, the Lord, the Witness, the Abode, the Refuge, the Friend, the Origin, the Dissolution, the Foundation, the Treasure-house, and the Seed Imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: The Supreme Self alone is the destination, protector, witness, and friend of all; during dissolution, the entire universe rests in Him as storehouse.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord alone is the sole ultimate goal, maintainer, and unselfish well-wisher of seekers; He is the imperishable seed-cause of creation and dissolution.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: I am the ultimate goal, maintainer, lord, witness, abode, friend, and imperishable seed of all beings; in Me all find rest.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The All-Witnessing and All-Befriending Lord is the supreme abode and dissolution storehouse of the manifested cosmos.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am the goal, the sustainer, the master, the witness, the abode, the refuge, and the most dear friend. I am the creation and the annihilation, the basis of everything, the resting place and the eternal seed.
