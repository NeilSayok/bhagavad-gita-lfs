Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sahasrayugaparyantamaharyad brahmaṇo viduḥ .
rātriṃ yugasahasrāntāṃ te.ahorātravido janāḥ

Word Meanings:
सहस्रयुगपर्यन्तमहर्यद् - a day spanning thousand yugas which
ब्रह्मणो - of Brahma
विदुः - they know
रात्रिं - the night
युगसहस्रान्तां - ending in thousand yugas
तेऽहोरात्रविदो - they knowers of day and night
जनाः - wise people

Life Application:
Understand cosmic time, where a single day or night of Brahma lasts a thousand cosmic ages.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Those who know that the day of Brahma lasts for a thousand yugas, and that his night also lasts for a thousand yugas, are knowers of day and night.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Those who know that Brahma"s day ends in a thousand yugas, and that his night also ends in a thousand yugas, they are knowers of day and night.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: A single day of Brahma spans a thousand mahayugas, and his night is of equal duration. At cosmic dissolution, all worlds merge into Brahma"s unmanifest state and emerge again; yet Lord Vishnu"s supreme realm remains eternal beyond this cycle.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Brahma"s day spans a thousand yugas"—those who understand the vast duration of Brahma"s day and night are true knowers of cosmic time.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Those who know that the day of Brahma lasts for a thousand yugas, and that his night also lasts for a thousand yugas, are knowers of day and night.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: A day of Brahma equals a thousand cycles of the four yugas, and his night is of equal length; because of this temporal boundary, even Brahma is subject to time.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those who know Brahma"s day lasting a thousand yugas and night lasting a thousand yugas are truly knowers of cosmic time. At daybreak, all beings manifest from the unmanifest (Brahma"s subtle state); at nightfall, they dissolve back into it. Thus the multitude of beings, helpless under karma, repeatedly emerge and dissolve.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Those who know that Brahma"s day ends in a thousand yugas, and his night ends in a thousand yugas, are knowers of day and night"—yogis meditating on cosmic time know that even Brahma"s immense day-night cycle is finite and subject to dissolution.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The duration of Brahma"s day and night reveals the limit of cosmic time; the cycle of creation and dissolution demonstrates the helplessness of souls, whereas the Lord"s realm is eternal and non-returning.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Brahma"s day and night mark the limit of cosmic time; Lord Krishna"s supreme abode alone is eternal beyond time.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Brahma"s day spans a thousand yugas"—Brahma"s full day and night equal two thousand yugas; seekers knowing this calculation comprehend the impermanence of all realms.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Those who know that Brahma"s day ends in a thousand yugas, and that his night also ends in a thousand yugas, they are knowers of day and night.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative explanation of Brahma"s day and night and cosmic time measurement.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Understanding the thousand-yuga measure of Brahma"s day and night reveals the transient nature of all worldly beings.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Even Brahma"s time is bounded; hence there is no eternality in any realm other than Lord Krishna"s.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Thousand yugas duration"—the bounds of relative time form the foundation of cosmic origin and dissolution.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
By human calculation, a thousand ages taken together form the duration of Brahma"s one day. And such also is the duration of his night.
