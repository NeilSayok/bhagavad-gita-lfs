Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
arjuna uvāca .
atha kena prayukto.ayaṃ pāpaṃ carati pūruṣaḥ .
anicchannapi vārṣṇeya balādiva niyojitaḥ

Word Meanings:
अर्जुन - O Arjuna
उवाच - said
अथ - now
केन - by what
प्रयुक्तोऽयं - impelled, this
पापं - sin
चरति - commits
पूरुषः - man
अनिच्छन्नपि - even unwilling
वार्ष्णेय - O Krishna
बलादिव - as if by force
नियोजितः - impelled

Life Application:
Investigate why you sometimes act against your best intentions, identifying the hidden internal urges that drive mistakes.
