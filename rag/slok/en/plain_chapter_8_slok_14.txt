Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ananyacetāḥ satataṃ yo māṃ smarati nityaśaḥ .
tasyāhaṃ sulabhaḥ pārtha nityayuktasya yoginaḥ

Word Meanings:
अनन्यचेताः - with undivided mind
सततं - constantly
यो - he who
मां - Me
स्मरति - remembers
नित्यशः - always
तस्याहं - to him I am
सुलभः - easily reached
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
नित्ययुक्तस्य - for the ever-united
योगिनः - yogi

Life Application:
Remember God constantly with single-pointed attention today, making the supreme path easy to attain.

I am easily attainable, O Partha, by that ever-steadfast yogi who constantly remembers Me with undivided mind.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "To him I am easily attainable"—Lord Sri Hari, highly pleased with the devotee of unswerving devotion and continuous remembrance, becomes easily accessible.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "One who remembers Me with undivided mind"—for a devotee meditating constantly on God with undivided focus, the Supreme Lord is easily accessible.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am easily attainable, O Partha, by that ever-steadfast yogi who constantly remembers Me with undivided mind.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "He who remembers Me constantly with undivided mind"—for that ever-steadfast devotee who desires no other reward or means but Me alone, I personally become easily attainable.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Restraining all sense-gates, locking the mind within the heart, and established in concentration uttering Om while remembering the Lord at departure, that ever-steadfast yogi easily attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I am easily attainable, O Partha, by that ever-steadfast yogi who remembers Me constantly with undivided mind"—for a seeker whose mind thinks of nothing other than Lord Vasudeva day after day, the Supreme Lord is effortlessly accessible.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Undivided consciousness is the primary cause of divine accessibility; the ever-steadfast yogi achieves direct realization of Vishnu effortlessly.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: For one who constantly worships Lord Krishna alone, the Lord Himself becomes easily attainable and bestows the bliss of His Self.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "To him I am easily attainable"—unswerving devotion is the most accessible path to God-realization, dispensing with arduous practices.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am easily attainable, O Partha, by that ever-steadfast yogi who constantly remembers Me with undivided mind.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Delineating the effortless attainment of God through unswerving devotion.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: For a devotee constantly remembering the Lord, Lord Vasudeva Himself becomes easily attainable to bestow salvation.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Through loving unswerving devotion, the Lord becomes bound to His devotee and is easily attained.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Undivided mind"—fixing mind in God without relying on secondary means is the secret of divine accessibility.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
For one who always remembers Me without deviation, I am easy to obtain, O son of Pritha, because of his constant engagement in devotional service.
