Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
prakṛtiṃ svāmavaṣṭabhya visṛjāmi punaḥ punaḥ .
bhūtagrāmamimaṃ kṛtsnamavaśaṃ prakṛtervaśāt

Word Meanings:
प्रकृतिं - material nature
स्वामवष्टभ्य - mastering My own
विसृजामि - I create
पुनः - again
पुनः - and again
भूतग्राममिमं - this host of beings
कृत्स्नमवशं - all helpless
प्रकृतेर्वशात् - by force of nature

Life Application:
Contemplate how divine power repeatedly projects the multitude of beings according to their natural karma.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Curbing My own Prakriti, I send forth again and again this whole multitude of beings, helpless by the force of their own nature (Karma).

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Presiding over My own nature, I send forth again and again this whole multitude of beings, helpless by the force of nature.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Animating My material nature, I project repeatedly this entire host of beings, driven helplessly by their past karmic tendencies.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Animating My nature, I send forth again and again this vast host of beings, helpless under the sway of their own nature.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Curbing My own nature, I send forth again and again this whole host of beings, helpless under the sway of nature.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Curbing My own nature, I send forth again and again this whole multitude of beings, helpless under the sway of nature.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Resorting to My own nature, I send forth again and again this whole multitude of beings, helpless under the sway of nature.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Employing His nature characterized by Maya, the Lord projects creatures bound by their past karmic impressions repeatedly into physical manifestation.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Curbing My own nature, I send forth again and again this whole multitude of beings, helpless by the force of their own nature.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The fourfold multitude of beings is helpless under the sway of their past karmic gunas; accepting My nature, I project them repeatedly according to their inherent nature.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Presiding over His own Maya-nature, the Lord projects souls bound by ignorance into cosmic existence repeatedly according to their latent karmic impressions.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: 'Curbing My own nature'—resorting to My nature characterized by Maya, I project again and again this whole multitude of beings who are helpless under the sway of ignorance and past Karmas.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The helplessness of souls bound by Karma is the cause of their rebirth, whereas Lord Vasudeva acts out of supreme independent will to project the cosmos through Nature.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Employing His own dynamic nature, the Lord brings forth the entire universe again and again according to souls' Karmas and His own divine play.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Presiding over His Maya-Prakriti, the Lord repeatedly manifests all creatures who are helpless under ignorance according to their past karmic impressions.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Employing My three-gunas-natured Prakriti, I project repeatedly this entire host of beings who are helpless under their past Karmas.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining God's absolute sovereignty versus souls' karmic dependence: The Supreme Lord repeatedly emits beings bound by Karma through His Prakriti.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Souls are helpless under their ignorance and karmic latencies; controlling His Prakriti, the Lord projects them repeatedly according to their Karmas.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Resorting to His nature, the Lord repeatedly manifests souls in the world according to their beginningless karmic seeds.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The Lord brings the multitude of creatures who are helpless under ignorance back into the cycle of creation through His Maya-Prakriti.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The whole cosmic order is under Me. By My will it is manifested again and again, and by My will it is annihilated at the end.
