Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
teṣāṃ jñānī nityayukta ekabhaktirviśiṣyate .
priyo hi jñānino.atyarthamahaṃ sa ca mama priyaḥ

Life Application:
Cultivate wise and steadfast devotion, knowing that one who loves God with single-pointed understanding is deeply cherished.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Of them, the wise one, ever steadfast and devoted to the One, excels; for I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and he is dear to Me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Among them, the wise devotee who is constantly united in mind and devoted exclusively to Me is the foremost; for I am supremely dear to the man of wisdom, and he is supremely dear to Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The wise devotee, comprehending Lord Vishnu"s unconditioned glory, renders selfless devotion, rendering him supremely dear to the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The wise devotee excels as the direct realizer of Vasudeva"s true essence; this reciprocal divine love between God and the wise soul is the ultimate liberation.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Of them, the wise one, ever steadfast and devoted to the One, excels; for I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and he is dear to Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among the four, the Jnani who considers Me alone as his ultimate goal is ever-united and supreme; I am his all-in-all and he is as dear to Me as My own Self.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Four types of virtuous men worship Me: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise. Of these, the wise one who is ever steadfast in exclusive devotion excels; for I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and he is dear to Me. Noble indeed are all these, but the wise man I consider My very Self. At the end of many births, the wise man surrenders unto Me, realizing "Vasudeva is all"; such a great soul is exceedingly rare.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Of them, the wise one ever steadfast and single-mindedly devoted excels; for I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and he is dear to Me"—because the Jnani realizes Lord Vasudeva as his own Self (Atman), the love between God and the sage is absolute and non-different.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Devotion combined with true wisdom is genuine single-minded devotion (Ekabhakti), making the wise devotee the primary recipient of Lord Vishnu"s pleasure.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Exclusive devotion infused with wisdom granted by Lord Krishna"s grace transforms the devotee into the Lord"s own intimately beloved.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Exceedingly dear to the wise"—the devotee partaking of the nectar of wisdom becomes non-different from Vasudeva, realizing absolute divine love.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Of them, the wise, ever steadfast and devoted to the One, excels; for I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and he is dear to Me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural demonstration of the supreme excellence of the wise devotee and his mutual love with the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva is the very Soul of the wise man, and the wise man is the Lord"s living body and eternal beloved.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The wise devotee remains submerged in Krishna"s love-nectar; the Lord considers him dear as His very Soul.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "The wise ever-united with exclusive devotion"—the devotee endowed with non-dual wisdom is the Lord"s true essence due to non-different love.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Of these, the one who is in full knowledge and who is always engaged in pure devotional service is the best. For I am very dear to him, and he is dear to Me.
