Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yathaidhāṃsi samiddho.agnirbhasmasātkurute.arjuna .
jñānāgniḥ sarvakarmāṇi bhasmasātkurute tathā

Word Meanings:
यथैधांसि - as kindled fuel
समिद्धोऽग्निर्भस्मसात्कुरुतेऽर्जुन - blazing fire turns to ashes, O Arjuna
ज्ञानाग्निः - the fire of wisdom
सर्वकर्माणि - all actions
भस्मसात्कुरुते - burns to ashes
तथा - so

Life Application:
Let the fire of self-knowledge consume your past guilt, regrets, and limiting beliefs today.

Sri Ramanuja states: The fire of knowledge regarding the true nature of the Self consumes the beginningless heap of accumulated karmas, rendering the seeker sinless.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:

Sri Shankaracharya states: "As a blazing fire reduces fuel to ashes, O Arjuna, so does the fire of Knowledge reduce all karmas to ashes"—explains how Self-knowledge burns up all accumulated (*Sanchita*) and upcoming (*Agami*) karmas by destroying their root, ignorance (*Avidya*).
