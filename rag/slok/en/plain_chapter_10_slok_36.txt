Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
dyūtaṃ chalayatāmasmi tejastejasvināmaham .
jayo.asmi vyavasāyo.asmi sattvaṃ sattvavatāmaham

Word Meanings:
द्यूतं - gambling (dice-play)
छलयतामस्मि - among cheats I am
तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् - splendor of the splendid I am
जयोऽस्मि - I am victory
व्यवसायोऽस्मि - I am resolve
सत्त्वं - goodness/strength
सत्त्ववतामहम् - of the virtuous I am

Life Application:
See God as the gambling of cheats, splendor of the splendid, victory, resolve, and purity of the pure.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am the gambling of deceivers, the splendor of the splendid; I am victory, resolve (effort), and the virtue/goodness of the good.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I am the gambling of the deceitful; I am the splendor of the splendid; I am victory, I am determination, and the goodness of the good.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I am the gambling of deceivers, the radiance of the radiant. I am victory, I am effort, and I am the goodness of the good.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among deceptive arts, Gambling; the Splendor of the splendid; Victory; unwavering Resolve; and the Goodness of the righteous represent the Lord.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among deceivers I am gambling; among the splendid I am splendor; I am victory, determination, and the goodness of the good.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Among deceivers I am gambling; among the splendid I am splendor; I am victory, determination, and the goodness of the good.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
I am the gambling of deceivers and the splendor of the splendid; I am victory, determination, and the goodness of the good.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Gambling among deceptions, splendor among the splendid, victory among victors, enterprise among effort-makers, and purity among the good are the Lord's chief opulences.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among deceivers I am gambling, and among the splendid I am splendor; I am victory, determination, and the goodness of the good.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among deceptions, gambling; among the splendid, splendor; among victors, victory; among enterprise-makers, resolve; and among good men, virtue is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among deceptions I am Gambling; among the splendid I am Splendor; I am Victory of the victorious; Resolve of the enterprising; and Goodness of the virtuous.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Gambling among deceptions, splendor among the brilliant, victory, resolve, and goodness among the pure manifest God's influence.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Gambling among deceivers, power among the mighty, victory among conquerors, effort among achievers, and goodness among the virtuous is My divine opulence.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am the gambling of deceivers, the splendor of the splendid; I am victory, determination, and the goodness of the good.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Gambling's allure, splendor's prowess, victory's joy, resolve's certainty, and purity's grace express God.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Gambling, splendor, victory, resolve, and goodness symbolize the Lord's wondrous power and governance in their realms.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Among deceivers I am gambling; I am splendor, victory, determination, and the goodness of the good.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Gambling among deceivers, splendor, victory, resolve, and goodness represent the Lord's transcendental power.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am also the gambling of cheats, and of the splendid I am the splendor. I am victory, I am adventure, and I am the strength of the strong.
