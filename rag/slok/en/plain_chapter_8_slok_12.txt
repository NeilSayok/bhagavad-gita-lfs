Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sarvadvārāṇi saṃyamya mano hṛdi nirudhya ca .
mūdhnyā^^rdhāyātmanaḥ prāṇamāsthito yogadhāraṇām

Word Meanings:
सर्वद्वाराणि - all the sensory gates
संयम्य - controlling
मनो - the mind
हृदि - within the heart
निरुध्य - confining
च - and
मूध्न्यार्धायात्मनः - fixing in the head one's
प्राणमास्थितो - life-breath established in
योगधारणाम् - concentration of yoga

Life Application:
Control all sensory gates, lock the mind in the heart, and draw vital breath to the head, established in yoga.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Having restrained all the gates of the body, confined the mind within the heart, and fixed the prana in the head, established in the practice of concentration—

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Having restrained all the gates, confined the mind in the heart, and fixed the prana in the head, established in yogic concentration—

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Controlling all the gates of the body, confining the mind within the heart-center, and fixing the life-force in the crown of the head through yogic absorption—

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Having restrained all the gates, confined the mind in the heart, and fixed the prana in the head, established in yogic concentration—

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Having restrained all the gates, confined the mind in the heart, and fixed the prana in the head, established in yogic concentration—

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Raising the life-breath through the Brahma-nadi (Sushumna) to the head and meditating on the sacred monosyllable Om as Lord Vishnu while quitting the body, the seeker attains the Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Restraining the nine sense gates, locking the mind in the heart, and establishing life-breath in the crown of the head constitutes yogic concentration.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Having restrained all the gates of the body, confined the mind within the heart, and fixed the prana in the head, established in the practice of concentration—

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Withdraw all senses from outer objects, fix the mind in the heart, and draw the life-force up to the crown of the head through Sushumna to engage in yogic concentration.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Restraining all sense-gates, locking the mind within the heart, and established in concentration uttering Om while remembering the Lord at departure, that ever-steadfast yogi easily attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Restraining all nine gates of perception from external objects, locking the willing mind inside the heart-lotus, and elevating the prana via Sushumna to the crown of the head, one must abide in unshakable yogic concentration.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The sacred syllable Om is the primary name of Supreme Lord Vishnu; restraining mind and life-breath in Sushumna while uttering Om at death bestows liberation.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Restraining senses and contemplating Lord Krishna in the heart with the sacred syllable Om at departure is the means to attain the Divine Realm.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Confining mind in heart"—withdrawing senses, locking mind in heart chakra and life-breath in head chakra marks the maturity of yogic concentration.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Having restrained all the gates, confined the mind in the heart, and fixed the prana in the head, established in yogic concentration—

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative explanation of the yogic steps of sense-restraint, mind-control, and life-breath concentration.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Drawing in all sense activities, confining mind to heart, and raising prana to crown constitutes yogic concentration.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Gathering mind and senses to become absorbed in the Lord within the heart is the supreme Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Restraint of the nine gates and locking prana in the brahmarandhra is the true nature of "Yogadharana".

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The condition of yoga is one of detachment from all sensory engagements. Closing all the doors of the senses and fixing the mind on the heart and the life air at the top of the head, one establishes himself in yoga.
