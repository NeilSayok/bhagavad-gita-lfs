Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
avyaktaṃ vyaktimāpannaṃ manyante māmabuddhayaḥ .
paraṃ bhāvamajānanto mamāvyayamanuttamam

Word Meanings:
अव्यक्तं - unmanifest (imperceptible)
व्यक्तिमापन्नं - assumed human form
मन्यन्ते - think / consider
मामबुद्धयः - Me the foolish
परं - supreme (transcendent)
भावमजानन्तो - not knowing My nature
ममाव्ययमनुत्तमम् - My imperishable supreme state

Life Application:
Do not mistake the unmanifest supreme divine reality for a mere temporary physical form.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Unintelligent men think of Me, the Unmanifest, as having come into manifestation, not knowing My supreme, imperishable, unexcelled nature.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Unintelligent men consider Me, the Unmanifest, as having assumed manifestation, not knowing My supreme, imperishable, and unexcelled nature.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Unintelligent men, unaware of My supreme, imperishable, unexcelled divine nature, imagine Me, the Unmanifest Eternal Spirit, to have assumed a mortal human manifestation.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Unintelligent men think of Me, the Unmanifest, as having come into manifestation, not knowing My supreme, imperishable, unexcelled nature.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Unmanifest assumed manifestation"—Lord Vishnu"s Avatar form is eternally divine and non-material (Aprakrita); considering It a material birth is sheer ignorance.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "The unintelligent consider Me assumed manifestation"—foolish men mistake the all-pervading unconditioned Brahman Vasudeva for an ordinary mortal bound by physical body.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Unintelligent men think of Me, the Unmanifest, as having come into manifestation, not knowing My supreme, imperishable, unexcelled nature.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Ignorant men mistake Lord Vasudeva Krishna"s divine Avatar for an ordinary mortal birth driven by Karma, failing to comprehend His eternal transcendental nature.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Unintelligent men, not knowing My supreme, unexcelled, imperishable state, consider Me, the Unmanifest, to have assumed human birth. Veiled by My divine Yoga-Maya, I am not revealed to all; hence this deluded world fails to comprehend Me, the Unborn and Imperishable Lord.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Unintelligent men consider Me, the Unmanifest, to have assumed manifestation, not knowing My supreme, imperishable, unexcelled nature"—reproves those who degrade Supreme Lord Vasudeva into an ordinary mortal subject to birth and rebirth.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Vishnu"s form is flawless and complete with divine attributes; ignorant men mistake His advent for ordinary physical embodiment.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna"s eternal supernatural personal form is the Supreme Brahman itself; considering It material deprives fools of divine bliss.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Unmanifest assumed body"—those possessed of ignorant vision consider Sat-Chit-Ananda Brahman as an ordinary body born of Maya evolutes.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Unintelligent men consider Me, the Unmanifest, as having assumed manifestation, not knowing My supreme, imperishable, and unexcelled nature.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of Supreme Ishvara"s imperishable, unmanifest essence beyond material delusion.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Mistaking Lord Vasudeva"s eternal supernatural divine form for an ordinary human body is a mark of deep ignorance.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna"s divine form is eternally accomplished Sat-Chit-Ananda; regarding It as transient is the delusion of the ignorant.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Unmanifest assumed form"—the mind clouded by ignorance fails to grasp the eternal unmanifest Self and reduces It to a finite soul.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Unintelligent men, who do not know Me perfectly, think that I, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Krishna, was impersonal before and have now assumed this personality. Due to their small knowledge, they do not know My higher nature, which is imperishable and supreme.
