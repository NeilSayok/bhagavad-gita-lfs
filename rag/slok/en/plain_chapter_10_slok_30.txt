Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
prahlādaścāsmi daityānāṃ kālaḥ kalayatāmaham .
mṛgāṇāṃ ca mṛgendro.ahaṃ vainateyaśca pakṣiṇām

Word Meanings:
प्रह्लादश्चास्मि - I am Prahlada and
दैत्यानां - among demons (Daityas)
कालः - time
कलयतामहम् - among reckoners I am
मृगाणां - among beasts
च - and
मृगेन्द्रोऽहं - lion (king of beasts) I am
वैनतेयश्च - Garuda (son of Vinata) and
पक्षिणाम् - among birds

Life Application:
See God as Prahlada among demons, Time among reckoners, the lion among beasts, and Garuda among birds.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among Daityas (demons) I am Prahlada; among reckoners/subduers I am Time; among beasts I am the Lion (King of beasts), and among birds I am Garuda (son of Vinata).

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
And I am Prahlada among Daityas; Time among reckoners am I; among beasts I am the lord of beasts (lion); and Vainateya (Garuda) among birds.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of the Daityas I am Prahlada; of reckoners I am Time. Of beasts I am the Lion, king of beasts, and of birds I am Garuda.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among Daityas, the supreme devotee Prahlada; among reckoners and transformers, Time; among beasts, the Lion (symbol of courage); and among birds, Garuda (son of Vinata) symbolizes the Divine.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among Daityas I am Prahlada; among reckoners I am Time; among beasts I am the lion; and among birds I am Garuda.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Among Daityas I am Prahlada; among reckoners I am Time; among beasts I am the lion; among birds I am Garuda.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The supreme devotee Prahlada born in the demonic lineage, all-destroying Time among reckoners, the mighty Lion among beasts, and Garuda (bearer of Vishnu) among birds are the Lord's chief Vibhutis.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among Daityas I am Prahlada; among reckoners I am Time; among beasts I am the lion, and among birds I am Garuda.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among descendants of Diti, the great devotee Prahlada; among reckoners, Time; among wild beasts, the Lion; and among birds, Garuda is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among Daityas (descendants of Diti) I am Prahlada; among reckoners or measures I am Time; among beasts I am the Lion (King of beasts); and among birds I am Garuda (son of Vinata).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Prahlada devoted to God among Daityas, all-destroying Time, the Lion among beasts, and Garuda among birds reveal the majesty of the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Among Daityas, Prahlada (crown jewel of devotees); among reckoners, Great Time; among beasts, the Lion; and among birds, Garuda (carrier of Vishnu) is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am Prahlada among Daityas; Time among reckoners; among beasts I am the lion, and among birds Garuda.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Prahlada's devotion among Daityas, Time's irresistible force among reckoners, the Lion's valor among beasts, and Garuda's speed among birds reflect God.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Prahlada, Time, Lion, and Garuda represent the Lord's supreme glory and divine will in their respective categories.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
