Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yo māṃ paśyati sarvatra sarvaṃ ca mayi paśyati .
tasyāhaṃ na praṇaśyāmi sa ca me na praṇaśyati

Life Application:
Realize that one who sees God everywhere and sees everything in God is never separated from divine love.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
He who sees Me everywhere and sees all things in Me, he does not become separated from Me, nor do I become separated from him.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
He who sees Me everywhere and sees all things in Me, he is never lost to Me, nor am I ever lost to him.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
He who sees Me, the Supreme Lord Vasudeva, everywhere in all beings and beholds all creation abiding in Me—to that loving devotee I am never lost or hidden, nor is he ever separated from Me.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
He who sees Me everywhere and sees all things in Me, he does not become separated from Me, nor do I become separated from him.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
He who sees Me everywhere and sees all things in Me, he does not become separated from Me, nor do I become separated from him.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
He who sees Me everywhere and sees all things in Me, he is never lost to Me, nor am I ever lost to him.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
He who sees Me everywhere and sees all things in Me, he is never lost to Me, nor am I ever lost to him.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "I am never lost to him"—the unswerving devotee who sees Vishnu everywhere never falls from grace; he remains eternally under Lord Vishnu"s supreme protection.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: For the sage who beholds non-difference between Lord Vasudeva and the universe, God is never unmanifest; He remains eternally revealed.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
He who sees Me everywhere and sees all things in Me, he does not become separated from Me, nor do I become separated from him.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: He who beholds My divine likeness in all conscious souls and beholds all souls in Me, he is never separated from Me, nor I from him.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: He whose heart is harmonized by Yoga sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings abiding in the Self, beholding the same everywhere. He who sees Me everywhere and sees all in Me, to him I am never lost, nor is he ever lost to Me. The Yogi who, established in unity, worships Me dwelling in all beings, lives in Me whatever be his mode of life. O Arjuna, he who sees equality in all beings by analogy with himself, whether in pleasure or in pain, is regarded as the highest Yogi.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "He who sees Me everywhere and sees all things in Me, he is never lost to Me, nor am I ever lost to him"—declares the eternal inseparable identity (*Abheda*) between the Supreme Self Vasudeva and the enlightened sage.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Is never lost" means that the Supreme Lord never deprives His enlightened devotee of eternal Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: He who beholds Krishna in all and all in Krishna becomes the Lord"s eternally beloved intimate devotee.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I am never lost to him"—in non-dual realization, distinction between worshipper and Worshipped dissolves, establishing eternal oneness.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
He who sees Me everywhere and sees all things in Me, he is never lost to Me, nor am I ever lost to him.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of seeing the Divine everywhere and the unbreakable reciprocal presence of God and devotee.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The devotee experiencing Lord Vasudeva"s all-pervading form in all beings abides in eternal closeness with the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna never lets His unswerving loving devotee slip out of His divine sight.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "I am never lost to him"—in non-dual knowledge, the Self is Brahman; thus eternal realization is ever accomplished.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
For one who sees Me everywhere and sees everything in Me, I am never lost, nor is he ever lost to Me.
