Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
tasmādajñānasambhūtaṃ hṛtsthaṃ jñānāsinātmanaḥ .
chittvainaṃ saṃśayaṃ yogamātiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhārata

Word Meanings:
तस्मादज्ञानसम्भूतं - therefore born of ignorance
हृत्स्थं - residing in heart
ज्ञानासिनात्मनः - by sword of wisdom
छित्त्वैनं - cutting asunder this
संशयं - doubt
योगमातिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ - take refuge in Yoga, arise!
भारत - O descendant of Bharata

Life Application:
Cut through lingering doubts with the sword of self-knowledge, standing up courageously to fulfill your life's purpose.
