Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yadṛcchālābhasantuṣṭo dvandvātīto vimatsaraḥ .
samaḥ siddhāvasiddhau ca kṛtvāpi na nibadhyate

Word Meanings:
यदृच्छालाभसन्तुष्टो - satisfied with whatever comes unsought
द्वन्द्वातीतो - transcending dualities
विमत्सरः - free from jealousy
समः - equal
सिद्धावसिद्धौ - in success and failure
च - and
कृत्वापि - even though acting
न - not
निबध्यते - is bound

Life Application:
Accept unexpected circumstances gracefully today, remaining free from envy and balanced through win or loss.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Content with whatever comes to him unasked, balanced in pleasure and pain, free from envy, even-minded in success and failure, though acting he is not bound.

Sri Ramanuja states: "Even while acting he is not bound"—content with spontaneous gains, equal in pleasure and pain, devoid of envy, an aspirant acting in the world incurs no bondage.

Sri Jayatirtha states: Transcendence of dualities and equanimity form the supreme weapons neutralizing karmic taint.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:

Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Equal in success and failure"—the wise person maintaining equanimity in gain or loss remains unbound by karma while working out Prarabdha.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A Karma-yogi depending on Vasudeva, remaining equal in profit or loss, moves in the world completely unattached.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: For a practitioner absorbed in Krishna"s love, all gains and losses become divine grace, maintaining unbroken equanimity.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Beyond dualities, free from envy"—complete freedom of mind from all dualities marks the perfection of non-action.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
He who is satisfied with gain which comes of its own accord, who is free from duality and does not envy, who is steady in both success and failure, is never entangled, although performing actions.
