Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
janma karma ca me divyamevaṃ yo vetti tattvataḥ .
tyaktvā dehaṃ punarjanma naiti māmeti so.arjuna

Word Meanings:
जन्म - birth
कर्म - action
च - and
मे - my
दिव्यमेवं - divine thus
यो - he who
वेत्ति - knows
तत्त्वतः - in reality
त्यक्त्वा - having abandoned
देहं - the body
पुनर्जन्म - rebirth
नैति - does not attain
मामेति - comes to Me
सोऽर्जुन - he, O Arjuna

Life Application:
Contemplate the divine nature behind sacred events to lift your awareness above temporary earthly attachments.

Sri Madhavacharya states: "He comes to Me, O Arjuna"—knowledge of the eternal divine nature of Lord Vishnu"s avatars and activities liberates the soul, bringing it to the Lord"s abode.

Sri Anandagiri states: Truthful knowledge of the Lord"s divine birth and activities destroys all sins of the seeker, granting immediate liberation.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: True understanding of the mystery of divine incarnation dissolves all karmic obstacles, bringing the seeker directly to God.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Through these slokas the Lord proves that His birth and action are not karmic like ordinary beings, but a divine sport of Pure Consciousness; knowing this in truth, one attains liberation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "He who thus knows in truth My divine birth and action, having abandoned the body, is not born again; he comes to Me, O Arjuna"—promises Moksha through the essential realization of the Lord"s transcendental nature (*Divya-Janma-Karma*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Truthful knowledge of the divine nature of Vishnu"s descents is the direct instrument for ultimate Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A loving devotee contemplating Krishna"s divine birth and activities severs all material bonds, entering the Lord"s eternal realm.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "He comes to Me, O Arjuna"—philosophical knowledge of God"s Saguna descents becomes the doorway to Nirguna Brahman-realization, bestowing Jivanmukti.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Meditation on Vasudeva"s divine birth and deeds destroys all sins, bestowing the eternal Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the soul beholds and experiences Lord Krishna"s eternal divine nature, its rebirth terminates forever.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Leaving the body he is not born again"—realizing the divine manifestation of God uproots nescience, bestowing ultimate Mukti.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
