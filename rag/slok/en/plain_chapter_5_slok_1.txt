Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
arjuna uvāca .
saṃnyāsaṃ karmaṇāṃ kṛṣṇa punaryogaṃ ca śaṃsasi .
yacchreya etayorekaṃ tanme brūhi suniścitam

Word Meanings:
अर्जुन - Prince Arjuna
उवाच - said
संन्यासं - renunciation
कर्मणां - of actions
कृष्ण - O Krishna
पुनर्योगं - again yoga of action
च - and
शंससि - You praise
यच्छ्रेय - which is better
एतयोरेकं - of these two, one
तन्मे - that to me
ब्रूहि - tell
सुनिश्चितम् - conclusively

Life Application:
Ask for clear, decisive guidance when choosing between stepping back from responsibilities and taking active, dedicated action.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Renunciation of action"—both renunciation of action (offering all to Vishnu) and Karma-Yoga are means to attain the Lord, but Arjuna seeks clarity on the superior path.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Hearing the praise of both renunciation of action and practice of Karma-Yoga at the end of Chapter 4, Arjuna experiences doubt regarding their mutual opposition and seeks a decisive path.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Arjuna said: Renunciation of actions, O Krishna, You praise, and again Yoga. Tell me conclusively that which is the better of the two.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Arjuna inquires which of the two—renunciation of action (Jnana-nishta) or performance of action (Karma-yoga)—is easier and quicker for a seeker of liberation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Arjuna said: O Krishna, You commend renunciation of action and again the Yoga of action; which one of these two is superior? Please tell me that for certain.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Arjuna said: Renunciation of actions, O Krishna, You praise, and again Yoga. Tell me conclusively that which is the better of the two"—formulates Arjuna"s confusion between Karma-Sannyasa (renunciation of work) and Karma-Yoga (performance of work).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Arjuna"s inquiry serves to clarify that devotion to God is the ultimate goal for both householders and renunciates.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Offering actions in the service of Lord Krishna is the true essence of both renunciation and Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Renunciation of actions"—the apparent conflict between total physical renunciation of work and dedicated Karma-Yoga prompts Arjuna"s inquiry.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Arjuna said: Renunciation of actions, O Krishna, You praise, and again Yoga. Tell me conclusively that which is the better of the two.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of the distinct nature, non-contradiction, and relative merit of Sannyasa and Karma-Yoga.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Selfless Karma-Yoga offered for Vasudeva"s pleasure is the true gateway to renunciation; Arjuna seeks confirmation of this.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Arjuna asks whether total renunciation in Krishna"s service or devotional work (Karma-Yoga) is superior.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Renunciation of actions"—Arjuna"s question aims to clarify the precise distinction between devotion to Knowledge and devotion to Action.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Arjuna said: O Krishna, first of all You ask me to renounce work, and then again You recommend work with devotion. Now will You kindly tell me definitely which of the two is more beneficial?
