Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
api cetsudurācāro bhajate māmananyabhāk .
sādhureva sa mantavyaḥ samyagvyavasito hi saḥ

Word Meanings:
अपि - even
चेत्सुदुराचारो - even if of very evil conduct
भजते - worships
मामनन्यभाक् - Me with single devotion
साधुरेव - righteous indeed
स - he
मन्तव्यः - should be regarded
सम्यग्व्यवसितो - rightly resolved
हि - certainly
सः - he

Life Application:
Even if a person of bad conduct worships God with undivided devotion, consider them righteous, for they have resolved rightly.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Even if the most sinful person worships Me with unswerving devotion, he must be regarded as righteous, for he has rightly resolved.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Even if the most sinful person worships Me with unswerving devotion, he must be regarded as righteous, for he has rightly resolved.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Even if a person of most degraded conduct worships Me with exclusive devotion, he must be regarded as righteous, for he has made the correct supreme resolve.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Even if most wicked"—by virtue of past merits, if a sinner resolves firmly upon devotion to Lord Hari, he is counted among the virtuous, for devotion consumes all sins.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Devotion to God possesses miraculous power; despite past wickedness, if a soul takes exclusive refuge in the Lord, his noble resolve makes him venerable like a saint.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Even if the most sinful person worships Me with unswerving devotion, he must be regarded as righteous, for he has rightly resolved.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Even a wicked transgressor of virtues, if he renounces all worldly objects and makes the single resolve to worship Lord Vasudeva, is worthy of being revered as a foremost devotee.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord is equal to all beings, without hatred or partiality; yet those who worship Him with devotion abide in Him, and He in them. Even a deeply wicked person worshipping with undivided devotion becomes virtuous and never perishes.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Even if a most sinful person worships Me with undivided devotion"—if a greatly degraded sinner surrenders solely to Lord Vasudeva with undivided mind, he must be regarded as saintly, because he has rightly resolved that devotion to God is the sole supreme path.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Unswerving devotion to God destroys all sins; the sinner"s right resolve of surrender to Lord Vishnu instantly renders him venerable like a saint.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Devotion to God depends neither on caste nor past conduct; the firm resolve of taking exclusive refuge in Krishna makes the soul saintly.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "He must be regarded as righteous"—an exclusive devotee, even if previously sinful, is deemed saintly owing to his firm resolve in Lord Vasudeva, which burns all sins.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Even if the most sinful person worships Me with unswerving devotion, he must be regarded as righteous, for he has rightly resolved.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the purifying power of devotion; the firm resolve of exclusive divine refuge burns all sins of even a wicked person, establishing him in saintliness.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The resolve of unswerving surrender is the ultimate atonement for all sins, instantly transforming a sinner into a revered saint eligible for grace.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Even a great sinner worshipping with exclusive love is equal to a saint, for taking refuge in the Lord instantly purifies his heart.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Exclusive devotion washes away all impurities of mind; "right resolve" is the true hallmark of saintliness.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Even if one commits the most abominable action, if he is engaged in devotional service he is to be considered saintly because he is properly situated in his determination.
