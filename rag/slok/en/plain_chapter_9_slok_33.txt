Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
kiṃ punarbrāhmaṇāḥ puṇyā bhaktā rājarṣayastathā .
anityamasukhaṃ lokamimaṃ prāpya bhajasva mām

Word Meanings:
किं - how much more
पुनर्ब्राह्मणाः - then of Brahmanas
पुण्या - holy
भक्ता - devoted
राजर्षयस्तथा - and royal sages
अनित्यमसुखं - transient and joyless
लोकमिमं - this world
प्राप्य - having attained
भजस्व - worship
माम् - Me

Life Application:
How much more then should noble seekers turn to God; having entered this transient unhappy world, worship the Divine.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
How much more then holy Brahmanas and devoted royal sages! Having obtained this impermanent and unhappy world, do you worship Me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
How much more then holy Brahmanas and devoted royal sages! Having obtained this impermanent and unhappy world, do you worship Me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
How much more then holy Brahmanas and devoted royal sages! Therefore, having come into this transient and joyless world, devote yourself constantly unto Me.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
How much more then holy Brahmanas and devoted royal sages! Having obtained this impermanent and unhappy world, do you worship Me.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
How much more then holy Brahmanas and devoted royal sages! Having obtained this impermanent and unhappy world, do you worship Me.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
How much more then holy Brahmanas and devoted royal sages! Having obtained this impermanent and unhappy world, do you worship Me.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
How much more then holy Brahmanas and devoted royal sages! Having obtained this impermanent and unhappy world, do you worship Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: If souls of sinful origin attain liberation through devotion, how much more so pious Brahmanas and royal sages! Therefore, abandon this transient joyless world and worship the Lord.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
How much more then holy Brahmanas and devoted royal sages! Having obtained this impermanent and unhappy world, do you worship Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Salvation is doubly assured for pure-minded pious Brahmanas and royal sages; therefore, to escape this temporary and painful world, worship the Supreme Lord continuously.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: O Arjuna! Taking exclusive refuge in Me, even women, Vaishyas, Shudras, and those of low origin attain the supreme goal; how much more then pious Brahmanas and royal sages! Therefore, having come into this impermanent joyless world, fix your mind on Me, become My exclusive devotee, and surrender unto Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "How much more then holy Brahmanas and royal sages"—if low-born people attain liberation, how much more certain is the goal for pure Brahmanas and royal sages! Having obtained this transient and painful mortal embodiment, engage unceasingly in My devotion.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: When even low-born souls cross over by divine grace, what of learned Brahmanas and royal sages! Having taken birth in this impermanent world, worship Lord Krishna with undivided devotion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "How much more then holy Brahmanas"—if even low-born souls attain Mukti through divine refuge, the salvation of pure Brahmanas is guaranteed; hence in this transient joyless world, worship the Supreme Lord alone.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: How much more then holy Brahmanas and devoted royal sages! Having obtained this impermanent and unhappy world, do you worship Me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Demonstrating the ease of devotion and impermanence of the world; human body is transient and sorrowful, hence one should immediately surrender to God.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Pious Brahmanas and royal sages are naturally eligible for Mukti; recognizing the rarity of human birth in this transient world, worship the Lord endlessly.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When even low-born souls cross over, what doubt for noble devotees! In this perishable world, worshipping the Lord is the sole essence.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The world is transient and an abode of sorrow; a seeker of liberation should abandon fleeting pleasures and engage exclusively in divine devotion.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
How much more this is so of the righteous brahmanas, the devotees and the saintly kings. Therefore, having come to this temporary, miserable world, engage in loving service unto Me.
