Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
tasmātsarveṣu kāleṣu māmanusmara yudhya ca .
mayyarpitamanobuddhirmāmevaiṣyasyasaṃśayaḥ

Word Meanings:
तस्मात्सर्वेषु - therefore at all
कालेषु - times
मामनुस्मर - remember Me
युध्य - fight
च - and
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयः - with mind fixed on Me you shall reach Me without doubt

Life Application:
Remember God constantly at all times while performing your duty; with mind and intellect dedicated, you will reach Him.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Therefore at all times remember Me and fight; with mind and intellect fixed on Me, you shall doubtless come to Me alone.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Therefore at all times remember Me and fight; with mind and intellect fixed on Me, you shall doubtless come to Me alone.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Therefore, remember Me at all times and perform your duty to fight; with your mind and intellect completely surrendered unto Me, you shall without doubt come to Me alone.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Therefore at all times remember Me and fight; with mind and intellect fixed on Me, you shall doubtless come to Me alone.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Therefore at all times remember Me and fight; with mind and intellect fixed on Me, you shall doubtless come to Me alone.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Therefore at all times remember Me and fight; with mind and intellect fixed on Me, you shall doubtless come to Me alone.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Therefore at all times remember Me and fight; with mind and intellect fixed on Me, you shall doubtless come to Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Therefore at all times"—remembering Lord Hari continuously while engaged in worldly duty is the true practice of devotion and unselfish Karma-Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Therefore at all times remember Me"—remembrance at death depends on lifelong discipline; hence constantly remembering God while fulfilling duty (fighting) is the true path to Mukti.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Therefore at all times remember Me and fight; with mind and intellect fixed on Me, you shall doubtless come to Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Therefore remember Me at all times"—to secure My remembrance at death, perform your prescribed duties of life (such as war) while constantly meditating on Me.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: One who withdraws the mind from other objects and constantly meditates through yoga practice on the Supreme Person (inconceivable, omniscient, sun-brilliant), fixing the life-force between the eyebrows at death with steady devotion, attains the Supreme Divine Person.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Therefore at all times remember Me and fight"—since final remembrance depends on lifelong meditation, O Arjuna, continuously contemplate Me, Lord Vasudeva, while performing your duty to fight. With mind and intellect dedicated to Me, you shall without doubt attain Me.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Synthesis of duty and continuous divine remembrance grants liberation; one should perform prescribed duties with mind and intellect surrendered to Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Practicing one"s duty while constantly contemplating Lord Krishna forms the foundational spirit of Pushti devotion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Remember Me and fight"—surrendering all mental modes to Lord Vasudeva while executing assigned duties unselfishly is the path to God-realization.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Therefore at all times remember Me and fight; with mind and intellect fixed on Me, you shall doubtless come to Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural demonstration of the necessity of practicing one"s duty along with continuous remembrance of God.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Performing one"s duties alongside continuous meditation on the Lord grants liberation without a doubt.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Maintaining continuous meditation on Lord Krishna while fulfilling one"s duties is the key to exclusive devotion.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Offering mind and intellect to Brahman while performing actions frees the seeker from karmic bonds to reach the Supreme Self.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Therefore, Arjuna, you should always think of Me in the form of Krishna and at the same time carry out your prescribed duty of fighting. With your activities dedicated to Me and your mind and intelligence fixed on Me, you will attain Me without doubt.
