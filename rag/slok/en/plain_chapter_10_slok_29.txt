Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
anantaścāsmi nāgānāṃ varuṇo yādasāmaham .
pitṝṇāmaryamā cāsmi yamaḥ saṃyamatāmaham

Word Meanings:
अनन्तश्चास्मि - I am Ananta (Sheshanaga) and
नागानां - among celestial serpents
वरुणो - Varuna
यादसामहम् - among water deities I am
पितॄणामर्यमा - Aryama among ancestors
चास्मि - and I am
यमः - Yama
संयमतामहम् - among dispensers of law I am

Life Application:
Recognize God as Ananta among Nagas, Varuna among water-deities, Aryaman among ancestors, and Yama among law-enforcers.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among Nagas I am Ananta (Sheshanaga); among water-dwellers I am Varuna; among ancestors I am Aryama, and among subduers/controllers I am Yama (god of justice).

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I am Ananta among Nagas; I am Varuna among water-gods; Aryaman among ancestors I am, and Yama among subduers I am.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of the multi-headed Nagas I am Ananta; of water-dwellers I am Varuna. Of ancestors I am Aryama, and of subduers I am Yama, god of justice.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among multi-hooded Nagas, Ananta (Shesha); among aquatic beings, Lord Varuna; among ancestors, Aryama; and among subduers and judges, Yama (Lord of justice) represents the Supreme Divine.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among Nagas I am Ananta; among water-gods I am Varuna; among ancestors I am Aryaman; among subduers I am Yama.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Among Nagas I am Ananta; among water-dwellers I am Varuna; among ancestors I am Aryaman; among subduers I am Yama.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The thousand-hooded Naga King Ananta, Lord Varuna among aquatic beings, King Aryama among ancestors, and Yama who regulates all beings according to Karma are the Lord's chief Vibhutis.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among Nagas I am Ananta; among water-dwellers I am Varuna; among ancestors I am Aryama, and among subduers I am Yama.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among multi-hooded Nagas, Ananta; among aquatic beings, Varuna; among ancestors, Aryama; and among governing figures, Yama is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among Nagas (non-poisonous multi-hooded serpents) I am Ananta (Shesha); among water-gods I am Varuna; among ancestors I am Aryama (king of ancestors); and among subduers (dispensers of punishment) I am Yama.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Shesha Ananta, Lord Varuna, Father Aryama, and Justicer Yama display the Lord's supreme divine sovereignty.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Among Nagas, Ananta (Shesha); among aquatic beings, Lord Varuna; among ancestors, Aryama; and among dispensers of justice, Dharmaraja Yama is My divine opulence.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am Ananta among Nagas; Varuna among water-dwellers; Aryaman among ancestors, and Yama among subduers.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Shesha's support among Nagas, Varuna's lordship among waters, Aryama's rank among ancestors, and Yama's impartial justice reveal the Lord.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Ananta among Nagas, Varuna, ancestral lord Aryama, and Yama govern their respective realms solely by the Lord's divine will.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Among Nagas I am Ananta; among water-dwellers I am Varuna; among ancestors I am Aryama; and among subduers I am Yama.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Ananta among Nagas, Varuna among waters, Aryama among ancestors, and Yama among regulators represent the Supreme Divine.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Of the heavenly Nāga snakes I am Ananta, and among the aquatics I am the demigod Varuṇa. Of departed ancestors I am Aryamā, and among the dispensers of law I am Yama, the lord of death.
