Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
sarvametadṛtaṃ manye yanmāṃ vadasi keśava .
na hi te bhagavanvyaktiṃ vidurdevā na dānavāḥ

Word Meanings:
सर्वमेतदृतं - all this as truth
मन्ये - I believe
यन्मां - which to me
वदसि - You tell
केशव - O Kesava.
न - not
हि - certainly
ते - they
भगवन्व्यक्तिं - O Lord, manifestation
विदुर्देवा - the gods know
न - not
दानवाः - demons

Life Application:
Accept all divine words as absolute truth, recognizing that neither gods nor demons fully know God's manifestation.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
O Keshava, I accept all this that You say to me as true. Neither the gods nor the demons, O Lord, know Your divine manifestation.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I believe all this that Thou sayest to me to be true, O Krishna; verily, O blessed Lord! neither the gods nor the demons know Thy manifestation.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O Keshava, I accept all that You tell me to be true. Neither the gods nor the demons, O Lord, comprehend Your divine manifestation.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Expressing unreserved faith in Sri Krishna, Arjuna states that neither the gods nor the demons can comprehend the Lord's Supreme Being, for they are created by Him. The divine nature can only be understood through His grace.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Arjuna said: O Keshava, I accept all that You speak to me to be true. O Lord, neither gods nor demons comprehend Your origin. O Supreme Being, Creator of beings, Lord of creatures, God of gods, Ruler of the universe, You alone know Yourself by Yourself!

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Arjuna said: O Keshava, I accept as true all that You speak to me. Neither gods nor demons, O Lord, know Your manifestation. You Yourself, O Supreme Person, know Yourself by Yourself, O Creator of beings, Lord of creatures, God of gods, Ruler of the universe!

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
O Keshava, I accept all this that You say to me to be true. Neither the gods nor the demons, O Lord, comprehend Your manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Param Brahman" signifies the All-complete, supreme entity that sustains the entire universe. Arjuna acknowledges that Lord Krishna is the Supreme Abode, Supreme Purifier, Eternal Divine Person, and All-pervading Lord declared in the Vedas.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Arjuna says that O Keshava, whatever You reveal to me, I accept not as figurative praise but as Absolute Truth. Neither the gods nor the demons can measure the unfathomable majesty of the Lord.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
O Keshava, I accept all that You tell me to be true. Neither the gods nor the demons, O Lord, know Your divine Form.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: O Keshava, I accept as literal Truth all that emanates from Your lotus lips, not as exaggerated praise. Neither demigods nor demons, with their limited intellects, can comprehend Your boundless glory.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Abhinavagupta did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: O Keshava (Ruler of Brahma and Siva), all this that You tell me, as also what the sages say, I hold to be true. Neither the gods nor the demons, O Lord, know Your divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Hearing the Lord's teachings, Arjuna validates and extols the supreme majesty of God. All authoritative scriptures and seers—Vyasa, Narada, etc.—proclaim Him alone as the Supreme Brahman and Ultimate Abode.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Hearing the Lord's divine words, Arjuna accepts His Sat-Chit-Ananda Supreme Brahman nature and declares Him to be the Ultimate Abode and Supreme Divine Being.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: O Keshava, I accept all that You speak unto me as absolute truth. O Lord of all wisdom and majesty, neither the gods with their sattvic minds nor the demons with their rajasic-tamasic intellects can fathom Your divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: O Keshava, I hold all this that You say to me to be true. Neither the gods nor the demons, O Lord, know Your divine origin.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: O Supreme Lord, full comprehension of Your infinite glory is completely beyond the limited intellects of both gods and demons; it is revealed solely through Your divine grace.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Expressing total allegiance to the Lord's words, Arjuna declares that the true nature and manifestation of the Lord are entirely beyond the comprehension of any conditioned being, whether god or demon.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: O Keshava, every word spoken by You is Absolute Truth. Neither the demigods nor the demons can comprehend Your limitless majesty.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: O Keshava, knowledge of Your supreme transcendent Form cannot be attained by gods or demons with limited minds; only by Your grace do devotees come to know it.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O Kṛṣṇa, I totally accept as truth all that You have told me. Neither the demigods nor the demons, O Lord, can understand Your personality.
