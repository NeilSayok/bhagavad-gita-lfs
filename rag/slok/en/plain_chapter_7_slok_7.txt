Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
mattaḥ parataraṃ nānyatkiñcidasti dhanañjaya .
mayi sarvamidaṃ protaṃ sūtre maṇigaṇā iva

Word Meanings:
मत्तः - beyond Me
परतरं - superior
नान्यत्किञ्चिदस्ति - nothing else exists
धनञ्जय - O conqueror of wealth
मयि - in Me
सर्वमिदं - all this
प्रोतं - strung
सूत्रे - on a thread
मणिगणा - pearls / clusters of gems
इव - like / as

Life Application:
Rest in the truth that nothing is superior to God, upon whom all creation is strung like pearls on a thread.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
There is nothing whatsoever higher than Me, O Dhananjaya. All this is strung on Me like pearls on a thread.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
There is nothing higher than Me, O Dhananjaya; all this is strung on Me like pearls on a thread.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O Dhananjaya, there exists no other cause superior to or independent of Me; this entire cosmos is strung upon Me alone like clusters of precious gems on a golden thread.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
There is nothing higher than Me, O Dhananjaya. All this is strung on Me like pearls on a thread.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "There is nothing higher than Me"—no independent agent or cause exists beyond Lord Sri Hari; He alone is the Supreme Sustainer and Sovereign Controller of all.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "There is nothing higher than Me"—Lord Vasudeva is the sole independent material and efficient cause of all creation, thread-like pervading every gem of existence.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
There is nothing whatsoever higher than Me, O Dhananjaya. All this is strung on Me like pearls on a thread.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: There exists no cause distinct from or superior to Me; just as gems are strung on a thread, so is the entire conscious and insentient cosmos supported in Me alone as My body.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: All beings arise from these two (Para and Apara) natures; hence I am the origin and dissolution of the entire universe. O Dhananjaya, there is nothing whatsoever higher than Me; all this universe is strung on Me like gems on a thread.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "There is nothing whatsoever higher than Me, O Dhananjaya; all this is strung on Me like pearls on a thread"—Lord Vasudeva is the supreme non-dual cause, supporting the entire cosmos just as a string sustains all the pearls threaded upon it.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Like pearls on a thread"—just as a thread holds pearls together, so does all-pervading Vishnu sustain all creation; Vishnu alone is the Absolute Independent Reality.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna Himself is the fundamental substratum of all creation; every atom of the universe is pervaded by His divine presence.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Nothing higher than Me exists"—no reality other than non-dual Supreme Brahman Lord Vasudeva possesses ultimate existence; the universe derives its reality from Him alone.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
There is nothing higher than Me, O Dhananjaya; all this is strung on Me like pearls on a thread.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural demonstration of the Lord"s absolute supremacy and cosmic sustainment through the thread-pearl analogy.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva is the sole Sustainer, Controller, and Master of the entire universe which forms His cosmic body.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna is the central thread holding all creation together; without Him no entity can exist.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "All this is strung on Me"—the superimposed world derives its illumination and existence from the Supreme Substratum alone.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O conqueror of wealth, there is no truth superior to Me. Everything rests upon Me, as pearls are strung on a thread.
