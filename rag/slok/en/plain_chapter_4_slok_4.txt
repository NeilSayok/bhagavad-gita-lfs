Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
arjuna uvāca .
aparaṃ bhavato janma paraṃ janma vivasvataḥ .
kathametadvijānīyāṃ tvamādau proktavāniti

Word Meanings:
अर्जुन - O Arjuna
उवाच - said
अपरं - later
भवतो - Your
जन्म - birth
परं - earlier / superior
जन्म - birth
विवस्वतः - of the Sun-god
कथमेतद्विजानीयां - how should I understand this
त्वमादौ - You in the beginning
प्रोक्तवानिति - taught, thus

Life Application:
Ask thoughtful questions when presented with claims that seem puzzling, seeking deeper understanding through honest inquiry.
