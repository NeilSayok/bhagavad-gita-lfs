Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
praśāntātmā vigatabhīrbrahmacārivrate sthitaḥ .
manaḥ saṃyamya maccitto yukta āsīta matparaḥ

Word Meanings:
प्रशान्तात्मा - serene in mind
विगतभीर्ब्रह्मचारिव्रते - fearless, in vow of celibacy
स्थितः - established
मनः - mind
संयम्य - controlling
मच्चित्तो - mind fixed on Me
युक्त - absorbed in meditation
आसीत - should sit
मत्परः - having Me as the supreme goal

Life Application:
Calm your heart, cast away fear, practice self-restraint, and sit with your mind absorbed in divine contemplation.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Serene-minded, fearless, firm in the vow of Brahmacharya, holding the mind in check, let him sit contemplating Me, intent on Me as the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Mind fixed on Me, intent on Me"—regarding Supreme Lord Vishnu as the ultimate attainment and absorbing the mind in His eternal form is the pinnacle of Dhyana-Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Fearlessness, strict celibacy, and absolute dedication to Vasudeva are the supreme keys for attaining uninterrupted mental focus.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Serene-minded, fearless, firm in the vow of Brahmacharya, holding the mind in check, let him sit contemplating Me, intent on Me as the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Serene, fearless, and firm in celibacy, surrender the mind to Vasudeva and sit in samadhi, regarding Me alone as the ultimate Goal.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Holding the body, head, and neck erect and still, gazing steadily at the tip of his nose without looking around, serene-minded, fearless, firm in the vow of celibacy, with mind controlled, let him sit concentrating on Me, intent on Me as the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Serene-minded, fearless, established in the vow of celibacy, controlling the mind, let him sit with mind fixed on Me, intent on Me as the Supreme"—defines the internal state of the meditator: inner tranquility, fearlessness, celibacy, and intense absorption in Supreme Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Fixation on God (Macchitta) and supreme devotion (Matpara) mean allowing the mind to wander to nothing other than Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Considering Lord Krishna alone as one"s sole refuge and supreme all-in-all, absorbing in His meditation is the pure Path of Grace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Mind fixed on Me, intent on Me"—the mental wave taking the form of Vasudeva is the direct means to unexcelled non-dual Samadhi.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Serene-minded, fearless, established in the vow of celibacy, controlling the mind, let him sit with mind fixed on Me, intent on Me as the Supreme.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural exposition of inner peace, fearlessness, celibacy, and total mind absorption in the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Allowing the mind to dwell on nothing other than Lord Vasudeva is the true essence of "Macchitta".

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Unswerving meditation on Lord Krishna"s lotus feet liberates the seeker forever from all fears.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Mind fixed on Me, intent on Me"—erasing all worldly thoughts to merge the mind"s wave into the Supreme Self alone is true Yoga.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Thus, with an unagitated, subdued mind, devoid of fear, completely free from sex life, one should meditate upon Me within the heart and make Me the ultimate goal of life.
