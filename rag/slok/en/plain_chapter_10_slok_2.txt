Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
na me viduḥ suragaṇāḥ prabhavaṃ na maharṣayaḥ .
ahamādirhi devānāṃ maharṣīṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ

Word Meanings:
न - not
मे - my
विदुः - understand
सुरगणाः - hosts of gods
प्रभवं - origin
न - not
महर्षयः - great sages
अहमादिर्हि - I am indeed origin
देवानां - of the gods
महर्षीणां - of the great sages
च - and
सर्वशः - all

Life Application:
Recognize God as the primeval origin of all gods and great sages, beyond their full comprehension.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Neither the hosts of demigods nor the great sages know My origin or manifestations; for in every respect I am the primal source of all demigods and sages.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Neither the hosts of the gods nor the great sages know My origin; for in every way I am the source of all the gods and the great sages.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Neither the professors of divinity nor the great ascetics know My origin, for I am the source of them all.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
When direct perception fails, we seek knowledge from higher authorities. However, neither the demigods nor the great sages know the Lord's divine origin or glory, because He is the supreme source of them all. An effect can never fully comprehend its cause.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Neither the hosts of gods, nor the great seers know My origin. For, I am the first, in every respect, among the gods and the great seers.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Neither the host of the gods nor the great seers know My power. Indeed, I am the only source of the gods and of the great seers in every way.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Neither the gods nor the great sages know My majesty. For, in all respects, I am the source of the gods and the great sages.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Prabhavam" signifies the Lord's divine majesty or His manifestation in the world. Even gods and sages, being dependent on Him, cannot fully comprehend His power; for the Lord is the ultimate source of all gods and sages.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Lest one think this knowledge could be obtained elsewhere, the Lord clarifies that even Brahma and other gods, along with seers of supersensuous truths, do not know His supreme origin and power, for He is the ultimate cause of them all.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Neither demigods nor great sages know My divine advent or glory, because I am the primal cause of all demigods and great sages in every respect. How can the effect comprehend its cause?

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Even demigods and great sages who possess supersensuous vision do not comprehend the unique majesty of My name, deeds, nature, and attributes; for I am the primal cause of their very existence, intellect, and powers.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 10.2–10.5 jointly): Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 10.1–10.5 jointly): To foster the devotion explained earlier and to reveal the all-pervading divine nature and infinite cosmic manifestations (vibhutis), the Supreme Lord resumes His teaching. 'Paramam vachah' signifies the supreme, wisdom-bestowing word spoken for Arjuna's highest welfare.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Neither the hosts of gods like Brahma nor the great sages know My "prabhava"—My supreme majestic power or divine origin. For in every way, I am the ultimate source and cause of all those gods and great sages.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The word "prabhava" denotes both divine power (majesty) and origin. Even demigods and sages cannot fully fathom the Lord's majesty because they are produced from Him and remain dependent on Him.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Even sages and demigods who possess supersensuous vision do not know the Lord's divine yoga-majesty or advent, for the Lord Himself is the origin of their creation, knowledge, and powers.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Neither demigods nor great sages comprehend the Lord's supreme majestic power or His advent with numerous divine opulences, for the Lord alone is their creator and the bestower of their intellect and power.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Though unborn, the glorious manifestations (prabhava) that the Lord assumes through His divine opulences are unknown even to gods and great sages, for the Lord is the origin of them all.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Demigods and sages, owing to their finite intellects, cannot independently know the Lord's divine majesty and origin; hence the Lord Himself graciously reveals this knowledge to Arjuna.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: To emphasize the rarity and reverential value of this knowledge, the Lord states that even gods and seers of supersensuous truths do not know His majesty, for He is the ultimate cause of their existence and powers.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Without the Lord's grace, His true nature is exceedingly difficult to comprehend. Neither gods nor sages can know His divine manifestation by their own capability, for the Lord alone is the source of them all.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The Lord's true nature is incomprehensible to finite minds. His supreme sovereignty and power to project space and the elements are unknown to gods and sages, for the Lord is the origin of them all.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Neither the hosts of demigods nor the great sages know My origin or opulences, for, in every respect, I am the source of the demigods and sages.
