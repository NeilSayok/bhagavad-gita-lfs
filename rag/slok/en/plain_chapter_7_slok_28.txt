Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yeṣāṃ tvantagataṃ pāpaṃ janānāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām .
te dvandvamohanirmuktā bhajante māṃ dṛḍhavratāḥ

Word Meanings:
येषां - whose
त्वन्तगतं - is completely destroyed
पापं - sin
जनानां - of persons
पुण्यकर्मणाम् - of pious deeds
ते - they
द्वन्द्वमोहनिर्मुक्ता - freed from dual delusions
भजन्ते - worship
मां - Me
दृढव्रताः - with firm resolve

Life Application:
Worship God with firm resolve when your pious deeds have cleansed away past sins and freed you from dualities.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Those whose accumulated sins have been completely destroyed by past righteous deeds become free from the delusion of dualities and worship Lord Vasudeva with firm resolve.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those virtuous souls whose sins born of ignorance are eradicated root and branch become free from dualistic delusion and worship Me with steadfast vows.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "But those men of virtuous deeds whose sin has come to an end worship Me with steadfast vows, freed from delusion"—purification of mind through virtuous actions destroys the delusion of dualities, establishing the seeker in unflinching devotion to Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Selfless action is the means to destroy sins, relieving dualities and establishing steadfast devotion to the Supreme Lord.

Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Steadfast in vows"—the selfless seeker endowed with spiritual discipline realizes "Vasudeva is All" with firm resolve, gaining Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:

Sri Neelakantha states: "Freed from delusion of opposites"—only when the mind is purified through mature virtue does the soul become qualified for non-dual wisdom.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
