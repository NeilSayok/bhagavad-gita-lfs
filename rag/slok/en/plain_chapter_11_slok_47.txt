Life Application:
Appreciate divine grace that revealed this supreme, radiant, infinite, primeval cosmic form never seen before by others.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
O Arjuna! Being pleased with you, I have shown you this supreme, radiant, primeval and infinite Cosmic Form of Mine by the power of My own Yoga (atma-yogat), which has not been seen before by anyone other than you.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The Blessed Lord said  O Arjuna, this Cosmic Form has graciously been shown to thee by Me by My own Yogic power; full of splendour, primeval, and infinite, this Cosmic Form of Mine has never been seen before by anyone other than thyself.

मया by Me? प्रसन्नेन gracious? तव to thee? अर्जुन O Arjuna? इदम् this? रूपम् form? परम् supreme? दर्शितम् has been shown? आत्मयोगात् by My own Yogic power? तेजोमयम् full of splendour? विश्वम् universal? अनन्तम् endless? आद्यम् primeval? यत् which? मे of Me? त्वत् from thee? अन्येन by another? न not? दृष्टपूर्वम् seen before.Commentary Lord Krishna eulogises the Cosmic Form because Arjuna should be regarded to have achieved all his ends by seeing this Cosmic Form.It is also an inducement to all spiritual aspirants to strive to attain this sublime vision. What they should do is explained by the Lord in verse 53 to 55.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Lord Shri Krishna replied: My beloved friend! It is only through My grace and power that thou hast been able to see this vision of splendour, the Universal, the Infinite, the Original. Never has it been seen by any but thee.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
The Lord Himself admits here that to be able to see His Cosmic Form is not a privilege shared by all devotees. It is only by the special grace of Lord Krishna, the ocean of infinite mercy, that Arjuna was able to enjoy this rare privilege. He also specifically says, "This radiant, infinite Cosmic Form of Mine has never been seen by anyone before you." This should not be understood to mean that Sage Vyasa, the author of the Gita, is establishing and explaining some new philosophy here, the truth of which he wants to get certified by the Lord. The intention of this statement is only that this intellectual introduction or experience of universal unity did not happen to any individual under such circumstances as it happened to Arjuna on the battlefield. A scattered mind, a tired body, and completely agitated mentally — this was the sorrowful and pitiable condition of Arjuna. Arjuna was miles away from the favourable condition of concentration of intellect required to see and understand unity in the diversity of the universe with its various names and forms. But Lord Krishna, through the influence of His extraordinary Yogic power, gifted him the necessary divine vision, and in a quiet moment of coincidence, showed him the Cosmic Form. The Lord clarifies His intention in the next verse.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
He introduces the Lord's statement spoken after Arjuna's words, 'Rightly, O Hrishikesha...' (11.36) etc., by using the word 'Arjuna'. With the intention of explaining that the vision (of the Cosmic Form) is attainable only through the grace of the Lord, He says 'By Me'.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The Blessed Lord said: O Arjuna! Being pleased, I have shown you, by My own power, this supreme, radiant, primeval, and infinite Cosmic Form, which has not been seen before by anyone other than you.

Commentary: 'mayā prasannena tavārjunedaṁ rūpaṁ darśitam' -- O Arjuna! You are repeatedly saying 'be gracious/pleased' (11.25, 11.31, 11.45). So, dear brother, though you have become frightened by seeing the terrible aspects of this Cosmic Form I have shown you, I did not show this terrifying form out of anger or to frighten you. I have shown you this Cosmic Form solely out of My pleasure/grace. Your own ability, eligibility, or devotion is not the cause here. Initially, you had only asked about My glory (vibhuti) and power (yoga). After describing My glory and power, I had said in the end that wherever you see any extraordinary manifestation, know it to be a part of My glory. In this way, I had already answered your question completely. However, there, on My own accord, I said: what is the use of your knowing so much? I stand supporting this entire universe, whatever is seen, heard, or understood, by a single fraction of Myself. Another sense is: what need do you have to know My glory and power? For all glories are dependent on My yogic power, and the repository of that yogic power is I Myself, sitting in front of you. I said this out of special grace. Based on this statement, you desired to see the Cosmic Form, and I gifted you divine eyes and showed you the Cosmic Form. This is nothing but My pure grace and pleasure. The implication is that there is no other reason for showing this Cosmic Form except My grace. Your desire to see it is merely an instrument (nimitta). 'ātmayogāt' -- I did not take anyone's help in showing this Cosmic Form; rather, I have shown you this form solely by My own power. 'param' -- This Cosmic Form of Mine is supreme.

'tejomayam' -- This Cosmic Form of Mine is extremely radiant. That is why, despite receiving divine vision, you called it hard to look at (11.17). 'viśvam' -- You yourself have addressed this form by names like Vishvarupa, Vishvamurti, etc. This form of Mine is all-pervading. 'anantamādyam' -- From the perspective of space, time, etc., this Cosmic Form of Mine has neither beginning nor end. It is the beginning of all, and is itself without beginning.

'yanme tvadanyena na dṛṣṭapūrvam' -- How did the Lord say that no one else had seen His Cosmic Form before you? Because Mother Kausalya in Rama's incarnation, Mother Yashoda in Krishna's incarnation, and Bhishma, Drona, Sanjaya, Vidura, and the sages in the Kaurava assembly had indeed seen the Lord's Cosmic Form! The answer to this is that the Lord used the term 'evaṁrūpaḥ' (11.48) for His Cosmic Form to indicate that this specific terrifying aspect of the Cosmic Form, in whose mouths great warriors, commanders, etc., are entering, had never been seen before by anyone. Secondly, because there was the occasion of war in front of Arjuna, showing such a terrifying Cosmic Form was necessary, and only the heroic Arjuna could behold such a form. However, there was no need to show such a form to Mother Kausalya and others, nor could they have beheld such a form; that is, they did not have the capacity to behold it.

The Lord has said that no one has seen this Cosmic Form before, but He has not said that no one is seeing it in the present. This is because along with Arjuna, Sanjaya is also beholding the Lord's Cosmic Form. If Sanjaya were not beholding it, how could he say at the end of the Gita that remembering the highly wonderful Cosmic Form of the Lord again and again, he is filled with great wonder and is rejoicing over and over again (18.77)?

Special Note:

By saying 'I have shown you this Cosmic Form solely out of My pleasure/grace', a unique idea emerges: the grace of the Lord is many times greater than what a seeker perceives. The seeker does not possess the capacity to comprehend the full extent of the Lord's grace. This is because the Lord's grace is boundless and infinite, while the seeker's capacity to comprehend it is limited. A seeker usually recognizes the Lord's grace only in favorable objects, individuals, situations, etc. -- that is, when they get good company (satsang), when their spiritual practice goes well, when their mental state is stable, when their mind is well-focused on the Lord, etc. Recognizing grace only in favorable circumstances is to bind grace within limits, due to which the infinite grace is not experienced. Rejoicing in that grace alone is the enjoyment (bhoga) of grace. A seeker should neither bind grace within limits nor enjoy it.

The happiness experienced when spiritual practice goes well, and being happy or content with that happiness, is also an enjoyment (bhoga) which leads to bondage -- 'sukha-saṅgena badhnāti jñāna-saṅgena cānagha' (Gita 14.6). The occurrence of happiness or the awareness of happiness is not faulty; rather, attachment to it, being happy and delighted by it, is the fault. By this, i.e., by enjoying the sattvik happiness arising from spiritual practice, there is an obstacle in rising above the three gunas (gunatita). Therefore, the seeker must be very careful to remain detached from this happiness. A seeker who does not detach from this happiness and continues to take pleasure in it with delight will, if they remain diligently engaged in their spiritual practice, naturally develop a disinterest in that happiness over time. But one who carefully remains detached from that happiness experiences the real Truth very quickly.

Connection: In the next verse, it is described with special emphasis that there is no other means for the vision of the Cosmic Form except the grace of the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
The Lord said  The 'luminous' form of Mine is a mass of luminosity. It is 'universal' i.e., constitutes the Self of the universe. It is 'infinite', endless. This is illustrated by describing it as having no beginning, middle or end. It is 'primeval,' namely, it constitutes the foundation of all beings other than Myself. It has nevr been seen before by any one other than you. Such a form is now revealed to you, who are My devotee, by Me who am gracious, by My own Yoga, namely, by the power of willing the truth associated with Me.

Sri Krsna proceeds to say, 'It is not possible that I can be realised as I am, through any means except exclusive Bhakti.'

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
The Blessed Lord said -- 'By Me' (mayā), that is, by Lord Krishna Himself, who is the source of all incarnations, the possessor of all parts, whose form is propounded in the Upanishads, who is two-armed, the ocean of beauty, extremely charming, beyond the three gunas, whose feet are to be known by all Vedanta, who is the Supreme Truth, the ocean of affection and compassion for those who take refuge in Him, and who is endowed with inconceivable divine yoga; being pleased, by the power of My own yogic energy, this supreme, excellent, and imperishable (akṣara) Cosmic Form has been shown. By this, it is indicated that this power to reveal other forms belongs only to that two-armed Sri Krishna who is the embodiment of eternal bliss, because of His incarnation as the original source. Thus it has been said by the learned: 'No other incarnation has the power to show the body of Sri Krishna. It is only in Sri Krishna that the power lies to reveal other forms (like the Cosmic Form).'

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
'Iti' (thus). In this manner, having been pleased by Arjuna, and perceiving Arjuna to be overcome by fear, the Lord, after withdrawing the Cosmic Form, consoles him with appropriate words in three verses starting with 'mayā' (by Me) etc. O Arjuna, do not be afraid; because, being pleased and having great compassion for you, I have shown you this supreme, excellent Cosmic Form by My own extraordinary power (ātmayoga). He explains its supremacy -- this form of Mine is full of splendour (radiant), universal (comprising the cosmos), infinite, and primeval, which has not been seen before by anyone else other than you.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Being prayed to in this manner, reassuring him, the Blessed Lord spoke in three verses starting with 'mayā' (by Me) etc. O Arjuna, why are you afraid? Because, being pleased out of compassion, I have shown you this supreme, excellent form of Mine through the power of My own Yogamaya (ātmayoga). He describes its supremacy itself -- this form of Mine is full of splendour, universal (comprising the cosmos), infinite, and primeval, which has never been seen before by anyone other than you (meaning, other than a devotee like you).

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Observing Arjuna to be frightened, hiding (withdrawing) the Cosmic Form, and consoling him with sweet words, the Blessed Lord said -- By Me, who am pleased and have a mindset of favouring you, this supreme, divine form has been shown to you. To imply that 'because you are free from attachment to fruits of action, you are My pure devotee', He addresses him as 'O Arjuna!'. Through the power of My own yogic lordship, this form which is full of splendour, universal (all-pervading/constituting all), infinite (without end), and primeval (existing at the very beginning of all), this Cosmic Form of Mine has never been seen before by anyone other than you.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
In 'tejomayam', implying the sense of abundance of the suffix 'mayat', he says 'a mass of luminosity'. 'Universal' (visva) means all-pervading, i.e., constituting the self of the universe. For selfhood is not possible for the insentient. Since 'having no beginning, middle, or end' was said earlier (11.19), the word 'infinite' (anantam) is illustrative of other qualities as well, which is why he says 'this is for illustration'. In 'primal' (adyam), since there is no specification of a relative entity, it is said 'constituting the foundation of all'. By 'other than you' (tvadanyena), the implied meaning is stated as 'by anyone'. If grace were without any cause, the faults of partiality and cruelty would apply to the Lord; therefore, indicating the cause of grace implied by the word 'te' (to you), he says 'to you, who are My devotee'. To dispel the misconception of the word 'yoga' as referring to meditation, he explains 'by the power of willing the truth (satyasankalpatva) associated with the Self'.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Being prayed to in this manner, reassuring Arjuna, the Lord revealed His form, and first He speaks the words of reassurance -- the Blessed Lord spoke starting with 'mayā' (by Me). O Arjuna, by Me who am always pleased with you, out of My own will and through My own power (ātmayoga) as well as the bond of belongingness (svakīyatva-yoga), this supreme form has been shown to you. What is it like? Luminous, universal (constituting the cosmos), infinite (without end), and primeval (eternal); this form of Mine has never been seen before by anyone other than you. The meaning is that such a form has been shown to you in accordance with your desire (for your sake).

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Being prayed to in this manner by Arjuna, praising him, the Lord spoke in three verses starting with 'mayā' (by Me) etc. O Arjuna, by Me who am pleased, this supreme form has been shown to you. 'ātmayogāt' means by My own power. It has been shown solely out of compassion, and not because you have a right to that vision. As has been said earlier: 'Your right is to action alone' (2.47). Luminous, i.e. of the nature of consciousness (cidrupa), divine, universal (all-comprising), primeval (without beginning), and infinite; this form of Mine has never been seen before by anyone other than you.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: My dear Arjuna, happily have I shown you, by My internal potency, this supreme universal form within the material world. No one before you has ever seen this primal form, unlimited and full of glaring effulgence.

 Arjuna wanted to see the universal form of the Supreme Lord, so Lord Kṛṣṇa, out of His mercy upon His devotee Arjuna, showed His universal form, full of effulgence and opulence. This form was glaring like the sun, and its many faces were rapidly changing. Kṛṣṇa showed this form just to satisfy the desire of His friend Arjuna. This form was manifested by Kṛṣṇa through His internal potency, which is inconceivable by human speculation. No one had seen this universal form of the Lord before Arjuna, but because the form was shown to Arjuna, other devotees in the heavenly planets and in other planets in outer space could also see it. They had not seen it before, but because of Arjuna they were also able to see it. In other words, all the disciplic devotees of the Lord could see the universal form which was shown to Arjuna by the mercy of Kṛṣṇa. Someone has commented that this form was shown to Duryodhana also when Kṛṣṇa went to Duryodhana to negotiate for peace. Unfortunately, Duryodhana did not accept the peace offer, but at that time Kṛṣṇa manifested some of His universal forms. But those forms are different from this one shown to Arjuna. It is clearly said that no one had ever seen this form before.
