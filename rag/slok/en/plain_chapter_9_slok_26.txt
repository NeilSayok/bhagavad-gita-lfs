Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
patraṃ puṣpaṃ phalaṃ toyaṃ yo me bhaktyā prayacchati .
tadahaṃ bhaktyupahṛtamaśnāmi prayatātmanaḥ

Word Meanings:
पत्रं - a leaf
पुष्पं - a flower
फलं - a fruit
तोयं - water
यो - whoever
मे - my
भक्त्या - with devotion
प्रयच्छति - offers
तदहं - that I
भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि - accept, offered with devotion
प्रयतात्मनः - of the pure-minded

Life Application:
Offer a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water with love and devotion, which God gladly accepts from a pure heart.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Whoever offers Me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water, that I accept, offered as it is with a pure heart by the self-controlled.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Leaf, flower"—even destitute devotees with minimal means, when offering leaves or flowers with devotion, satisfy Supreme Lord Sri Hari completely.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Expensive materials are unnecessary in divine worship; pure devotion alone is paramount; even humble leaves or water offered with love are joyfully accepted by God.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Whoever offers Me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water, that I accept, offered as it is with a pure heart by the self-controlled.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Worship of the Lord requires no rare wealth; even easily accessible leaves, flowers, fruits, or water, when offered with unswerving love, are personally relished by the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Leaf, flower, fruit, water offered with devotion"—great wealth is unnecessary for God"s grace; I directly accept the humble offering of leaf, flower, fruit, or water when presented with pure devotion by a self-controlled soul.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Devotion alone is the sole means to bind the Lord; material value matters not, but purity of surrender opens the door to Lord Hari"s grace.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: In the path of service (Pushtimarga), love is paramount; the Lord Himself accepts even humble leaves and flowers when offered with exclusive affection.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Offered with devotion"—Lord Janardana accepts inner feeling; even a blade of grass offered with pure unselfish devotion becomes a grand offering.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Whoever offers Me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water, that I accept, offered as it is with a pure heart by the self-controlled.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Demonstrating the simplicity of divine worship: even destitute persons lacking wealth can fully attain the Supreme Lord through loving devotion alone.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord desires not material wealth, but relishes the nectar of devotion; even a leaf offered by an exclusive devotee is most dear to Him.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Even a single leaf or drop of water offered with love captivates the Lord; God is hungry only for pure devotion.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Unselfish love endows even ordinary leaves and flowers with the sanctity of a supreme sacrifice offered to Brahman.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
If one offers Me with love and devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit or water, I will accept it.
