Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
jñānaṃ te.ahaṃ savijñānamidaṃ vakṣyāmyaśeṣataḥ .
yajjñātvā neha bhūyo.anyajjñātavyamavaśiṣyate

Word Meanings:
ज्ञानं - knowledge
तेऽहं - unto you I
सविज्ञानमिदं - this with realization
वक्ष्याम्यशेषतः - shall declare completely
यज्ज्ञात्वा - which having known
नेह - not here in this world
भूयोऽन्यज्ज्ञातव्यमवशिष्यते - nothing more remains to be known

Life Application:
Seek both intellectual knowledge and direct spiritual experience, after which nothing more remains to be known.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I shall declare to you in full this knowledge combined with realization, after knowing which nothing more remains to be known in this world.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I shall declare to you in full this knowledge combined with experience, knowing which nothing further remains to be known in this world.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: General understanding of Lord Vishnu"s divine essence is "Jnana", while direct realization of His extraordinary attributes and distinctions is "Vijnana".

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Both indirect scriptural wisdom (Jnana) and direct intuitive experience (Vijnana) of the Lord are imparted without remainder, attaining which omniscience is realized.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I shall declare to you in full this knowledge combined with realization, after knowing which nothing more remains to be known in this world.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: I shall impart to you in full the general knowledge of My unique Divine Nature as well as the detailed realizations (Vijnana) of My glories without omitting anything.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: With mind intently attached to Me, taking Me as your sole refuge and practicing Yoga, hear how you shall know Me, the Supreme Lord, completely without doubt. I shall impart to you in full this wisdom combined with realization, knowing which nothing further remains to be known in this world.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I shall impart to you fully this knowledge together with direct realization, knowing which nothing further remains to be known"—comprehending Supreme Self through direct experience leaves no further knowledge or duty to be accomplished for human liberation.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The realization of knowledge and direct perception of Lord Vishnu revealed in all scriptures is the supreme fruit and fulfillment of human life.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Knowledge of Lord Krishna"s true form and direct experience of His divine sports (Vijnana) is the supreme wealth of an exclusive devotee.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Nothing further remains to be known"—Brahman-knowledge is the culmination of all wisdom, extinguishing ignorance completely.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I shall declare to you in full this knowledge combined with experience, knowing which nothing further remains to be known in this world.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural distinction between indirect knowledge and direct realization and the resulting omniscience.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Realizing the knowledge and wisdom of Lord Vasudeva grants the seeker eternal peace and oneness.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Direct experience of Krishna"s true form is the supreme wisdom that frees the seeker from all doubts.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "With realization"—when indirect scriptural knowledge turns into direct experience, ignorance is destroyed completely.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I shall now declare unto you in full this knowledge, both phenomenal and numinous. This being known, nothing further shall remain for you to know.
