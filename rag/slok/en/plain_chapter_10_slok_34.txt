Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
mṛtyuḥ sarvaharaścāhamudbhavaśca bhaviṣyatām .
kīrtiḥ śrīrvākca nārīṇāṃ smṛtirmedhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā

Word Meanings:
मृत्युः - death
सर्वहरश्चाहमुद्भवश्च - all-devouring death I am, and origin
भविष्यताम् - of things to come
कीर्तिः - fame
श्रीर्वाक्च - prosperity, speech and
नारीणां - among feminine qualities
स्मृतिर्मेधा - memory, intelligence
धृतिः - firmness
क्षमा - forgiveness

Life Application:
Recognize God as all-devouring death, the origin of future things, and feminine virtues of fame, fortune, speech, memory, intellect, courage, forgiveness.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am all-devouring Death, and I am the origin of things yet to be; among feminine qualities/virtues I am Fame, Prosperity (Sri), Speech (Vak), Memory, Intelligence, Steadfastness, and Forgiveness.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
And I am all-devouring death, and the prosperity of those who are to be prosperous; among feminine qualities I am fame, prosperity, speech, memory, intelligence, firmness, and forgiveness.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I am all-devouring Death, and I am the origin of things yet to be. Among feminine qualities I am Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Steadfastness, and Forgiveness.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
All-devouring Death; the origin of all future manifestation; and among feminine virtues, Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Steadfastness, and Forgiveness represent the Divine.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among destroyers I am all-devouring Death, and I am the origin of things yet to be; among feminine virtues I am Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Firmness, and Forgiveness.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
I am all-devouring Death, and I am the origin of things yet to be; among feminine qualities I am Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Firmness, and Forgiveness.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
And I am all-devouring Death, and the origin of things yet to be; among feminine virtues I am Fame, Prosperity, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Firmness, and Forgiveness.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: All-devouring Death, the primal origin of future manifestations, and the seven divine qualities (Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Steadfastness, Forgiveness) embodied in noble women are the Lord's chief opulences.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am all-devouring Death, and the origin of things yet to be; among feminine virtues I am Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Firmness, and Forgiveness.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: All-devouring Death; the origin of future prosperous beings; and the seven divine feminine virtues—Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Steadfastness, Forgiveness—are My divine Vibhuti.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: I am all-devouring Death; I am the origin of future prosperous beings; and among female deities (daughters of Daksha), Fame, Fortune (Sri), Speech (Vak), Memory, Intelligence, Steadfastness, and Forgiveness are My divine opulences.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: All-devouring Death, origin of future entities, and the seven divine feminine attributes manifest the Lord's supreme power.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: All-devouring Death; the primal origin of future manifestations; and the seven divine qualities imparting supreme grace to women (Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Steadfastness, Forgiveness) are My divine opulences.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am all-devouring Death; I am the origin of things yet to be; and among feminine virtues I am Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Firmness, and Forgiveness.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Death's all-destroying form, future creative power, and the seven divine virtues of the female heart shine solely through God.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: All-devouring Death, source of future prosperity, and the seven sacred feminine virtues demonstrate the Lord's supreme governance and beauty.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Among destroyers I am Death; I am the origin of future beings; among women I am Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intelligence, Firmness, and Forgiveness.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: All-devouring Death, origin of future beings, and the seven female deities (Fame, Fortune, Speech, etc.) represent the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am all-devouring death, and I am the generating principle of all that is yet to be. Among women I am fame, fortune, fine speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness, and patience.
