Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
saṅkalpaprabhavānkāmāṃstyaktvā sarvānaśeṣataḥ .
manasaivendriyagrāmaṃ viniyamya samantataḥ

Word Meanings:
सङ्कल्पप्रभवान्कामांस्त्यक्त्वा - abandoning desires born of imagination
सर्वानशेषतः - all without exception
मनसैवेन्द्रियग्रामं - senses by mind alone
विनियम्य - restraining
समन्ततः - from all sides

Life Application:
Abandon all desires born of imagination, completely restraining your senses from all sides using your conscious mind.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Desires born of resolves"—abandoning all material cravings arising in the mind and engaging senses in Lord Vishnu"s service is true restraint.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Eradicating all desires born of mental resolves root and branch and withdrawing the senses by the mind is the principal means to Meditation-Yoga.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Abandoning without reserve all desires born of sankalpa, completely restraining the group of senses from all sides by the mind—

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Completely dropping all desires born of mental resolves, use the discriminating mind to withdraw the senses from their objects.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Completely renouncing all desires born of mental resolves without reserve, and thoroughly restraining the entire group of senses from all sides by the mind; let him attain quietude little by little with intellect held in steadfast fortitude, and making the mind firmly established in the Self, let him not think of anything whatsoever.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Abandoning without reserve all desires born of sankalpa, completely restraining the group of senses from all sides by the mind"—provides the practical method for Samadhi: eliminating desire at its source (Sankalpa) and curbing sense movements.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Comprehensive restraint of the sense organs from all directions is the sole path to mental concentration.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Engaging the mind and senses in hearing and singing Krishna"s divine sports is the true renunciation of all resolves.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "By the mind alone the senses"—only the discriminating mind is capable of withdrawing senses from objects and anchoring them in the Inner Self.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Abandoning without reserve all desires born of mental resolve, completely restraining the group of senses from all sides by the mind—

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural exposition of eradicating desire at its source and governing the senses through mental discrimination.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Casting away all non-Self desires, fix the mind immovably at the feet of Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the practitioner"s mind delights in Krishna"s divine beauty, longing for external objects naturally fades away.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Desires born of resolves"—sublating mental resolves is the sole remedy to extinguish the fire of desire.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
One should engage oneself in the practice of yoga with determination and faith, undeviated from the path. One should abandon, without exception, all material desires born of mental speculation and thus control the senses on all sides by the mind.
