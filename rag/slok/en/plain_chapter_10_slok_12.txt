Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
arjuna uvāca .
paraṃ brahma paraṃ dhāma pavitraṃ paramaṃ bhavān .
puruṣaṃ śāśvataṃ divyamādidevamajaṃ vibhum

Word Meanings:
अर्जुन - O Arjuna
उवाच - said
परं - Supreme
ब्रह्म - Brahman
परं - Supreme
धाम - Abode
पवित्रं - purifier
परमं - the Supreme
भवान् - Thou
पुरुषं - Divine Person
शाश्वतं - eternal
दिव्यमादिदेवमजं - divine, primal God, unborn
विभुम् - all-pervading Lord

Life Application:
Acknowledge God as the supreme Brahman, ultimate abode, supreme purifier, eternal divine person, primeval deity.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode, the Supreme Purifier, the Eternal Divine Person, the Primal God, Unborn and All-pervading.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Arjuna said: Thou art the Supreme Brahman, the supreme abode, the supreme purifier, the eternal, divine Person, the primeval God, unborn and omnipresent.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the ultimate abode and supreme purifier, the Eternal Divine Person, the Primal God, Unborn and All-pervading Lord.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Arjuna is familiar with Vedic literature. He states that all the great sages, along with divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa, have proclaimed You as the Eternal Divine Person, Supreme Brahman, and Supreme Abode; and now You Yourself tell me the same. Detailed commentary given under 10.13.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode, the Supreme Purifier. All the sages, the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa declare You to be the Eternal Divine Person, the Primal God, Unborn and All-pervading; and You Yourself also tell me the same.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode, the Supreme Purifier. All the sages, divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala and Vyasa proclaim You to be the eternal, divine Person, the primeval God, unborn and all-pervading; and You Yourself also tell Me the same.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode, the Supreme Purifier. All the sages, divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala and Vyasa proclaim You as the eternal divine Person, the primeval God, unborn and all-pervading; and You Yourself tell me the same.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Param Brahman" signifies the All-complete, supreme entity that sustains the entire universe. Arjuna acknowledges that Lord Krishna is the Supreme Abode, Supreme Purifier, Eternal Divine Person, and All-pervading Lord declared in the Vedas.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Hearing the Lord's words, Arjuna experiences supreme joy and praises Him as the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Light, and Supreme Purifier, whom all seers proclaim as the eternal, all-pervading Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode, the Supreme Purifier. You are the Eternal Divine Person, the Primal God, Unborn and All-pervading.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: You alone are He whom the Vedas announce as the Supreme Brahman, Supreme Abode, and Supreme Purifier. All sages—Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa—proclaim You as the Eternal Divine Person, and You Yourself have confirmed this to me.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Abhinavagupta did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Arjuna says—You are the Supreme Brahman (the Supreme Self), the Supreme Abode (the Supreme Light), the Supreme Purifier. You are the Eternal Divine Person, the Primal Deity, Unborn, and All-pervading.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Hearing the Lord's teachings, Arjuna validates and extols the supreme majesty of God. All authoritative scriptures and seers—Vyasa, Narada, etc.—proclaim Him alone as the Supreme Brahman and Ultimate Abode.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Hearing the Lord's divine words, Arjuna accepts His Sat-Chit-Ananda Supreme Brahman nature and declares Him to be the Ultimate Abode and Supreme Divine Being.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Param Brahman" means the unexcelled Supreme Truth; "Param Dhama" means the Supreme Effulgence or Abode; "Pavitram Paramam" means the Supreme Purifier destroying ignorance. Arjuna acknowledges the Lord as the Eternal Divine Person.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Arjuna says—You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode (Supreme Light), and Supreme Purifier. All the sages declare You to be the Eternal Divine Person, Primal God, Unborn, and All-pervading.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Hearing the Lord's infinite glories, Arjuna expresses his absolute conviction that Lord Krishna alone is the Supreme Brahman, Ultimate Abode, and Supreme Primal Deity proclaimed in the Vedas.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Welcoming the Lord's words, Arjuna acknowledges that all scriptures and supersensuous seers proclaim Lord Krishna alone as the Supreme Brahman, Supreme Abode, and Supreme Purifier.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Experiencing the Lord's supreme sovereignty, Arjuna declares that Lord Krishna alone is the Supreme Brahman, Ultimate Abode, and Eternal Divine Person.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Arjuna, accepting the Lord's true nature, says that all Vedas, sages, Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa proclaim Lord Krishna as the Supreme Brahman, Supreme Abode, and Eternal Divine Person.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the ultimate, the supreme abode and purifier, the Absolute Truth, the eternal, divine, original person, the unborn, the greatest.
