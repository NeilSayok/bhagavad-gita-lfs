Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yatroparamate cittaṃ niruddhaṃ yogasevayā .
yatra caivātmanātmānaṃ paśyannātmani tuṣyati

Word Meanings:
यत्रोपरमते - where becomes still
चित्तं - the mind
निरुद्धं - restrained
योगसेवया - by practice of yoga
यत्र - wherever
चैवात्मनात्मानं - and oneself by the self
पश्यन्नात्मनि - seeing within oneself
तुष्यति - rejoices

Life Application:
Experience the quiet joy of a quieted mind where thought ceases and you behold your true inner self.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: When the mind, restrained by the practice of Yoga, attains absolute quietude, and when beholding the Self by the self, one rests satisfied within the Self alone; when one experiences that boundless joy which transcends the senses and is grasped solely by purified intellect, and established in which one never swerves from the Truth; obtaining which one regards no other gain as superior, and anchored in which one is not shaken even by heavy sorrow—know that severance from union with pain to be called Yoga. That Yoga should be practiced with unshakeable resolve and an undaunted heart.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
In the stage of perfection called samadhi, or trance, one"s mind is completely restrained from material mental activities by practice of yoga. This perfection is characterized by one"s ability to see the self by the pure mind and to relish and rejoice in the self.
