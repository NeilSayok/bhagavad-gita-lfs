Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sa tayā śraddhayā yuktastasyārādhanamīhate .
labhate ca tataḥ kāmānmayaivavihitānhi tān

Word Meanings:
स - he
तया - with that
श्रद्धया - with faith
युक्तस्तस्याराधनमीहते - endowed, worships that deity
लभते - obtains
च - and
ततः - then
कामान्मयैव - desires, by Me alone
विहितान्हि - ordained indeed
तान् - those

Life Application:
Understand that benefits obtained through lesser desires are ultimately granted by the one supreme divine source.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Endowed with that faith, he engages in the worship of that deity and obtains from him his desires, which are in reality ordained by Me alone.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Endowed with that faith, he performs the worship of that deity, and receives from him his desired objects, which are in truth ordained by Me alone.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Endowed with that unshakeable faith, he performs the worship of that deity and obtains his cherished desires, which are in truth dispensed by Me alone through that deity.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Endowed with that faith, he engages in the worship of that deity and obtains his desires, which are in truth ordained by Me alone.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Endowed with that faith, he engages in the worship of that deity and obtains his desires, which are in truth ordained by Me alone.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Endowed with that faith, he performs the worship of that deity, and receives from him his desired objects, which are in truth ordained by Me alone.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Endowed with that faith, he performs the worship of that deity, and receives from him his desired objects, which are in truth ordained by Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Ordained by Me alone"—demigods are merely instruments; in reality, Lord Sri Vishnu Himself dispenses the fruits of all desires.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Demigods possess no independent power to grant fruits; Lord Vasudeva alone dispenses desired fruits to worshippers through the agency of those deities.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Endowed with that faith, he engages in the worship of that deity and obtains from him his desires, which are in reality ordained by Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Demigods like Indra are My divine limbs; hence all rewards obtained through their worship are ultimately granted by Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those whose wisdom is carried away by diverse desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature. Whichever form of a deity a devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that faith steady. Endowed with that faith, they obtain their desires, which are in truth ordained by Me alone. But finite is the fruit of those of small intellect; worshippers of deities go to deities, but My devotees come unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Endowed with that faith, he engages in the worship of that deity and receives his desires, which are in reality ordained by Me alone"—clarifies that all karmic fruits, even when sought through demigods, are allocated solely by the Omniscient Supreme Ishvara.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Vishnu alone is the supreme Dispenser of all fruits; assuming independence in demigods is ignorance, for Vishnu governs all outcomes.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Fruits granted through demigods are in truth the working of Lord Krishna"s potency, though the ignorant attribute it to demigods.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Ordained by Me alone"—Supreme Ishvara alone is the Dispenser of all Karmic fruits; other deities are mere channels.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Endowed with that faith, he performs the worship of that deity, and receives from him his desired objects, which are in truth ordained by Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural analysis of the Lord as the true Dispenser of fruits and demigods as mere instruments.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva, acting as the Inner Soul of demigods, fulfills the desires of fruitive worshippers.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: All desires of devotees are fulfilled solely by Lord Krishna"s supreme will; He alone is the ultimate Bestower of all fruits.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Ordained by Me alone"—the Supreme Self alone dispenses fruits according to the Karma of each living entity.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Endowed with such a faith, he endeavors to worship a particular demigod and obtain his desires. But in actuality these benefits are bestowed by Me alone.
