Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sādhibhūtādhidaivaṃ māṃ sādhiyajñaṃ ca ye viduḥ .
prayāṇakāle.api ca māṃ te viduryuktacetasaḥ

Word Meanings:
साधिभूताधिदैवं - along with Adhibhuta and Adhidaiva
मां - Me
साधियज्ञं - along with Adhiyajna
च - and
ये - who
विदुः - know
प्रयाणकालेऽपि - even at the time of death
च - and
मां - Me
ते - they
विदुर्युक्तचेतसः - know, with steadfast mind

Life Application:
Know God as the governing principle of all physical elements, divine forces, and sacrifices, remaining conscious at death.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Those who know Me with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, steadfast in mind, know Me even at the time of death.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Those who know Me together with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, steadfast in mind, know Me even at the hour of death.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Those who know Me as the governing principle of the material realm (Adhibhuta), the divine realm (Adhidaiva), and all sacrifices (Adhiyajna), being steadfast in mind, comprehend Me even at the moment of death.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Those who know Me with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, steadfast in mind, know Me even at the time of death.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Those who know Me with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, steadfast in mind, know Me even at the time of death.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Those who know Me together with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, steadfast in mind, know Me even at the hour of death.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Those who know Me together with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, steadfast in mind, know Me even at the hour of death.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Even at the hour of death"—the wise who know Lord Vishnu as the Inner Controller of elements, demigods, and sacrifices remain steadfast in Vishnu"s remembrance at the moment of death.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Those seekers who perceive Lord Vasudeva as the indwelling Spirit in elements, deities, and sacrifices remain undisturbed in Divine Consciousness even during the final moment of death.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Those who know Me with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, steadfast in mind, know Me even at the time of death.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The wise devotees who know Me as the material cause of elements (Adhibhuta), Lord of deities (Adhidaiva), and Master of sacrifices (Adhiyajna) attain Me at the hour of death.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those who, taking refuge in Me, strive for liberation from old age and death, know that Supreme Brahman, the whole Self-knowledge (Adhyatma), and all Karma. Those who know Me with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, with mind steadfast in Yoga, comprehend Me even at the hour of death.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Those who know Me with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, steadfast in mind, know Me even at the time of death"—concludes Chapter 7 by assuring that those who perceive Brahman Vasudeva as the Essence of elements, gods, and sacrifices remain established in Him even during the moment of physical death.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The culmination of knowledge of Vishnu is undoubted remembrance at the hour of death, which leads the seeker directly to Vishnu"s supreme abode.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The loving devotee who beholds Lord Krishna in all existence attains the direct vision of Lord Krishna even at the moment of death.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Even at the time of death"—due to continuous practice, the mind remains absorbed in Vasudeva at the end, granting non-returning Liberation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Those who know Me together with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, steadfast in mind, know Me even at the hour of death.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of knowing the Lord with Adhibhuta and remembrance at the hour of death; thus ends Chapter 7.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The wise devotee, realizing Lord Vasudeva"s eternal form even at the hour of death, attains the Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Remembrance of Lord Krishna at the moment of death grants the soul entrance into the Lord"s eternal abode.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "They know Me even at the time of death"—those who realized Brahman while living maintain that unbroken realization even at the time of death.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those in full consciousness of Me, who know Me, the Supreme Lord, to be the governing principle of the material manifestation, of the demigods, and of all methods of sacrifice, can understand and know Me, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, even at the time of death.
