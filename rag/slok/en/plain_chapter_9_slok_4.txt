Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
mayā tatamidaṃ sarvaṃ jagadavyaktamūrtinā .
matsthāni sarvabhūtāni na cāhaṃ teṣvavasthitaḥ

Word Meanings:
मया - by Me
ततमिदं - pervaded is this
सर्वं - all
जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना - universe by unmanifest form
मत्स्थानि - abide in Me
सर्वभूतानि - all beings
न - not
चाहं - and I
तेष्ववस्थितः - situated in them

Life Application:
Realize that God pervades this entire universe in unmanifest form; all beings exist in God, but God is not contained in them.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "All this is pervaded by Me"—all-pervading Vishnu is the Supreme Ruler of the cosmos; the universe depends on the Lord, but the Lord is independent. Such is His divine sovereign majesty.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Supreme Self pervades all in an unmanifest form; projected effects (world) abide in their substratum (Brahman), but the substratum depends not on the effect.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
By Me, in My unmanifested form, all this world is pervaded; all beings dwell in Me, but I do not dwell in them.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The unmanifest Lord pervades all conscious and non-conscious beings as inner controller; all entities dwell under His sovereign sway, but He depends on none.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: All this cosmos is pervaded by My unmanifest form; all beings abide in Me, but I am not dependent on them; nor do beings abide in Me physically—behold My divine mystery (Yoga), My Self sustains all beings without being attached to them.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "By Me all this world is pervaded in My unmanifest form"—unseen by senses, Lord Vasudeva pervades all. "All beings abide in Me, but I do not abide in them"—all projected forms rest upon Me as Substratum, but as the unattached Self, I depend on no effect.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Demonstrating the cosmos" dependence on God and the Lord"s absolute independence; incomprehensible is the sustaining power of Supreme Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The Lord sustains the universe by His transcendental unmanifest form; all beings rest in Him, yet He remains completely beyond worldly modifications.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "All beings abide in Me"—like a snake superposed on a rope, the entire universe is projected upon Brahman; being unattached, the Supreme Self is never tainted by cosmic attributes.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: By Me, in My unmanifested form, all this world is pervaded; all beings dwell in Me, but I do not dwell in them.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Demonstrating Brahman"s detachment and all-pervasiveness; the entire world appearance is superposed upon the Supreme Lord as Substratum.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Supreme Lord pervades and sustains the entire cosmos by His invisible power, without any modification or dependence.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: God is present everywhere; world existence depends on the Lord, but the Lord is self-existent and independent.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Projected names and forms appear to rest in Brahman; the Substratum Brahman, being unattached, remains untouched by cosmic faults.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
By Me, in My unmanifested form, this entire universe is pervaded. All beings are in Me, but I am not in them.
