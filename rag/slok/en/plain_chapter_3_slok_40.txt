Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
indriyāṇi mano buddhirasyādhiṣṭhānamucyate .
etairvimohayatyeṣa jñānamāvṛtya dehinam

Word Meanings:
इन्द्रियाणि - senses
मनो - mind
बुद्धिरस्याधिष्ठानमुच्यते - intellect are said its seat
एतैर्विमोहयत्येष - by these this deludes
ज्ञानमावृत्य - veiling knowledge
देहिनम् - the embodied soul

Life Application:
Guard your senses, thoughts, and opinions, recognizing them as the entry points where temptation clouds your wisdom.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The senses (Indriyani), the mind (Manah), and the intellect (Buddhih) are said to be its seats of dwelling (Asya adhishthanam ucyate); by means of these, it deludes the embodied soul (Vimohayati dehinam), veiling his discrimination (Avritya jnanam).
