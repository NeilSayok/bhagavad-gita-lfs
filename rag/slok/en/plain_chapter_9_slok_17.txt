Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
pitāhamasya jagato mātā dhātā pitāmahaḥ .
vedyaṃ pavitramoṃkāra ṛksāma yajureva ca

Word Meanings:
पिताहमस्य - I am the father of this
जगतो - universe
माता - mother
धाता - sustainer
पितामहः - grandfather
वेद्यं - to be known
पवित्रमोंकार - the purifier syllable Om
ऋक्साम - Rig and Sama Vedas
यजुरेव - Yajur Veda indeed
च - and

Life Application:
Honor God as the father, mother, sustainer, grandfather, purifier, sacred syllable, and ancient scripture.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am the Father of this world, the Mother, the Sustainer, the Grandfather, the Purifier, the goal of knowledge, the sacred Om, and also Rig, Sama, and Yajur Vedas.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I am the Father of this world, the Mother, the Dispenser, the Grandfather, the Purifier, the knowable, the sacred monosyllable Om, and also the Rig, the Sama and the Yajur.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I am the Father of this universe, the Mother, the Sustainer, the Grandfather, the one Holy Object of knowledge, Omkara, and the Rig, Sama, and Yajur Vedas.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
I am the Father of this world, the Mother, the Sustainer, the Grandfather, the Purifier, the Knowable, the sacred Om, and also the Rig, the Sama, and the Yajur.

I am the Father of this world, the Mother, the Sustainer, the Grandfather, the Purifier, the knowable, the sacred Om, and also Rig, Sama and Yajur.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The procreator, sustainer, ancestor of the cosmos, sacred Omkara, and the three Vedas are all the direct glory of the Lord.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am the Father of this world, the Mother, the Sustainer, the Grandfather, the Purifier, the goal of knowledge, the sacred Om, and also Rig, Sama, and Yajur Vedas.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The Father, Mother, Fruit-Bestower, Grandfather of the universe, sacred Omkara, and the three Vedas are all manifestations of the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The ritual, offering, medicine, mantra, oblation, fire, parents and sustainer of the cosmos, sacred Omkara, Vedas, refuge, dissolution, immortality, death, and being and non-being—all this is solely the divine expansion of the All-Facing Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I am the Father of this world, Mother, Sustainer, Grandfather"—I am the origin, mother, dispenser of karmic fruits, ancestor, knowable Reality, purifying Omkara, and the three Vedas.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: All cosmic relations, the sacred Om, and the Vedas are direct loving and wise manifestations of Lord Krishna Himself.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Cosmic parents, sustainer, grandfather, purifying Omkara, and the three Vedas are all the universal expansion of Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am the Father of this world, the Mother, the Sustainer, the Grandfather, the Purifier, the sacred Om, and also Rig, Sama, and Yajur Vedas.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Establishing the divine nature of all causal relationships and means of knowledge (Omkara and Vedas).

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord is the parent, protector, sacred Om, and Vedas; He is the ultimate substratum of all relationships.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: I am the Father, Mother, Sustainer, Grandfather, Om, and the three Vedas; nothing exists apart from Me.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The All-Producing and All-Sustaining Supreme Lord manifests Himself as the relations of the cosmos, Omkara, and the three Vedas.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am the father of this universe, the mother, the support and the grandfather. I am the object of knowledge, the purifier and the syllable om. I am also the Rig, the Sama and the Yajur Vedas.
