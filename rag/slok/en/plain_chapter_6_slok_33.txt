Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
arjuna uvāca .
yo.ayaṃ yogastvayā proktaḥ sāmyena madhusūdana .
etasyāhaṃ na paśyāmi cañcalatvātsthitiṃ sthirām

Word Meanings:
अर्जुन - O Arjuna
उवाच - said
योऽयं - this which
योगस्त्वया - yoga by You
प्रोक्तः - has been described
साम्येन - by equanimity
मधुसूदन - O Madhusudana
एतस्याहं - of this I
न - not
पश्यामि - see
चञ्चलत्वात्स्थितिं - state due to restlessness
स्थिराम् - steady

Life Application:
Acknowledge how difficult it is to keep a restless mind steady when trying to maintain continuous equanimity.

Arjuna said: O Madhusudana, this Yoga of equality which has been taught by You, I do not see its lasting duration owing to the restlessness of the mind.

Sri Shankaracharya states: "Arjuna said: O Madhusudana, this Yoga of equality which has been taught by You, I do not see its steady continuance owing to the restlessness of the mind"—presents Arjuna"s practical objection questioning how a flickering mind can sustain absolute Samadarshana.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Arjuna"s question serves as a universal inquiry on behalf of all seekers to learn the exact means of mastering the restless mind.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: As long as the mind remains restless, abiding in equality is difficult; divine grace of Krishna alone renders it easy.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Yoga of equality, O Madhusudana"—Arjuna voices the doubt of how a mind prone to duality can achieve unbroken Samadhi in non-duality.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Arjuna asks how an intensely flickering mind can be anchored in Vasudeva"s divine equality.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Expressing his human limitation, Arjuna asks Lord Krishna for an easy remedy to steady the mind.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
