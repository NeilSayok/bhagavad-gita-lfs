Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
balaṃ balavatāṃ cāhaṃ kāmarāgavivarjitam .
dharmāviruddho bhūteṣu kāmo.asmi bharatarṣabha

Word Meanings:
बलं - strength
बलवतां - of the strong
चाहं - and I
कामरागविवर्जितम् - devoid of passion and desire
धर्माविरुद्धो - in harmony with dharma (virtue)
भूतेषु - in living beings
कामोऽस्मि - desire I am
भरतर्षभ - O best of the Bharatas

Life Application:
Express strength that is free from lust and passion, and desire that accords with righteousness and duty.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Desire uncontrary to Dharma"—desire in accordance with scriptural duty and strength free from lust reflect Lord Vishnu"s divine governance.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Sattvic strength utilized for duty devoid of selfish passion and righteous desire aligned with virtue manifest the Lord"s supreme glory.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The pure strength of the mighty applied toward righteousness and desire aligned with scriptural Dharma are expressions of My divine sovereignty.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: O Partha, know Me to be the eternal seed of all beings. I am the intellect of the intelligent and splendour of the resplendent. I am the strength of the strong devoid of lust and attachment, and in all beings, desire uncontrary to Dharma.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I am the strength of the strong devoid of lust and attachment; I am desire in beings unopposed to Dharma"—clarifies that physical strength used for righteous duty without selfish attachment and desires aligned with divine order are expressions of Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Actions unopposed to Dharma and pure physical/mental strength are dear to and enlivened by Supreme Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Pure strength aligned with divine will and desire fulfilling Dharma are the direct gracious sports of Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Desire unopposed to Dharma"—strength devoid of egoistic craving and desire in harmony with righteousness are glories of Brahman Vasudeva.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Pure strength for preserving Dharma and desire sanctioned by scripture are limbs of worshipping Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Desire aligned with Dharma and pure selfless strength are auspicious signs of Lord Krishna"s divine presence.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Desire unopposed to Dharma"—pure desire prompting actions in harmony with virtue is the divine glory of the Self.
