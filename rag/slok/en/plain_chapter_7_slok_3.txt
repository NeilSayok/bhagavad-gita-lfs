Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
manuṣyāṇāṃ sahasreṣu kaścidyatati siddhaye .
yatatāmapi siddhānāṃ kaścinmāṃ vetti tattvataḥ

Word Meanings:
मनुष्याणां - among men
सहस्रेषु - among thousands
कश्चिद्यतति - hardly someone strives
सिद्धये - for perfection
यततामपि - even among those striving
सिद्धानां - of the perfected ones
कश्चिन्मां - hardly anyone Me
वेत्ति - knows
तत्त्वतः - in reality

Life Application:
Persevere in your spiritual pursuit, realizing that among thousands of people, only a few strive for ultimate truth.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among thousands of men, scarcely one strives for perfection; and among those who strive and succeed, scarcely one knows Me in truth.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among thousands of human beings, scarcely one strives for perfection; and among those striving yogis, scarcely one knows Me in truth.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Among thousands of human beings, scarcely one strives for spiritual perfection; and even among those earnest strivers who succeed, scarcely one knows Me in My absolute divine essence.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among thousands of men, scarcely one strives for perfection; and among those who strive and succeed, scarcely one knows Me in truth.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among thousands of men, scarcely one strives for perfection; and among those who strive and succeed, scarcely one knows Me in truth.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Among thousands of human beings, scarcely one strives for perfection; and among those striving yogis, scarcely one knows Me in truth.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Among thousands of human beings, scarcely one strives for perfection; and among those striving yogis, scarcely one knows Me in truth.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Emphasizing the rarity of wisdom"—direct perception of Lord Vishnu is extremely rare; among thousands of souls, scarcely one becomes a genuine seeker of Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Demonstrating the extreme rarity of Divine Knowledge; among millions, barely one becomes a true seeker, and among seekers, barely one realizes God in truth.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among thousands of men, scarcely one strives for perfection; and among those who strive and succeed, scarcely one knows Me in truth.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among thousands of humans qualified for scriptures, scarcely one strives for the perfection of Bhakti, and even among those, scarcely one truly comprehends My divine nature.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: This divine knowledge is exceedingly rare; among thousands, barely one strives for it, and among strivers, barely one comprehends It in truth.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Among thousands of men, scarcely one strives for perfection; among those who strive, scarcely one knows Me in truth"—highlights the extreme rarity of true Brahman-realization even among advanced spiritual practitioners.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Attaining unswerving devotion and true knowledge of Lord Vishnu is the rarest perfection, possible solely through divine grace.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The grace of exclusive devotion to Lord Krishna is exceedingly rare, bestowed upon a fortunate soul among millions.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Scarcely one knows Me in truth"—even after purification of mind and long discipline, direct realization of the Supreme Lord remains extremely rare.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Among thousands of human beings, scarcely one strives for perfection; and among those striving yogis, scarcely one knows Me in truth.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural demonstration of the extreme rarity of God-realization and realized sages.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Realizing the true nature of Lord Vasudeva is attained by a single loving devotee among millions.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Unswerving devotion arises solely by divine grace, revealing the supreme Krishna-reality distinct from the world.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Scarcely one knows Me in truth"—direct Self-realization through destruction of ignorance is the rarest spiritual attainment.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Out of many thousands among men, one may endeavor for perfection, and of those who have achieved perfection, hardly one knows Me in truth.
