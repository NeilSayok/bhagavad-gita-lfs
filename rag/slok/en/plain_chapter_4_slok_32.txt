Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
evaṃ bahuvidhā yajñā vitatā brahmaṇo mukhe .
karmajānviddhi tānsarvānevaṃ jñātvā vimokṣyase

Word Meanings:
एवं - thus
बहुविधा - manifold
यज्ञा - sacrifices
वितता - spread out
ब्रह्मणो - of the Vedas
मुखे - in the mouth
कर्मजान्विद्धि - know as born of action
तान्सर्वानेवं - all them thus
ज्ञात्वा - having known
विमोक्ष्यसे - you will be liberated

Life Application:
Understand that all constructive human actions can serve as spiritual practices when performed with noble intent.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Thus manifold sacrifices are spread out before Brahman. Know them all as born of action; knowing this, thou shalt be liberated.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Thus manifold sacrifices are spread out before Brahman. Know them all as born of action; knowing this, thou shalt be liberated.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Thus manifold forms of Yajnas are expounded in detail in the Vedas (the mouth of Brahman). Know them all to be born of mental, vocal, and physical action (Karmajan); knowing this truth, thou shalt be completely liberated.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Thus manifold sacrifices are spread out before Brahman. Know them all as born of action; knowing this, thou shalt be liberated.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Know them all born of action"—all scriptural Yajnas originate from physical, vocal, and mental actions; offering them to Lord Vishnu grants liberation.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: All Yajnas expounded in the Vedas are born of body, speech, and mind; realizing the Self as detached and non-agent liberates the seeker.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Thus manifold sacrifices are spread out before Brahman. Know them all as born of action; knowing this, thou shalt be liberated.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Know all these components of Yajna to be born of mental, vocal, and physical actions; recognizing the Atman as non-doer grants liberation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Evam etc. All these manifold sacrifices are spread out before Brahman, know them all to be born of action; knowing this thou shalt be liberated.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Thus manifold sacrifices are spread out before Brahman. Know them all as born of action; knowing this, thou shalt be liberated"—emphasizes that all Yajnas are empirical actions (*Karmaja*), whereas the true Self is ever actionless.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Discerning the non-doer Atman from action-born Yajna elements is the paramount means for Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Realizing all scriptural Yajnas as part of Krishna"s lila-creation and surrendering them selflessly liberates the seeker.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Knowing this thou shalt be liberated"—all sacrifices are non-Self actions of body, speech, and mind, while the Atman is detached; this discrimination grants Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Thus manifold sacrifices are spread out before Brahman. Know them all as born of action; knowing this, thou shalt be liberated.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis demonstrating how realizing all Yajnas as action-born establishes Atman"s detachment, yielding Mukti.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: All sacrifices dedicated to Vasudeva are born of physical, vocal, and mental work; realizing Atman"s non-doership bestows the Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the soul comprehends that all sacrifices are modifications of Krishna"s nature and the Self is His eternal part, liberation ensues.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Know them all born of action"—realizing all sacrifices to be non-Self actions while Atman is immutable non-agent opens the door to liberation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
All these different types of sacrifice are approved by the Vedas, and all of them are born of different types of work. Knowing them as such, you will become liberated.
