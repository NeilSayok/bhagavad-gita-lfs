Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ajo.api sannavyayātmā bhūtānāmīśvaro.api san .
prakṛtiṃ svāmadhiṣṭhāya sambhavāmyātmamāyayā

Word Meanings:
अजोऽपि - though unborn
सन्नव्ययात्मा - being of imperishable nature
भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि - though Lord of all beings
सन् - being
प्रकृतिं - nature
स्वामधिष्ठाय - subjugating My own nature
सम्भवाम्यात्ममायया - I manifest by My divine potency

Life Application:
Remember that divine grace can enter world events unexpectedly to restore order and support righteousness.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "I manifest through My Maya"—Lord Vishnu, by His inconceivable potency and sovereign will, manifests in His complete divine form without any material bondage.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Supreme Lord does not assume a body out of karmic compulsion; rather, He manifests voluntarily through His pure Maya-power for global welfare.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Though I am unborn, of imperishable nature, and the Lord of all beings, yet ruling over My own Nature, I come into being by My own Maya.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Lord"s incarnation is not composed of five material elements, but assumes a divine non-material body (Suddha-Sattva) by His supreme will.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.5–4.9 jointly): Through these slokas the Lord proves that His birth and action are not karmic like ordinary beings, but a divine sport of Pure Consciousness; knowing this in truth, one attains liberation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Though I am unborn, of imperishable nature, and the Lord of all beings, yet ruling over My own Nature, I come into being by My own Maya"—elucidates the illusion of divine incarnation (*Atmamaya*) assumed by the Supreme Ruler.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The Lord"s body is transcendental, devoid of material elements, made of pure Knowledge and Bliss, assumed by His supreme free will.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna, through His unfathomable lila-potency, manifests Himself before the world in His eternal imperishable form.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I manifest by My own Maya"—unbound Ishvara resort to His pure Sattvic Maya to voluntarily assume lila-incarnations.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Though I am unborn, of imperishable nature, and the Lord of all beings, yet ruling over My own Nature, I come into being by My own Maya.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving how the unborn, immutable Supreme Lord manifests via Maya for the redemption of the world.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Vasudeva"s descent is not assuming a material body, but manifesting a divine auspicious form by His supreme will.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna"s compassionate potency is that divine Maya through which He manifests before His devotees.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "I manifest through My Maya"—God manifests a sportful body not through Avidya, but through His Sattvic Maya.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Although I am unborn and My transcendental body never deteriorates, and although I am the Lord of all living entities, I still appear in every millennium in My original transcendental form.
