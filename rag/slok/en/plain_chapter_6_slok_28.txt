Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī vigatakalmaṣaḥ .
sukhena brahmasaṃsparśamatyantaṃ sukhamaśnute

Word Meanings:
युञ्जन्नेवं - thus practicing meditation
सदात्मानं - constantly the self
योगी - yogi
विगतकल्मषः - free from impurities
सुखेन - easily
ब्रह्मसंस्पर्शमत्यन्तं - infinite bliss of Brahman contact
सुखमश्नुते - experiences happiness

Life Application:
Engage in regular self-alignment to touch the infinite bliss of divine presence easily and continuously.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Thus constantly keeping the mind absorbed in the Self, the Yogi freed from sin easily enjoys the infinite bliss of contact with Brahman.
