Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
prāpya puṇyakṛtāṃ lokānuṣitvā śāśvatīḥ samāḥ .
śucīnāṃ śrīmatāṃ gehe yogabhraṣṭo.abhijāyate

Word Meanings:
प्राप्य - having attained
पुण्यकृतां - of the meritorious
लोकानुषित्वा - having dwelt in worlds
शाश्वतीः - for many
समाः - years
शुचीनां - of the pure
श्रीमतां - of the prosperous
गेहे - in the home
योगभ्रष्टोऽभिजायते - one fallen from yoga is born

Life Application:
Trust that incomplete spiritual effort earns a favorable rebirth in a pure and prosperous home to resume progress.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and lived there for endless years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and lived there for innumerable years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Having attained the auspicious, righteous heavenly realms and dwelt there for innumerable years, that practitioner who fell from Yoga is reborn in this mortal world in the home of pure, pious, and prosperous souls.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and lived there for endless years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and lived there for endless years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and lived there for innumerable years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and lived there for innumerable years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Attaining the worlds of the righteous"—a seeker engaged in Vishnu-devotion, even if faltering in Yoga, enjoys holy realms and takes rebirth in a pure lineage.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The beginner who fell from Yoga enjoys heavenly realms of the righteous for long years and is then reborn in a pure and prosperous household.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and lived there for endless years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: A fallen yogi of lesser practice enjoys the worlds of virtue and is reborn into a holy, prosperous family favorable for Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and dwelt there for innumerable years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous; or he is born in a family of wise Yogis. Verily, a birth like this in this world is exceedingly rare to obtain.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and lived there for endless years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous"—explains the first category of Yoga-bhrashta: the beginner who gets heavenly pleasures before taking rebirth in a virtuous wealthy family.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Rebirth in a pure and prosperous home provides the seeker an advantageous setting to resume devotional practice.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Impressions of remembering Krishna are never erased; the devotee enjoys holy realms and re-emerges in a family fit for Krishna"s service.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "In the house of the pure and prosperous"—the novice fallen yogi, after enjoying heavenly delights, is reborn into a righteous wealthy household.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and lived there for innumerable years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of the novice fallen yogi"s journey through heavenly realms to a pure prosperous rebirth.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Initial practice of Vasudeva-meditation grants the soul enjoyment of holy worlds followed by rebirth in a noble family.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna never lets His practitioner"s spiritual merits fade, granting him rebirth in a supportive family.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "In the house of pure and prosperous"—the novice yogi takes birth in a virtuous wealthy family to resume meditation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The unsuccessful yogi, after many, many years of enjoyment in the planets of the pious living entities, is born into a family of righteous people, or into a family of rich aristocracy.
