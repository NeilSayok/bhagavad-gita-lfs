Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
athavā yogināmeva kule bhavati dhīmatām .
etaddhi durlabhataraṃ loke janma yadīdṛśam

Word Meanings:
अथवा - or
योगिनामेव - of yogis indeed
कुले - in the family
भवति - accrues
धीमताम् - of the wise
एतद्धि - this indeed
दुर्लभतरं - more difficult to obtain
लोके - in the world
जन्म - birth
यदीदृशम् - which is such

Life Application:
Value being born into a family of wise spiritual practitioners as a rare and precious opportunity for growth.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Or he is born in a family of wise Yogis itself; verily, a birth like this is very rare to obtain in this world.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Or he is born in a family of wise Yogis itself; verily, a birth like this is exceedingly rare to obtain in this world.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Or if he was a advanced, long-practicing Yogi, he is born directly into a family of enlightened Brahmana sages and wise Yogis; verily, such a highly elevated birth in this world is exceedingly rare to obtain.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "In a family of wise yogis"—the intensely detached seeker is born directly in the home of enlightened Vaishnava yogis, making Mukti easily accessible.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: He who has practiced meditation for a long time takes birth directly in a family of poor but wise Yogis, which is exceedingly rare.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Or he is born in a family of wise Yogis itself; verily, a birth like this is very rare to obtain in this world.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: A fallen yogi of advanced practice takes birth directly in the lineage of self-realized sages, a birth rarest in this world.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Having attained to the worlds of the righteous and dwelt there for innumerable years, he who fell from Yoga is reborn in the house of the pure and prosperous; or he is born in a family of wise Yogis. Verily, a birth like this in this world is exceedingly rare to obtain.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Or he is born in a family of wise Yogis itself; verily, a birth like this is very rare to obtain in this world"—defines the second advanced category of Yoga-bhrashta: one who skips heavenly pleasures and is born directly into a family of enlightened Yogis.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Taking birth in a family of wise yogis opens the immediate door to Mukti for a practitioner cultivated across past lives.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: By divine grace, the seeker takes birth in a family where an atmosphere of Krishna-devotion is present from birth.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "This birth is rarer still"—taking birth in a family of poor sages grants absolute freedom from sense pleasures and total focus on Self-inquiry.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Or he is born in a family of wise Yogis itself; verily, a birth like this is exceedingly rare to obtain in this world.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of the advanced fallen yogi"s rare rebirth directly in a family of wise, self-realized meditators.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Taking birth in the home of Lord Vasudeva"s unswerving sages is the ultimate fruition of past virtuous deeds.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the seeker"s spiritual longing is deep, Lord Krishna manifests his rebirth directly in a family of perfected yogis.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Rarest in the world"—birth in a wise family grants total freedom from worldly distractions, enabling focused inquiry into Brahman.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Or he takes his birth in a family of successful yogis who are truly endowed with great wisdom. Surely, such a birth is rare in this world.
