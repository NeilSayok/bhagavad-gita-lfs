Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
athavā bahunaitena kiṃ jñātena tavārjuna .
viṣṭabhyāhamidaṃ kṛtsnamekāṃśena sthito jagat

Word Meanings:
अथवा - or
बहुनैतेन - by much of this
किं - what
ज्ञातेन - knowledge
तवार्जुन - to you, O Arjuna
विष्टभ्याहमिदं - pervading, I this
कृत्स्नमेकांशेन - entire universe with a single portion
स्थितो - am situated
जगत् - the world

Life Application:
Realize that God pervades and supports this entire universe with a single small fraction of divine power.
