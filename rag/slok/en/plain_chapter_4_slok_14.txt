Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
na māṃ karmāṇi limpanti na me karmaphale spṛhā .
iti māṃ yo.abhijānāti karmabhirna sa badhyate

Word Meanings:
न - not
मां - Me
कर्माणि - actions
लिम्पन्ति - taint / bind
न - not
मे - my
कर्मफले - in fruits of action
स्पृहा - desire
इति - thus
मां - Me
योऽभिजानाति - he who knows thus
कर्मभिर्न - by actions not
स - he
बध्यते - is bound

Life Application:
Act without selfish motives or desire for personal credit, staying untouched by the karmic entanglements of work.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "I have no desire for action-fruits"—Lord Vishnu is ever self-fulfilled; a devotee worshipping Him selflessly remains freed from karmic ties.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Lord is totally devoid of egoism and desire for fruits; the practitioner who realizes this non-agent nature of God becomes freed from karmic ties.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Actions do not taint Me"—cosmic actions do not touch the Lord"s nature; one who realizes this non-doership and desirelessness becomes free from karma.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Though Creator of the fourfold order, the Supreme Lord is a non-doer, for actions do not taint Him nor has He longing for fruits; one who knows Me thus is not bound by actions.
