Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
pralapanvisṛjangṛhṇannunmiṣannimiṣannapi .
indriyāṇīndriyārtheṣu vartanta iti dhārayan

Word Meanings:
प्रलपन्विसृजन्गृह्णन्नुन्मिषन्निमिषन्नपि - speaking, releasing, grasping, opening and closing eyes also
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेषु - senses in sense-objects
वर्तन्त - operate
इति - thus
धारयन् - holding the view

Life Application:
Observe your bodily movements and sensory functions calmly today without claiming personal ego-driven ownership over them.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Senses move among sense-objects"—the senses act under Lord Vishnu"s regulation; the jiva is not an independent actor.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Understanding that speech, movement, and bodily excretion are mere activities of the senses among sense-objects, the unattached Self remains pure.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Recognizing that organs of action perform their functions while the soul is distinct and pure, the seeker transcends karmic bondage.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: He who is devoted to Yoga, pure in self, master of his body and senses, who realizes his own Self as the Self of all beings, is not tainted though acting. The knower of Truth, centered in Yoga, while seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, walking, sleeping, breathing, speaking, releasing, grasping, opening and closing eyes, thinks: "I do nothing at all; the senses merely move among their objects." He who performs actions offering them to Brahman, abandoning attachment, is not stained by sin, even as a lotus leaf is untouched by water. Yogis perform actions without attachment, with body, mind, intellect, and senses alone, solely for the purification of the self.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Speaking, releasing, grasping, opening and closing the eyes—convinced that the senses move among the sense-objects"—emphasizes that the Self is a silent witness to all motor and sensory actions.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: All physical activities belong to the material nature of the senses; the Self is untainted and a mere witness.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: All sensory functions are executed by the power bestowed by the Lord; hence the devotee remains non-doer and remembers Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving that speaking, grasping, and opening/closing eyes are activities of the senses, leaving the Soul untouched.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A devotee of Vasudeva views all sensory activities as workings of God"s energy, remaining free from egoism.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Senses move among objects"—understanding activities as the operation of nature"s modes, the wise man remains unshakable in his unattached Self.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Because while speaking, evacuating, receiving, or opening or closing his eyes, he always knows that only the material senses are engaged with their objects and that he is aloof from them.
