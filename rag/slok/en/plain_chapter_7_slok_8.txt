Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
raso.ahamapsu kaunteya prabhāsmi śaśisūryayoḥ .
praṇavaḥ sarvavedeṣu śabdaḥ khe pauruṣaṃ nṛṣu

Word Meanings:
रसोऽहमप्सु - taste I am in water
कौन्तेय - O son of Kunti
प्रभास्मि - radiance I am
शशिसूर्ययोः - of the moon and sun
प्रणवः - sacred syllable Om (Pranava)
सर्ववेदेषु - in all the Vedas
शब्दः - sound
खे - in ether
पौरुषं - ability (manhood / vigor)
नृषु - in men

Life Application:
Perceive the divine presence as the taste in water, light in sun and moon, sacred sound in air, and manhood in men.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am the taste in waters, O son of Kunti; I am the light in moon and sun, the sacred syllable Om in all Vedas, sound in ether, and manhood in men.

O son of Kunti, I am the essential taste in water; I am the resplendence in the sun and moon; I am the sacred Om in all the Vedas; I am the sound in ether, and the virility and capability in human beings.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The taste in water, light of sun and moon, and Om of Vedas represent Lord Sri Hari"s governing presence and divine potency in all things.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The taste in water, light of sun and moon, sacred Om of Vedas, sound of ether, and virility of men are all manifestations of Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am the taste in waters, O son of Kunti; I am the light in moon and sun, the sacred syllable Om in all Vedas, sound in ether, and manhood in men.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The essential taste of water, radiance of sun and moon, monosyllable Om of Vedas, sound in ether, and prowess in men are sustained by My divine presence.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: O Kaunteya, I am the taste in waters, light in sun and moon, sacred syllable Om in all Vedas, sound in ether, and manhood in men. I am the pure fragrance in earth, brilliance in fire, life in all beings, and austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I am the taste in waters, O Kaunteya, light in moon and sun, Om in all Vedas, sound in space, manhood in men"—these key essences holding each entity together are the direct manifestations of Supreme Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The essential core of all entities is the presence of Vishnu; without Vishnu, liquidity in water or luminosity in sun cannot exist.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The sweet essence and brilliant light of all entities is the direct divine manifestation of Purushottama Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Om in all Vedas"—the essential defining attribute of every entity is the pervading glory of Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am the taste in waters, O Kaunteya; I am the light in moon and sun, the sacred syllable Om in all Vedas, sound in ether, and manhood in men.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural analysis of the Lord"s divine manifestations as the essence of all elemental qualities.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The essential attributes of all entities, forming Lord Vasudeva"s body, directly denote the Lord Himself.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The sweetness of water and the Omkara of Vedas are the direct divine beauty and form of Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "I am the taste in water"—Substratum Brahman abides as the core essence of all evolutes to lend them reality.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O son of Kunti, I am the taste of water, the light of the sun and the moon, the syllable Om in the Vedic mantras; I am the sound in ether and ability in man.
