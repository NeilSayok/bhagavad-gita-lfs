Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
etadyonīni bhūtāni sarvāṇītyupadhāraya .
ahaṃ kṛtsnasya jagataḥ prabhavaḥ pralayastathā

Word Meanings:
एतद्योनीनि - born from these two
भूतानि - all living beings
सर्वाणीत्युपधारय - all, thus understand
अहं - I
कृत्स्नस्य - of the entire
जगतः - of the world
प्रभवः - the source
प्रलयस्तथा - and dissolution as well

Life Application:
Realize that all living beings originate from divine energy, which is the source and dissolution of the entire universe.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Know that all beings have their birth in these two natures; and I am the origin and dissolution of the whole universe.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "I am the origin of the whole universe"—Lord Sri Hari creates and dissolves the entire universe through His conscious and insentient potencies, as the sole independent Cause.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Comprehend that all beings have these two as origin"—all moving and unmoving creation arises from Para and Apara Prakriti, and Lord Vasudeva is the sole Creator and Dissolver.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Know that all beings have their birth in these two natures; and I am the origin and dissolution of the whole universe.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: All moving and unmoving entities emerge through the union of My lower insentient and higher conscious natures; hence Lord Vasudeva alone is the Master of universal origin and dissolution.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: All beings arise from these two (Para and Apara) natures; hence I am the origin and dissolution of the entire universe. O Dhananjaya, there is nothing whatsoever higher than Me; all this universe is strung on Me like gems on a thread.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Know that all beings originate from these two natures; I am the origin and dissolution of the entire universe"—since both Apara and Para Prakritis belong to the Lord, Lord Vasudeva is the sole efficient and material cause of cosmic creation and dissolution.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The origin and dissolution of all beings is under Lord Vishnu"s control; without Vishnu"s supreme will, no entity possesses existence or activity.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The manifestation and dissolution of the entire universe is the pure playful sport (Leela) of Purushottama Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I am the origin of the whole world"—Lord Vasudeva, associated with Maya, is the non-different material and efficient cause of the entire cosmos.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Know that all beings have their birth in these two natures; and I am the origin and dissolution of the whole universe.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of the emergence of beings from Prakriti and the Lord"s role as the Ultimate Cause.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Supreme Lord Vasudeva alone creates and dissolves the entire universe through His divine natures.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna is the supreme seed and Lord of all worlds; from Him alone the entire universe manifests.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "I am the origin of the whole world"—the Supreme Self is the matchless cause of origin, sustenance, and dissolution.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
All created beings have their source in these two natures. Of all that is material and all that is spiritual in this world, know for certain that I am both the origin and the dissolution.
