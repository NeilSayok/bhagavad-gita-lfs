Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
adhiyajñaḥ kathaṃ ko.atra dehe.asminmadhusūdana .
prayāṇakāle ca kathaṃ jñeyo.asi niyatātmabhiḥ

Word Meanings:
अधियज्ञः - Lord of sacrifice
कथं - how
कोऽत्र - who here
देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन - in this body, O Madhusudana
प्रयाणकाले - at the time of death
च - and
कथं - how
ज्ञेयोऽसि - are You to be known
नियतात्मभिः - by the self-controlled

Life Application:
Ask how the supreme Lord resides in the body and how a self-controlled person remembers Him at death.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Arjuna seeks clarification regarding the nature of Adhiyajna, His presence within the body, and the process and outcome of remembering God at the time of death.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Who and how is Adhiyajna here"—Arjuna asks who is the object of worship in sacrifices, how He resides in embodiment, and how devotees attain Him at death.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: In response to Arjuna"s seven queries (Brahman, Adhyatma, Karma, Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, Adhiyajna, and divine remembrance at death), the Lord explains that the Supreme Imperishable is Brahman, one"s essential nature is Adhyatma, and the creative offering that originates cosmic existences is Karma.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Arjuna asks two final crucial questions: Who is Adhiyajna in this physical body and how does He dwell here? And how is the Supreme Lord to be remembered and realized by self-controlled souls at the moment of departing from the body?

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Adhiyajna Lord Vishnu is the inner controller and bestower of fruits of all sacrifices; divine remembrance at death determines the soul"s final destination.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Divine remembrance at death is the ultimate fruit of all spiritual practices; Lord Krishna as Adhiyajna dwells in the hearts of all.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "In this body"—the nature of Adhiyajna in the body and the method of divine contemplation at death form the core of the seeker"s practice.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explanation of Arjuna"s final two inquiries concerning the nature of Adhiyajna and realization at the moment of death.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Remembering the Lord with exclusive devotion at death is the infallible direct means to God-realization.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord as Adhiyajna is the bestower of sacrifice fruits; remembering His holy name at death liberates the soul.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Witness Consciousness abiding in all bodies is Adhiyajna; a mind attuned to God at death attains Supreme Brahman.
