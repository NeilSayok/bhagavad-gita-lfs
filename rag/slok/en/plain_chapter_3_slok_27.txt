Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
prakṛteḥ kriyamāṇāni guṇaiḥ karmāṇi sarvaśaḥ .
ahaṅkāravimūḍhātmā kartāhamiti manyate

Word Meanings:
प्रकृतेः - of material nature
क्रियमाणानि - being performed
गुणैः - by the qualities
कर्माणि - actions
सर्वशः - in all respects
अहङ्कारविमूढात्मा - one deluded by egoism
कर्ताहमिति - I am the doer, thus
मन्यते - thinks

Life Application:
Release personal pride over your achievements today, recognizing that nature and circumstances enable all your actions.

All actions are performed, in all cases, by the qualities of Nature. He whose mind is deluded by egoism thinks: "I am the doer."

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
All actions are wrought in all cases by the qualities of Nature (Prakriteh gunaih); but he whose mind is deluded by egoism (Ahankaravimudhatma) foolishly thinks: "I am the doer" (Karta aham iti).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: A soul trapped in nescience fails to perceive the driving force of Prakriti"s Gunas and falsely claims independent agency.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: A soul deluded by egoism superimposes upon himself the agency of actions performed by body and senses (born of Nature).

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Due to nescience, the deluded soul falsely assumes agency over actions executed by the three Gunas of Nature.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.27–3.28 jointly): Prakriteh etc. and Tattvavit tu etc. He who knows the truth about Gunas and action realizes that Gunas act upon Gunas, remaining unattached.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "All actions are performed, in all cases, by the qualities of Nature. He whose mind is deluded by egoism thinks: I am the doer"—elucidates the profound distinction between the true non-acting Self (*Atman*) and the acting Prakriti.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: False identification of the Self with non-self body/mind is the root cause of bondage and mother of all ignorance.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Turning away from Krishna, the soul is driven by Gunas and foolishly deems himself the independent doer.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: The intellect deluded by egoism becomes bound to false doership and suffers karmic consequences.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving that all acts belong to Prakriti"s Gunas and that egoistic doership is sheer illusion.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The three Gunas born of Vasudeva"s Maya perform all actions; the soul merely boasts out of nescience.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a seeker surrenders the illusion of agency and acknowledges the Lord"s power, he gains freedom from ego.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Egoism falsely identifies the Self with non-Self, trapping the soul in karmic bondage.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The spirit soul bewildered by the influence of false ego thinks himself the doer of activities that are in actuality carried out by the three modes of material nature.
