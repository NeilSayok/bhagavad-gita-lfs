Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
na hi jñānena sadṛśaṃ pavitramiha vidyate .
tatsvayaṃ yogasaṃsiddhaḥ kālenātmani vindati

Word Meanings:
न - not
हि - certainly
ज्ञानेन - by knowledge
सदृशं - equal / comparable
पवित्रमिह - purifying here
विद्यते - exists
तत्स्वयं - that himself
योगसंसिद्धः - perfected in yoga
कालेनात्मनि - in time in the self
विन्दति - finds / attains

Life Application:
Cultivate spiritual understanding patiently through daily practice, trusting that self-knowledge will blossom naturally within you.
