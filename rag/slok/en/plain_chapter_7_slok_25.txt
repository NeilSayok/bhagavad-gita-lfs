Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
nāhaṃ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yogamāyāsamāvṛtaḥ .
mūḍho.ayaṃ nābhijānāti loko māmajamavyayam

Word Meanings:
नाहं - not I
प्रकाशः - manifest (visible to all)
सर्वस्य - to everyone
योगमायासमावृतः - veiled by yogamaya
मूढोऽयं - this foolish
नाभिजानाति - does not know
लोको - world
मामजमव्ययम् - Me the unborn and imperishable

Life Application:
Realize that divine truth remains hidden from those blinded by worldly illusion, unveiled only through devotion.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am not revealed to all, veiled as I am by My Yoga-Maya. This deluded world knows Me not, the Unborn and Imperishable.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Veiled by My Yoga-Maya, I am not revealed to all; hence this deluded world does not recognize Me, the Unborn and Imperishable.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Veiled by My divine power of illusion (Yoga-Maya), I am not revealed to the eyes of all. This deluded world fails to perceive Me as the Eternal, Unborn, and Imperishable Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Veiled by My Yoga-Maya, I am not revealed to all; hence this deluded world knows Me not, the Unborn and Imperishable.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Veiled by Yoga-Maya"—Lord Vishnu veils the vision of ignorant souls through His divine Maya, preventing them from comprehending His absolute eternity.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The veil of three-fold Yoga-Maya covers human discernment, preventing souls from perceiving Lord Vasudeva"s imperishable divinity.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am not revealed to all, veiled as I am by My Yoga-Maya. This deluded world knows Me not, the Unborn and Imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: My assumption of human Avatar form by My supreme will is My Yoga-Maya; veiled by It, I appear to ignorant men as an ordinary mortal.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Unintelligent men, not knowing My supreme, unexcelled, imperishable state, consider Me, the Unmanifest, to have assumed human birth. Veiled by My divine Yoga-Maya, I am not revealed to all; hence this deluded world fails to comprehend Me, the Unborn and Imperishable Lord.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I am not revealed to all, veiled as I am by Yoga-Maya; this deluded world knows Me not, the Unborn and Imperishable"—the cosmic veil of Maya prevents worldly minds from recognizing the Supreme Lord indwelling in all.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The veil of Vishnu-Maya is the root cause of the wheel of birth and death; only by Vishnu"s grace is this veil dispelled.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna keeps worldly souls deluded by His Yoga-Maya, bestowing knowledge of His supreme form exclusively upon His loving devotees.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Veiled by Yoga-Maya"—due to divine Maya composed of Gunas, Supreme Lord Vasudeva remains unrevealed to the deluded world.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Veiled by My Yoga-Maya, I am not revealed to all; hence this deluded world does not recognize Me, the Unborn and Imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural analysis of the Lord remaining unrevealed behind the veil of Yoga-Maya.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva"s divine Avatar incarnation is the sport of His independent Yoga-Maya, mistaken by fools for human birth.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Without Lord Krishna"s grace, the veil of Yoga-Maya is not lifted and His divine form remains unmanifest to the soul.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Veiled by Yoga-Maya"—Brahman associated with Maya remains concealed from foolish souls, becoming revealed only through pure devotion.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am never manifest to the foolish and unintelligent. For them I am covered by My internal potency, and therefore they do not know that I am unborn and infallible.
