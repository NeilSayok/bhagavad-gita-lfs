Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
aśvatthaḥ sarvavṛkṣāṇāṃ devarṣīṇāṃ ca nāradaḥ .
gandharvāṇāṃ citrarathaḥ siddhānāṃ kapilo muniḥ

Word Meanings:
अश्वत्थः - peepal tree (Ashvattha)
सर्ववृक्षाणां - among all trees
देवर्षीणां - among celestial sages
च - and
नारदः - Narada
गन्धर्वाणां - among Gandharvas
चित्ररथः - Chitraratha
सिद्धानां - among perfected beings
कपिलो - Kapila
मुनिः - sage

Life Application:
Perceive God as the banyan tree among trees, Narada among divine sages, Chitraratha among singers, Kapila among perfected souls.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among all trees I am the Peepal (Ashvattha); among divine sages I am Narada; among Gandharvas I am Chitraratha, and among perfected beings I am Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among all trees I am the Ashvattha (Banyan); among divine sages I am Narada; among Gandharvas I am Chitraratha; among perfected ones I am the Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of all trees I am the sacred Ashvattha; of divine sages I am Narada; of heavenly singers [Gandharvas] I am Chitraratha, and of perfected souls I am Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among trees, the sacred 'Ashvattha'; among divine sages, Narada (master of devotion); among celestial singers, Chitraratha; and among perfected seers, Sage Kapila represents the Supreme Divine.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among all trees I am the Ashvattha; among divine seers I am Narada; among Gandharvas I am Chitraratha; among perfected ones I am the Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Among all trees I am the Ashvattha; among divine sages I am Narada; among Gandharvas I am Chitraratha; among perfected beings I am Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Among all trees I am the Ashvattha; among divine seers I am Narada; among Gandharvas I am Chitraratha; among perfected ones I am Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The name 'Kapila' signifies Him who is of the nature of Supreme Bliss, by whom the universe is sustained and dissolved. As declared in Vedic Sruti (Svetasvatara Upanishad & Babhravya Samaveda), Lord Vishnu Himself is called 'Kapila' because He is the source of infinite bliss, protection, and dissolution.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The sacred Ashvattha tree, the divine sage Narada, the Gandharva chief Chitraratha, and the naturally perfected Sage Kapila are the Lord's principal opulences.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among all trees I am the Ashvattha; among divine seers I am Narada; among Gandharvas I am Chitraratha, and among perfected souls I am Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among trees, the revered Ashvattha; among divine seers, Narada; among Gandharvas, Chitraratha; and among perfected seers, Sage Kapila is My divine Vibhuti.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among all trees I am Ashvattha (Peepal); among divine sages (who are both demigods and seers) I am Narada; among Gandharvas I am Chitraratha; and among perfected ones (born with inherent wisdom and detachment) I am Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Etymologically, 'Kapila' represents 'Ka' (Blissful), 'Pi' (Protector of the world), and 'La' (Dissolver of creation). Among perfected beings, Sage Kapila is an incarnation of the omniscient Lord Vishnu who sustains all existence through eternal wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Ashvattha among trees, Narada among seers, Chitraratha among Gandharvas, and the Kapila Avatar among perfected beings are the Lord's own manifestations.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Ashvattha among trees, Narada among divine sages, Chitraratha among celestial musicians, and Sage Kapila (propounder of Samkhya) among naturally perfected beings is My divine opulence.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Among all trees I am the Ashvattha; among divine seers I am Narada; among Gandharvas I am Chitraratha; among perfected ones I am Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Peepal among trees, Narada among seers, Chitraratha among Gandharvas, and Sage Kapila among perfected souls introduce the Lord's supernatural splendor.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Ashvattha among trees, Narada among divine seers, Chitraratha among Gandharvas, and Sage Kapila among perfected souls are specific manifestations of the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Among trees I am Ashvattha; among divine seers I am Narada; among Gandharvas I am Chitraratha; and among perfected ones I am Sage Kapila.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Ashvattha among trees, Narada among divine seers, Chitraratha among Gandharvas, and omniscient Sage Kapila among perfected ones embody the Lord.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Of all trees I am the banyan tree, and of the sages among the demigods I am Nārada. Of the Gandharvas I am Citraratha, and among perfected beings I am the sage Kapila.
