Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
prayatnādyatamānastu yogī saṃśuddhakilbiṣaḥ .
anekajanmasaṃsiddhastato yāti parāṃ gatim

Word Meanings:
प्रयत्नाद्यतमानस्तु - striving with perseverance
योगी - yogi
संशुद्धकिल्बिषः - purified of all sins
अनेकजन्मसंसिद्धस्ततो - perfected through many births thereafter
याति - goes to
परां - supreme
गतिम् - condition .

Life Application:
Strive with patient effort across time, purifying your heart until you reach the ultimate spiritual goal.
