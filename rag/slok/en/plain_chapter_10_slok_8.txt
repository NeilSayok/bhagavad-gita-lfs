Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ahaṃ sarvasya prabhavo mattaḥ sarvaṃ pravartate .
iti matvā bhajante māṃ budhā bhāvasamanvitāḥ

Word Meanings:
अहं - I
सर्वस्य - of all
प्रभवो - source
मत्तः - from Me
सर्वं - all
प्रवर्तते - operates
इति - thus
मत्वा - regarding
भजन्ते - worship
मां - Me
बुधा - wise ones
भावसमन्विताः - endowed with devotion

Life Application:
Realize God as the source of all from which everything emanates, worshipping with loving intellectual devotion.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am the source of everything; from Me everything evolves. Realizing this, the wise, endowed with devotion and love, worship Me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I am the source of all; from Me everything evolves; understanding thus, the wise, endowed with loving devotion, worship Me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I am the source of all spiritual and material worlds. Everything emanates from Me. The wise who perfectly know this engage in My devotional service with all their hearts.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
The distinction between individual and universal is due to limiting adjuncts alone. The Supreme Lord is the origin and source of vitality for the entire universe. Realizing this, enlightened souls absorb themselves in unalloyed devotion to Him.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
I, the Supreme Brahman known as Vasudeva, am the source of all; from Me everything proceeds. Realizing this, the wise, imbued with loving devotion, worship Me.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
I am the origin of all; from Me everything proceeds. Realizing this, the wise worship Me, endowed with supreme love.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
I am the origin of all; from Me everything moves on. Understanding thus, the wise ones, filled with devotion, worship Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The Lord declares that He alone is the creator (prabhava) of this universe, and by His command alone everything moves. Realizing this truth, selfless devotees meditate on and worship Him with pure love.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Lord Vasudeva is Himself the Supreme Brahman, the ultimate cause of creation, sustenance, and dissolution. Enlightened devotees, realizing this, engage in divine worship with absolute devotion.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am the source of the entire universe; from Me everything functions. Understanding this, wise devotees endowed with love and faith worship Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: I alone am the cause of creation for the entire universe of conscious and unconscious entities, and everything functions by My resolve. Knowing My infinite auspicious attributes, wise souls worship Me with intense devotion.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The great sages, Kumaras, and Manus govern the cosmic order solely through the will and power of the Supreme Lord. Enlightening one another regarding the Lord's divine glory, these enlightened souls abide in supreme bliss.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: I, the Supreme Brahman known as Vasudeva, am the origin of the entire universe, and from Me alone proceed sustenance, dissolution, action, and enjoyment of fruits. Realizing this, wise ones abiding in the Absolute Reality worship Me with love.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The Supreme Lord alone is the creator, maintainer, and dissolver of all worlds. Knowing this, wise devotees continuously worship and remember Him with unalloyed love.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The entire cosmic manifestation is the Lord's own divine Form, and all divine play proceeds from Him. Realizing this, great souls endowed with devotional love delight in singing His glories.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: I, Vasudeva, am the creator of the world, and from Me alone proceed all movements and activities. Having this firm conviction, wise seekers worship Me with supreme love.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am the origin of the entire universe, and from Me everything proceeds. Realizing this, wise devotees worship Me with loving devotion.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: I, the Supreme Brahman Vasudeva, am the root cause of all existence. Experiencing this rightly, wise seekers filled with loving devotion worship Me.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Realizing that the Lord is the universal cause and promptor of everything is the chief catalyst for fulfilling Bhakti-yoga, absorbing the devotee's mind in Him.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: I am the origin of all worlds, and everything functions by My divine will. Knowing this, wise souls worship Me with intense devotion.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: One who knows Me as the origin and controller of the entire universe becomes filled with loving devotion and worships Me, the Supreme Ruler of all.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am the source of all spiritual and material worlds. Everything emanates from Me. The wise who perfectly know this engage in My devotional service and worship Me with all their hearts.
