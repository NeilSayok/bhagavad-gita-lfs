Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
apareyamitastvanyāṃ prakṛtiṃ viddhi me parām .
jīvabhūtāṃ mahābāho yayedaṃ dhāryate jagat

Word Meanings:
अपरेयमितस्त्वन्यां - this is inferior, but beyond this another
प्रकृतिं - energy / nature
विद्धि - know
मे - my
पराम् - superior (higher nature)
जीवभूतां - comprising living beings
महाबाहो - O mighty-armed one
ययेदं - by which this
धार्यते - is sustained
जगत् - the universe

Life Application:
Understand your higher spiritual nature that sustains life, distinct from the lower physical elements of creation.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
This is My lower nature; but know My other higher nature, O mighty-armed, the Life-Element by which this universe is sustained.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
This is My lower nature; know My other higher nature, O mighty-armed, which is the spirit-element by which this universe is sustained.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O mighty-armed Arjuna, this eightfold nature is My lower material nature; but know My other higher, superior nature—the conscious soul-element—by which this entire universe is sustained and held together.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
This is My lower nature; know My other higher nature, O mighty-armed, the Life-Element by which this universe is sustained.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
This is My lower nature; know My other higher nature, O mighty-armed, the Life-Element by which this universe is sustained.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
This is My lower nature; know My other higher nature, O mighty-armed, which is the spirit-element by which this universe is sustained.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
This is My lower nature; know My other higher nature, O mighty-armed, which is the spirit-element by which this universe is sustained.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "The conscious spirit nature is Lakshmi"—Para Prakriti is Sri Lakshmi (the conscious divine energy), mistress of all souls, who, relying on Lord Vishnu, sustains the cosmos.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Superior to insentient Apara Prakriti is the Lord"s conscious higher nature (Para Prakriti / Jivabhuta) which sustains and enlivens the entire universe.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
This is My lower nature; but know My other higher nature, O mighty-armed, the Life-Element by which this universe is sustained.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The aforementioned insentient matter is My lower nature; know My other higher conscious nature (Para Prakriti consisting of individual souls) which animates and sustains the universe.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—this is My eightfold lower unmanifest nature (Apara Prakriti). O mighty-armed, know My other higher consciousness-nature (Para Prakriti), the life-force, by which this entire universe is sustained.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "This is My lower nature; know My other higher nature, O mighty-armed, the Spirit-Element by which this universe is sustained"—beyond the lower bound nature lies the Lord"s higher Nature (Para Prakriti / Kshetrajna Consciousness), which enters into and sustains the entire universe.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Para Prakriti is Mahalakshmi of conscious nature, the controller of all souls, governing the universe as Lord Vishnu"s intimate potency.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Para Prakriti is the inherent conscious potency of Lord Krishna, infusing life into the universe and sustaining it.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Being the conscious soul"—the supreme inner Consciousness (Kshetrajna) is God"s higher nature, sustaining the illusory world as its absolute substratum.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: This is My lower nature; know My other higher nature, O mighty-armed, which is the spirit-element by which this universe is sustained.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Subtle discernment between lower and higher natures and the conscious sustaining principle according to Upanishads.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Para Prakriti is the collection of conscious individual souls forming the body of Lord Vasudeva to sustain matter.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna"s higher conscious nature infuses life into the world and reveals the divine identity of the Supreme.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Being the living soul"—the inner conscious potency sustaining cosmic life is Para Prakriti.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Besides these, O mighty-armed Arjuna, there is another, superior energy of Mine, which comprises the living entities who are exploiting the resources of this material, inferior nature.
