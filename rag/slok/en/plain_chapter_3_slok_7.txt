Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yastvindriyāṇi manasā niyamyārabhate.arjuna .
karmendriyaiḥ karmayogamasaktaḥ sa viśiṣyate

Word Meanings:
यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि - he who the senses
मनसा - by the mind
नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन - controlling begins, O Arjuna
कर्मेन्द्रियैः - by organs of action
कर्मयोगमसक्तः - Karma Yoga unattached
स - he
विशिष्यते - is superior

Life Application:
Channel your energy into useful work with a focused mind, performing every task without clinging to personal rewards.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: A practitioner performing unattached karma for the pleasure of Lord Vishnu while controlling senses by mind is superior.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: A practitioner who uses discrimination to control senses and performs selfless work is far superior to a hypocritical ascetic.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
But he who, controlling the senses by the mind, O Arjuna, engages unattached his organs of action in Karma-yoga, he excels.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: A seeker who controls senses with the mind and performs unattached actions as divine offering excels above all.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Yastu etc. He who controls the senses by the mind and engages unattached in Karma-yoga with organs of action excels.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "But he who, controlling the senses by the mind, O Arjuna, engages unattached his organs of action in Karma-yoga, he excels"—lauds the unattached householder practicing Karma-yoga over the hypocritical renunciate.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Sensory control by mind and unattached Karma-yoga form the authoritative means for mental purity.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A devotee anchoring mind in Krishna"s feet and serving with organs of action excels above all.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: The selfless Karma-yogi acquiring fitness for knowledge excels over the pseudo-renunciate.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving the superiority of commencing Karma-yoga unattached via mental control of senses.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Performing selfless duties devoted to Vasudeva with controlled senses is the seeker"s true path.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a practitioner"s mind merges in Krishna and controls senses, his Karma-yoga becomes holy.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Unattached Karma-yoga serves as the means for mental purity, elevating the seeker to steadfast wisdom.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
On the other hand, if a sincere person tries to control the active senses by the mind and begins karma-yoga [in Krsna consciousness] without attachment, he is by far superior.
