Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
jarāmaraṇamokṣāya māmāśritya yatanti ye .
te brahma tadviduḥ kṛtsnamadhyātmaṃ karma cākhilam

Word Meanings:
जरामरणमोक्षाय - for liberation from old age and death
मामाश्रित्य - taking refuge in Me
यतन्ति - strive
ये - who
ते - they
ब्रह्म - the Supreme Brahman
तद्विदुः - they know that
कृत्स्नमध्यात्मं - entire spiritual knowledge
कर्म - action
चाखिलम् - and all

Life Application:
Strive for liberation from old age and death by taking refuge in God, knowing complete spiritual wisdom and action.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Those who strive for liberation from old age and death by taking refuge in Me know that Brahman, the whole Adhyatma, and all Karma.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Those who strive for freedom from old age and death, taking refuge in Me, know that Brahman, the whole Adhyatma, and all Karma.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Those who, relying upon Me alone as refuge, strive for liberation from decay and death, come to know fully that Supreme Brahman, the whole knowledge of Self (Adhyatma), and the complete truth of Action (Karma).

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Those who strive for liberation from old age and death by taking refuge in Me know that Brahman, the whole Adhyatma, and all Karma.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Those who strive for liberation from old age and death by taking refuge in Me know that Brahman, the whole Adhyatma, and all Karma.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Those who strive for freedom from old age and death, taking refuge in Me, know that Brahman, the whole Adhyatma, and all Karma.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Those who strive for freedom from old age and death, taking refuge in Me, know that Brahman, the whole Adhyatma, and all Karma.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "They know that Brahman in full"—selfless seekers relying on Supreme Lord Vishnu comprehend Brahman Vishnu, the nature of Jiva (Adhyatma), and all Karma in truth.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Seekers desiring absolute liberation from the cycle of old age and death, taking refuge in Vasudeva, comprehend Supreme Brahman, entire Self-knowledge, and all Karma.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Those who strive for liberation from old age and death by taking refuge in Me know that Brahman, the whole Adhyatma, and all Karma.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Those who seek refuge in Me to attain freedom from decay and birth realize Supreme Brahman, individual Self (Adhyatma), and all Karma required for liberation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those who, taking refuge in Me, strive for liberation from old age and death, know that Supreme Brahman, the whole Self-knowledge (Adhyatma), and all Karma. Those who know Me with Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, and Adhiyajna, with mind steadfast in Yoga, comprehend Me even at the hour of death.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Those who strive for liberation from old age and death taking refuge in Me know Brahman, whole Adhyatma, and all Karma"—prepares the foundation for Chapter 8 by introducing six profound cosmic terms (Brahman, Adhyatma, Karma, Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, Adhiyajna).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Taking refuge in Lord Vishnu is the sole means to overcome old age and death; knowing Vishnu clarifies all mysteries of Self and Action.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A seeker taking refuge in Lord Krishna attains, through divine grace, complete realization of Supreme Brahman, Inner Self, and Karma.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "They know that Brahman in full"—by exclusive refuge in the Supreme Lord, the sage directly realizes the unbroken essence of Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Those who strive for freedom from old age and death, taking refuge in Me, know that Brahman, the whole Adhyatma, and all Karma.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural demonstration of the necessity of knowing Brahman, Adhyatma, and Karma for those seeking release from birth and death.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Devotees seeking Kaivalya liberation, taking refuge in Lord Vasudeva, realize the pure nature of Self (Adhyatma) and Karma.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Upon taking total refuge in Lord Krishna, the soul becomes freed from all ignorance and attains the supreme state.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "They know that Brahman in full"—only the selfless seeker reliant on the Lord obtains direct intuition of Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Intelligent persons who are endeavoring for liberation from old age and death take refuge in Me in devotional service. They are actually Brahman because they entirely know everything about transcendental activities.
