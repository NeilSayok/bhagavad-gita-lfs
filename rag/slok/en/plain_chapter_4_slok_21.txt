Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
nirāśīryatacittātmā tyaktasarvaparigrahaḥ .
śārīraṃ kevalaṃ karma kurvannāpnoti kilbiṣam

Word Meanings:
निराशीर्यतचित्तात्मा - free from desires with mind controlled
त्यक्तसर्वपरिग्रहः - renounced all possessions
शारीरं - bodily
केवलं - merely
कर्म - action
कुर्वन्नाप्नोति - performing, incurs not
किल्बिषम् - sin

Life Application:
Simplify your desires and lifestyle today, taking only what is necessary while performing your duties without greed.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Without desire, with mind and self controlled, having abandoned all possessions, performing action by the body alone, he incurs no sin.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Without desire, with mind and self controlled, having abandoned all possessions, performing action by the body alone, he incurs no sin.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Free from expectations, with self and mind completely controlled, having relinquished all sense of personal ownership (possessiveness), performing action merely for physical subsistence, he incurs no karmic fault or sin.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Doing so he incurs no sin"—action performed devoid of ego and fruit-attachment, purely as an offering to God, generates no karmic sin.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Performing bodily actions solely for bare sustenance, a Sage grounded in Knowledge incurs neither karmic merit nor demerit.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Without desire, with mind and self controlled, having abandoned all possessions, performing action by the body alone, he incurs no sin.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Relinquishing all possessiveness and acting merely for bodily sustenance, the practitioner escapes the sin of karmic bondage.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Renouncing attachment to action-fruits, ever content, without desire, with mind controlled, performing action by the body alone, he incurs no sin.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Without desire, with mind and self controlled, having abandoned all possessions, performing action by the body alone, he incurs no sin"—elucidates the total freedom from karmic taint (*Kilbisa-abhava*) enjoyed by a desireless renunciate.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Selfless action is the direct cause for terminating sin, as it is animated by non-doership.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A loving devotee working without possessions for Krishna"s service never falls into the bondage of sin or merit.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Action by the body alone"—a Jivanmukta renunciate performing alms-gathering solely for physical preservation remains sinless due to absence of nescience.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Without desire, with mind and self controlled, having abandoned all possessions, performing action by the body alone, he incurs no sin.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving why a desireless renunciate with controlled mind incurs no sin doing body-sustaining work.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A practitioner devoted to Vasudeva acts without possessions solely for physical sustenance, remaining free.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When all feelings of the seeker are dedicated at Krishna"s feet, even physical activities become a holy sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Action of body alone"—a Sage acting solely to exhaust Prarabdha karma is untainted by results.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Such a man of understanding acts with his mind and intelligence perfectly controlled, gives up all sense of proprietorship over his possessions, and acts only for the bare necessities of life. Thus working, he is not affected by sinful reactions.
