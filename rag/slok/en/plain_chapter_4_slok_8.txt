Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
paritrāṇāya sādhūnāṃ vināśāya ca duṣkṛtām .
dharmasaṃsthāpanārthāya sambhavāmi yuge yuge

Word Meanings:
परित्राणाय - for the protection
साधूनां - of the righteous
विनाशाय - for the destruction
च - and
दुष्कृताम् - of evildoers
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय - for establishing Dharma
सम्भवामि - I appear
युगे - in every age
युगे - in every age

Life Application:
Align yourself with positive, uplifting forces, contributing to the defense of virtue in your community.

For the protection of the good, for the destruction of the wicked, and for the firm establishment of righteousness, I am born in age after age.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
For the protection of the good, for the destruction of the wicked, and for the establishment of righteousness, I am born in age after age.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
For the ultimate protection of the righteous, for the total destruction of evil-doers, and for the firm re-establishment of eternal Truth and Dharma, I manifest Myself from age to age.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "For establishing Dharma I appear age after age"—Lord Vishnu manifests across all epochs to protect devotees and uphold righteousness.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Protecting the righteous and establishing Dharma form the primary purpose of incarnation, while destroying demoniac forces is a secondary result.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
For the protection of the good, for the destruction of the wicked, and for the firm establishment of righteousness, I am born in age after age.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "For protection of the pious"—granting vision of His divine form to rescue ardent devotees suffering separation from Him is the prime motive of Avatarhood.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.5–4.9 jointly): Through these slokas the Lord proves that His birth and action are not karmic like ordinary beings, but a divine sport of Pure Consciousness; knowing this in truth, one attains liberation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "For the protection of the good, for the destruction of the wicked, and for the establishment of righteousness, I am born in age after age"—declares the threefold mission of Ishvara"s descents.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The protection of holy souls and firm establishment of Dharma represent the unalterable purpose of Divine Incarnation.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: To bestow the bliss of His divine form upon His loving devotees (sadhus), compassionate Krishna manifests in age after age.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I appear from age to age"—the Lord reincarnates to preserve moral order by nurturing the righteous and subduing the wicked.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
For the protection of the good, for the destruction of the wicked, and for the establishment of righteousness, I am born in age after age.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis substantiating why the Lord appears in every era to rescue the pious, eliminate the evil, and anchor Dharma.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Vasudeva"s descent occurs across all ages to relieve His dedicated devotees" distress and ensure their eternal good.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna manifests by His sovereign will in every age to protect His devotees and distribute divine love.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "For establishing Dharma I appear in age after age"—anchoring righteousness firmly is the supreme purpose of divine descent.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
To deliver the pious and to annihilate the miscreants, as well as to reestablish the principles of religion, I Myself appear, millennium after millennium.
