Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
nātyaśnatastu yogo.asti na caikāntamanaśnataḥ .
na cātisvapnaśīlasya jāgrato naiva cārjuna

Word Meanings:
नात्यश्नतस्तु - neither for one who overeats
योगोऽस्ति - yoga is attained
न - not
चैकान्तमनश्नतः - nor for one who fasts completely
न - not
चातिस्वप्नशीलस्य - nor for one who sleeps excessively
जाग्रतो - of one who stays awake
नैव - never at all
चार्जुन - and, O Arjuna

Life Application:
Avoid extremes in eating and sleeping, maintaining healthy balance in all your physical habits today.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Verily, yoga is not possible for him who eats too much, nor for him who does not eat at all; nor for him who sleeps too much, nor for him who keeps awake, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Yoga is not possible for him who eats too much, nor for him who starves; nor for him who sleeps too much, nor for him who keeps awake, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Verily, O Arjuna, Meditation-Yoga is not perfected by him who eats too much, nor by him who starves completely; nor by him who sleeps excessively, nor by him who remains sleeplessly awake.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Not for one who overeats"—measured food and sleep for bodily sustenance serve as an integral part of Yoga performed for Lord Vishnu"s pleasure.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Overeating or absolute fasting creates obstacles in meditation; hence moderate diet (Mitahara) is indispensable for yogis.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Verily, yoga is not possible for him who eats too much, nor for him who does not eat at all; nor for him who sleeps too much, nor for him who keeps awake, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Yoga is not accomplished by one who overeats or starves, nor by one who sleeps excessively or stays awake continuously; regulated body maintenance is essential.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: O Arjuna, Yoga is not for him who eats too much, nor for him who starves; nor for him who sleeps excessively, nor for him who keeps awake continuously. Yoga, which destroys all pain, is attained by one who is moderate in food and recreation, moderate in efforts and actions, and balanced in sleep and wakefulness.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Yoga is not possible for him who eats too much, nor for him who starves; nor for him who sleeps too much, nor for him who keeps awake, O Arjuna"—condemns extreme physical austerities or extreme sense indulgences as fatal barriers to Dhyana-Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Overeating and extreme fasting both destroy mental wellness; the Middle Path alone is the doorway to Yoga perfection.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Regulated diet and sleep are essential to keep the body fit for continuous devotional service of Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Not for the overeater"—imbalance in food and rest provokes bodily humors, creating mental restlessness in meditation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Yoga is not possible for him who eats too much, nor for him who starves; nor for him who sleeps too much, nor for him who keeps awake, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of the flaws of overeating and starvation as severe obstacles to Yoga practice.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Regulated food and sleep suitable for body maintenance support contemplation of Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Living a balanced life for the pleasure of Lord Krishna is the true path of Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Not for the overeater"—excess of food and sleep increases Tamas and Rajas, destroying meditative absorption.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
There is no possibility of one"s becoming a yogi, O Arjuna, if one eats too much or eats too little, sleeps too much or does not sleep enough.
