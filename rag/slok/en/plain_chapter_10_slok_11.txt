Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
teṣāmevānukampārthamahamajñānajaṃ tamaḥ .
nāśayāmyātmabhāvastho jñānadīpena bhāsvatā

Word Meanings:
तेषामेवानुकम्पार्थमहमज्ञानजं - out of compassion for them, I, ignorance-born
तमः - ignorance
नाशयाम्यात्मभावस्थो - destroy, dwelling in their heart
ज्ञानदीपेन - by the lamp of knowledge
भास्वता - luminous

Life Application:
Out of compassion for His devotees, God dwelling in their hearts destroys the darkness born of ignorance with wisdom's lamp.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Out of mere compassion for them, I, dwelling in their heart, destroy the darkness born of ignorance by the luminous lamp of wisdom.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Out of mere compassion for them, I, dwelling within their Self, destroy the darkness born of ignorance by the luminous lamp of knowledge.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Out of compassion for them alone, I, dwelling in their hearts, destroy with the shining lamp of knowledge the darkness born of ignorance.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Though the Truth is ever-present, it remains veiled by ignorance. When a devotee meditates upon the Divine with pure love, the Lord Himself, dwelling within his intellect, ignites the radiant lamp of wisdom and destroys all darkness of illusion.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Out of compassion for them alone, I, residing in their internal organ, destroy the darkness born of delusion, by the radiant lamp of knowledge.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Out of compassion for them alone, I, dwelling in their minds, destroy the darkness born of ignorance by the luminous lamp of knowledge.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Out of compassion for them alone, I, dwelling in their hearts, destroy the darkness born of ignorance by the luminous lamp of knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: For the ultimate spiritual good of His devotees, the Lord abides as the Witness in their intellect and dispels all darkness of ignorance by means of the luminous lamp of direct intuitive realization born of scriptural mahavakyas.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Out of compassion for them alone, I, abiding in their inner heart, destroy the darkness born of ignorance by the luminous lamp of wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Out of pure grace towards them, I abide in their mental contemplation as the Supreme Object of devotion, and with the radiant lamp of divine knowledge reflecting My infinite auspicious qualities, I dispel all darkness born of past sins.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The great sages, Kumaras, and Manus govern the cosmic order solely through the will and power of the Supreme Lord. Enlightening one another regarding the Lord's divine glory, these enlightened souls abide in supreme bliss.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Out of sheer compassion for them, I abide in their intellect, and by means of the shining lamp of wisdom—kindled by devotion, meditation, and detachment—I completely destroy the darkness of ignorance born of illusion.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Self-knowledge is granted to devotees by Me alone. The most compassionate Lord reflects that out of mercy for souls wandering in illusion, He abides as their innermost Self and, with the radiant mirror of wisdom, completely destroys all darkness of ignorance and bodily identification.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Out of infinite mercy for My devotees, I manifest in the purity of their intellect and, with the luminous lamp of Self-knowledge ignited through hearing and contemplation, I eradicate the beginningless darkness of ignorance.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Out of compassion alone, dwelling in their internal organ, I destroy the darkness of delusion born of ignorance with the radiant lamp of intuitive wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Residing in the lotus heart of His unalloyed devotees, the Lord eradicates the darkness of ignorance—the cause of worldly rebirth—with the unclouded lamp of Non-dual Brahman-realization.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Showering uncaused grace upon His devotees, the Lord manifests within the temple of their mind, and with the divine lamp of wisdom illuminating His auspicious attributes, He destroys all darkness born of ignorance.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: For the ultimate good of His devotees, the Lord abides firmly in their intellect and dispels the darkness of illusion with the radiant lamp revealing His divine Self.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Out of pure grace, the Supreme Lord takes His seat in the heart of His devotee and, with the radiant lamp of Non-dual Self-knowledge, completely extinguishes the darkness of doubt and ignorance.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
To show them special mercy, I, dwelling in their hearts, destroy with the shining lamp of knowledge the darkness born of ignorance.
