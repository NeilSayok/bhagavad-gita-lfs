Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
saktāḥ karmaṇyavidvāṃso yathā kurvanti bhārata .
kuryādvidvāṃstathāsaktaścikīrṣurlokasaṃgraham

Word Meanings:
सक्ताः - attached
कर्मण्यविद्वांसो - the ignorant in action
यथा - as
कुर्वन्ति - act
भारत - O descendant of Bharata
कुर्याद्विद्वांस्तथासक्तश्चिकीर्षुर्लोकसंग्रहम् - so should the wise act unattached desiring world welfare

Life Application:
Work with the same energy as the most driven people, but direct your passion toward helping others instead of self-aggrandizement.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
As the ignorant act from attachment to action, O Bharata, so should the wise act without attachment, desiring the welfare of the world.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
As the ignorant act from attachment to action, O Bharata, so should the wise act without attachment, desiring the welfare of the world.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
As the ignorant act from attachment to action (Saktah karmani avidvamsah), O Bharata, so should the wise man (Vidvan) act without attachment (Asaktah), desiring solely the welfare and guidance of the world (Cikirshur lokasangraham).

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
As the ignorant act from attachment to action, O Bharata, so should the wise act without attachment, desiring the welfare of the world.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
As the ignorant act from attachment to action, O Bharata, so should the wise act without attachment, desiring the welfare of the world.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
As the ignorant act from attachment to action, O Bharata, so should the wise act without attachment, desiring the welfare of the world.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
As the ignorant act from attachment to action, O Bharata, so should the wise act without attachment, desiring the welfare of the world.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: A learned Sage must execute selfless duties for cosmic welfare and obedience to the Lord"s command.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Ignorant people act from personal attachment, whereas enlightened Sages perform duties unattached purely for Lokasangraha.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
As the ignorant act from attachment to action, O Bharata, so should the wise act without attachment, desiring the welfare of the world.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: A wise soul should act with equal diligence as the attached, but completely free from desire, for society"s guidance.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.25–3.26 jointly): "Saktah etc. and Na buddhibhedam etc."—a wise man should not unsettle the minds of attached ignorant ritualists, but inspire them by selfless personal practice.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "As the ignorant act from attachment to action, O Bharata, so should the wise act without attachment, desiring the welfare of the world"—instructs Sages to act with zeal for world welfare without any desire for personal gain.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: External actions of the wise and unwise look identical, but internal non-attachment marks the true distinction.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A wise devotee filled with Krishna"s love performs all duties with great enthusiasm for the Lord"s joy and world welfare.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Unattached execution of duty by the wise for Lokasangraha forms the eternal support of society.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
As the ignorant act from attachment to action, O Bharata, so should the wise act without attachment, desiring the welfare of the world.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing why enlightened Sages should act with equal fervor as the attached, but unattached for Lokasangraha.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A wise soul devoted to Vasudeva performs obligatory duties unattached for public education.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a wise devotee acts for Krishna"s joy, his selfless example inspires all souls.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: An unattached Sage engages in work solely to uphold social and spiritual traditions.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
As the ignorant perform their duties with attachment to results, the learned may also act, but without attachment, for the sake of leading people on the right path.
