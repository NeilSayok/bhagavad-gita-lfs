Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yatsāṅkhyaiḥ prāpyate sthānaṃ tadyogairapi gamyate .
ekaṃ sāṅkhyaṃ ca yogaṃ ca yaḥ paśyati sa paśyati

Word Meanings:
यत्साङ्ख्यैः - which by Sankhyas
प्राप्यते - is attained
स्थानं - the state
तद्योगैरपि - that by yogis also
गम्यते - is reached
एकं - as one
साङ्ख्यं - Sankhya path
च - and
योगं - yoga path
च - and
यः - who
पश्यति - sees
स - he
पश्यति - truly sees

Life Application:
Unify your intellectual insights and practical efforts, seeing them as complementary steps toward a single spiritual goal.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
That place which is reached by the Samkhyas is also reached by the Yogis. He sees who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
That place which is reached by the Samkhyas (Jnana Yogis) is reached by the Yogis (Karma Yogis) also. He sees, who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
That supreme abode of Liberation which is reached by the Samkhya-yogis (Jnana-yogis) is reached also by the selfless Karma-yogis; therefore, he alone sees truthfully who beholds Jnana Yoga and Karma Yoga as one in their ultimate fruit.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
That place which is reached by the Samkhyas is also reached by the Yogis. He sees who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
That place which is reached by the Samkhyas is also reached by the Yogis. He sees who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
That place which is reached by the Samkhyas (Jnana Yogis) is reached by the Yogis (Karma Yogis) also. He sees, who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
That place which is reached by the Samkhyas (Jnana Yogis) is reached by the Yogis (Karma Yogis) also. He sees, who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Samkhya and Yoga are one"—both Knowledge and selfless action, as limbs of devotion to the Lord, carry the soul to supreme Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The ultimate state of Liberation reached by Samkhya-sannyasis is reached equally by selfless Karma-yogis through knowledge.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
That place which is reached by the Samkhyas is also reached by the Yogis. He sees who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Both Jnana-nishta and Karma-nishta yield the single fruit of Self-perception; beholding their unity of purpose is true vision.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Children (the ignorant) speak of Samkhya (Knowledge) and Yoga (Action) as distinct, but not the wise. He who is firmly established in even one obtains the fruit of both. The supreme state which is reached by Samkhya-yogis is also reached by Karma-yogis; therefore he who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one in fruit, sees truly.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "That place which is reached by the Samkhyas is reached by the Yogis also. He sees, who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one"—asserts that both disciplines culminate in Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: He alone possesses true vision who perceives Samkhya and Yoga as one in fruit, since both terminate in reaching Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The knowledge of Samkhya and the practice of selfless Yoga both admit the devotee into Krishna"s eternal realm.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "He who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one, sees truly"—he alone is a true seer who views both paths as non-different from the perspective of their non-dual fruit.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
That place which is reached by the Samkhyas (Jnana Yogis) is reached by the Yogis (Karma Yogis) also. He sees, who sees Samkhya and Yoga as one.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving that the state reached by Samkhya-yogis is identical to that attained by Karma-yogis.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The ultimate goal of both Samkhya and Yoga is the direct realization of Lord Vasudeva; hence both are one in fruit.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Attaining the lotus feet of Lord Krishna is the sole supreme fruit of both Samkhya and Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Beholds Samkhya and Yoga as one"—perceiving their oneness through the relationship of means and end is the true resolution of all doubts.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
One who knows that the position reached by means of analytical study can also be attained by devotional service, and who therefore sees analytical study and devotional service to be on the same level, sees things as they are.
