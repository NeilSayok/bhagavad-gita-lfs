Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
vaktumarhasyaśeṣeṇa divyā hyātmavibhūtayaḥ .
yābhirvibhūtibhirlokānimāṃstvaṃ vyāpya tiṣṭhasi

Word Meanings:
वक्तुमर्हस्यशेषेण - You should describe fully
दिव्या - divine
ह्यात्मविभूतयः - indeed Your own glories
याभिर्विभूतिभिर्लोकानिमांस्त्वं - by which glories You these worlds
व्याप्य - pervading
तिष्ठसि - exist

Life Application:
Ask to hear the divine manifestations by which God pervades and abides in all these worlds.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
You alone are able to declare fully Your divine opulences by which You remain pervading all these worlds.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Thou shouldst indeed tell me without reserve of Thy divine glories by which Thou standest pervading all these worlds.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Please tell me without reserve of Your divine glories by which You remain pervading all these worlds.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Arjuna prays to Lord Krishna to reveal His divine opulences completely. He recognizes that only the Infinite Supreme Lord can accurately describe His own divine attributes through which He pervades the entire cosmos.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
You alone are able to declare without remainder Your divine manifestations, by which manifestations You stay pervading all these worlds.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
You alone are able to declare without reserve Your divine manifestations, by which manifestations You remain pervading all these worlds.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
You alone are able to declare without reserve Your divine manifestations, by which manifestations You remain pervading all these worlds.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Arjuna prays to the Lord, acknowledging that since His divine manifestations are infinite, the Lord alone is capable of revealing them—those opulences by which He pervades all worlds.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Arjuna recognizes that the Lord's manifestations are limitless. Hence he prays that only the Lord Himself can fully describe His own transcendent extension.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
You alone are capable of fully describing Your divine opulences, by which You remain pervading all these worlds.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: You alone are completely capable of describing Your infinite auspicious manifestations by which You abide as Ruler, pervading all these conscious and unconscious worlds.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Abhinavagupta did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: You alone are able to declare without reserve Your divine Self-glories (the expansive majesty of the Divine), by which opulences You remain pervading all these worlds.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Perfect knowledge of the all-pervading Supreme Lord's opulences is impossible for any created entity other than God Himself; hence Arjuna prays directly to Lord Krishna for their revelation.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Arjuna requests the Supreme Lord, acknowledging that He alone can fully reveal His divine Yogic majesty and manifestations by which He pervades everywhere in the universe.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Being unexcelled and omniscient, You alone are worthy of declaring without remainder Your divine manifestations by which You remain pervading all worlds as their ultimate Substratum.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: You alone are able to declare completely Your divine opulences by which You remain pervading all these worlds.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Knowledge of the Lord's boundless manifestations belongs solely to the self-luminous Supreme Ruler; therefore Arjuna humbly prays to the Lord Himself for their full revelation.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The divine opulences establishing the Lord's universal omnipresence and inner governance can only be expounded by the Supreme Lord Himself; this is Arjuna's intent.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: You alone are capable of describing Your divine manifestations by which You pervade the entire universe and conduct Your divine play.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: You alone can fully declare Your infinite divine opulences by which You sustain this entire universe in the relationship of pervader and pervaded.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Please tell me in detail of Your divine opulences by which You pervade all these worlds.
