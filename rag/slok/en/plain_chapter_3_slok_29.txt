Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
prakṛterguṇasammūḍhāḥ sajjante guṇakarmasu .
tānakṛtsnavido mandānkṛtsnavinna vicālayet

Word Meanings:
प्रकृतेर्गुणसम्मूढाः - deluded by nature's qualities
सज्जन्ते - are attached
गुणकर्मसु - in qualities and actions
तानकृत्स्नविदो - those of incomplete knowledge
मन्दान्कृत्स्नविन्न - dull persons, the all-knower should not
विचालयेत् - unsettle

Life Application:
Exercise patience with those who are attached to material results, offering quiet support rather than condescending judgment.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Those deluded by the qualities of Nature are attached to the functions of the qualities. The man of perfect knowledge should not unsettle the ignorant of imperfect knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: A perfectly enlightened master must not disrupt the nascent faith and duties of beginner souls.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: A Sage possessing full knowledge should not confuse dull-witted, attached ritualists, but guide them steadily in righteous karma.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Abruptly detaching dull-witted people from active rituals could ruin their faith; hence a Sage must not confuse them.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.29–3.30 jointly): Mayi sarvani etc. Renouncing all actions in Me, with mind fixed on the Self, free from hope and ego, fight without mental fever.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Those deluded by the qualities of Nature are attached to the functions of the qualities. The man of perfect knowledge should not unsettle the ignorant of imperfect knowledge"—reiterates pedagogical wisdom for spiritual teachers.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Gradual elevation of beginner souls is scripturally ordained; the Sage protects their initial faith.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Forcing unready souls into abstract path causes their ruin; hence the wise keep them gently engaged in duty.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: It is the duty of fully realized Sages to gently transition beginner seekers from selfish to selfless action.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving why fully enlightened Sages must refrain from disrupting the ritual practice of beginner souls.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A wise devotee of Vasudeva protects the faith of beginners by engaging them in Lord-dedicated duty.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: A wise devotee lovingly directs naive souls into Krishna"s service without raising doubts in their minds.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Preserving the ritual steadfastness of novice seekers is the solemn scriptural responsibility of the Sage.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Bewildered by the modes of material nature, the ignorant fully engage themselves in material activities and become attached. But the wise should not unsettle them, although these duties are inferior due to the performers" lack of knowledge.
