Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
mayādhyakṣeṇa prakṛtiḥ sūyate sacarācaram .
hetunānena kaunteya jagadviparivartate

Word Meanings:
मयाध्यक्षेण - under My supervision
प्रकृतिः - nature
सूयते - brings forth
सचराचरम् - the moving and unmoving
हेतुनानेन - by this cause
कौन्तेय - O son of Kunti
जगद्विपरिवर्तते - the world revolves

Life Application:
See nature working under divine supervision to produce moving and unmoving things, keeping the world revolving.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Under My supervision, Nature produces the moving and the unmoving; because of this, O Kaunteya, the world revolves.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Under My supervision, Nature produces the moving and the unmoving; because of this, O Kaunteya, the world revolves.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Under My supreme presidership, Prakriti projects the entire cosmos of moving and non-moving entities; for this reason, O son of Kunti, the world revolves.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Under My supervision, Nature produces the moving and the unmoving; because of this, O Kaunteya, the world revolves.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Under My supervision, Nature produces the moving and the unmoving; because of this, O Kaunteya, the world revolves.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: By the mere presence (supervision) of the Supreme Self, Maya-Prakriti brings forth the universe; just as iron moves near a magnet, Nature acts in the light of Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Under My supervision, Nature produces the moving and the unmoving; because of this, O Kaunteya, the world revolves.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Under My presidership Prakriti produces the moving and unmoving"—by My divine gaze alone, Prakriti manifests the cosmos; hence the world cycle rotates.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: By the mere witnessing glance (supervision) of the Supreme Self, Nature projects the moving and non-moving cosmos; remaining changeless Himself, God impels Nature into motion.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Under My supervision Prakriti produces all moving and unmoving"—by the mere witnessing presence of Lord Vasudeva as Consciousness, Maya projects all creation. By this sole presence, O son of Kunti, the cosmic wheel of projection and dissolution rotates continuously.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The Lord"s supreme will is the presiding power over Nature; impelled by the Lord, Nature brings forth the entire universe.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Under My supervision"—like a lodestone causing movement without action, Brahman"s mere presence causes Maya to transform into the universe, preserving Brahman"s non-agency.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Under My supervision, Nature produces the moving and the unmoving; because of this, O Kaunteya, the world revolves.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Demonstrating world creation through the mere presence of God; Witness Brahman remains unattached while illuminating Maya.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Empowered by the Lord"s omniscient presidership, Nature manifests the world; cosmic revolution is solely subordinate to divine will.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The Lord"s presence itself is Nature"s power; by divine light alone, Nature constructs and dissolves all creation.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Under the presidership of Witness Consciousness Brahman alone, agency is sparked in Maya-Prakriti, keeping the cosmic wheel turning.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
This material nature, which is one of My energies, is working under My direction, O son of Kunti, producing all moving and nonmoving beings. Under its rule this manifestation is created and annihilated again and again.
