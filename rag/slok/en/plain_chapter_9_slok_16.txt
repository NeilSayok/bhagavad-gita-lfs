Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ahaṃ kraturahaṃ yajñaḥ svadhāhamahamauṣadham .
mantro.ahamahamevājyamahamagnirahaṃ hutam

Word Meanings:
अहं - I
क्रतुरहं - I am Vedic ritual
यज्ञः - sacrifice
स्वधाहमहमौषधम् - I am oblation and herb
मन्त्रोऽहमहमेवाज्यमहमग्निरहं - I am mantra, ghee, and fire
हुतम् - the offering

Life Application:
Recognize God as the ritual, sacrifice, offering, healing herb, sacred chant, clarified butter, fire, and oblation.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am the Vedic ritual, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering to the ancestors, I am the medicinal herb, I am the sacred chant, I am the clarified butter, I am the fire, and I am the oblation.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I am the Vedic rite, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering, I am the medicinal herb, I am the mantra, I am the ghee, I am the fire, and I am the oblation.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I am the Vedic ritual, I am the sacrifice, I am the oblation to the ancestors, I am the healing herb, I am the sacred mantra, I am the butter, I am the fire, and I am the act of offering.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
I am the Vedic rite, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering to ancestors, I am the medicinal herb, I am the mantra, I am the clarified butter, I am the fire, and I am the oblation.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
I am the Vedic rite, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering, I am the medicinal herb, I am the mantra, I am the ghee, I am the fire, and I am the oblation.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
I am the Vedic rite, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering, I am the medicinal herb, I am the mantra, I am the ghee, I am the fire, and I am the oblation.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
I am the Vedic rite, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering, I am the medicinal herb, I am the mantra, I am the ghee, I am the fire, and I am the oblation.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "I am the sacrifice"—Lord Vishnu Himself is the supreme master and fruit-giver of all sacrifices, mantras, oblations, herbs, ghee, and fire.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: All Vedic and Smarta rituals, mantras, oblations, fire, and sacrificial acts are direct manifestations of the Supreme Lord as cause and effect.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am the Vedic ritual, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering to ancestors, I am the medicinal herb, I am the mantra, I am the clarified butter, I am the fire, and I am the oblation.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The Vedic rituals like Jyotishtoma, Smarta sacrifices, ancestor offerings, herbs, mantras, ghee, fires, and oblations are all My body and forms.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The ritual, offering, medicine, mantra, oblation, fire, parents and sustainer of the cosmos, sacred Omkara, Vedas, refuge, dissolution, immortality, death, and being and non-being—all this is solely the divine expansion of the All-Facing Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I am the Vedic rite, I am the sacrifice"—I am the Vedic ritual, Smarta sacrifice, ancestor food (svadha), herbs, mantras, ghee, sacrificial fires, and the act of offering.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The Supreme Lord, enjoyer of all sacrifices, abides as the sacrificial components (rites, mantras, fire, oblations), making all rituals fruitful.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: For His devoted seekers, the Lord Himself manifests as the sacrifice, mantras, fire, and oblations, revealing the all-embracing nature of wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Vedic rites, ancestral offerings, medicinal herbs, mantras, ghee, fire, and oblations are all the all-embracing glory of Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am the Vedic rite, I am the sacrifice, I am the offering to ancestors, I am the medicinal herb, I am the mantra, I am the ghee, I am the fire, and I am the oblation.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the divine nature of sacrificial components: all actions, means, and materials are manifestations of the All-Facing Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: All sacrificial components constitute the Lord's divine body; He alone is the object of worship and dispenser of fruits in all Vedic rites.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: I am all sacrifices, mantras, herbs, and oblations; without Me no Vedic ritual attains perfection.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The All-Facing Supreme Lord constitutes the very essence of the seeker's wisdom-sacrifice as rites, mantras, oblations, and fire.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
But it is I who am the ritual, I the sacrifice, the offering to the ancestors, the healing herb, the transcendental chant. I am the butter and the fire and the offering.
