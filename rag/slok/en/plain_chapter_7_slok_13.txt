Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
tribhirguṇamayairbhāvairebhiḥ sarvamidaṃ jagat .
mohitaṃ nābhijānāti māmebhyaḥ paramavyayam

Word Meanings:
त्रिभिर्गुणमयैर्भावैरेभिः - by these states of three gunas
सर्वमिदं - all this
जगत् - world
मोहितं - deluded
नाभिजानाति - does not know
मामेभ्यः - Me from these
परमव्ययम् - supreme (transcendent) and imperishable

Life Application:
Look beyond the delusion caused by the three modes of nature, recognizing the unchangeable supreme spirit beyond them.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, all this world knows Me not, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, all this world knows Me not, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Deluded by these three states born of the modes of material nature, all this unreasoning world fails to recognize Me, who am eternal, imperishable, and beyond all Gunas.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, all this world knows Me not, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, all this world knows Me not, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, all this world knows Me not, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, all this world knows Me not, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Know Me not who am imperishable beyond Gunas"—souls deluded by three Gunas fail to behold Lord Vishnu"s supreme nature; devotion alone pierces this veil of Maya.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Bound by the delusive states of the three Gunas born of Maya, the living entity forgets its true Master, Supreme Lord Vasudeva, and wanders in Samsara.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, all this world knows Me not, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Deluded by sense-pleasures born of the three Gunas due to past Karmas, souls fail to recognize Me, the supreme Ruler beyond Gunas.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Whatever states that are Sattvic, Rajasic, or Tamasic, know them to proceed from Me alone; I am not in them, but they are in Me. Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, this entire world fails to recognize Me, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Deluded by these three states born of Gunas, all this world knows Me not, who am beyond them and imperishable"—the illusion created by Gunas blinds worldly beings from discerning the Supreme Self (Brahman), which is ever-pure, ever-free, and unchangeable.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The influence of ignorance and the three Gunas veils human discernment, preventing souls from comprehending Lord Vishnu"s eternity and independence.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The veil of the Lord"s three-fold Maya deprives souls of tasting the supreme nectar of Krishna-devotion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Beyond them and imperishable"—the obscuring and projecting powers of Maya prevent the soul from direct perception of unconditioned Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, all this world knows Me not, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural exposition of the delusive three-fold creation and the Lord"s imperishable state beyond Gunas.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Entangled in triple material desires due to beginningless Karma, the soul fails to behold Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: As long as the veil of illusory Maya is not removed, realization of indwelling Lord Krishna remains impossible.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Deluded knows Me not"—the mind submerged in three-fold worldly attachment cannot realize the Self-luminous Lord.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Deluded by the three modes [goodness, passion and ignorance], the whole world does not know Me, who am above the modes and inexhaustible.
