Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
puruṣaḥ sa paraḥ pārtha bhaktyā labhyastvananyayā .
yasyāntaḥsthāni bhūtāni yena sarvamidaṃ tatam

Word Meanings:
पुरुषः - the Supreme Lord
स - He
परः - transcendent
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
भक्त्या - by devotion
लभ्यस्त्वनन्यया - attainable by unswerving
यस्यान्तःस्थानि - within whom abide
भूतानि - all beings
येन - by whom
सर्वमिदं - all this universe
ततम् - is pervaded

Life Application:
Attain that supreme divine person in whom all beings reside and by whom all is pervaded through unswerving devotion.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
That Supreme Person, O Partha, in whom all beings abide and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
That Supreme Person, O Partha, in whom all beings abide and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
That Supreme Person, in whom all created beings reside and by whom this entire cosmos is pervaded, O Partha, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
That Supreme Person, O Partha, in whom all beings abide and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
That Supreme Person, O Partha, in whom all beings abide and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
That Supreme Person, O Partha, in whom all beings abide and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
That Supreme Person, O Partha, in whom all beings abide and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Attainable by undivided devotion alone"—all-pervading, all-supporting Supreme Lord Sri Hari is reachable by His devotee solely through unswerving devotion.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "That Supreme Person is reachable by undivided devotion"—the all-pervading Supreme Lord Vasudeva in whom all universe resides is accessible only through exclusive self-surrender.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
That Supreme Person, O Partha, in whom all beings abide and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: That Supreme Person Lord Narayana, the inner ruler of all, in whom all beings reside and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Superior to that unmanifest is the Eternal Unmanifest Supreme Lord, who perishes not even when all beings perish. That is declared as the Imperishable and Supreme Goal, attaining which souls return not; that Supreme Person is reachable through exclusive devotion.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "That Supreme Person, in whom all beings abide and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by undivided devotion alone, O Partha"—the unconditioned Supreme Brahman, holding all creation within Itself and pervading everything like space, is realized solely through non-dual devotion (Jnana-Nishtha).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Unswerving devotion is the matchless means to attain all-pervading Lord Vishnu; pleased by devotion alone, the Supreme Lord bestows Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Purushottama Lord Krishna, the abode of all universe, is attainable solely through exclusive loving devotion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "In whom all beings abide"—the Supreme Cause and all-pervading Lord is realized solely through non-dual devotion of Self-knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: That Supreme Person, O Partha, in whom all beings abide and by whom all this is pervaded, is attainable by unswerving devotion alone.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural proof of attaining the all-pervading Supreme Person through undivided devotion.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva, the foundation of all universe, becomes easily accessible through exclusive surrender to grant salvation.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Unswerving loving devotion is the key by which the all-pervading Lord manifests in the devotee"s heart.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Attainable by undivided devotion"—an unswerving vision free of duality in Consciousness Self is the path to Supreme Person.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Supreme Personality of Godhead, who is greater than all, is attainable by unalloyed devotion. Although He is present in His abode, He is all-pervading, and everything is situated within Him.
