Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sparśānkṛtvā bahirbāhyāṃścakṣuścaivāntare bhruvoḥ .
prāṇāpānau samau kṛtvā nāsābhyantaracāriṇau

Word Meanings:
स्पर्शान्कृत्वा - shutting out contacts
बहिर्बाह्यांश्चक्षुश्चैवान्तरे - externals outside, eyes between
भ्रुवोः - of the eyebrows
प्राणापानौ - prana and apana breaths
समौ - equal
कृत्वा - having made
नासाभ्यन्तरचारिणौ - moving within nostrils

Life Application:
Take time for quiet meditation, drawing your attention inward and balancing your breath to calm your nervous system.
