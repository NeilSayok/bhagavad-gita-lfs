Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
na buddhibhedaṃ janayedajñānāṃ karmasaṅginām .
joṣayetsarvakarmāṇi vidvānyuktaḥ samācaran

Word Meanings:
न - not
बुद्धिभेदं - confusion of intellect
जनयेदज्ञानां - should create in the ignorant
कर्मसङ्गिनाम् - attached to action
जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि - should engage in all actions
विद्वान्युक्तः - the wise steadfast
समाचरन् - properly performing

Life Application:
Encourage others gently in their current responsibilities instead of criticizing their motives or disturbing their peace of mind.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Let no wise man unsettle the minds of ignorant people attached to action; he should engage them in all actions, himself performing them with devotion.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Let no wise man unsettle the minds of ignorant people attached to action; he should engage them in all actions, himself performing them with devotion.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Let no wise man unsettle the intellect (Buddhibhedam na janayet) of ignorant people attached to fruits of action; rather, performing all actions himself with devotion and harmony (Samacaran vidvan), he should inspire them to engage in all noble actions (Joshayet sarvakarmani).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: A wise soul, performing Lord-dedicated selfless duties, purifies and strengthens the intellect of the unlearned.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The enlightened Sage should not disturb the faith of ignorant attached ritualists, but encourage them in righteous duties by personal example.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Let no wise man unsettle the minds of ignorant people attached to action; he should engage them in all actions, himself performing them with devotion.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: A wise Sage, himself engaging in scriptural duties as divine offerings, should gently encourage ignorant people to perform duties.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: "Saktah etc. and Na buddhibhedam etc."—a wise man should not unsettle the minds of attached ignorant ritualists, but inspire them by selfless personal practice.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Let no wise man unsettle the minds of ignorant people attached to action; he should engage them in all actions, himself performing them with devotion"—warns against creating doubt in novice minds, advising gentle engagement.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Safeguarding the faith of ignorant people is the paramount social duty of the enlightened Sage.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A wise Krishna-devotee must avoid confusing the devotional service and swadharma of ordinary people.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: According to qualification, the Sage must preserve the faith of beginners and gradually guide them toward selfless action.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Let no wise man unsettle the minds of ignorant people attached to action; he should engage them in all actions, himself performing them with devotion.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving why wise Sages must refrain from disturbing beginner faith and lead them by example.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A wise soul devoted to Vasudeva, practicing selfless Karma-yoga, anchors ordinary men in their Swadharma.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The foremost duty of a wise devotee is to joyously engage all souls in Krishna"s devotional service.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Preserving intact the action-faith of novice practitioners for world-welfare is the Sage"s solemn duty.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
So as not to disrupt the minds of ignorant men attached to the fruitive results of prescribed duties, a learned person should not induce them to stop work. Rather, by working in the spirit of devotion, he should engage them in all sorts of activities [for the gradual development of Krsna consciousness].
