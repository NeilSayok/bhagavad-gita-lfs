Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
icchādveṣasamutthena dvandvamohena bhārata .
sarvabhūtāni sammohaṃ sarge yānti parantapa

Word Meanings:
इच्छाद्वेषसमुत्थेन - arisen from desire and aversion
द्वन्द्वमोहेन - by delusion of duality
भारत - O descendant of Bharata
सर्वभूतानि - all living beings
सम्मोहं - delusion (ignorance)
सर्गे - at birth (into creation)
यान्ति - fall into / attain
परन्तप - O conqueror of foes

Life Application:
Beware of the dualities of attraction and aversion born of delusion, which confuse all living beings from birth.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
By the delusion of pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings fall into delusion at birth, O scorcher of foes.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
By the delusion of the pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings fall into delusion, O scorcher of foes.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O descendant of Bharata, O scorcher of enemies, all living beings fall into deep delusion right at birth owing to the dualities born of desire and aversion.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
By the delusion of pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings fall into delusion, O scorcher of foes.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
By the delusion of pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings fall into delusion, O scorcher of foes.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
By the delusion of the pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings fall into delusion, O scorcher of foes.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
By the delusion of the pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings fall into delusion, O scorcher of foes.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Duality born of egoism and sense-desire alienates all souls from recognizing the absolute eternity of Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Delusion born of the duality of desire and aversion bewilders the human intellect, causing the soul to forget its eternal Master, Supreme Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
By the delusion of pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings fall into delusion at birth, O scorcher of foes.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Duality born of attachment to the pleasant and aversion to the unpleasant, stemming from past Karma, binds creatures in ignorance right from birth.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: O Arjuna, I know the beings of the past, present, and future, but Me no one knows. O Bharata, scorcher of foes, by the delusion of opposites arising from desire and aversion, all beings fall into deep delusion at the time of creation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "By the delusion of pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings fall into delusion at birth"—explains that attraction to pleasant objects and repulsion from painful ones create a delusive haze preventing Self-realization.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Attachment and aversion blind the soul"s eye of discrimination; as long as duality persists, God-realization remains impossible.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Veiled by the delusion of dualities, the soul fails to perceive Lord Krishna"s supernatural beauty; divine grace alone pierces this veil.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Fall into delusion at birth"—right from the origin of creation, the duality of attachment and aversion born of ignorance deludes all beings.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
By the delusion of the pairs of opposites arising from desire and aversion, O Bharata, all beings fall into delusion, O scorcher of foes.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural exposition of the emergence of dualistic delusion and its blinding impact on all created beings.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: From birth, attachment to favorable objects and repulsion toward unfavorable ones turn souls away from Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Dualistic delusion binds the soul in worldly attachment, preventing it from surrendering completely to Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "By delusion born of desire and aversion"—attraction and repulsion born of egoism are the primeval seeds of all ignorance.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O scion of Bharata, O conqueror of the foe, all living entities are born into delusion, bewildered by dualities arisen from desire and hate.
