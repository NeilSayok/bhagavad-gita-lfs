Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
apare niyatāhārāḥ prāṇānprāṇeṣu juhvati .
sarve.apyete yajñavido yajñakṣapitakalmaṣāḥ

Word Meanings:
अपरे - others
नियताहाराः - regulating food
प्राणान्प्राणेषु - life-breaths into life-breaths
जुह्वति - sacrifice
सर्वेऽप्येते - all these even
यज्ञविदो - knowers of sacrifice
यज्ञक्षपितकल्मषाः - whose sins are purged by sacrifice

Life Application:
Practice moderation in eating and consumption, purifying your body and mind through intentional habits.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Others, restricting their food, offer vital breaths into vital breaths. All these are knowers of sacrifice, their sins destroyed by sacrifice.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Others, restricting their food, offer vital breaths into vital breaths. All these are knowers of sacrifice, their sins destroyed by sacrifice.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Others, restricting their food (Mitahara), offer the functions of the senses (Pranas) into the vital breath itself (Prana); all these seekers are knowers of Yajna (Yajnavidah), their sins and karmic impurities fully erased through the practice of sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "All these are knowers of sacrifice"—seekers practicing all branches of Yajna with realization of Lord Vishnu as Supreme Lord become sinless and attain Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: All these knowers of sacrifice, having their sins washed away by their respective Yajnas, become fully qualified for Brahman-Knowledge.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Others offer inhalation into exhalation and exhalation into inhalation, intent on Pranayama, while others restricting food offer vital breaths into vital breaths; all these are knowers of sacrifice, cleansed of sins.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Restricting food, mastering breath-control, and performing Yajnas cleanses seekers of all sins, qualifying them for God-realization.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Through breath-control (Pranayama) and regulated diet, all these practitioners are knowers of sacrifice whose sins have been erased by sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Others, restricting their food, offer vital breaths into vital breaths. All these are knowers of sacrifice, their sins destroyed by sacrifice"—summarizes that all twelve Yajnas purify the mind of all karmic sins (*Yajna-ksapita-kalmasa*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Erasure of sin through sacrificial practice forms the authoritative proof of mental purity.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: All devotees performing Yajna with mind dedicated to Krishna become sinless and eligible for the Lord"s exclusive grace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Sins destroyed by sacrifice"—when all sins are destroyed via diverse sacrifices, the mind becomes pure and fit for Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving how restricting food and offering breath into breath destroys all sins.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Practitioners performing sacrifices for Vasudeva"s pleasure are released from all sins, attaining eternal Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a seeker"s life turns into a continuous sacrifice, all impurities and sins are washed away by Krishna"s grace.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Sins destroyed by sacrifice"—selfless practice of various Yajnas is the ultimate destroyer of all nescience-impurities.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Others, who curtail the eating process, offer the outgoing breath into itself, as a sacrifice. All these performers who know the meaning of sacrifice become cleansed of sinful reactions.
