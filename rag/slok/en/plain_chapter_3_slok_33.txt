Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sadṛśaṃ ceṣṭate svasyāḥ prakṛterjñānavānapi .
prakṛtiṃ yānti bhūtāni nigrahaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati

Word Meanings:
सदृशं - in accordance with
चेष्टते - acts
स्वस्याः - one's own
प्रकृतेर्ज्ञानवानपि - nature even the wise
प्रकृतिं - nature
यान्ति - follow
भूतानि - beings
निग्रहः - restraint
किं - what
करिष्यति - will do

Life Application:
Work in harmony with your innate strengths and nature rather than forcing yourself into roles that misalign with your character.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Even a wise man acts in accordance with his own nature; beings follow nature; what will restraint avail?

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Even a wise man acts in accordance with his own nature; beings follow nature; what will restraint avail?

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Even a wise man (Jnanavan api) acts in accordance with his own nature (Sadrisham svasyah prakriteh); all living beings follow their nature (Bhutani prakritim yanti); what can mere forced restraint (Nigrahah kim karishyati) achieve?

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Souls act according to their beginningless nature, yet purification of nature via scriptural discipline is attainable.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The powerful nature formed by past impressions influences both the wise and unwise; mere forcible repression yields no success.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Even a wise man acts in accordance with his own nature; beings follow nature; what will restraint avail?

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Nature built from beginningless impressions is so potent that forcible suppression without spiritual wisdom fails.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.33–3.34 jointly): Sadrsam etc. and Indriyasya etc. Attraction and aversion reside in senses toward their objects; a wise man, not succumbing to them, remains free in action.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Even a wise man acts in accordance with his own nature; beings follow nature; what will restraint avail"—explains the irresistible force of past latent impressions (*Samskaras*) over human behaviour.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The strength of Prakriti generates attraction and aversion; spiritual discrimination alone uproots them completely.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Until divine grace awakens bhakti, the soul remains bound to act under the compelling influence of Prakriti.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Mere external sensory repression is futile unless internal attachments and aversions are wisely abandoned.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Even a wise man acts in accordance with his own nature; beings follow nature; what will restraint avail?

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis substantiating why even a wise man acts according to nature and why external force alone fails.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Without devotion to Vasudeva, suppressing the force of Prakriti through ascetic force alone is impossible.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a seeker surrenders his nature into Krishna"s service, all obstacles of Prakriti vanish automatically.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Forcible restraint cannot dissolve past impressions; only spiritual discrimination and unattached Karma-yoga erode them.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Even a man of knowledge acts according to his own nature, for everyone follows the nature he has acquired from the three modes. What can restraint accomplish?
