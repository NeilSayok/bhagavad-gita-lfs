Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
na ca māṃ tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya .
udāsīnavadāsīnamasaktaṃ teṣu karmasu

Word Meanings:
न - not
च - and
मां - Me
तानि - those
कर्माणि - actions
निबध्नन्ति - bind
धनञ्जय - O Dhananjaya
उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं - seated unattached like indifferent
तेषु - in those
कर्मसु - actions

Life Application:
Remain unattached and indifferent like the divine presence, untouched by the cosmic work you perform.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Nor do these actions bind Me, O Dhananjaya, sitting like one unconcerned, unattached to these actions.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Nor do these actions bind Me, O Dhananjaya, sitting like one unconcerned, unattached to these actions.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
These cosmic acts of creation and dissolution do not bind Me, O Dhananjaya, for I remain unattached and indifferent as a witness.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Supreme Self, free from egoism of doership and attachment to results, remains like an indifferent witness; hence cosmic creation binds Him not.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Nor do these actions bind Me, O Dhananjaya, sitting like one unconcerned, unattached to these actions.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Diverse cosmic creations do not bind the Lord, for He remains utterly unattached to fruits and abides as an indifferent witness.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Supreme Lord abides as immutable Witness, like one indifferent; hence grand cosmic operations of creation and dissolution cause no attachment or bondage in Him.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Nor do these actions bind Me, O Dhananjaya"—creation and dissolution do not shackle Lord Vasudeva. "Sitting like one unconcerned, unattached to actions"—devoid of agency and fruit-desire, the Lord abides as immutable Witness, unsullied by cosmic works.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The Lord"s cosmic play is inherently unattached and detached; He sports by His sovereign desire yet remains completely beyond karmic bondage.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Sitting like one unconcerned"—God is merely an indifferent Witness; in the absence of egoic agency, no taint of action can ever arise.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Nor do these actions bind Me, O Dhananjaya, sitting like one unconcerned, unattached to these actions.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the untainted nature of the Supreme Self; as Witness Consciousness, Brahman remains beyond all actions and ever unattached.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord abides as Witness without bias or cruelty; responsibility for world diversity rests on individual souls" karma, leaving God faultless.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The Lord, while enacting everything, remains a non-doer; for He is completely free from attachment to fruits and acts as Witness.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: In the indifferent Witness Consciousness lacking agency, actions of creation and dissolution touch Brahman not in the least.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O Dhananjaya, all this work cannot bind Me. I am ever detached from all these material activities, seated as though neutral.
