Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
tapāmyahamahaṃ varṣaṃ nigṛhṇāmyutsṛjāmi ca .
amṛtaṃ caiva mṛtyuśca sadasaccāhamarjuna

Word Meanings:
तपाम्यहमहं - I give heat, I
वर्षं - rain
निगृह्णाम्युत्सृजामि - withhold and send forth
च - and
अमृतं - immortality
चैव - and also
मृत्युश्च - and death
सदसच्चाहमर्जुन - being and non-being I am, Arjuna

Life Application:
See God giving heat, withholding and sending rain, being both immortality and death, existence and non-existence.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
As Sun I give heat; I withhold and send forth the rain; I am Immortality and also Death, both Being (Sat) and Non-being (Asat), O Arjuna.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
As Sun I give heat; I withhold and send forth the rain; I am immortality and also death, existence and non-existence, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I radiate heat as the sun, I withhold and release the rain, I am immortality and death, and I am both manifest existence and unmanifest non-being, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
As Sun I give heat; I withhold and send forth the rain; I am immortality and also death, existence and non-existence, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Being and Non-being am I"—manifest effect is Sat and unmanifest cause is Asat. Lord Vishnu alone is the supreme regulator of both Sat and Asat.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Regulating solar heat, rain, immortality of demigods, mortality of creatures, and effect (Sat) and cause (Asat) are all manifestations of the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
As Sun I give heat; I withhold and send forth the rain; I am Immortality and also Death, both Being and Non-being, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Giving heat and rain as the sun, life (immortality), death, present existing things (Sat), and past/future things (Asat) are all the modes of the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The ritual, offering, medicine, mantra, oblation, fire, parents and sustainer of the cosmos, sacred Omkara, Vedas, refuge, dissolution, immortality, death, and being and non-being—all this is solely the divine expansion of the All-Facing Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I give heat, I withhold and release rain"—As the sun I radiate heat, absorb water and release rain. I am immortality of gods, death of mortals, manifest existence (Sat), and unmanifest cause (Asat); all is the expansion of Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Asat" denotes not absolute voidness, but the unmanifest subtle state; all-pervading Lord Vishnu governs both gross (Sat) and subtle (Asat) realms.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Heat, rain, immortality, death, being, and non-being are manifold play-manifestations of the Lord; He Himself is the substratum of all states.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Solar heat, rain, immortality, death, manifest effect (Sat), and unmanifest cause (Asat) are all glorious light-expansions of Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: As Sun I give heat; I withhold and send forth rain; I am immortality and also death, existence and non-existence, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Establishing the divinity of natural phenomena, temporal divisions, and essential states, revealing the Lord's absolute omnipresence.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord regulates heat and rain through solar bodies; immortality, death, Sat, and Asat are all His divine attributes and body.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: I radiate heat, withhold and release rain; immortality, death, Sat, and Asat are all My blissful Form.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The All-Facing Supreme Lord alone plays everywhere as solar heat, rain, immortality, death, and effect-cause (Sat-Asat).

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O Arjuna, I give heat, and I withhold and send forth the rain. I am immortality, and I am also death personified. Both matter and spirit are in Me.
