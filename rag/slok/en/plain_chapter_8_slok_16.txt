Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ābrahmabhuvanāllokāḥ punarāvartino.arjuna .
māmupetya tu kaunteya punarjanma na vidyate

Word Meanings:
आब्रह्मभुवनाल्लोकाः - worlds up to realm of Brahma
पुनरावर्तिनोऽर्जुन - subject to return, O Arjuna
मामुपेत्य - having attained Me
तु - indeed
कौन्तेय - O son of Kunti
पुनर्जन्म - rebirth
न - not
विद्यते - takes place (exists)

Life Application:
Realize that all worlds up to the highest heaven are subject to return, but reaching God ends rebirth forever.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
All the worlds, including the realm of Brahma, are subject to return, O Arjuna; but after attaining Me, O son of Kunti, there is no rebirth.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
All the worlds, including the realm of Brahma, are subject to return, O Arjuna; but after attaining Me, O Kaunteya, there is no rebirth.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
All worlds, up to the highest abode of Brahma, are subject to rebirth, O Arjuna; but having reached Me alone, O son of Kunti, a soul is never reborn.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
All the worlds, including the realm of Brahma, are subject to return, O Arjuna; but after attaining Me, O Kaunteya, there is no rebirth.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Up to the abode of Brahma"—all beings up to Brahma"s realm return upon merit exhaustion; only exclusive devotees reaching Lord Vishnu"s realm are liberated forever.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: All worlds up to Brahmaloka are subject to return because saguna rewards are temporary; only attainment of Saguna/Nirguna Lord Vasudeva grants eternal non-return.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
All the worlds, including the realm of Brahma, are subject to return, O Arjuna; but after attaining Me, O Kaunteya, there is no rebirth.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "All worlds up to Brahmaloka are subject to return"—all enjoyments up to Brahma"s realm are bound by time and perishable; but for one attaining Me, time has no sway, hence no rebirth.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: All worlds up to Brahmaloka are subject to return; but upon attaining Me, there is no rebirth.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "All worlds up to Brahmaloka are subject to return, O Arjuna; but attaining Me there is no rebirth"—all realms up to Brahma"s abode are bounded by time, hence those reaching them return when merit is exhausted. But attaining Lord Vasudeva, who is beyond time, frees the soul from rebirth forever.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The limitation and transience of all material realms is proven; the unlimited nature of God"s abode is the foundation of eternal Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: All realms up to Brahma are perishable being achieved by karma; direct grace of Lord Krishna alone bestows non-returning supreme liberation.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Attaining Me there is no rebirth"—destruction of all worlds born of ignorance is inevitable; realizing Supreme Brahman is absolute end of evil and supreme Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: All the worlds, including the realm of Brahma, are subject to return, O Arjuna; but after attaining Me, O Kaunteya, there is no rebirth.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural proof of the limited nature of all worlds versus the infinite eternity of God"s abode.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: All enjoyments up to Brahmaloka perish with merit exhaustion; attaining Vasudeva alone bestows eternal Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Except the Lord"s supreme abode, all other realms are perishable and places of birth and death.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Up to Brahmaloka"—Brahma"s status, being a created effect, is bound by time limits; attaining Causal Brahman alone is eternal.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
From the highest planet in the material world down to the lowest, all are places of misery wherein repeated birth and death take place. But one who attains to My abode, O son of Kunti, never takes birth again.
