Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
omityekākṣaraṃ brahma vyāharanmāmanusmaran .
yaḥ prayāti tyajandehaṃ sa yāti paramāṃ gatim

Word Meanings:
ओमित्येकाक्षरं - the monosyllable Om
ब्रह्म - Brahman
व्याहरन्मामनुस्मरन् - chanting and remembering Me
यः - whoever
प्रयाति - departs
त्यजन्देहं - relinquishing the body
स - he
याति - attains
परमां - the supreme
गतिम् - destination

Life Application:
Utter the sacred sound while remembering the divine presence as you leave the body to attain the supreme goal.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Uttering the monosyllable Om, the Brahman, and remembering Me, he who departs, leaving the body, attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Uttering the monosyllable Om, the Brahman, and remembering Me, he who departs, leaving the body, attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Uttering the single-syllabled Om, the Brahman, and remembering Me constantly as he quits the body, such a seeker attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Uttering the monosyllable Om, the Brahman, and remembering Me, he who departs leaving the body attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Uttering the monosyllable Om, the Brahman, and remembering Me, he who departs, leaving the body, attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Uttering the monosyllable Om, the Brahman, and remembering Me, he who departs, leaving the body, attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Uttering the monosyllable Om, the Brahman, and remembering Me, he who departs, leaving the body, attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Raising the life-breath through the Brahma-nadi (Sushumna) to the head and meditating on the sacred monosyllable Om as Lord Vishnu while quitting the body, the seeker attains the Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Uttering monosyllable Om, the Brahman"—Om is the primary name and symbol of Brahman; uttering Om while remembering Lord Vasudeva at death bestows Mukti.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Uttering the monosyllable Om, the Brahman, and remembering Me, he who departs, leaving the body, attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Uttering the monosyllable Om, the name of Lord Vasudeva, and meditating on His divine supreme form while quitting the body, one reaches the Supreme Abode Vaikuntha.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Restraining all sense-gates, locking the mind within the heart, and established in concentration uttering Om while remembering the Lord at departure, that ever-steadfast yogi easily attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Uttering monosyllable Om, remembering Me, he who departs leaving the body attains Supreme Goal"—one who utters the sacred name Om representing Brahman and meditates on its signified Reality, Lord Vasudeva, upon relinquishing the body, reaches the highest destination of Kaivalya Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The sacred syllable Om is the primary name of Supreme Lord Vishnu; restraining mind and life-breath in Sushumna while uttering Om at death bestows liberation.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Restraining senses and contemplating Lord Krishna in the heart with the sacred syllable Om at departure is the means to attain the Divine Realm.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Uttering monosyllable Om"—the union of Pranava chanting and divine meditation at the departure of life-breath is the supreme means for Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Uttering the monosyllable Om, the Brahman, and remembering Me, he who departs, leaving the body, attains the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the attainment of the Supreme Goal at death through Pranava chanting combined with divine meditation.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Quitting the body while chanting Om and meditating on Lord Vasudeva leads the seeker to Vaikuntha.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Chanting Om while meditating lovingly on Lord Krishna is the supreme path to Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Uttering Om, the Brahman"—contemplation on Supreme Brahman through Pranava establishes the seeker in Kaivalya.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
After being situated in this yoga practice and uttering the sacred syllable om, the supreme combination of letters, if one thinks of the Supreme Personality of Godhead and quits his body, he will certainly reach the spiritual planets.
