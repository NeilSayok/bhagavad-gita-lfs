Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yajñārthātkarmaṇo.anyatra loko.ayaṃ karmabandhanaḥ .
tadarthaṃ karma kaunteya muktasaṅgaḥ samācara

Word Meanings:
यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र - except for sacrifice work
लोकोऽयं - this world
कर्मबन्धनः - bound by action
तदर्थं - for that purpose
कर्म - action
कौन्तेय - O son of Kunti
मुक्तसङ्गः - free from attachment
समाचर - perform well

Life Application:
Transform your routine chores into selfless service for others, freeing yourself from the bondage of selfish motives.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The world is bound by actions other than those performed for the sake of Yajna; therefore, O son of Kunti, perform thou action for that sake alone, free from attachment.

The world is bound by action other than that performed for the sake of Sacrifice (Yajna or God); therefore, O son of Kunti, perform thou action for that sake alone, free from all attachment (Muktasangah).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Yajna indeed is Lord Vishnu"—according to Sruti, Yajna denotes Vishnu; action performed for Him never binds.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Except for work dedicated to Yajna (Lord Vishnu), all other selfish acts cause bondage; hence perform work unattached for Yajna.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The world is bound by actions other than those performed for the sake of Yajna; therefore, O son of Kunti, perform thou action for that sake alone, free from attachment.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Actions performed for any reason other than pleasing Lord Vishnu (the Lord of Yajna) cause bondage; hence act selflessly for Yajna.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Yajnarthat etc. The world is bound by actions other than those performed for Yajna; perform work for that sake alone.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "The world is bound by actions other than those performed for the sake of Yajna; therefore, O son of Kunti, perform thou action for that sake alone, free from attachment"—defines Yajna as Vishnu or God-dedicated sacrifice which neutralizes karmic bondage.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Surrendering all actions for Vishnu"s delight completely arrests karmic bondage.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: All activities performed for Krishna-yajna become divine service, rendering the practitioner liberated.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Performing sacrifices with God-dedicated intellect and non-attachment is the paramount means for freedom from karma.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
The world is bound by actions other than those performed for the sake of Yajna; therefore, O son of Kunti, perform thou action for that sake alone, free from attachment.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis establishing how unattached action performed for Yajna frees man from karmic bondage.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Performing ordained duties as a sacrifice to Vasudeva releases the seeker from samsara.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When every action is surrendered to Krishna-yajna, that action itself becomes the portal to liberation.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: All selfish work lacking God-dedication binds; hence work for Yajna alone is duty.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Work done as a sacrifice for Visnu has to be performed, otherwise work causes bondage in this material world. Therefore, O son of Kunti, perform your prescribed duties for His satisfaction, and in that way you will always remain free from bondage.
