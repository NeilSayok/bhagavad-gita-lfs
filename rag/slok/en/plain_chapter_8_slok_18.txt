Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
avyaktād vyaktayaḥ sarvāḥ prabhavantyaharāgame .
rātryāgame pralīyante tatraivāvyaktasaṃjñake

Word Meanings:
अव्यक्ताद् - from the unmanifest
व्यक्तयः - all manifestations
सर्वाः - all
प्रभवन्त्यहरागमे - emanate at the break of day
रात्र्यागमे - at the arrival of night
प्रलीयन्ते - they dissolve
तत्रैवाव्यक्तसंज्ञके - in that very unmanifest

Life Application:
Recognize how all manifest beings emerge from the unmanifest at daybreak and dissolve back into it at nightfall.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
At the coming of day all the manifested spring from the unmanifested; at the coming of night they dissolve into that alone which is called the unmanifested.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
At the coming of day all the manifested spring from the unmanifested; at the coming of night they dissolve into that alone which is called the unmanifested.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
At the dawn of Brahma"s day, all manifested creation emerges from the unmanifest state; at the approach of his night, it dissolves back into that very unmanifest cause.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
At the coming of day all the manifested spring from the unmanifested; at the coming of night they dissolve into that alone which is called the unmanifested.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: A single day of Brahma spans a thousand mahayugas, and his night is of equal duration. At cosmic dissolution, all worlds merge into Brahma"s unmanifest state and emerge again; yet Lord Vishnu"s supreme realm remains eternal beyond this cycle.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The term "Unmanifest" here refers to the slumbering causal state of Hiranyagarbha/Brahma from which cosmic manifestation and dissolution occur.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
At the coming of day all the manifested spring from the unmanifested; at the coming of night they dissolve into that alone which is called the unmanifested.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: At daybreak of Brahma, all differentiated universe of names and forms emerges from Brahma"s subtle unmanifest state, and merges into it at nightfall.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those who know Brahma"s day lasting a thousand yugas and night lasting a thousand yugas are truly knowers of cosmic time. At daybreak, all beings manifest from the unmanifest (Brahma"s subtle state); at nightfall, they dissolve back into it. Thus the multitude of beings, helpless under karma, repeatedly emerge and dissolve.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "At the coming of day all manifested beings emerge from the unmanifest; at nightfall they dissolve into that very unmanifest"—at Brahma"s daybreak, all name-and-form creation manifests from Brahma"s subtle unmanifest cause (sleep state), and dissolves back into it at cosmic night.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The duration of Brahma"s day and night reveals the limit of cosmic time; the cycle of creation and dissolution demonstrates the helplessness of souls, whereas the Lord"s realm is eternal and non-returning.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: At Brahma"s daybreak the manifested world emerges and merges at night; only Krishna"s abode is eternal.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "From the Unmanifest"—the unmanifest cause is the subtle state of ignorance/Brahma from which the dualistic universe repeatedly arises and dissolves.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: At the coming of day all the manifested spring from the unmanifested; at the coming of night they dissolve into that alone which is called the unmanifested.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative analysis of the unmanifest cause (Hiranyagarbha/Prakriti) governing origin and dissolution.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Through Brahma"s day-night cycle, dissolution of the effect-world into the unmanifest and re-emergence is demonstrated.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Even Brahma"s unmanifest state undergoes change; only Lord Krishna"s own realm is immutable and eternal.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "From Unmanifest all manifestations spring"—proves the cycle of origin and dissolution of names and forms from Maya cause.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
At the beginning of Brahma"s day, all living entities become manifest from the unmanifest state, and thereafter, when night falls, they are merged into the unmanifest again.
