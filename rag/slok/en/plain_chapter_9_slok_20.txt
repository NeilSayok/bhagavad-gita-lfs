Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
traividyā māṃ somapāḥ pūtapāpā yajñairiṣṭvā svargatiṃ prārthayante .
te puṇyamāsādya surendralokaṃ aśnanti divyāndivi devabhogān

Word Meanings:
त्रैविद्या - knowers of three Vedas
मां - Me
सोमपाः - drinkers of Soma
पूतपापा - purified of sins
यज्ञैरिष्ट्वा - worshiping with sacrifices
स्वर्गतिं - passage to heaven
प्रार्थयन्ते - pray for
ते - they
पुण्यमासाद्य - reaching the holy
सुरेन्द्रलोक - realm of Indra
मश्नन्ति - they enjoy
दिव्यान्दिवि - celestial in heaven
देवभोगान् - pleasures of the gods

Life Application:
Realize that seeking heavenly pleasures through rituals brings only temporary enjoyment before returning to mortal life.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The knowers of the three Vedas, the drinkers of Soma, purified from sin, worshipping Me by sacrifices, pray for the way to heaven; reaching the holy world of the Lord of Devas, they enjoy in heaven the divine pleasures of the Devas.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The knowers of the three Vedas, the drinkers of Soma, purified of sins, worshipping Me by sacrifices, pray for the way to heaven; they reach the holy world of the Lord of Devas and enjoy in heaven the divine pleasures of the Devas.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The knowers of the three Vedas, drinking Soma and cleansed of sins, worship Me through sacrifices and pray for passage to heaven; reaching the sacred realm of Indra, they enjoy celestial delights.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Performers of sacrifices seeking desires worship demigods to enjoy heavenly realms, but return to earth upon exhaustion of merit. Conversely, those who worship the Supreme Lord residing within the demigods with unselfish devotion attain supreme liberation.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Performers of Vedic rites with material desires reach heavenly realms by worshipping God in the form of Indra and demigods, yet such enjoyment is impermanent.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The knowers of the three Vedas, the drinkers of Soma, purified from sin, worshipping Me by sacrifices, pray for passage to heaven; reaching the holy world of the King of Devas, they enjoy divine celestial pleasures.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Performers of Vedic rituals motivated by desires worship the Supreme Lord residing in demigods to reach heaven, yet miss out on the eternal fruit of God-realization.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Desire-driven practitioners of the three Vedas attain heavenly delights after drinking Soma, but return to the mortal world when their merits are exhausted. Only unselfish devotees of the Lord attain eternal liberation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "The knowers of the three Vedas, drinkers of Soma"—desire-driven performers of the three Vedas, purified of obstacles through Soma rites, worship Me in demigod forms and pray for heavenly access; reaching Indra's realm, they enjoy celestial pleasures.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Elucidating the supreme truth, he clarifies that desire-driven worshippers of demigods remain bound to the cycle of coming and going, whereas unselfish worshippers of all-pervading Lord Vishnu attain eternal mukti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Practitioners of the three Vedas with material desires enjoy celestial realms and return to earth; repeated birth and death is the fruit of desired-based rites, whereas pure devotees attain eternal freedom.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Desire-driven performers of the three Vedas, purified by Soma rites, pray for heaven and enjoy celestial delights in Indra's realm; yet upon merit-exhaustion they return to earth, proving the impermanence of fruitive works.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: The knowers of the three Vedas, drinkers of Soma, purified of sin, worshipping Me by sacrifices, pray for the way to heaven; reaching the holy world of Devas, they enjoy divine pleasures.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the limitations of desired-based rites: Seekers who fail to realize the Inner Ruler and seek only demigod gratification gain heavenly pleasures but miss out on mukti.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Desire-driven Vedic ritualists gain only perishable heavenly fruits because their intention is limited to temporary celestial realms.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Souls practicing desired-based worship attain heavenly pleasures, but lacking unselfish love, miss out on eternal divine bliss.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The result of desire-driven rites is temporary heavenly enjoyment; those who fail to recognize the Inner Ruler remain content with petty rewards.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those who study the Vedas and drink the Soma juice, seeking the heavenly planets, worship Me indirectly. Purified of sinful reactions, they take birth on the pious, heavenly planet of Indra, where they enjoy godly delights.
