Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sarvāṇīndriyakarmāṇi prāṇakarmāṇi cāpare .
ātmasaṃyamayogāgnau juhvati jñānadīpite

Word Meanings:
सर्वाणीन्द्रियकर्माणि - all actions of senses
प्राणकर्माणि - actions of breath
चापरे - and others
आत्मसंयमयोगाग्नौ - in fire of self-restraint yoga
जुह्वति - sacrifice
ज्ञानदीपिते - kindled by knowledge

Life Application:
Bring your physical breath and sensory habits under gentle conscious control through regular quiet meditation.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "In the fire of self-restraint kindled by wisdom"—dissolving sense and breath activities into the mind illuminated by meditation on Lord Vishnu is the supreme Yajna.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Dhyana-yogis offer all sensory and vital breath functions into the fire of self-restraint kindled by Self-realization, entering Samadhi.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Others offer all the actions of the senses and the functions of life-breath into the fire of the Yoga of self-restraint, kindled by Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Restraining the mind in the Self, offering all sensory and breath activities into the fire of wisdom is the Yajna of meditation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.27–4.28 jointly): Offering all sensory and vital breath acts into self-restraint, sacrifice of wealth, penance, Yoga, and study of wisdom—all these are sacrifices of self-controlled ascetics.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Others offer all the actions of the senses and the functions of life-breath into the fire of the Yoga of self-restraint, kindled by Knowledge"—describes Samadhi wherein sensory and vital activities are dissolved into Self-awareness.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Restraint of senses and vital breath in a mind illuminated by wisdom is direct Samadhi-yoga.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Dissolving all sensory and breath movements into consciousness illuminated by Krishna-remembrance is the devotee"s Dhyana-yajna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Kindled by Knowledge"—offering all sensory and breath activities into the fire of self-restraint lit by discrimination is Savikalpa Samadhi.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Others offer all the actions of the senses and the functions of life-breath into the fire of the Yoga of self-restraint, kindled by Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing how offering sensory and vital breath acts into self-restraint constitutes Dhyana-yajna.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Merging sensory and breath activities in a mind illuminated by Vasudeva-dhyana is Yogis" paramount sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When all senses and vital breaths of the practitioner merge in Krishna"s meditation, that itself becomes the supreme Yoga-fire.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "In the fire of self-restraint lit by knowledge"—termination of sensory and breath modifications via Self-realization is the fruit of meditation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Others, who are interested in self-realization through control of the mind and senses, offer the functions of all the senses, and of the life breath, as oblations into the fire of the controlled mind.
