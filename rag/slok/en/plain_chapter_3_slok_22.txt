Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
na me pārthāsti kartavyaṃ triṣu lokeṣu kiñcana .
nānavāptamavāptavyaṃ varta eva ca karmaṇi

Word Meanings:
न - O Partha, there is
मे - duty to perform
पार्थास्ति - in the three
कर्तव्यं - in the worlds
त्रिषु - anything whatever
लोकेषु - in the worlds
किञ्चन - anything whatever
नानवाप्तमवाप्तव्यं - nothing unattained to be attained
वर्त - I remain engaged
एव - I remain engaged
च - and
कर्मणि - in action

Life Application:
Engage in helpful work enthusiastically even when you have no personal need or obligation forcing you to do so.

There is nothing in the three worlds, O Partha, that has to be done by Me, nor is there anything unattained that should be attained by Me; yet I engage in action.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Even the self-fulfilled Supreme Lord Vishnu acts solely to educate souls and establish righteous harmony.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Though the Supreme Lord Krishna has no desire or obligation in the three worlds, He voluntarily engages in action for world welfare.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Supreme Purushottama, despite accomplishing everything by His mere will, incarnates and acts for cosmic protection.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.22–3.24 jointly): "These worlds would perish if I did not perform action; I would be the cause of confusion of castes, and would destroy these creatures"—concludes that divine action preserves cosmic harmony.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "There is nothing in the three worlds, O Partha, that has to be done by Me, nor is there anything unattained that should be attained by Me; yet I engage in action"—cites Krishna"s own divine conduct as the supreme ideal for Lokasangraha.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: God"s selfless action-orientation forms the foundational basis for sustaining the entire cosmos.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna is the desireless Purushottama; His pastimes and work are purely grace for devotees and cosmic good.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: The Supreme Lord"s selfless action serves as the ultimate benchmark for all living beings.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis substantiating why the Supreme Lord, possessing all desires fulfilled, acts for Lokasangraha.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva"s incarnational activities serve to protect the pious and preserve cosmic harmony.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When Lord Krishna Himself engages in action, it becomes the devotee"s obligation to follow Him.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The Divine"s action-orientation is purely to establish an ideal for world protection.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O son of Prtha, there is no work prescribed for Me within all the three planetary systems. Nor am I in want of anything, nor have I a need to obtain anything – and yet I am engaged in prescribed duties.
