Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
maharṣīṇāṃ bhṛgurahaṃ girāmasmyekamakṣaram .
yajñānāṃ japayajño.asmi sthāvarāṇāṃ himālayaḥ

Word Meanings:
महर्षीणां - among great sages
भृगुरहं - Bhrigu I am
गिरामस्म्येकमक्षरम् - among words I am the single syllable (Om)
यज्ञानां - among sacrifices
जपयज्ञोऽस्मि - I am japa-sacrifice
स्थावराणां - among immovable things
हिमालयः - Himalayas

Life Application:
See God as Bhrigu among great sages, Om among words, silent repetition (japa) among sacrifices, and Himalayas among mountains.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among the great sages I am Bhrigu; among words I am the single syllable Om; among sacrifices I am Japa-yajna (repetition of holy names); among immovable things I am the Himalayas.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among the great sages I am Bhrigu; among words I am the one syllable (Om); among sacrifices I am the sacrifice of silent repetition (Japa-yajna); among immovable things I am the Himalayas.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of the great sages I am Bhrigu; of vibrations I am the transcendental Om. Of sacrifices I am the chanting of holy names [Japa], and of immovable things I am the Himalayas.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among great sages, the enlightened Bhrigu; among all words, the monosyllable 'Om' (symbol of Brahman); among spiritual sacrifices, the harmless 'Japa-yajna'; and among immovable structures, the Himalayas represents the Divine.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among the great sages I am Bhrigu; among words I am the single syllable Om; among sacrifices I am the Japa sacrifice; among immovable things I am the Himalayas.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Among the great sages I am Bhrigu; among words I am the one syllable (Om); among sacrifices I am the Japa sacrifice; among immovable things I am the Himalayas.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Among great sages, Bhrigu, mind-born son of Brahma; among words, the sacred monosyllable Om; among sacrifices, the violence-free Japa-yajna; and among immovable mountains, the holy Himalayas are the Lord's supreme opulences.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among the great sages I am Bhrigu; among words I am the one syllable (Om); among sacrifices I am Japa-yajna, and among immovable things I am the Himalayas.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among great sages, Bhrigu; among expressive words, the sacred Pranava (Om); among sacrifices, the non-violent Japa-yajna; and among immovable things, the holy Himalayas is My divine Vibhuti.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among the great sages I am Bhrigu; among words I am the one syllable Om (Pranava); among sacrifices I am Japa-yajna (superior as it is free from violence); and among immovable things I am the Himalayas.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Bhrigu, who brings divine bliss; the supreme monosyllable Om; the highest Japa-yajna; and the holy Himalayas among immovable mountains embody the Lord's playful manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Among great sages, Bhrigu; among words, the single syllable Om designating Brahman; among all sacrifices, the purest Japa-yajna; and among immovable things, the Himalayas is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Among the great sages I am Bhrigu; among words I am the single syllable Om; among sacrifices I am the Japa sacrifice; among immovable things I am the Himalayas.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Bhrigu's wisdom among sages, Om's divine nature among words, Japa-yajna's purity among sacrifices, and the Himalayas' sanctity among mountains reveal the Lord's presence.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Bhrigu among seers, Om among Vedic sound vibrations, Japa-yajna among sacrifices, and the Himalayas among mountain ranges constitute the Lord's supreme Vibhutis.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Among great sages I am Bhrigu; among words I am Om; among sacrifices I am Japa-yajna; and among mountains I am the Himalayas.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Bhrigu among sages, Om among words, selfless Japa-yajna among sacrifices, and the Himalayas among immovable entities manifest the Supreme Lord.

Of the great sages I am Bhṛgu; of vibrations I am the transcendental oṁ. Of sacrifices I am the chanting of holy names [Japa], and of immovable things I am the Himalayas.
