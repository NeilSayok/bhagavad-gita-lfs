Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
bhoktāraṃ yajñatapasāṃ sarvalokamaheśvaram .
suhṛdaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ jñātvā māṃ śāntimṛcchati

Word Meanings:
भोक्तारं - the enjoyer
यज्ञतपसां - of sacrifices and austerities
सर्वलोकमहेश्वरम् - Supreme Lord of all worlds
सुहृदं - benefactor (friend of all)
सर्वभूतानां - of all beings
ज्ञात्वा - having known
मां - Me
शान्तिमृच्छति - attains peace

Life Application:
Remember that God is the loving friend of all living beings, resting in the deep comfort of divine protection.

Sri Ramanuja states: "Friend of all beings"—knowing Lord Vasudeva as the supreme enjoyer of sacrifices and eternal benefactor of all entities, the devotee attains supreme peace.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Knowing Me as the Enjoyer of sacrifices and austerities, the Great Lord of all worlds, and the Friend of all beings, he attains Peace"—concludes the chapter by establishing the direct realization of Lord Narayana (*Bhokta, Maheshvara, Suhrid*) as the supreme key to everlasting Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Direct knowledge of Vishnu"s doership, supreme rulerhood, and universal benevolence is the sole cause of absolute peace (Moksha).

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna is the supreme enjoyer of all austerities and the highest friend of all souls; by this loving wisdom, the seeker reaches eternal peace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Knowing Me, he attains peace"—realizing the Supreme Lord who is both Saguna and Nirguna bestows absolute cessation of worldly transmigration.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Knowing Vasudeva as the Sovereign and Friend of all worlds, the seeker attains the blissful peace of Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna alone is the sole supreme well-wisher and Master of all; by this wisdom, the soul merges in eternal peace.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Knowing Me, he attains peace"—knowing Lord Narayana as the Friend of all beings and Supreme Master of worlds, the soul reaches ultimate cessation of worldliness.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
A person in full consciousness of Me, knowing Me to be the ultimate beneficiary of all sacrifices and austerities, the Supreme Lord of all planets and demigods, and the benefactor and well-wisher of all living entities, attains peace from the pangs of material miseries.
