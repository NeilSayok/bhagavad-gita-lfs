Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yo māmajamanādiṃ ca vetti lokamaheśvaram .
asammūḍhaḥ sa martyeṣu sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate

Word Meanings:
यो - he who
मामजमनादिं - Me unborn and without beginning
च - and
वेत्ति - knows
लोकमहेश्वरम् - supreme lord of worlds
असम्मूढः - undeluded
स - he
मर्त्येषु - among mortals
सर्वपापैः - from all sins
प्रमुच्यते - is liberated

Life Application:
Know God as unborn, beginningless, and great Lord of the worlds, freeing yourself from all delusion and sin.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
He who knows Me as unborn, beginningless, and the Supreme Lord of the worlds—he, undeluded among mortals, is freed from all sins.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
He who knows Me as unborn and beginningless, as the great Lord of the worlds, he, among mortals, is undeluded and is liberated from all sins.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
He who knows Me as the unborn, without beginning, the Lord of the universe, he, stripped of his delusion, becomes free from all sins.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Knowing the Lord as "unborn" and "beginningless" is not a mere intellectual concept, but a direct spiritual realization. One who knows the Supreme Lord as the Master of all worlds becomes free from delusion and is liberated from all sins.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Whosoever knows Me as the unborn and beginningless Absolute Lord of the universe, that person, not deluded among mortals, is released from all sins.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
He who knows Me as unborn and without a beginning and the great Lord of the worlds - he among the mortals is undeluded and is released from all sins.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
He who knows Me-the birthless, the beginningless, and the great Lord of the worlds, he, the undeluded one among mortals, becomes freed from all sins.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Anaadi" signifies the impeller and source of everything. Because the Lord is the primal cause of all, He Himself is birthless (aja) and beginningless. Knowing Him as the Supreme Lord of the worlds frees one from sin.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Knowing the true majesty of the Lord yields supreme fruit. One who realizes the Supreme Lord as causeless (anaadi), birthless (aja), and the Sovereign of all worlds is freed from the delusion of ignorance and released from all binding sins.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The human being who knows (firmly accepts) Me as birthless, beginningless, and the Supreme Sovereign of all the worlds—that undeluded wise person among mortals is released from all sins.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Aja" and "anaadi" denote that the Lord is distinct from perishable matter, bound souls, and liberated souls alike. Knowing Him as the Supreme Sovereign of all worlds liberates one from all sins opposing devotion.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 10.2–10.5 jointly): Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 10.1–10.5 jointly): To foster the devotion explained earlier and to reveal the all-pervading divine nature and infinite cosmic manifestations (vibhutis), the Supreme Lord resumes His teaching. 'Paramam vachah' signifies the supreme, wisdom-bestowing word spoken for Arjuna's highest welfare.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Since I am the primal cause of gods and sages, no prior cause exists for Me; hence I am birthless (aja) and beginningless (anaadi). He who knows Me as the Great Lord of all worlds becomes undeluded among mortals and is freed from all sins.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The Lord is the impeller and first cause of all. "Aja" and "anaadi" imply that He has no cause or progenitor. Realizing the Lord thus as the Sovereign of all worlds destroys one's delusion.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The Lord is the unborn Reality revealed through scripture and spiritual experience. One who realizes Him as the Supreme Lord of the entire universe becomes free from ignorance and liberated from all sins.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Being the cause of all, He has no prior cause, and is therefore beginningless (anaadi); being beginningless, He is birthless (aja). Knowing Him as the Supreme Sovereign of worlds frees one from illusion and all sins.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: The Lord declares the fruit of knowing His true nature. Being the cause of all, He is without cause (anaadi) and birthless (aja). The person who knows Him as the Great Lord of worlds becomes undeluded and free from sins.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: One who, free from delusion, realizes the Lord as unborn, beginningless, and the Supreme Sovereign of all worlds—this very knowledge burns away all past and present sins.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Direct realization of the Lord as unborn and the Supreme Ruler removes all delusion and completely destroys all sins that obstruct the path of devotion.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: By the Lord's grace, the human being who realizes Him as birthless, beginningless, and the Supreme Sovereign of worlds becomes foremost among mortals and is freed from all sins.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: One who is undeluded (possessing discrimination between matter and spirit) and knows the Lord as beginningless and Supreme Lord of all worlds is released from all sins and attains the supreme state.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
He who knows Me as the unborn, as the beginningless, as the Supreme Lord of all the worlds – he only, undeluded among men, is freed from all sins.
