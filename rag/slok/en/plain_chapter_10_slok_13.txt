Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
āhustvāmṛṣayaḥ sarve devarṣirnāradastathā .
asito devalo vyāsaḥ svayaṃ caiva bravīṣi me

Word Meanings:
आहुस्त्वामृषयः - sages call You
सर्वे - all
देवर्षिर्नारदस्तथा - divine sage Narada as well
असितो - Asita
देवलो - Devala
व्यासः - Vyasa
स्वयं - Yourself
चैव - and also
ब्रवीषि - You tell
मे - my

Life Application:
Confirm divine truth as declared by ancient sages like Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa, and now by God Himself.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
All the sages (Vasishtha and others) have declared You to be so; as also the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa; and You Yourself declare it to me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
All the sages have thus declared Thee, as also the divine sage Narada; so also Asita, Devala and Vyasa; and Thou Thyself declarest it to me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
All the great sages such as Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa proclaim this of You, and now You Yourself tell it to me.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Arjuna states that the great seers of antiquity, such as Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa, have all proclaimed this eternal truth regarding You; and today You Yourself are confirming the very same to me.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode, the Supreme Purifier. All the sages, the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa declare You to be the Eternal Divine Person, the Primal God, Unborn and All-pervading; and You Yourself also tell me the same.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode, the Supreme Purifier. All the sages, divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala and Vyasa proclaim You to be the eternal, divine Person, the primeval God, unborn and all-pervading; and You Yourself also tell Me the same.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode, the Supreme Purifier. All the sages, divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala and Vyasa proclaim You as the eternal divine Person, the primeval God, unborn and all-pervading; and You Yourself tell me the same.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Param Brahman" signifies the All-complete, supreme entity that sustains the entire universe. Arjuna acknowledges that Lord Krishna is the Supreme Abode, Supreme Purifier, Eternal Divine Person, and All-pervading Lord declared in the Vedas.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Hearing the Lord's words, Arjuna experiences supreme joy and praises Him as the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Light, and Supreme Purifier, whom all seers proclaim as the eternal, all-pervading Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Arjuna said: You are the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Abode, the Supreme Purifier. You are the Eternal Divine Person, the Primal God, Unborn and All-pervading.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: You alone are He whom the Vedas announce as the Supreme Brahman, Supreme Abode, and Supreme Purifier. All sages—Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa—proclaim You as the Eternal Divine Person, and You Yourself have confirmed this to me.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Abhinavagupta did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: All the sages like Vasishtha, the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa speak of You as the Supreme Brahman; and You Yourself also declare the same unto me.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Hearing the Lord's teachings, Arjuna validates and extols the supreme majesty of God. All authoritative scriptures and seers—Vyasa, Narada, etc.—proclaim Him alone as the Supreme Brahman and Ultimate Abode.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Hearing the Lord's divine words, Arjuna accepts His Sat-Chit-Ananda Supreme Brahman nature and declares Him to be the Ultimate Abode and Supreme Divine Being.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: All the sages including Vasishtha, Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa declare You to be the Supreme Brahman; and now You Yourself also declare this truth unto me.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: All the sages, the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa proclaim You to be the Supreme Brahman; and You Yourself tell it to me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: All the sages, Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa accept You as the Supreme Brahman, Lord of all worlds; and You Yourself in Your teaching tell me the very same.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Welcoming the Lord's words, Arjuna acknowledges that all scriptures and supersensuous seers proclaim Lord Krishna alone as the Supreme Brahman, Supreme Abode, and Supreme Purifier.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: That divine Supreme Brahman Form of Yours which Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa sing of, You Yourself are manifesting unto me today.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Arjuna, accepting the Lord's true nature, says that all Vedas, sages, Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa proclaim Lord Krishna as the Supreme Brahman, Supreme Abode, and Eternal Divine Person.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
All the great sages such as Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa proclaim this of You, and now You Yourself declare it to me.
