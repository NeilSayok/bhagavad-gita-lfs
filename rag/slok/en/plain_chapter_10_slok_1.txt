Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
bhūya eva mahābāho śṛṇu me paramaṃ vacaḥ .
yatte.ahaṃ prīyamāṇāya vakṣyāmi hitakāmyayā

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
भूय - again
एव - also
महाबाहो - O mightyarmed
शृणु - hear
मे - my
परमं - the Supreme
वचः - word
यत्तेऽहं - which I to you
प्रीयमाणाय - who are beloved
वक्ष्यामि - will speak
हितकाम्यया - for your welfare

Life Application:
Listen further to supreme divine words spoken for your benefit because you take delight in spiritual truth.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Supreme Lord speaks His words again to Arjuna, because Arjuna feels delight in hearing them. Here, "paramam" means supreme, noble, and auspicious teachings.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The Lord said: Again, O mighty-armed, hear My supreme word which I will declare to thee who art beloved, for thy welfare.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Lord Shri Krishna said: Listen further, O mighty-armed! to My supreme word, which I, for thy welfare, who art beloved, will declare unto thee.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
The Lord says "bhuya eva", meaning listen once again. Arjuna was experiencing immense delight in relishing the Lord's teachings, and therefore the Lord, seeking his ultimate welfare, speaks again of His supreme manifestations.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
The Lord said: Listen again, O long-armed one, to My supreme word, which I shall speak to you who are taking delight [in My words], out of a desire for your welfare.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
The Lord said: Hear again, O mighty-armed, My supreme word, which I, wishing your welfare, will speak to you who take delight [in My words].

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
The Lord said: O mighty-armed one, listen again to My supreme word, which I, wishing your welfare, shall speak to you who are taking delight (in My words).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: In this chapter, the Lord describes His divine manifestations (vibhutis) and His special agency in creating certain faculties and sages for the purpose of worship. "Priyamanaaya" means to Arjuna, who attains supreme joy and satisfaction upon hearing My words.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: In the seventh and subsequent chapters, the Lord's qualified (sopatika) and unconditioned (nirupatika) nature, along with a brief account of His divine manifestations, was expounded. Now, in this tenth chapter, those manifestations are described in detail so that the seeker may easily contemplate the Divine presence everywhere.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The Lord says: O mighty-armed one, listen to My supreme, highly auspicious words. As you are drinking My words like nectar and feeling immensely delighted, I speak this supreme knowledge again out of a desire for your ultimate welfare.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Thus in the preceding chapters, the Supreme Brahman, the Lord of Sri, was described along with His endless auspicious qualities and the nature of devotion. Now, in this chapter, to generate and foster devotion, the Lord's infinite glorious attributes and His cosmic sovereignty (vibhuti) are expounded in detail. "Priyamanaaya" signifies that as Arjuna hears the Lord's words, his love and joy overflow.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: To foster the growth of devotion declared earlier, and to impart the knowledge of His cosmic omnipresence and infinite divine powers (vibhutis), the Lord resumes His teaching. "Paramam vachah" signifies the supreme word of wisdom spoken for Arjuna's ultimate spiritual welfare.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: O mighty-armed one, listen again to My supreme word—that which reveals the unexcelled, absolute Reality. I shall speak it to you out of a desire for your welfare, because you derive immense delight from My words as if drinking nectar.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The purpose of this chapter is to reveal the Lord's divine manifestations and His special agency for the sake of worship. The sixth chapter described meditation, and the ninth chapter concluded with "fix your mind on Me", which requires an object of meditation. "Priyamanaaya" indicates that whereas Arjuna was previously grief-stricken, he now feels deep joy and satisfaction in hearing the Lord's words.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: To nourish the previously described devotion and to establish the vision of the Divine in all things, Lord Hari describes His manifestations. Since this entire universe originates from Sat-Chit-Ananda and is essentially divine, the Lord reveals His vibhutis to impart the vision of universal unity.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: In the seventh, eighth, and ninth chapters, the Lord's conditioned and unconditioned truth was expounded. Now, as an aid to meditating on the conditioned form and as a means to gaining knowledge of the unconditioned Reality, the Lord's glorious manifestations (vibhutis) are elaborated in this chapter.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: In the seventh and preceding chapters, the Lord's divine manifestations were briefly mentioned. Now in the tenth chapter, they are elaborated in detail to cultivate the vision of God everywhere.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: In the preceding chapters, the Lord's manifest and unmanifest aspects along with His divine powers were briefly indicated. Now, as a practical means for qualified meditation and unqualified knowledge, those specific entities and states in which the Supreme Lord should be contemplated are described in detail.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Explaining the contextual connection of the tenth chapter, he notes that knowledge of the Lord's infinite auspicious attributes and absolute sovereignty awakens and strengthens devotion. For this purpose, the Supreme Lord elaborates His supreme auspicious teachings.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: To accomplish the devotion described in the ninth chapter, Lord Hari graciously describes the nature of His divine manifestations in the tenth chapter. The Lord alerts Arjuna to hear His auspicious words attentively.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: In the seventh, eighth, and ninth chapters, the individual soul and God, along with worship, were expounded. But those whose minds are tainted by attachment and aversion struggle to see God everywhere. Therefore, for their elevation, the Lord describes His divine manifestations to help fix the mind on Divinity in specific objects.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: Listen again, O mighty-armed Arjuna. Because you are My dear friend, for your benefit I shall speak to you further, giving knowledge that is better than what I have already explained. The word bhagavān is explained thus by Parāśara Muni: one who is full in all riches, strength, fame, beauty, knowledge, and renunciation.
