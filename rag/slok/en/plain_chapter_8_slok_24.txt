Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
agnirjotirahaḥ śuklaḥ ṣaṇmāsā uttarāyaṇam .
tatra prayātā gacchanti brahma brahmavido janāḥ

Word Meanings:
अग्निर्जोतिरहः - fire light day
शुक्लः - the bright fortnight
षण्मासा - the six months
उत्तरायणम् - of northern course
तत्र - there
प्रयाता - departed souls
गच्छन्ति - go to
ब्रह्म - Brahman
ब्रह्मविदो - knowers of Brahman
जनाः - persons

Life Application:
Understand the bright path of light, fire, day, and northern sun, by which knowers of Brahman reach the Supreme.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern solstice—departing by that path, knowers of Brahman go to Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern solstice—departing by that path, knowers of Brahman go to Brahman.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Fire, flame, daytime, the bright fortnight of the moon, and the six months of the northern solstice—departing by this luminous path, knowers of Brahman attain Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern solstice—departing by that path, knowers of Brahman go to Brahman.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern solstice—departing by that path, knowers of Brahman go to Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern solstice—departing by that path, knowers of Brahman go to Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern solstice—departing by that path, knowers of Brahman go to Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Fire, Light"—the escorting deities of the Archiradi path (Agni, Ahar, Shukla, etc.), under Lord Hari"s command, guide the enlightened soul to Vishnu"s realm.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The presiding deities of Fire, Light, Day, Bright Fortnight, and Northern Solstice escort the soul of the knower of Brahman step by step to Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern solstice—departing by that path, knowers of Brahman go to Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The escorting deities of the Archiradi path (Agni, Day, Bright Fortnight, Uttarayana) conduct the worshiper of Brahman step by step to Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, and six months of northern solstice symbolize the luminous path by which knowers of Brahman reach Supreme Brahman. Smoke, night, dark fortnight, and six months of southern solstice symbolize the shadowy path by which souls reach lunar light and return.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, six months of northern solstice—departing by this path, knowers of Brahman go to Brahman"—indicated by these time-deities, the divine escorts of the Archiradi Devayana path guide meditators on Saguna Brahman step by step to the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The Archiradi deities act as guides to lead the practitioner to Brahmaloka; this Northern Path is accessible exclusively to devotees of the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Divine souls departing via the luminous Devayana path directly enter the eternal abode of Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Knowers of Brahman go to Brahman"—meditators on Saguna Brahman reach Supreme Brahman through gradual liberation via the Archiradi path.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern solstice—departing by that path, knowers of Brahman go to Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural exposition of the five stages and escorting deities of the Archiradi Northern Path.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The twelve escorting deities of Archiradi path conduct the worshiper to Supreme Brahman via the Amanava person.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Unswerving devotees departing by the radiant Devayana path merge into Lord Krishna"s divine consciousness.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Fire, light"—the time-deities of Uttarayana path bestow gradual liberation upon Saguna worshipers.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those who know the Supreme Brahman attain that Supreme by passing away from the world during the influence of the fiery god, in the light, at an auspicious moment of the day, during the fortnight of the waxing moon, or during the six months when the sun travels to the north.
