Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yastvātmaratireva syādātmatṛptaśca mānavaḥ .
ātmanyeva ca santuṣṭastasya kāryaṃ na vidyate

Word Meanings:
यस्त्वात्मरतिरेव - he who delights in the Self
स्यादात्मतृप्तश्च - and satisfied in the Self
मानवः - man
आत्मन्येव - in the Self alone
च - content, for him
सन्तुष्टस्तस्य - duty to perform
कार्यं - exists
न - not
विद्यते - exists

Life Application:
Cultivate inner contentment until your happiness relies entirely on self-awareness rather than external conditions.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
But the man who delights in the Self alone, who is satisfied with the Self, and content in the Self alone—for him there is no duty.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
But the man who delights in the Self alone, who is satisfied with the Self, and content in the Self alone—for him there is no duty.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
But the self-realized man who delights only in the Self (Atmaratih), who is satisfied with the Self alone (Atmatriptah), and who is contented in the Self (Atmanyeva ca santushtah)—for him, no obligatory duty remains to be done.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: For a wise soul satisfied within the Self through direct perception of Lord Vishnu, no preliminary duty remains.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: A Jivanmukta who rejoices in the nectar of the Self rather than sense-objects is completely freed from all prescribed injunctions.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
But the man who delights in the Self alone, who is satisfied with the Self, and content in the Self alone—for him there is no duty.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "For him no duty exists"—one who experiences infinite bliss within the Self via knowledge-steadfastness has no obligatory practice left.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Yastu etc. But the man who delights in the Self alone and is satisfied with the Self—for him no duty exists.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "But the man who delights in the Self alone, who is satisfied with the Self, and content in the Self alone—for him there is no duty"—proclaims that the self-realized Sage (*Atma-jnani*) transcends all obligatory injunctions.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Rejoicing and satisfaction in the Self is the direct hallmark of the perfected state.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A devotee immersed in Krishna"s love-nectar abides content in the Self, needing no ritualistic injunctions.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: For a Sage endowed with non-dual Self-realization, no mandatory work remains to be accomplished.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
But the man who delights in the Self alone, who is satisfied with the Self, and content in the Self alone—for him there is no duty.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving why a Sage delighted and satisfied in the Self has no obligatory actions remaining.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: For a Sage satisfied in the Self via Vasudeva-realization, Karma-yoga is no longer a means but self-accomplished.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a soul is absorbed in Krishna"s love within the Self, all material duties end automatically.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: For a Knower of Supreme Reality, no title to or bondage of action remains.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
But for one who takes pleasure in the self, whose human life is one of self-realization, and who is satisfied in the self only, fully satiated – for him there is no duty.
