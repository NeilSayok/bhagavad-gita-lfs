Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
brahmārpaṇaṃ brahma havirbrahmāgnau brahmaṇā hutam .
brahmaiva tena gantavyaṃ brahmakarmasamādhinā

Word Meanings:
ब्रह्मार्पणं - Brahman is the offering ladle
ब्रह्म - Brahman
हविर्ब्रह्माग्नौ - oblation in fire of Brahman
ब्रह्मणा - by Brahman
हुतम् - offered
ब्रह्मैव - Brahman alone
तेन - by him
गन्तव्यं - to be attained
ब्रह्मकर्मसमाधिना - by one absorbed in Brahman-action

Life Application:
Perceive the sacred presence in your everyday tasks, treating ordinary duties as acts of divine worship.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Brahman alone is to be reached"—perceiving Lord Vishnu"s presence and divine control in all sacrificial elements constitutes Brahma-karma-samadhi.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Perceiving all instruments, oblations, fire, and actions as Brahman Himself constitutes the Jnana-Yajna, dissolving all duality.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Brahman is the offering, Brahman is the oblation, by Brahman is it poured into the fire of Brahman; Brahman alone is to be reached by him who sees Brahman in action.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: All cosmic offerings and instruments are attributes forming the body of Supreme Brahman (Lord Vishnu); hence all activities lead to realizing Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Brahmarpanam etc. The instrument, offering, fire, act of offering, and goal are all Brahman; he who sees Brahman in action reaches Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Brahman is the offering, Brahman is the oblation, by Brahman is it poured into the fire of Brahman; Brahman alone is to be reached by him who sees Brahman in action"—expounds the quintessential Vedantic realization of Non-duality (*Jnana-yajna*) in every act.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Directly intuiting the entire cosmos as rooted in and forming the body of Vishnu is the true Brahma-yajna.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: All objects, actions, and fruits are forms of Krishna Himself; a devotee blessed with this divine vision realizes Krishna everywhere.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "All this is indeed Brahman"—perceiving the non-dual Brahman in all factors and activities is Jnana-yajna, making the seeker Brahman Himself.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Brahman is the offering, Brahman is the oblation, by Brahman is it poured into the fire of Brahman; Brahman alone is to be reached by him who sees Brahman in action.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing how beholding non-dual Brahman in all instruments of sacrifice yields immediate realization of Brahman.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Contemplating Vasudeva as the supreme Sovereign in all sacrificial components is the Karma-yogi"s paramount Jnana-yajna.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the practitioner experiences all his actions as permeated by Krishna, his entire life transforms into Brahma-yajna.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Brahman alone is reached"—direct realization of non-dual Brahman in all phenomena is the ultimate culmination of action.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
A person who is fully absorbed in Krsna consciousness is sure to attain the spiritual kingdom because of his full contribution to spiritual activities, in which the consummation is absolute and that which is offered is of the same spiritual nature.
