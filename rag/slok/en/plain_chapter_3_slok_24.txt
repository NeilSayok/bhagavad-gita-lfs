Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
utsīdeyurime lokā na kuryāṃ karma cedaham .
saṅkarasya ca kartā syāmupahanyāmimāḥ prajāḥ

Word Meanings:
उत्सीदेयुरिमे - would perish these
लोका - worlds
न - not
कुर्यां - should perform
कर्म - action
चेदहम् - if I
सङ्करस्य - of confusion of castes
च - and
कर्ता - author
स्यामुपहन्यामिमाः - would be, destroy these
प्रजाः - people

Life Application:
Uphold your commitments faithfully so that those who depend on your presence experience stability and clarity.
