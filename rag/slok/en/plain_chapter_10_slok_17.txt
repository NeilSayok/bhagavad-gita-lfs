Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
kathaṃ vidyāmahaṃ yogiṃstvāṃ sadā paricintayan .
keṣu keṣu ca bhāveṣu cintyo.asi bhagavanmayā

Word Meanings:
कथं - how
विद्यामहं - may I know
योगिंस्त्वां - O Yogi, You
सदा - always
परिचिन्तयन् - meditating
केषु - in which
केषु - in which
च - and
भावेषु - aspects
चिन्त्योऽसि - are to be meditated upon
भगवन्मया - O Lord, by me

Life Application:
Inquire how to know God through constant meditation, and upon which divine aspects to contemplate.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
How may I know You, O Supreme Mystic, by constant meditation? In what forms or aspects, O Blessed Lord, are You to be contemplated by me?

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
How shall I, ever meditating, know Thee, O Yogin? In what aspects or things, O blessed Lord, art Thou to be thought of by me?

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
How shall I, constantly meditating on You, know You, O Supreme Mystic? In what various forms and aspects are You to be contemplated by me, O Blessed Lord?

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Arjuna inquires about the practical path of contemplation. How should a seeker meditate upon the Supreme Lord amidst the varied forms of the manifested world so as to perceive the Divine in everything—this is Arjuna's earnest question.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
How shall I know You, O Yogin, constantly contemplating You? And in what particular aspects are You to be contemplated by me, O Lord?

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
How shall I know You, O Yogin, by meditating constantly on You? In what aspects, O Lord, are You to be thought of by me?

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
How shall I, constantly meditating, know You, O Yogin? And in what particular aspects are You to be thought of by me, O Lord?

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Arjuna inquires: O Supreme Master of Yoga, how may I concentrate my mind to constantly meditate on You, and in which specific manifestations should I worship You so as to attain direct realization?

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
How may I know You, O Supreme Mystic, by meditating constantly on You? And in what particular aspects or things are You to be contemplated by me, O Lord?

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: O Master of Bhakti-yoga, constantly contemplating You with selfless devotion, how may I fully know You? In which specific conscious and unconscious entities should I meditate upon Your cosmic immanence?

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Abhinavagupta did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: How may I, constantly meditating, know You, O Master of Yoga? And in what particular entities or aspects are You to be thought of by me, O Lord?

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: For the ease of spiritual aspirants, Arjuna prays to the Lord to specify in which principal entities meditating upon God quickly purifies and steadies the mind.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Arjuna requests the Supreme Lord, stating that even while constantly hearing of the Lord's infinite Yogic power and opulences, his soul feels no satiation; hence he begs the Lord to describe them again in detail.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: O Supreme Mystic, how may I directly realize You through constant meditation? In which specific objects of contemplation (Vibhutis) should I focus my mind upon You?

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: How shall I know You, O Yogin, by meditating constantly on You? In what aspects, O Lord, are You to be thought of by me?

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Arjuna asks the Lord for accessible objects of meditation so that, by seeing the Divine in all things, he can absorb his mind into the Attributeless Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Arjuna inquires in which specific entities one should remember the Lord's governance and divine splendor so as to cultivate devotion.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: O Supreme Lord of Yoga, in which manifestations should I constantly meditate upon You so that I may enjoy the eternal bliss of Your divine Presence?

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: O Yogin, how shall I withdraw my mind from sense-objects to constantly meditate on You, and in which divine entities should I worship You?

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O Kṛṣṇa, O supreme mystic, how shall I constantly think of You, and how shall I know You? In what various forms art You to be remembered, O Supreme Personality of Godhead?
