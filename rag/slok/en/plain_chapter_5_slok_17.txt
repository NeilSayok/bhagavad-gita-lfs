Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
tadbuddhayastadātmānastanniṣṭhāstatparāyaṇāḥ .
gacchantyapunarāvṛttiṃ jñānanirdhūtakalmaṣāḥ

Word Meanings:
तद्बुद्धयस्तदात्मानस्तन्निष्ठास्तत्परायणाः - intellect and self merged in That, steadfast and devoted to That
गच्छन्त्यपुनरावृत्तिं - attain non-return (liberation)
ज्ञाननिर्धूतकल्मषाः - sins cleansed by knowledge

Life Application:
Fix your mind and devotion on the ultimate truth, letting spiritual clarity wash away all accumulated mental impurities.
