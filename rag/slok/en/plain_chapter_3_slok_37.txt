Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
kāma eṣa krodha eṣa rajoguṇasamudbhavaḥ .
mahāśano mahāpāpmā viddhyenamiha vairiṇam

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
काम - desire
एष - this
क्रोध - wrath
एष - this
रजोगुणसमुद्भवः - born of mode of passion
महाशनो - all-devouring
महापाप्मा - great sinner
विद्ध्येनमिह - know this here
वैरिणम् - the enemy

Life Application:
Recognize insatiable desire and knee-jerk anger as internal obstacles that plunder your peace of mind and self-control.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Blessed Lord said: It is desire, it is anger, born of Rajoguna, all-devouring, all-sinful; know this to be the foe here in this world.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Desire and anger born of Rajoguna are the primary foes turning the soul away from devotion to Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Desire born of Rajoguna transforms into anger when thwarted; this desire alone is the supreme foe of the soul.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Sense-desire born of Rajoguna is the root cause of all sins, morphing into anger when unfulfilled.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.37–3.39 jointly): Kama esa etc., Dhumena etc., and Avritam etc. Desire born of Rajoguna veils wisdom; it is the constant enemy of the wise, insatiable as fire.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "It is desire, it is anger, born of Rajoguna, all-devouring, all-sinful; know this to be the foe here in this world"—identifies Kama (uncontrolled desire) as the sole arch-enemy of human liberation.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Without complete subduing of desire, peace and manifestation of devotion in the heart are impossible.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Desire born of Rajoguna forms the veil obscuring pure divine love within the soul"s heart.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Desire is the progenitor of all sins and destroyer of steadfast wisdom; hence it is the supreme enemy.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving why desire originating from Rajoguna is all-devouring, sinful, and the true enemy of man.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Desire turning souls away from Vasudeva is the primary origin of all sins and sorrows.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a seeker"s mind fails to rejoice in Krishna, desire arises and destroys all devotional wealth.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Insatiable like fire, desire is the ultimate root of all human transgression.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: It is lust only, Arjuna, which is born of contact with the material mode of passion and later transformed into wrath, and which is the all-devouring sinful enemy of this world.
