Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
vītarāgabhayakrodhā manmayā māmupāśritāḥ .
bahavo jñānatapasā pūtā madbhāvamāgatāḥ

Word Meanings:
वीतरागभयक्रोधा - freed from passion, fear and anger
मन्मया - absorbed in Me
मामुपाश्रिताः - taking refuge in Me
बहवो - many
ज्ञानतपसा - by austerity of wisdom
पूता - purified
मद्भावमागताः - attained My divine nature

Life Application:
Purify your mind of anger, fear, and craving today, allowing spiritual knowledge to refine your character.

Freed from worldly attachment, fear, and anger, absorbed in Me, taking absolute refuge in Me, and purified by the supreme austerity of Knowledge (Jnana-Tapas), many in ancient times have attained to My divine Being.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Attained My Being"—purified by the austerity of wisdom, devotees relying solely on Lord Vishnu achieve eternal communion with Him.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Purified by the austerity of Knowledge, destroying nescience, numerous selfless seekers have attained communion with Supreme Ishvara.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Purified by the austerity of divine knowledge, cleansed of all sins, many devotees fixing their minds in Me have attained My supreme state.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Vita-etc. Therefore many persons, being free from passion, fear, and anger, becoming filled with Me, taking refuge in Me, and purified by the austerity of Knowledge, have attained My state.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Freed from attachment, fear, and anger, absorbed in Me, taking refuge in Me, purified by the fire of Knowledge, many have attained My Being"—proclaims that *Jnana-tapas* (the fire of Self-knowledge) purifies souls to grant immediate Brahmanhood (*Mad-bhavam*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The austerity of Knowledge is the supreme means for mental purification, rendering the practitioner truly eligible for divine communion.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Taking sole refuge in Krishna, freed from sensory attachments, the loving devotee attains divine nature in the Lord"s eternal realm.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Purified by fire of Knowledge"—the supreme austerity of Mahavakya-born non-dual realization terminates Avidya, bestowing Videha-mukti.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis confirming how freedom from passions and purification through Knowledge leads to Brahman-realization.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Immersed in Vasudeva, purified by Knowledge, practitioners attain eternal divine likeness (Mukti).

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: By unswerving refuge in Lord Krishna and loving wisdom, the seeker"s heart turns supremely pure, merging in the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Purified by austerity of Knowledge"—non-dual Self-knowledge is the ultimate penance that consumes all impurities, bestowing Brahmanhood.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Being freed from attachment, fear and anger, being fully absorbed in Me and taking refuge in Me, many, many persons in the past became purified by knowledge of Me – and thus they all attained transcendental love for Me.
