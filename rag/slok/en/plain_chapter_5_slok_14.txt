Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
na kartṛtvaṃ na karmāṇi lokasya sṛjati prabhuḥ .
na karmaphalasaṃyogaṃ svabhāvastu pravartate

Word Meanings:
न - not
कर्तृत्वं - agency
न - not
कर्माणि - actions
लोकस्य - of the world
सृजति - creates
प्रभुः - the Lord
न - not
कर्मफलसंयोगं - union with fruits of action
स्वभावस्तु - nature however
प्रवर्तते - operates

Life Application:
Understand that natural laws govern physical events, freeing your mind from false pride or misplaced guilt.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Neither agency nor actions does the Lord create for the world, nor union with the fruits of actions. But it is Nature that acts.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Neither agency nor actions does the Lord create for the world, nor union with the fruits of actions. But Nature acts.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The Supreme Lord creates neither the sense of agency (doership), nor actions for people, nor the connection between actions and their fruits; rather, it is the innate cosmic Nature (Prakriti and its modes) that operates blindly.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Nature operates"—the jiva"s individual nature impels actions; God, while supreme and independent, prompts according to the jiva"s intrinsic nature.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Lord (Supreme Self) creates neither agency nor action nor association with fruits for people; it is illusory nature (Maya/Prakriti) alone that operates.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Neither agency nor actions does the Lord create for the world, nor union with the fruits of actions. But it is Nature that acts.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The pure soul naturally possesses no agency for worldly acts; it is the latent tendencies of past karma (nature) that drive activities.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Ramanuja states: The pure soul naturally possesses no agency for worldly acts; it is the latent tendencies of past karma (nature) that drive activities.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Neither agency nor actions does the Lord create for the world, nor union with the fruits of actions. But it is Nature that acts"—distinguishes the transcendent non-agency of the Supreme Self from the empirical operations of Maya/Prakriti.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Swabhava" refers to the latent impressions of past lives and cosmic gunas that impel the individual soul to work.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna remains unattached and neutral, orchestrating the system of karmic fruits according to each soul"s inherent nature.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Nature alone operates"—Maya or Avidya constructs doership and enjoyership; the Self is eternally unattached and immutable.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Neither agency nor actions does the Lord create for the world, nor union with the fruits of actions. But Nature acts.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing that the Lord is not the creator of human egoism or karma-fruits, but cosmic Prakriti drives all activity.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva does not forcibly interfere with individual actions; souls act according to their beginningless tendencies.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna is eternally non-doer and witness; when the soul surrenders its ego to Him, its nature turns divine.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Nature operates"—Maya born of ignorance drives the jiva into activity, while the Supreme Self remains eternally unattached.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The embodied spirit, master of the city of his body, does not create activities, nor does he induce people to act, nor does he create the fruits of action. All this is enacted by the modes of material nature.
