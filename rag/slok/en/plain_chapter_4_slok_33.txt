Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śreyāndravyamayādyajñājjñānayajñaḥ parantapa .
sarvaṃ karmākhilaṃ pārtha jñāne parisamāpyate

Word Meanings:
श्रेयान्द्रव्यमयाद्यज्ञाज्ज्ञानयज्ञः - wisdom-sacrifice is superior to material sacrifice
परन्तप - O scorcher of foes
सर्वं - all
कर्माखिलं - action in its entirety
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
ज्ञाने - in knowledge
परिसमाप्यते - culminates

Life Application:
Value the pursuit of spiritual wisdom above accumulating material goods, as understanding completes all human effort.

Superior is the sacrifice of knowledge to the sacrifice with material objects, O Parantapa. All action in its entirety, O Partha, culminates in Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Wisdom-sacrifice is superior to material sacrifice"—because Jnana-yajna is the direct means for perception of Lord Vishnu, it surpasses all material offerings.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Material sacrifice yields perishable results, whereas Wisdom-Sacrifice terminates all karmas, bestowing absolute Moksha.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: All practice-oriented actions ultimately find their successful end in Self-knowledge (Jnana); hence Knowledge-sacrifice is paramount.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta did not comment separately on this sloka, as the topic of the superiority of wisdom-sacrifice is subsumed in the preceding verses.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya did not write a separate commentary on this sloka, as the supremacy of Jnana-Yajna was already expounded in verse 4.24 and subsequent verses.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The fruit of all karma culminates in gaining Knowledge; hence Wisdom-Sacrifice is the crown-jewel of all disciplines.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The sacrifice of knowledge concerning Krishna"s form and lilas excels all external rituals, uniting the devotee directly with the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "All actions culminate in Knowledge"—non-dual Self-realization born of mental purity completely absorbs all karmas and their fruits.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving why Wisdom-Sacrifice is superior to material sacrifice as all actions dissolve into Knowledge.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Sacrificial action imbued with Vasudeva-knowledge is supreme, for the ultimate fruit of work is gaining Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a seeker"s actions transform into the light of Krishna"s wisdom, that constitutes the supreme Wisdom-Sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Culminates in Knowledge"—Self-knowledge is the ultimate destination and foundation of all prescribed duties.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O chastiser of the enemy, the sacrifice performed in knowledge is better than the mere sacrifice of material possessions. After all, O son of Prtha, all sacrifices of work culminate in transcendental knowledge.
