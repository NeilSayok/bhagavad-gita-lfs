Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
svayamevātmanātmānaṃ vettha tvaṃ puruṣottama .
bhūtabhāvana bhūteśa devadeva jagatpate

Word Meanings:
स्वयम् - Thyself
एव - only
आत्मना - by Thyself
आत्मानम् - Thyself
वेत्थ - knowest
त्वम् - Thou
पुरुषोत्तम - O Purusha Supreme
भूतभावन - O source of beings
भूतेश - O Lord of beings
देवदेव - O God of,gods
जगत्पते - O ruler of the worlds (Jagadpate)

Life Application:
Know that God alone knows Himself by Himself, O Supreme Person, source and Lord of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Indeed, You Yourself know Yourself by Yourself, O Supreme Person, O Creator of beings, O Lord of beings, O God of gods, O Ruler of the world!

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Swami Sivananda did not comment on this sloka

स्वयम् Thyself? एव only? आत्मना by Thyself? आत्मानम् Thyself? वेत्थ (Thou) knowest? त्वम् Thou? पुरुषोत्तम O Purusha Supreme? भूतभावन O source of beings? भूतेश O Lord of beings? देवदेव O God of,gods? जगत्पते O ruler of the world.Commentary Purushottama means the best among all Purushas. He assumes the four forms? viz.? the source of beings? the Lord of beings? God of gods and ruler of the world. Hence He is called Purushottama.Devadeva is He who is worshipped even by Indra and other gods.Jagatpati The Lord protects the world and guides the people through the instructions given in the Vedas. Hence the name ruler of the world.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Indeed, You alone know Yourself by Your own internal potency, O Supreme Person, Origin of all beings, Lord of all creatures, God of gods, Master of the universe!

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Arjuna glorifies Lord Krishna using five profound titles—Purushottama, Bhutabhavana, Bhutesha, Devadeva, and Jagatpati. The Supreme Lord alone, through His unexcelled self-luminous knowledge, is capable of knowing His own absolute nature.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Arjuna said: O Keshava, I accept all that You speak to me to be true. O Lord, neither gods nor demons comprehend Your origin. O Supreme Being, Creator of beings, Lord of creatures, God of gods, Ruler of the universe, You alone know Yourself by Yourself!

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Arjuna said: O Keshava, I accept as true all that You speak to me. Neither gods nor demons, O Lord, know Your manifestation. You Yourself, O Supreme Person, know Yourself by Yourself, O Creator of beings, Lord of creatures, God of gods, Ruler of the universe!

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Swami Gambirananda did not comment on this sloka

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Param Brahman" signifies the All-complete, supreme entity that sustains the entire universe. Arjuna acknowledges that Lord Krishna is the Supreme Abode, Supreme Purifier, Eternal Divine Person, and All-pervading Lord declared in the Vedas.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: True realization of the Supreme Lord cannot be attained through limited worldly means. The Lord alone comprehends His own Absolute Self by His unexcelled self-luminous knowledge, majesty, and power.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
O Supreme Person, Creator of all beings, Lord of creatures, God of gods, Ruler of the universe! You Yourself alone know Yourself by Your own Self.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: O Supreme Person, Creator of all entities, Controller of all, God of gods, Master of the universe! By Your own inherent, unexcelled knowledge alone do You know Your nature endowed with infinite auspicious attributes; no individual soul can fathom it completely.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Abhinavagupta did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: O Supreme Person, Creator of beings, Lord of creatures, God of gods, Master of the universe! You Yourself know Yourself by Yourself through Your unexcelled wisdom, sovereignty, strength, and power.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Hearing the Lord's teachings, Arjuna validates and extols the supreme majesty of God. All authoritative scriptures and seers—Vyasa, Narada, etc.—proclaim Him alone as the Supreme Brahman and Ultimate Abode.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Who then knows You? You Yourself know Yourself by Your own divine nature, not through any external means.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: O Supreme Person, Creator of beings, Lord of creatures, God of gods, Master of the universe! Being endowed with unexcelled self-luminous knowledge, You alone know Your Vasudeva Form; it remains unapproachable for maya-encapsulated souls.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: O Supreme Person, Creator of beings, Lord of creatures, God of gods, Ruler of the universe! You Yourself alone know Yourself by Your own infinite wisdom and majesty.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Being the supreme self-luminous Consciousness, the Supreme Lord alone is the knower of His own absolute nature; no finite entity can measure Him through limited means.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Glorifying the Lord with five matchless vocatives, Arjuna declares that the Lord's inherent, unexcelled knowledge alone is capable of directly comprehending His infinite divine Self.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: O Supreme Person, Creator and Master of all beings! You alone, by Your infinite knowledge, know Your own divine Form.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Being omniscient and self-luminous, the Supreme Lord alone knows Himself.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Indeed, You alone know Yourself by Your own internal potency, O Supreme Person, origin of all, Lord of all creatures, God of gods, Lord of the universe!
