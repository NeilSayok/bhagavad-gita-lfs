Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
pārtha naiveha nāmutra vināśastasya vidyate .
na hi kalyāṇakṛtkaścid durgatiṃ tāta gacchati

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
नैवेह - neither here
नामुत्र - nor in the next world
विनाशस्तस्य - destruction of him
विद्यते - is.
न - not
हि - certainly
कल्याणकृत्कश्चिद् - anyone who does good
दुर्गतिं - misfortune
तात - O dear one
गच्छति - attains

Life Application:
Rest assured that no good effort is ever lost, and one who strives for righteousness never meets a tragic end.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Blessed Lord said: O Partha, neither in this world nor in the next is there destruction for him; for never does anyone who does good meet with an evil fate, My son.

The Blessed Lord said: O Partha, neither in this world nor in the next is there destruction or ruin for that fallen practitioner; for never, My beloved son, does anyone who engages in auspicious, noble work meet with an evil fate or degradation.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "None doing good meets an evil fate"—the soul engaged in Lord Vishnu"s worship never suffers degradation; his devotion grows continually in future lives.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Comforting Arjuna, the Lord declares that anyone engaged in auspicious self-realization faces no destruction here or hereafter; good deeds never go in vain.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The Blessed Lord said: O Partha, neither in this world nor in the next is there destruction for him; for never does anyone who does good meet with an evil fate, My son.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Never does one doing good meet an evil fate"—the practitioner walking the path of Self-realization never falls into degradation; his spiritual merits remain intact.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Blessed Lord said: O Partha, neither in this world nor in the next is there destruction for him; for never does anyone who does good meet with an evil fate, My son.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "The Blessed Lord said: O Partha, neither in this world nor in the next is there destruction for him; for never does anyone who does good meet with an evil fate, My son"—pronounces the eternal spiritual guarantee (*Kalyana-krit na durgatim gachhati*): no spiritual effort is ever lost.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "One doing good" refers to the seeker engaged in divine devotion, to whom God extends His eternal grace and protection.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A devotee taking refuge in Lord Krishna never perishes; the Lord Himself carries the burden of his protection.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "No one doing good meets an evil fate"—the virtue generated by Yoga practice is never destroyed; it leads the seeker only toward elevation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of the eternal protection guaranteed to seekers of self-welfare in all realms.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Even a trace of devotion to Lord Vasudeva protects the practitioner forever from evil and degradation.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna Himself assumes responsibility for His devotee"s welfare; hence his destruction is impossible.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "No one doing good meets an evil fate"—practice performed with desire for liberation never goes fruitless; it matures in future lives to grant Mukti.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: Son of Prtha, a transcendentalist engaged in auspicious activities does not meet with destruction either in this world or in the spiritual world; one who does good, My friend, is never overcome by evil.
