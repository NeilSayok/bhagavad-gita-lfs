Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
na ca matsthāni bhūtāni paśya me yogamaiśvaram .
bhūtabhṛnna ca bhūtastho mamātmā bhūtabhāvanaḥ

Word Meanings:
न - not
च - and
मत्स्थानि - dwell in Me
भूतानि - beings
पश्य - behold
मे - my
योगमैश्वरम् - divine yoga power
भूतभृन्न - sustainer of beings, not
च - and
भूतस्थो - dwelling in beings
ममात्मा - My Self
भूतभावनः - originator of beings

Life Application:
Contemplate the divine mystery where beings do not constrain God, yet God sustains and creates all beings.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Nor do beings dwell in Me; behold My Divine Yoga! Sustaining all beings, but not dwelling in them, is My Self, the cause of beings.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Nor do beings dwell in Me; behold My divine mystery! Sustaining all beings, but not dwelling in them, is My Self, the cause of beings.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Nor do created beings dwell in Me in truth; behold My Divine Mystery! Sustaining all beings and creating all entities, My Self is not contained in them.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "All this is pervaded by Me"—all-pervading Vishnu is the Supreme Ruler of the cosmos; the universe depends on the Lord, but the Lord is independent. Such is His divine sovereign majesty.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Nor do beings dwell in Me"—from the ultimate standpoint, the ignorant projection of the world exists not in Brahman; divine detachment is God"s mystery.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Nor do beings dwell in Me; behold My Divine Yoga! Sustaining all beings, but not dwelling in them, is My Self, the cause of beings.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Beings do not abide in Me as physical contents in a container, but by My sovereign will alone; behold My divine power of infallible will!

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: All this cosmos is pervaded by My unmanifest form; all beings abide in Me, but I am not dependent on them; nor do beings abide in Me physically—behold My divine mystery (Yoga), My Self sustains all beings without being attached to them.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Nor do beings dwell in Me, behold My divine mystery"—from the absolute viewpoint, no illusion-created object dwells in partless unattached Brahman; behold My incomprehensible Yoga! Sustaining all beings, My Self is never bound.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Demonstrating the cosmos" dependence on God and the Lord"s absolute independence; incomprehensible is the sustaining power of Supreme Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Incomprehensible is the Lord"s sporting power; being the Sustainer of all, He remains completely unattached, which is the supreme secret of His divine majesty.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: From the ultimate viewpoint of non-duality, there is neither dependence of world on God nor God on world; this projected appearance is divine mystery.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Nor do beings dwell in Me; behold My Divine Yoga! Sustaining all beings, but not dwelling in them, is My Self, the cause of beings.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining Brahman"s changeless purity; world connection is only from the perspective of ignorance, but in absolute truth everything is non-dual Brahman.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord holds the universe by His inconceivable power without being dependent on it, which is His unique Supreme Godhead.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The entire cosmos is created and sustained by the Lord"s inconceivable Yoga-shakti, yet God is ever unattached and free.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Behold My divine mystery"—appearance of the world in Brahman is Maya alone; like rope-snake, the Substratum is real and projected object unreal.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
And yet everything that is created does not rest in Me. Behold My mystic opulence! Although I am the maintainer of all living entities and although I am everywhere, I am not a part of this cosmic manifestation, for My Self is the very source of creation.
