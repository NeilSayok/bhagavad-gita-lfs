Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
annādbhavanti bhūtāni parjanyādannasambhavaḥ .
yajñādbhavati parjanyo yajñaḥ karmasamudbhavaḥ

Word Meanings:
अन्नाद्भवन्ति - from food arise
भूतानि - living beings
पर्जन्यादन्नसम्भवः - production of food from rain
यज्ञाद्भवति - from sacrifice arises
पर्जन्यो - rain
यज्ञः - sacrifice
कर्मसमुद्भवः - born of action

Life Application:
Recognize the interconnected web of life that feeds you, honoring your role in maintaining ecological and social balance.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
All living beings originate from food; food is produced by rain (Parjanya); rain comes forth from sacrifice (Yajna); and sacrifice is born of action (Karma-samudbhavah).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Actions performed for Supreme Vishnu accomplish Yajna, bringing rain and abundance of food.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Expounds the cosmic chain: creatures from food, food from rain, rain from sacrifice, and sacrifice from human action.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Demonstrates the cause-and-effect relationship among food, rain, sacrifice, and action sustaining nature and souls.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.14–3.15 jointly): Action is born of the Veda, and the Veda from the Imperishable; therefore the all-pervading Brahman is ever established in sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "From food creatures come forth; from rain food is produced; from sacrifice rain comes forth; sacrifice is born of action"—delineates the fivefold cosmic cycle of creation and sustenance (*Jagac-chakra*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The cause-and-effect chain of food, rain, sacrifice, and action in the cosmic wheel proves divine order.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: In Sri Hari"s cyclic cosmic arrangement, sacrificial action forms the sustenance of all living beings.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: This cycle of food, rain, and sacrifice demonstrates the cosmic law instituted by the Divine.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
From food creatures come forth; from rain food is produced; from sacrifice rain comes forth; sacrifice is born of action.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving the cosmic sequence from food to creatures, rain to food, sacrifice to rain, and action to sacrifice.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: In Vasudeva"s cosmic wheel, sacrificial action forms the root of rain and sustenance.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When souls surrender work to Krishna, rain and food satisfy the entire universe.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Action-born Yajna forms the ultimate foundation for the practical cycle of Nature and Spirit.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
All living bodies subsist on food grains, which are produced from rains. Rains are produced by performance of yajna [sacrifice], and yajna is born of prescribed duties.
