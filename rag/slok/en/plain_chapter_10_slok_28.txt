Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
āyudhānāmahaṃ vajraṃ dhenūnāmasmi kāmadhuk .
prajanaścāsmi kandarpaḥ sarpāṇāmasmi vāsukiḥ

Word Meanings:
आयुधानामहं - among weapons I am
वज्रं - thunderbolt
धेनूनामस्मि - among cows I am
कामधुक् - Kamadhenu (wish-yielding cow)
प्रजनश्चास्मि - I am progenitor and
कन्दर्पः - Kamadeva (Cupid)
सर्पाणामस्मि - among serpents I am
वासुकिः - Vasuki

Life Application:
See God as the thunderbolt among weapons, Kamadhenu among cows, Kandarpa among progenitors, and Vasuki among serpents.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among weapons I am the Thunderbolt; among cows I am Kamadhenu (the wish-fulfilling cow); I am Kandarpa (Cupid) for procreation, and among poisonous serpents I am Vasuki.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among weapons I am the thunderbolt; among cows I am Kamadhuk; I am Kandarpa for procreation; among serpents I am Vasuki.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of weapons I am the Thunderbolt; of cows I am Kamadhenu, the cow of plenty. Of causes of offspring I am Kandarpa, the god of love, and of serpents I am Vasuki.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among weapons, the invincible Thunderbolt; among cows, the wish-fulfilling Kamadhenu; for righteous procreation, Kandarpa (god of love); and among single-headed serpents, King Vasuki represents the Lord.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among weapons I am the Thunderbolt; among cows I am Kamadhuk; I am Kandarpa for procreation; among serpents I am Vasuki.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Among weapons I am the Thunderbolt; among cows I am Kamadhuk; among procreators I am Kandarpa; among serpents I am Vasuki.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The invincible thunderbolt formed from Sage Dadhichi's bones, the wish-fulfilling Kamadhenu, Kandarpa for virtuous procreation, and King Vasuki among serpents are the Lord's chief manifestations.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among weapons I am the Thunderbolt; among cows I am Kamadhenu; among procreators I am Kandarpa, and among serpents I am Vasuki.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among weapons, the Thunderbolt; among cows, Kamadhenu; among procreators, Kandarpa; and among single-headed serpents, Vasuki is My divine Vibhuti.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among weapons I am the Thunderbolt; among cows I am Kamadhuk (the wish-fulfilling cow); among causes of offspring I am Kandarpa (Cupid); and among poisonous serpents I am Vasuki.

Sri Vallabhacharya states: The Thunderbolt, Kamadhenu, Kandarpa for righteous procreation, and King Vasuki among serpents symbolize the Lord's divine power.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Among weapons, the Thunderbolt born of Dadhichi's bones; among cows, the sacrifice-fulfilling Surabhi; among causes of offspring, Kandarpa; and among poisonous serpents, Vasuki is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: The thunderbolt's splendor among weapons, Kamadhenu's bounty among cows, Kandarpa's charm in procreation, and Vasuki's majesty among serpents represent the Lord.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Thunderbolt, Kamadhenu, Kandarpa for sacred procreation, and Vasuki among serpents excel in their realms solely through the Lord's governance.
