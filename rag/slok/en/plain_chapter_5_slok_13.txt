Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sarvakarmāṇi manasā saṃnyasyāste sukhaṃ vaśī .
navadvāre pure dehī naiva kurvanna kārayan

Word Meanings:
सर्वकर्माणि - all actions
मनसा - by the mind
संन्यस्यास्ते - renouncing, rests
सुखं - happily
वशी - the self-controlled
नवद्वारे - in nine-gated
पुरे - in city of body
देही - the embodied soul
नैव - never at all
कुर्वन्न - acting nor
कारयन् - causing to act

Life Application:
Rest peacefully in your inner awareness, realizing your true self is the quiet witness within your physical body.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Mentally renouncing all actions and self-controlled, the embodied one rests happily in the nine-gated city, neither acting nor causing to act.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Mentally renouncing all actions and self-controlled, the embodied one rests happily in the nine-gated city, neither acting nor causing to act.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The self-controlled master of the senses, having mentally renounced all actions by wisdom, dwells happily in the physical body—the nine-gated city of the soul—neither performing actions nor impelling others to act.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "In the nine-gated city"—the body is a nine-gated citadel wherein the individual soul rests happily by meditating on Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Renouncing illusory doership and enjoyership through discrimination, the self-controlled sage rests blissfully in the body (the city of nine gates), neither acting nor causing action.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Mentally renouncing all actions and self-controlled, the embodied one rests happily in the nine-gated city, neither acting nor causing to act.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Understanding through discrimination that the Self dwelling in the nine-gated body is non-doer and unattached, the wise man rests happily free from bondage.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Just as a person residing inside a house has no connection with the dilapidation or changes occurring in the house, in the same way, for me dwelling in this body-house beautified with nine window-openings, there is no association with its physical attributes.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Renouncing all actions mentally and self-controlled, the embodied one rests happily in the nine-gated city, neither acting nor causing to act"—expounds the state of Jivanmukti where the Self rests in Supreme Bliss inside the body (*Navadvare pure*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The embodied soul is a conscious entity distinct from the physical body, acting as a witness rather than an agent.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A practitioner under Krishna"s refuge, though dwelling in the body-citadel, remains absorbed in the divine bliss of devotion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Neither acting nor causing action"—established in Knowledge, the sage realizes non-agency and non-impulsion in bodily activities, rejoicing in Self-bliss.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Mentally renouncing all actions and self-controlled, the embodied one rests happily in the nine-gated city, neither acting nor causing to act.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of the self-governed sage residing blissfully in the nine-gated body after mentally relinquishing all actions.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: By Vasudeva"s grace, the soul dwelling in the physical citadel remains completely untainted by karma, ever joyous.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the practitioner"s mind finds rest in Krishna"s lotus feet, physical operations touch him not in the least.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "The embodied one in nine-gated city"—sensory activities transpire through the body"s nine gates while the Self remains an unmoving witness.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
When the embodied living being controls his nature and mentally renounces all actions, he resides happily in the city of nine gates [the material body], neither working nor causing work to be done.
