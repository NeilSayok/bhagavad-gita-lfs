Life Application:
Share timeless spiritual wisdom generously with others, performing the most cherished service to the Divine.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Nor is there among men any who can perform a service dearer to Me than this, or any man on earth more beloved by Me than he.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
This is also in praise of the teacher (Acharya). Here the Lord explains how such a great teacher attains the divine nature. The teacher of the Gita, Lord Krishna, emphasizes that there is none who does a more loving service to Me in this world than he. Not only this, but also in the future, there shall be none on earth dearer to Me than he. Throughout the Gita, this psychological truth is repeated. We do not need to wait to become perfect scholars. Whatever knowledge we are able to grasp, we must immediately and lovingly share with those who are completely ignorant of it. Along with dissemination, we must also live our lives according to this knowledge—such a person is extremely dear to Me. This is the promise of Lord Krishna. Not only the teacher, but also the sincere student of this knowledge is worthy of praise. The Lord says in the next verse.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
And there is none among men who does a more loving service to Me than he; nor shall there be another on earth dearer to Me than he. The transmission of this knowledge is the highest form of worship, and the teacher becomes the most beloved of the Lord.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
And there is none among men who does a more loving service to Me than he; nor shall there be another on earth dearer to Me than he. Commentary: Swami Ramsukhdas emphasizes the unique position of the Gita teacher: 1. None who does a more loving service (na ca tasmān... priyakṛttamaḥ): In the entire human race, there is no one who does a greater service to the Lord than one who propagates His words. This is because by showing the path of truth, he helps many souls to return to the Lord. 2. Nor shall there be another dearer (priyataro bhuvi): The Lord declares that in the present and the future, no one on this earth will be dearer to Him than the teacher of the Gita. This shows the boundless grace and love of the Lord for those who selflessly share His message of wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
There never exists nor has existed anyone other than such a person as described, who does greater service to Me than he. In the future too, there will not be another such. The first reference is to those who are not worthy to hear the Gita. It is meant to teach that explaining it to them is more displeasing to the Lord than not teaching to those who are worthy.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 18.68–18.72 jointly): Ya idam etc. upto Dhananjaya. The very act of explaining this among My devotees is itself an act of cultivating devotion towards Me.  Whosoever shall declare etc :  Whosoever directs the mind [of the devotees] by face to face method, following the best procedure prescribed in the scriptures; i.e., he who shall impart  [this knowledge] - he becomes one with Me.  This is an injunction  [concerning the result]  and not a sentence of praise.  Likewise is  [the case] in other places also.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Ca, and; tasmat, as compared with him, with the one who hands down the Scripture; na kascit, none else; manusyesu, among human beings; is priya-krt-tamah, the best accomplisher of what is dear; me, to Me, i.e., among the present human beings, surely none else other than him exists who is a better accomplisher of what I cherish. Moreover, na bhavita, nor will there be in future; anyah, anyone else, a second person; bhuvi, in he world, here; priyatarah, dearer; tasmat, than him. [It may be argued that, since for a seeker of Liberation meditation is the best means for It, therefore he will have no inclination to transmit scriptural teachings. To this the Lord's answer is: One longing for Liberation has a duty to impart this scriptural teaching to one possessing the aforesaid alities.]

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Furthermore, the Lord says: 'na ca tasmat...'. Among all men in the present, there is none who performs a more pleasing service to Me, displaying intense love for Me. Nor was there anyone in the past, nor will there be another in the future on this earth who is dearer to Me than the teacher of this scripture. By repeating the negation, the Lord's absolute love for the disseminator is established.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
The Lord states that teaching this scripture is the direct means to attain Him. There is none among men in the present who performs a more pleasing service to Me, nor was there in the past, nor will there be in the future on this earth dearer to Me than the teacher of this scripture. By explaining this, the teacher establishes the path of devotion.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
The Lord explains how teaching or hearing leads to Him: 'na ca...'. Among qualified humans, there is none who performs a more beloved act than the teacher of this path, who displays his effort to connect My devotees with Me. Furthermore, there shall not be anyone dearer on earth than the listener who serves according to My instructions.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
The Lord addresses the doubt about whether one can obtain devotion by teaching without full faith initially: 'na ca...'. There is none among men who does a more pleasing service to Me than the teacher of this scripture. This teaching itself is a great verbal worship (vachiki bhakti), through which one gradually climbs the steps to attain supreme non-dual devotion and liberation. There is no other means on earth superior to this.
