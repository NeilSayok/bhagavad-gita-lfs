Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
samo.ahaṃ sarvabhūteṣu na me dveṣyo.asti na priyaḥ .
ye bhajanti tu māṃ bhaktyā mayi te teṣu cāpyaham

सर्वभूतेषु - in all beings
न - not

Life Application:
Realize God is equal to all beings, hating none and favoring none; yet those who worship with love abide in God.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The same am I to all beings; to Me there is none hateful or dear; but those who worship Me with devotion, they are in Me and I also in them.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The same am I to all beings; to Me there is none hateful or dear; but those who worship Me with devotion, they are in Me and I also in them.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I am equally disposed to all beings; to Me there is none hateful nor dear. But those who worship Me with loving devotion abide naturally in Me, and I am manifested in them.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "I am impartial"—the Supreme Lord maintains just equality toward all souls; variation in rewards accords with karma and devotion, without any fault of partiality in Him.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Like fire, the Lord is impartial to all; whoever approaches Him with devotion receives light and warmth (grace), refuting any charge of partiality.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The same am I to all beings; to Me there is none hateful or dear; but those who worship Me with devotion, they are in Me and I also in them.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The Lord is impartial to all beings high or low; He holds neither attachment nor aversion to any. Yet those who worship Him with intense love dwell uniquely in Him.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord is equal to all beings, without hatred or partiality; yet those who worship Him with devotion abide in Him, and He in them. Even a deeply wicked person worshipping with undivided devotion becomes virtuous and never perishes.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I am equal to all beings, none is hateful or dear to Me"—like the sun shedding light equally, I have no bias. Yet like fire giving warmth only to those who draw near, those worshipping with devotion abide in Me and I in them.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Variance in rewards stems from gradations of devotion; like sunlight, God is faultlessly impartial, yet bestows special grace upon exclusive devotees.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The Lord is inherently equal to all; yet within Pushti devotees of exclusive love, He manifests His own Self and abides eternally.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I am equal to all beings"—like a desire-fulfilling tree or fire, the Lord is absolutely faultless and impartial; divine bliss shines forth in the worshipping seeker.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: The same am I to all beings; to Me there is none hateful or dear; but those who worship Me with devotion, they are in Me and I also in them.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Establishing divine impartiality; there is no inequality or bias in God; the manifestation of God in devotees is solely the result of their pure devotion.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord is equal to all beings; devotion creates a transcendental connection whereby the soul abides in the Lord and the Lord in the soul.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: I am equal to all creatures; those who worship Me with love dwell in My heart and I in theirs.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The all-pervading Supreme Self is impartial like the sun; a mind attuned to devotion becomes pure like a mirror, reflecting the Lord.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I envy no one, nor am I partial to anyone. I am equal to all. But whoever renders service unto Me in devotion is a friend, is in Me, and I am also a friend to him.
