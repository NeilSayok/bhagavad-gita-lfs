Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sarvabhūtasthamātmānaṃ sarvabhūtāni cātmani .
īkṣate yogayuktātmā sarvatra samadarśanaḥ

Word Meanings:
सर्वभूतस्थमात्मानं - the Self in all beings
सर्वभूतानि - all beings
चात्मनि - and in the Self
ईक्षते - sees
योगयुक्तात्मा - harmonized by yoga
सर्वत्र - everywhere
समदर्शनः - viewing equally

Life Application:
See the divine presence in all beings and all beings in the divine presence, cultivating universal empathy.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
With the heart concentrated by Yoga, he sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings abiding in the Self; he sees the same everywhere.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
With mind integrated by Yoga, beholding equality everywhere, he sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings in the Self.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Endowed with equal vision seeing the one Supreme Consciousness in all, the perfected Yogi whose heart is firmly absorbed in Meditation-Yoga sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings residing within the Self.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
With the heart concentrated by Yoga, he sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings abiding in the Self; he sees the same everywhere.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Self abiding in all beings"—the devotee in Samadhi constantly experiences Supreme Lord Vishnu indwelling all creatures as the Antaryamin.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The yogi of integrated mind beholds the single conscious Self in all beings from Brahma down to a blade of grass, and all beings in his Self without non-duality.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
With the heart concentrated by Yoga, he sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings abiding in the Self; he sees the same everywhere.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Experiencing the essential equality of all souls in their pure nature, the yogi sees the self-same conscious essence in all beings.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: He whose heart is harmonized by Yoga sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings abiding in the Self, beholding the same everywhere. He who sees Me everywhere and sees all in Me, to him I am never lost, nor is he ever lost to Me. The Yogi who, established in unity, worships Me dwelling in all beings, lives in Me whatever be his mode of life. O Arjuna, he who sees equality in all beings by analogy with himself, whether in pleasure or in pain, is regarded as the highest Yogi.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "With the heart concentrated by Yoga, he sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings abiding in the Self; he sees the same everywhere"—reveals the direct cosmic realization of Advaita: seeing non-different Self everywhere (*Sarvatra Samadarshana*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Abiding in all beings means knowing God"s all-pervading nature and His inner rulership over all creation.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Beholding Krishna in all souls and all souls in Krishna is the state of total universal love (Sarvatmabhava).

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Beholding equality everywhere"—through unbroken spiritual vision, phenomenal universe is sublated, revealing only the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
With mind integrated by Yoga, beholding equality everywhere, he sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings in the Self.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of equal vision, mind integrated by Yoga, and realizing the Self in all beings.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Beholding the essential purity of all individual souls as equal is the true meaning of "Samadarshana".

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a devotee beholds his worshipful Lord Krishna everywhere, his life becomes blessed and fulfilled.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Beholding equality everywhere"—sublating names and forms to realize the single Sat-Chit-Ananda everywhere is true equal vision.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
A true yogi observes Me in all beings and also sees every being in Me. Indeed, the self-realized person sees Me, the same Supreme Lord, everywhere.
