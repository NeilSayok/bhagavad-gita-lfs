Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sukhamātyantikaṃ yattad buddhigrāhyamatīndriyam .
vetti yatra na caivāyaṃ sthitaścalati tattvataḥ

Word Meanings:
सुखमात्यन्तिकं - infinite bliss
यत्तद् - which that
बुद्धिग्राह्यमतीन्द्रियम् - grasped by intellect beyond senses
वेत्ति - knows
यत्र - wherever
न - not
चैवायं - and indeed this yogi
स्थितश्चलति - established, swerves
तत्त्वतः - in truth

Life Application:
Taste the boundless joy that lies beyond sensory pleasures, remaining firm in truth without ever wavering.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
When he knows that infinite joy which is grasped by the intellect beyond the senses, and standing wherein he never departs from reality—

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
When the seeker experiences that infinite joy which transcends the senses and is grasped solely by purified intellect, and established in which he never swerves from the Truth—

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
When in deep Samadhi the practitioner directly experiences that infinite, transcendent bliss which lies beyond the reach of senses and is realized solely through a purified intellect, and established in which he never swerves even a hair"s breadth from Supreme Reality—

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Grasped by intellect beyond senses"—the eternal bliss of Lord Vishnu is not accessible to material senses; it is attained solely through devotionally purified intellect.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The bliss of the Self is not an object of sense organs; it is directly experienced solely through pure intellect endowed with right knowledge.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
When he knows that infinite joy which is grasped by the intellect beyond the senses, and standing wherein he never departs from reality—

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: When the practitioner experiences the infinite transcendent bliss born solely of Self-realization, never wavering from the Self-essence.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: When the mind, restrained by the practice of Yoga, attains absolute quietude, and when beholding the Self by the self, one rests satisfied within the Self alone; when one experiences that boundless joy which transcends the senses and is grasped solely by purified intellect, and established in which one never swerves from the Truth; obtaining which one regards no other gain as superior, and anchored in which one is not shaken even by heavy sorrow—know that severance from union with pain to be called Yoga. That Yoga should be practiced with unshakeable resolve and an undaunted heart.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "When he knows that infinite joy which is grasped by the intellect beyond the senses, and standing wherein he never departs from reality"—describes transcendental bliss (Atyantika Sukha) experienced through refined pure intellect, which anchors the sage immovably in Self-realization.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Transcendent bliss is the true bliss of Mukti; established therein, the enlightened soul never returns to worldly delusion.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The supernatural bliss of Krishna"s form is revealed exclusively to the purified intellect touched by divine grace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Grasped by intellect beyond senses"—unconditioned, self-luminous non-dual bliss free from sensory contact is called the ultimate joy.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
When the seeker experiences that infinite joy which transcends the senses and is grasped solely by purified intellect, and established in which he never swerves from the Truth—

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of transcendent bliss being comprehended solely by refined intellect during meditation.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The yogi enjoying the supernatural bliss of Lord Vasudeva is never tempted by any material pleasure.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The boundless bliss of Lord Krishna"s form releases the soul forever from all suffering.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "That infinite joy"—when worldly pleasure is completely sublated, non-dual bliss shines forth as Mukti.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
In that joyous state, one is situated in boundless transcendental happiness and enjoys himself through transcendental senses. Established thus, one never departs from the truth.
