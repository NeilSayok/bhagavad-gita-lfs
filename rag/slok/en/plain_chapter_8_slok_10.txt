Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
prayāṇakāle manasā.acalena bhaktyā yukto yogabalena caiva .
bhruvormadhye prāṇamāveśya samyak sa taṃ paraṃ puruṣamupaiti divyam

Word Meanings:
प्रयाणकाले - at the hour of death
मनसाऽचलेन - with an unmoving mind
भक्त्या - with devotion
युक्तो - endowed
योगबलेन - by the strength of yoga
चैव - and also
भ्रुवोर्मध्ये - between the eyebrows
प्राणमावेश्य - placing the life-force
सम्यक् - perfectly
स - he
तं - that
परं - Supreme
पुरुषमुपैति - attains the Divine Person
दिव्यम् - the effulgent

Life Application:
Focus your mind with devotion at the time of death, drawing life energy between the eyebrows to reach the Supreme.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
At the time of death, with an unmoving mind, endued with devotion and the power of Yoga, fixing the prana between the eyebrows, he reaches that Supreme Resplendent Person.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
At the time of death, with an unmoving mind, endued with devotion and the power of Yoga, fixing the prana between the eyebrows, he reaches that Supreme Resplendent Person.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
At the moment of departure, with a steady mind filled with devotion, through the power of Yoga fixing the life-breath between the eyebrows, one reaches that Supreme Divine Person.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "By yogic power"—drawing the life-force to the ajna-chakra through yogic strength and devotion at death elevates the devotee to Lord Hari"s supreme abode.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "At departure with unmoving mind"—raising the life-breath to the ajna-chakra between the eyebrows through yogic power born of practice at death grants direct attainment of the Supreme Divine Person.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
At the time of death, with an unmoving mind, endued with devotion and the power of Yoga, fixing the prana between the eyebrows, he reaches that Supreme Resplendent Person.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: At the final moment, fixing the life-force between the eyebrows through devotion and yogic strength with an unmoving mind, the practitioner reaches the Supreme Person.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: One who withdraws the mind from other objects and constantly meditates through yoga practice on the Supreme Person (inconceivable, omniscient, sun-brilliant), fixing the life-force between the eyebrows at death with steady devotion, attains the Supreme Divine Person.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "At departure with unmoving mind, yoked with devotion and yogic power, fixing prana between eyebrows, he reaches Supreme Resplendent Person"—one who at death draws the life-force through Sushumna up to the ajna-chakra with undivided devotion and yogic mastery directly reaches Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Meditation at the moment of departure is impossible without yogic power and devotion; restraining life-breath in the ajna-chakra is the supreme perfection of departure.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Yogic strength is perfected through divine grace; with unswerving devoted mind, restraining life-breath at death leads the soul to Krishna"s abode.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Fixing prana between eyebrows"—raising life-breath through Sushumna to the ajna-chakra with steady devotion is the supreme success of Northern Path.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: At the time of death, with an unmoving mind, endued with devotion and the power of Yoga, fixing the prana between the eyebrows, he reaches that Supreme Resplendent Person.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative explanation of the yogic technique of fixing life-breath between eyebrows at death and attaining Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Through yogic power born of practice and exclusive devotion, focusing the life-breath upward at death leads to Supreme Realm.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: A devotee meditating on the Lord at departure with unmoving mind and exclusive love directly reaches Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Fixing prana between eyebrows"—drawing life-force through Sushumna to ajna-chakra constitutes final samadhi of an accomplished yogi.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
One who, at the time of death, fixes his life air between the eyebrows and, by the strength of yoga, with an undeviating mind, engages himself in remembering the Supreme Lord in full devotion, will certainly attain to the Supreme Personality of Godhead.
