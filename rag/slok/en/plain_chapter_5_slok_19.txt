Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ihaiva tairjitaḥ sargo yeṣāṃ sāmye sthitaṃ manaḥ .
nirdoṣaṃ hi samaṃ brahma tasmād brahmaṇi te sthitāḥ

Word Meanings:
इहैव - here itself (in this life)
तैर्जितः - by them conquered
सर्गो - mortal creation
येषां - whose
साम्ये - in equanimity
स्थितं - established
मनः - mind
निर्दोषं - flawless
हि - certainly
समं - equal
ब्रह्म - Supreme Brahman
तस्माद् - therefore
ब्रह्मणि - in Brahman
ते - they
स्थिताः - established

Life Application:
Conquer daily stress by cultivating an even mind, remaining grounded in the calm equality of divine awareness.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Even here (in this world) birth (or world) is conquered by those whose minds rest in equality; Brahman is spotless indeed and equal; therefore they are established in Brahman.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Even here in this very life, the cycle of birth and rebirth is completely conquered by those whose minds are established in absolute equality (samya); for Supreme Brahman is eternally flawless and equal, and therefore they abide forever in Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Brahman is spotless and equal"—Lord Vishnu is completely flawless and equal everywhere; fixing the mind in Him makes the soul eligible for Mukti in this very life.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Those whose minds are established in equality conquer worldly transmigration in this very lifetime, for spotless equal Brahman is their true nature.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Those whose minds are established in the eternal equality of all souls conquer worldly birth and death while still dwelling in this body.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: This sloka does not exist in Gitarthasamgraha text.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Even here birth is conquered by those whose minds rest in equality; Brahman is spotless indeed and equal; therefore they are established in Brahman"—glorifies *Jivanmukti* (liberation while living) achieved through steadfast equality of mind.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Establishment in flawless equal Lord Vishnu is victory over worldly transmigration, ending the cycle of births.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A devotee whose mind is balanced in Krishna"s love is liberated while living, enjoying the Lord"s eternal joy here in this world.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Conquered birth even here"—upon destruction of Avidya through equanimity of vision, the practitioner enjoys Jivanmukti in this very life.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving that those whose minds are rooted in cosmic equality conquer worldly transmigration right here in this body.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A seeker holding the vision of Vasudeva-equality shatters all karmic bonds in this very lifetime.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Establishment in Lord Krishna"s divine nature is the eternal Jivanmukta state of a selfless devotee.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Conquered birth right here"—in this very life, non-dual Knowledge sublates the delusion of worldliness, granting Mukti.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those whose minds are established in sameness and equanimity have already conquered the conditions of birth and death. They are flawless like Brahman, and thus they are already situated in Brahman.
