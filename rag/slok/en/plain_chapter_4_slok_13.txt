Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
cāturvarṇyaṃ mayā sṛṣṭaṃ guṇakarmavibhāgaśaḥ .
tasya kartāramapi māṃ viddhyakartāramavyayam

Word Meanings:
चातुर्वर्ण्यं - fourfold order
मया - by Me
सृष्टं - created
गुणकर्मविभागशः - by divisions of qualities and actions
तस्य - his
कर्तारमपि - though the author
मां - Me
विद्ध्यकर्तारमव्ययम् - know as non-doer and immutable

Life Application:
Appreciate people based on their actual qualities and actions rather than rigid social labels or background.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The fourfold order was created by Me according to the divisions of Guna and Karma. Though I am the author thereof, know Me to be the non-doer and changeless.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The fourfold order was created by Me according to the divisions of Guna and Karma. Though I am the author thereof, know Me to be the non-doer and changeless.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The fourfold order of society (Brahmana, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Shudra) was created by Me according to the distinct proportions of Gunas (qualities) and Karmas (inherent aptitudes); yet, although I am the Creator of this cosmic order, know Me, the immutable Supreme Lord, to be eternally a Non-doer.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Know Me as non-doer and changeless"—though creating the order of varnas based on qualities and work, Lord Vishnu Himself remains immutable and detached.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The fourfold social order is crafted by Divine Power according to gunas and past karma, yet the Supreme Self remains untainted and a non-doer.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The fourfold order was created by Me according to the divisions of Guna and Karma. Though I am the author thereof, know Me to be the non-doer and changeless.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The four social divisions are created based on gunas (Sattva etc.) and duties (tranquility, control etc.); though Creator of all, I am a non-doer and unchangeable.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.13–4.14 jointly): Though Creator of the fourfold order, the Supreme Lord is a non-doer, for actions do not taint Him nor has He longing for fruits; one who knows Me thus is not bound by actions.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "The fourfold order was created by Me according to the divisions of Guna and Karma; though I am the author thereof, know Me to be the non-doer and changeless"—distinguishes empirical creation (*Maya-sristi*) from the ultimate non-agency (*Akartritva*) of Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The Varna classification is based on cosmic gunas and aptitudes, not rigid birth-prejudice; God, though its regulator, remains a non-doer.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna, through His divine lila, organizes gunas and duties in creation, yet remains a non-doer in His unbroken sovereignty.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Know Me as non-doer and imperishable"—as the Illusory Creator, God is termed doer; yet from the non-dual standpoint, He is strictly a non-doer.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
The fourfold order was created by Me according to the divisions of Guna and Karma. Though I am the author thereof, know Me to be the non-doer and changeless.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing how the Lord, though creating the four social orders based on Gunas, remains an untainted non-doer.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Vasudeva regulates the four orders based on Gunas, yet remains untainted by karmic doership.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Discrimination of Guna and Karma in Krishna"s creation establishes cosmic balance, where the Lord abides as eternal witness.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Though Creator, know Me as non-doer"—though Creator from Maya"s viewpoint, God in essence is unmoving and non-agent.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
According to the three modes of material nature and the work associated with them, the four divisions of human society were created by Me. And although I am the creator of this system, you should know that I am yet the nondoer, being unchangeable.
