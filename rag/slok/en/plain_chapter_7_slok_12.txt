Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ye caiva sāttvikā bhāvā rājasāstāmasāśca ye .
matta eveti tānviddhi na tvahaṃ teṣu te mayi

Word Meanings:
ये - whatever
चैव - and certainly
सात्त्विका - in the mode of goodness
भावा - states of being
राजसास्तामसाश्च - of passion and ignorance also
ये - whatever
मत्त - from Me
एवेति - certainly thus
तान्विद्धि - know them
न - not
त्वहं - but I
तेषु - in them
ते - they
मयि - in Me

Life Application:
Understand that all states of goodness, passion, and ignorance originate from God, but God transcends them all.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Whatever states there are of Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas, know them to proceed from Me alone; yet I am not in them, but they are in Me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Whatever states that are Sattvic, Rajasic, and Tamasic, know them to proceed from Me alone; yet I am not in them, but they are in Me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Whatever states of Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas arise in creatures, know them to originate from My lower nature alone; yet I am not bound by or dependent upon them, rather they are fully dependent upon Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "I am not in them, but they are in Me"—the three Gunas function under Lord Vishnu, while Vishnu remains eternally untainted by their flaws.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: All three Gunas proceed under the supreme control of the Lord; yet the Self remains unattached and untouched as sovereign Master.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Whatever states there are of Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas, know them to proceed from Me alone; yet I am not in them, but they are in Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Sattvic, Rajasic, and Tamasic states of beings proceed from Me and rest in Me as My divine body, while I remain independent of their limitations.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Whatever states that are Sattvic, Rajasic, or Tamasic, know them to proceed from Me alone; I am not in them, but they are in Me. Deluded by these states composed of the three Gunas, this entire world fails to recognize Me, who am beyond them and imperishable.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Whatever states that are Sattvic, Rajasic, and Tamasic, know them to proceed from Me; I am not in them, but they are in Me"—all Guna-modifications arise from the Lord"s Maya, but the Supreme Self remains unattached and ever-free from Gunas.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: All states of the cosmos are dependent on Vishnu; Lord Vishnu, being the independent Ruler, is never constrained by physical Gunas.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The creation of all Gunas takes place solely by Lord Krishna"s will; as Witness and Master of Gunas, He remains unattached.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I am not in them"—superimposed states depend upon the Substratum Truth, but the Substratum is never stained by projected qualities.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Whatever states that are Sattvic, Rajasic, and Tamasic, know them to proceed from Me alone; yet I am not in them, but they are in Me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural demonstration of the Lord as the cause of all three Gunas while remaining unattached in Himself.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: All Guna-modifications are states of Lord Vasudeva"s cosmic body, continuously under His divine governance.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Three-fold Maya is the servant of Lord Krishna; the Lord as her Sovereign remains eternally seated in His own divine nature.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "I am not in them, but they are in Me"—the effects of Maya delude the soul, but Supreme Self, Master of Maya, is never deluded.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Know that all states of being—be they of goodness, passion or ignorance—are manifested by My energy. I am, in one sense, everything, but I am independent. I am not under the control of the modes of material nature, for they are on the contrary within Me.
