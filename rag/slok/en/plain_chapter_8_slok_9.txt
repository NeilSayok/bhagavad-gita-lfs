Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
kaviṃ purāṇamanuśāsitāraṃ aṇoraṇīyaṃsamanusmaredyaḥ .
sarvasya dhātāramacintyarūpaṃ ādityavarṇaṃ tamasaḥ parastāt

Word Meanings:
कविं - the all-knowing seer
पुराणमनुशासितार - the Ancient and Supreme Ruler
मणोरणीयंसमनुस्मरेद्यः - subtler than an atom who meditates upon
सर्वस्य - of everything
धातारमचिन्त्यरूप - the Sustainer of inconceivable form
मादित्यवर्णं - sun-like brilliant
तमसः - beyond darkness
परस्तात् - far beyond

Life Application:
Meditate on the omniscient, ancient ruler, subtler than the atom, sustainer of all, luminous like the sun beyond darkness.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Whoever meditates on the Omniscient, the Ancient, the Ruler of all, subtler than the subtle, the Sustainer of all, of form inconceivable, resplendent like the sun, and beyond darkness—

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Omniscient, Ancient"—continuous remembrance of Lord Sri Hari, who is all-knowing, eternal, supreme ruler, and of inconceivable divine potency, elevates the devotee to the Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Meditating on the Lord with His eight attributes—Omniscient, Ancient, Ruler, subtler than the subtle, Sustainer, Inconceivable, Sun-resplendent, and Beyond Darkness—grants liberation.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: One should cultivate exclusive meditation on the Supreme Person who is Omniscient, Ancient Creator, Universal Controller, subtler than individual souls, Sustainer of all creation, possessing inconceivable divine form, radiant like the sun, and beyond darkness.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: One who withdraws the mind from other objects and constantly meditates through yoga practice on the Supreme Person (inconceivable, omniscient, sun-brilliant), fixing the life-force between the eyebrows at death with steady devotion, attains the Supreme Divine Person.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Whoever meditates on the Supreme Lord as Omniscient (Kavi), Ancient (Purana), Universal Ruler (Anushasitaram), Subtler than the subtle (Anor aniyamsam), Sustainer and Dispenser of action-fruits (Sarvasya dhataram), of Inconceivable Form (Achintyarupa), Sun-resplendent Consciousness (Adityavarna), and Beyond the darkness of ignorance (Tamasah parastat)—

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Lord Vishnu endowed with the eight divine attributes is the supreme object of worship; His constant remembrance is the seeker"s ultimate shelter.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Sun-resplendent, inconceivably beautiful Purushottama Lord Krishna is the Sustainer of all universe and supreme worshipable Lord.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Beyond darkness"—meditating on the eight divine marks of self-luminous Supreme Brahman Lord Vasudeva beyond Maya bestows direct liberation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Whoever meditates on the Omniscient, the Ancient, the Ruler of all, subtler than the subtle, the Sustainer of all, of form inconceivable, resplendent like the sun, and beyond darkness—

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative delineation of the Lord"s eight divine attributes and meditation process.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Meditating on Omniscient, Supreme Ruler Lord Vasudeva of inconceivable form carries the seeker to Vaikuntha.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The Lord is self-luminous like the sun and the Sustainer of all; His unceasing remembrance brings ultimate auspiciousness.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Beyond darkness"—meditating on Consciousness Self entirely beyond ignorance-slumber liberates the seeker from bondage.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
One should meditate upon the Supreme Person as the one who knows everything, as He who is the oldest, who is the controller, who is smaller than the smallest, who is the maintainer of everything, who is beyond all material conception, who is inconceivable, and who is always a person. He is luminous like the sun, and He is transcendental, beyond this material nature.
