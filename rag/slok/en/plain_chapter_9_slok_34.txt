Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru .
māmevaiṣyasi yuktvaivamātmānaṃ matparāyaṇaḥ

Word Meanings:
मन्मना - fix your mind on Me
भव - become
मद्भक्तो - My devotee
मद्याजी - My worshipper
मां - to Me
नमस्कुरु - bow down
मामेवैष्यसि - to Me alone you shall come
युक्त्वैवमात्मानं - having thus united your soul
मत्परायणः - intent on Me as supreme goal

Life Application:
Fix your mind on God, be devoted, worship, and bow down; uniting yourself thus, you will surely reach God.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Fixing mind on God, becoming His devotee, worshipping Him, and bowing down to Him to surrender everything is the supreme climax of Royal Knowledge and Secret.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Fix your mind constantly on Me, the Lord of all (man-mana), cultivate boundless love for Me (mad-bhakta), worship Me (mad-yaji), and prostrate before Me (man-namaskuru); doing so, you shall directly attain Me.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: O Arjuna! Taking exclusive refuge in Me, even women, Vaishyas, Shudras, and those of low origin attain the supreme goal; how much more then pious Brahmanas and royal sages! Therefore, having come into this impermanent joyless world, fix your mind on Me, become My exclusive devotee, and surrender unto Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Fix your mind on Me, be My devotee, worship Me, bow to Me"—concentrate your mind solely on Lord Vasudeva, become His loving devotee, perform all worship for Him, and bow down to Him. Attuning your self thus with Me as your supreme refuge, you shall attain Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Fix mind on Krishna (man-mana), cherish exclusive love (mad-bhakta), render divine service (mad-yaji), and surrender all (man-namaskuru)—this fourfold path grants direct realization of Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Fix your mind on Me, be My devotee"—surrendering all mental modes to Lord Vasudeva, worshipping Him and bowing down to Him continuously is the ultimate instruction of Sovereign Science, leading directly to Brahman-realization.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the supreme climax of Chapter Nine; through "man-mana" and associated practices, the seeker becomes completely divine and attains Supreme Brahman Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Through the fourfold surrender of man-mana, mad-bhakta, mad-yaji, and man-namaskuru, the seeker attains eternal divine service in Vaikuntha.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Fix your mind on Me, become My devotee, worship Me, and bow down to Me; this is the supreme secret of unselfish devotion and essence of Chapter 9.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Total surrender of all mental modes (man-mana) and exclusive refuge is the essence of Royal Science, bestowing direct liberation (Kaivalya).

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Engage your mind always in thinking of Me, become My devotee, offer obeisances unto Me and worship Me. Being completely absorbed in Me, surely you will come to Me.
