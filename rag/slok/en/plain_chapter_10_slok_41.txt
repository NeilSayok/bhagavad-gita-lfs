Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yadyadvibhūtimatsattvaṃ śrīmadūrjitameva vā .
tattadevāvagaccha tvaṃ mama tejoṃśasambhavam

Word Meanings:
यद्यद्विभूतिमत्सत्त्वं - whatever glorious being
श्रीमदूर्जितमेव - graceful or mighty indeed
वा - or
तत्तदेवावगच्छ - know all that
त्वं - you
मम - my
तेजोंऽशसम्भवम् - born of a part of My splendor

Life Application:
Whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty—know that to spring from a fragment of divine splendor.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Whatever being is glorious, prosperous, or mighty, know that to be born of a fragment of My divine splendor.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Whatever creature is glorious, prosperous, or powerful, know that to be born of a fragment of My splendor.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Whatever entity is glorious, prosperous, or powerful, know that to be born of a fragment of My divine splendor.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Whatever entity in the universe appears glorious, beautiful, or mighty, know it to be a manifestation of a mere fraction of the Divine Splendor.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Whatever creature is glorious, prosperous, or powerful, know that to be born of a fragment of My splendor.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Whatever creature is glorious, prosperous, or powerful, know that to be born of a fragment of My splendor.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Whatever creature is glorious, prosperous, or powerful, know that to be born of a fragment of My splendor.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Whatever being possesses opulence is endowed with a portion of the Lord's divine splendor. According to the Paingi and Gautama Khila Shruti texts, exalted beings like Rudra, Garuda, Indra, and Prithu possess a fragment of His power, whereas Krishna, Vyasa, Rama, Kapila, and Yajna are direct full manifestations of Lord Vishnu Himself.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Whatever glorious, beautiful, or mighty object is perceived in the universe is solely a manifestation of a subtle fragment of the Lord's infinite splendor.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Whatever entity is glorious, prosperous, or powerful, know that to be born of a fragment of My splendor.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Whatever creature is endowed with power of governance, prosperity, or high enthusiasm, know all that to be derived from a fragment of My divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Whatever entity in the world is glorious (endowed with opulence), prosperous (endowed with grace or wealth), or powerful (endowed with energy), know that to be born of a fragment of My (the Lord's) divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: 'Born of a fragment of My divine splendor'—ordinary glorious beings derive power from a fraction of the Lord's luster, whereas direct Vishnu-avataras (Krishna, Varaha, etc.) are non-different, complete personal manifestations of the Supreme Lord Himself.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: All opulence, beauty, and power express a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor; this is the core principle of realizing His manifestations.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Whatever animate or inanimate object is glorious, beautiful, or mighty is manifested solely from a minute fraction of the Supreme Lord's boundless luster.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Whatever entity is glorious, prosperous, or mighty, know that to be born of a fragment of My splendor.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: The supreme majesty, wealth, and power in the world are but a reflection of a fraction of God's splendor, representing the universal criterion of divine Vibhuti.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Being glorious, prosperous, and mighty are revered everywhere solely because they arise from a fragment of the Lord's divine luster.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Whatever being is glorious, prosperous, or powerful, know that to be born of a fraction of My splendor.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Wherever glory, wealth, or power is observed, it is entirely the manifestation of a fragment of the Supreme Lord's luster.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Know that all opulent, beautiful and glorious creations blossom from but a spark of My splendor.
