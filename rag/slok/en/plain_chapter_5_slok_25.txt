Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
labhante brahmanirvāṇamṛṣayaḥ kṣīṇakalmaṣāḥ .
chinnadvaidhā yatātmānaḥ sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ

Word Meanings:
लभन्ते - attain
ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृषयः - liberation in Brahman, the seers
क्षीणकल्मषाः - whose sins are destroyed
छिन्नद्वैधा - whose doubts are severed
यतात्मानः - self-controlled
सर्वभूतहिते - in welfare of all beings
रताः - engaged

Life Application:
Dedicate yourself to the well-being of all creatures while working to eliminate personal faults and doubts.
