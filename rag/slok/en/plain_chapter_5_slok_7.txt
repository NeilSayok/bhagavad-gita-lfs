Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yogayukto viśuddhātmā vijitātmā jitendriyaḥ .
sarvabhūtātmabhūtātmā kurvannapi na lipyate

Word Meanings:
योगयुक्तो - engaged in yoga
विशुद्धात्मा - pure-hearted
विजितात्मा - self-controlled
जितेन्द्रियः - master of senses
सर्वभूतात्मभूतात्मा - self of all beings
कुर्वन्नपि - even while acting
न - not
लिप्यते - is tainted

Life Application:
Keep your mind pure and senses disciplined while serving others, seeing the same divine life in everyone.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
He who is devoted to the path of action, whose mind is quite pure, who has conquered the self, who has subdued his senses, and who realizes his Self as the Self in all beings, though acting, is not tainted.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Self as the Self in all beings"—the devotee beholding Lord Vishnu indwelling all living entities remains sinless while acting.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Seeing the single Divine Self in all creatures, a Karma-Yogi performing actions for the welfare of the world remains unstained by merit or sin.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: One whose mind is purified by divine contemplation and who looks upon all beings with equal vision remains untouched by karmic consequences while acting.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: He who is devoted to Yoga, pure in self, master of his body and senses, who realizes his own Self as the Self of all beings, is not tainted though acting. The knower of Truth, centered in Yoga, while seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, walking, sleeping, breathing, speaking, releasing, grasping, opening and closing eyes, thinks: "I do nothing at all; the senses merely move among their objects." He who performs actions offering them to Brahman, abandoning attachment, is not stained by sin, even as a lotus leaf is untouched by water. Yogis perform actions without attachment, with body, mind, intellect, and senses alone, solely for the purification of the self.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "He who is devoted to the path of action, whose mind is quite pure, who has conquered the self, who has subdued his senses, and who realizes his Self as the Self in all beings, though acting, is not tainted"—describes the advanced stage of a Karma-Yogi whose vision reaches non-dual unity.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Beholding the presence of the Supreme Lord in all living beings bestows genuine untaintedness (aliptata).

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A Krishna devotee sees his Beloved Lord abiding in all creatures; hence all actions performed by him turn into worship.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Though acting, is not tainted"—one established in non-dual Brahman vision, though acting empirically, is never bound due to awareness of non-doership.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis detailing how purity of mind, sense control, and vision of non-dual Self prevent karmic bonding.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: All activities of a Karma-Yogi who sees the universe filled with Vasudeva are pure divine service; hence he remains untainted.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the practitioner"s ego dissolves into Lord Krishna, all deeds executed through him become sinless and divine.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Self as the Self of all"—the yogi experiencing the single inner Self in all creatures remains untouched by karmic fruits while preserving social harmony.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
One who works in devotion, who is a pure soul, and who controls his mind and senses is dear to everyone, and everyone is dear to him. Though always working, such a person is never entangled.
