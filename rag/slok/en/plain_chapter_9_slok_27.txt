Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yatkaroṣi yadaśnāsi yajjuhoṣi dadāsi yat .
yattapasyasi kaunteya tatkuruṣva madarpaṇam

Word Meanings:
यत्करोषि - whatever you do
यदश्नासि - whatever you eat
यज्जुहोषि - whatever you offer in sacrifice
ददासि - you give
यत् - whatever
यत्तपस्यसि - whatever austerity you perform
कौन्तेय - O son of Kunti
तत्कुरुष्व - do that
मदर्पणम् - as an offering to Me

Life Application:
Whatever you do, eat, offer in sacrifice, give away, or practice as austerity—do it as an offering to God.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, whatever austerity you practice, O son of Kunti, do that as an offering unto Me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, whatever austerity you practice, O Kaunteya, do that as an offering unto Me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Whatever action you perform, whatever food you eat, whatever sacrifice you offer, whatever charity you give, and whatever austerity you practice, O son of Kunti, offer it all unto Me.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, whatever austerity you practice, O Kaunteya, do that as an offering unto Me.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, whatever austerity you practice, O Kaunteya, do that as an offering unto Me.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, whatever austerity you practice, O Kaunteya, do that as an offering unto Me.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, whatever austerity you practice, O Kaunteya, do that as an offering unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Whatever you do"—all actions, secular and sacred, must be performed solely for the pleasure of Lord Vishnu; this dedicated offering is the pinnacle of devotion.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Dedicating all secular and sacred activities (eating, sacrifices, charity, austerities) unto the Supreme Lord constitutes true Samnyasa-Yoga.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, whatever austerity you practice, O Kaunteya, do that as an offering unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Regarding the Supreme Lord as the sole agent, enjoyer, and object of worship in all natural worldly activities and scriptural rites, dedicate everything unto His lotus feet.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Whatever worldly or Vedic action, charity, austerity, or eating you perform, offer it all to the Supreme Lord; with mind attuned to Samnyasayoga, freed from good and evil karmic bonds, you shall attain the Supreme Lord Himself.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Whatever you do, eat, offer, or give"—natural worldly actions, eating, sacrifices, charity, and austerities—offer it all unto Me, Lord Vasudeva, surrendering the sense of agency.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Dedicating actions is the essence of Karma-Yoga; surrendering all deeds to Lord Vishnu as Master frees the soul completely from karmic taint.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Offer one"s self and all daily activities to God—dedicating eating, charity, and austerities to Lord Krishna constitutes the path of total self-surrender.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Offer that unto Me"—dedicating all worldly and Vedic actions without sense of doership to Lord Vasudeva is the essence of Sannyasa-Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away, whatever austerity you practice, O son of Kunti, do that as an offering unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the surrender of all actions; by offering all endeavors to God, the seeker becomes desireless and qualified for wisdom.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Maintaining the attitude of "offering unto Me" in all secular and sacred deeds shifts the responsibility to the Lord, leaving the soul unsullied.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Surrendering one"s entire life—eating, sacrifices, charity, austerities—to the Lord is the natural practice of unselfish devotion.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Offering all endeavors to Supreme Brahman constitutes true "Sannyasa", destroying egoism of agency and purifying the mind.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer or give away, and whatever austerities you perform—do that, O son of Kunti, as an offering to Me.
