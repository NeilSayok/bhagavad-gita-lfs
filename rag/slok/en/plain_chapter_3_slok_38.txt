Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
dhūmenāvriyate vahniryathādarśo malena ca .
yatholbenāvṛto garbhastathā tenedamāvṛtam

Word Meanings:
धूमेनाव्रियते - is enveloped by smoke
वह्निर्यथादर्शो - fire as a mirror
मलेन - by dust
च - and
यथोल्बेनावृतो - as enveloped by amnion
गर्भस्तथा - embryo, so
तेनेदमावृतम् - by that this is covered

Life Application:
Clear away accumulated cravings and distractions so your innate wisdom and clarity can shine through clearly.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Nescience and desire veil the true nature of the soul, which God-knowledge alone unveils.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Three degrees of veiling of wisdom (smoke-fire, dust-mirror, womb-embryo) are illustrated according to Sattvika, Rajasa, and Tamasa seekers.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The innate spiritual knowledge of the soul is covered by desire just as fire is veiled by smoke.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.37–3.39 jointly): Kama esa etc., Dhumena etc., and Avritam etc. Desire born of Rajoguna veils wisdom; it is the constant enemy of the wise, insatiable as fire.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "As fire is enveloped by smoke, as a mirror by dust, as an embryo by the womb, so is knowledge enveloped by this desire"—uses vivid natural analogies to illustrate the obscuring impact of desire on wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The three analogies illustrate varying intensities of knowledge-obscuration across Sattvika, Rajasa, and Tamasa minds.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: In a heart obscured by desire"s smoke, the light of Krishna-devotion shines when divine grace blows.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Just as a mirror shines when dirt is wiped away, Self-knowledge shines automatically when desire is discarded.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Complete destruction of the veil of desire is possible only through Vasudeva"s divine grace.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a soul surrenders his heart at Krishna"s feet, the veil of desire shatters instantly.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Desire acts as the constant veil over wisdom, bewildering the human soul.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
