Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śubhāśubhaphalairevaṃ mokṣyase karmabandhanaiḥ .
saṃnyāsayogayuktātmā vimukto māmupaiṣyasi

Word Meanings:
शुभाशुभफलैरेवं - thus from good and evil fruits
मोक्ष्यसे - you will be freed
कर्मबन्धनैः - from bonds of action
संन्यासयोगयुक्तात्मा - mind fixed on yoga of renunciation
विमुक्तो - liberated
मामुपैष्यसि - you shall attain Me

Life Application:
Free yourself from the bondage of good and bad action fruits through renunciation, attaining liberation and reaching God.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Thus you shall be freed from the bonds of actions, yielding good and evil fruits; with your mind steadfast in the Yoga of renunciation, being liberated, you shall come unto Me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Thus shall you be freed from the bonds of actions yielding good and evil fruits; with the mind steadfast in the Yoga of renunciation and liberated, you shall come unto Me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Thus, with mind attuned to the Yoga of renunciation through divine offering, you shall be liberated from the bonds of good and evil karma, and being free, you shall attain Me.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Thus shall you be freed from the bonds of actions yielding good and evil fruits; with the mind steadfast in the Yoga of renunciation, and liberated, you shall come unto Me.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Thus shall you be freed from bonds of action yielding good and evil fruits; steadfast in the Yoga of renunciation, liberated, you shall come to Me.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Thus shall you be freed from the bonds of actions yielding good and evil fruits; with the mind steadfast in the Yoga of renunciation, and liberated, you shall come unto Me.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Thus shall you be freed from the bonds of actions yielding good and evil fruits; with the mind steadfast in the Yoga of renunciation, and liberated, you shall come unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Dedicating all actions to God is Samnyasa-Yoga; this purifies the mind, freeing the soul from karmic bonds of virtue and vice to attain liberation.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Thus you shall be freed from the bonds of actions, yielding good and evil fruits; with your mind steadfast in the Yoga of renunciation, being liberated, you shall come unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Through Samnyasa-Yoga of divine offering, karmic bondage of favorable and unfavorable fruits vanishes; upon body"s release, the soul attains the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Whatever worldly or Vedic action, charity, austerity, or eating you perform, offer it all to the Supreme Lord; with mind attuned to Samnyasayoga, freed from good and evil karmic bonds, you shall attain the Supreme Lord Himself.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Thus you shall be freed from good and evil fruits"—by dedicating all deeds to God, you are liberated from the bonds of merit and sin. Dedication is renunciation (Sannyasa) and union (Yoga); attuned to this, you become liberated in life and attain Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Dedicating all acts is Samnyasa-Yoga; by its potency, all karmic consequences are dissolved, and the devotee attains the Lord"s eternal abode through divine grace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Through Samnyasa-Yoga of divine offering, the seeker"s ignorance and karmic bonds are destroyed, attaining liberation in life and Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Thus you shall be freed from the bonds of actions yielding good and evil fruits; with your mind steadfast in the Yoga of renunciation, being liberated, you shall come unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Demonstrating the supreme glory of Samnyasa-Yoga; offering actions leads to liberation, freeing the seeker from karma to merge into God"s true Self.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A seeker acting with the consciousness of "offering unto Me" breaks karmic bonds and through Samnyasa-Yoga attains eternal divine service.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Offering all deeds to the Lord dissolves karmic bondage, freeing the soul forever to enjoy divine bliss.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Surrendering deeds is "Samnyasa-Yoga", which burns away karmic bonds of virtue and sin to grant direct spiritual liberation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
In this way you will be freed from bonding to work and its auspicious and inauspicious results. With your mind fixed on Me in this principle of renunciation, you will be liberated and come to Me.
