Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
gatasaṅgasya muktasya jñānāvasthitacetasaḥ .
yajñāyācarataḥ karma samagraṃ pravilīyate

Word Meanings:
गतसङ्गस्य - of one devoid of attachment
मुक्तस्य - of the liberated
ज्ञानावस्थितचेतसः - whose mind is poised in wisdom
यज्ञायाचरतः - acting for sacrifice
कर्म - action
समग्रं - entirely
प्रविलीयते - dissolves

Life Application:
Perform your work as a selfless offering, allowing pure intentions to dissolve past regrets and future worries.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Of the liberated man who is unattached, whose mind is grounded in Knowledge, who acts for the sake of sacrifice, his whole karma dissolves away.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of the liberated soul who is completely devoid of attachment (Gatasangasya), whose mind is steadfastly established in Divine Knowledge (Jnanavasthitacetasah), and who performs work solely as a Yajna (offering to God)—his entire store of Karma dissolves completely without leaving a trace.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Action for sacrifice dissolves completely"—all selfless work performed for the worship of Lord Vishnu completely dissolves the soul"s cycle of birth and death.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: He whose mind is established in non-dual wisdom, acting solely for cosmic sacrifice, finds all his actions dissolving entirely without producing karmic fruits.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Of the liberated man who is unattached, whose mind is grounded in Knowledge, who acts for the sake of sacrifice, his whole karma dissolves away.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "His whole karma dissolves"—for a salvation-seeker fixed in Knowledge who works solely for divine worship (Yajna), all past and present karmas are uprooted.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Gatasangasya etc. For sacrifice (yajna), the action performed by a liberated person unattached and grounded in Knowledge dissolves away completely.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Of the liberated man who is unattached, whose mind is grounded in Knowledge, who acts for the sake of sacrifice, his whole karma dissolves away"—proclaims the total dissolution (*Praviliyate*) of all actions for a liberated soul acting for Yajna.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Action dissolved in the fire of Knowledge can never sprout into karmic fruits, enabling direct attainment of Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The entire web of karma of a loving devotee who acts selflessly for Krishna"s Yajna dissolves by the Lord"s grace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Mind grounded in Knowledge"—one whose consciousness is anchored in Sat-Chit-Ananda Atman performs altruistic work that vanishes without leaving karmic residues.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing how actions performed for sacrifice by an unattached, liberated, wise soul dissolve without trace.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: All past and future karmas of a wise soul working for Vasudeva"s Yajna dissolve, granting Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When every action of the seeker assumes the form of Krishna-Yajna, his karmic bondage terminates forever.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "His whole karma dissolves"—the seed-sprouting potency of actions performed by one grounded in Knowledge is destroyed.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The work of a man who is unattached to the modes of material nature and who is fully situated in transcendental knowledge merges entirely into transcendence.
