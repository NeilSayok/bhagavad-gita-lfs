Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
bahūnāṃ janmanāmante jñānavānmāṃ prapadyate .
vāsudevaḥ sarvamiti sa mahātmā sudurlabhaḥ

Word Meanings:
बहूनां - of many
जन्मनामन्ते - at the end of births
ज्ञानवान्मां - the wise person unto Me
प्रपद्यते - surrenders
वासुदेवः - Lord Vasudeva
सर्वमिति - is everything, thus
स - that
महात्मा - great soul
सुदुर्लभः - very rare

Life Application:
After many lives of learning, surrender to the truth that the divine presence is everything, a rare and great soul.

At the end of many births, the wise man surrenders unto Me, realizing that Vasudeva is all; such a great soul is very rare.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Vasudeva is All"—realizing that Lord Vishnu is the sole Master, Cause, and Refuge of all creation, the soul who surrenders to Him is a most rare Mahatma.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Vasudeva is all, such a Mahatma is very rare"—after many births of spiritual practice, when the direct realization dawns that "Vasudeva is everything", supreme Mukti is attained.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
At the end of many births, the wise man surrenders unto Me, realizing that Vasudeva is all; such a great soul is very rare.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: At the end of many births of pious deeds, the wise soul surrenders unto Me, realizing "Lord Vasudeva alone is my ultimate Sustainer and all-in-all"; such a great soul is exceedingly rare.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Four types of virtuous men worship Me: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise. Of these, the wise one who is ever steadfast in exclusive devotion excels; for I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and he is dear to Me. Noble indeed are all these, but the wise man I consider My very Self. At the end of many births, the wise man surrenders unto Me, realizing "Vasudeva is all"; such a great soul is exceedingly rare.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "At the end of many births, the man of wisdom surrenders unto Me, realizing "Vasudeva is All"; such a great soul is very rare"—culmination of all spiritual evolution is the non-dual realization that Lord Vasudeva is the sole Reality pervading everything.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Vasudeva is All" signifies the firm realization of the universe"s absolute dependence on Vishnu; such an exclusive Vaishnava Mahatma is rarest of all.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Beholding the direct manifestation of Lord Krishna in all existence is "Sarva-Bhava", through which the soul achieves supreme divine nourishment.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Vasudeva is All"—direct intuitive realization of all creation as Lord Vasudeva Himself is the ultimate summit of non-dual wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
At the end of many births, the wise man surrenders unto Me, realizing that Vasudeva is all; such a great soul is very rare.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural exposition of universal divine vision ("Vasudeva is All") and the extreme rarity of such a realized Mahatma.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Realizing that Lord Vasudeva is the sole Sustainer and all-in-all of the cosmos marks the rarest of great souls.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: "Everything is Lord Krishna"—the dawn of this universal divine consciousness is the highest climax of the path of devotion.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Vasudeva is All"—beholding Brahman Vasudeva in all effects and causes through non-different vision defines the supreme sage.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
After many births and deaths, he who is actually in knowledge surrenders unto Me, knowing Me to be the cause of all causes and all that is. Such a great soul is very rare.
