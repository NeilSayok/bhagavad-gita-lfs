Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
praśāntamanasaṃ hyenaṃ yoginaṃ sukhamuttamam .
upaiti śāntarajasaṃ brahmabhūtamakalmaṣam

Word Meanings:
प्रशान्तमनसं - of tranquil mind
ह्येनं - this indeed
योगिनं - to the yogi
सुखमुत्तमम् - supreme happiness
उपैति - comes
शान्तरजसं - whose passions are stilled
ब्रह्मभूतमकल्मषम् - become Brahman and sinless

Life Application:
Experience supreme peace as your mind grows tranquil, your passions settle, and your heart rests in purity.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Supreme bliss comes to this Yogi whose mind is completely quieted, whose passion is calmed, who has become Brahman, and who is free from sin.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Supreme bliss comes to this Yogi whose mind is perfectly quieted, whose Rajas is subdued, who is free from sin, and who has become one with Brahman.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
For supreme, unexcelled bliss spontaneously comes to this Yogi whose mind is perfectly quieted, whose passion (Rajas) is totally quieted, who is freed from all sin, and who has become one with Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Supreme bliss comes to this Yogi whose mind is completely quieted, whose passion is calmed, who has become Brahman, and who is free from sin.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Supreme bliss comes to this Yogi whose mind is completely quieted, whose passion is calmed, who has become Brahman, and who is free from sin.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Become Brahman"—attaining direct knowledge of Lord Vishnu"s supreme blissful form and reaching communion with Vishnu yields ultimate joy.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: With mind quieted, Rajas subdued, and ignorance destroyed, the practitioner becoming Brahman enjoys unexcelled non-dual bliss.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Supreme bliss comes to this Yogi whose mind is completely quieted, whose passion is calmed, who has become Brahman, and who is free from sin.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Whose mind is detached from non-Self objects and settled in the Self alone, freed from sin, he attains the supreme bliss of Self-realization.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Supreme bliss comes spontaneously to this Yogi whose mind is perfectly tranquil, whose Rajas is subdued, who is free from all sin, and who has attained oneness with Brahman. Thus constantly absorbing the Self in the Divine, the sinless Yogi easily enjoys the infinite joy of union with Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Supreme bliss comes to this Yogi whose mind is completely quieted, whose passion is calmed, who has become Brahman, and who is free from sin"—defines the state of Jivanmukti where one abides as Brahman (*Brahma-bhuta*), filled with unexcelled supreme joy.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Subduing Rajas and attaining sinlessness are essential prerequisites for attaining Brahma-bhava (divine communion).

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: When Rajas and Tamas are destroyed and the heart becomes pure, the supreme joy of Krishna-bliss manifests automatically.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Become Brahman"—total negation of nescience leading to automatic experience of essential non-dual bliss is supreme happiness.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Supreme bliss comes to this Yogi whose mind is perfectly quieted, whose Rajas is subdued, who is free from sin, and who has become one with Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of quiet mind, subdued passion, sinlessness, and becoming Brahman.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Freed from all sins and established in Lord Vasudeva, the yogi attains unexcelled bliss of Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the soul, freed from sins, realizes Lord Krishna, supreme divine bliss overflows.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Become Brahman"—the cessation of individual jiva-hood and manifestation of Sat-Chit-Ananda Brahman is supreme joy.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The yogi whose mind is fixed on Me verily attains the highest perfection of transcendental happiness. He is beyond the mode of passion, he realizes his qualitative identity with the Supreme, and thus he is freed from all reactions to past deeds.
