Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
kaccinnobhayavibhraṣṭaśchinnābhramiva naśyati .
apratiṣṭho mahābāho vimūḍho brahmaṇaḥ pathi

Word Meanings:
कच्चिन्नोभयविभ्रष्टश्छिन्नाभ्रमिव - fallen from both like torn cloud
नश्यति - perishes
अप्रतिष्ठो - without support
महाबाहो - O mightyarmed
विमूढो - deluded
ब्रह्मणः - of Brahman
पथि - on the path

Life Application:
Inquire whether a confused seeker who falls short in spiritual practice is destroyed like a scattered cloud.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Fallen from both, unsteadfast and bewildered in the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a rent cloud, O mighty-armed?

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Fallen from both, unsteadfast and bewildered on the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a broken cloud, O mighty-armed?

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O mighty-armed Krishna, fallen from both heaven-giving rituals and liberating Meditation-Yoga, unsupported and bewildered on the path to Supreme Brahman—does he not perish like a scattered, dissolved cloud in the sky?

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Fallen from both, unsteadfast and bewildered in the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a rent cloud, O mighty-armed?

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Fallen from both, unsteadfast and bewildered in the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a rent cloud, O mighty-armed?

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Fallen from both, unsteadfast and bewildered on the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a broken cloud, O mighty-armed?

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Fallen from both, unsteadfast and bewildered on the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a broken cloud, O mighty-armed?

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Perishes like a broken cloud"—Arjuna inquires if the fallen yogi suffers total destruction like a cloud dissolving in space.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Does the aspirant fallen from both ritual karma (heaven) and meditation (liberation) perish completely like a shattered cloud without support?

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Fallen from both, unsteadfast and bewildered in the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a rent cloud, O mighty-armed?

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Deprived of both ritual rewards (heaven) and Yoga fruition (Self-realization), does the aspirant perish like a scattered cloud?

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Arjuna said: He who possesses faith but lacks self-restraint, whose mind wanders away from Yoga, failing to reach perfection in Yoga—what end does he meet, O Krishna? Fallen from both karma and yoga, unsupported and bewildered on the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a rent cloud, O mighty-armed? You ought to dispel this doubt of mine completely, O Krishna, for there is none other than You who can destroy this doubt.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Fallen from both, unsteadfast and bewildered on the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a broken cloud, O mighty-armed?"—expounds Arjuna"s fear that failing in Yoga robs the seeker of both worldly ritual merit (*Karma-phala*) and spiritual liberation (*Moksha*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Fallen from both" means being deprived of both the heavenly realms gained by rituals and the Mukti gained by Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A soul trusting in Krishna"s devotion never perishes like a rent cloud, as the Lord will assure next.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Unsteady and bewildered in Brahman"s path"—does the unsupported practitioner on the path of Self-knowledge perish completely?

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Fallen from both, unsteadfast and bewildered on the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a broken cloud, O mighty-armed?

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of the fear of double failure (losing heaven and liberation) likened to a rent cloud.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A practitioner set on Vasudeva"s path cannot suffer absolute ruin; Arjuna seeks clarification on this doubt.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Arjuna asks whether a person engaged in Krishna-devotion could ever be left helpless and destroyed.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Perishes like a broken cloud"—being deprived of both ritual merit and spiritual knowledge is like dissolving like a rent cloud.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O mighty-armed Krishna, does not such a man, who is bewildered from the path of transcendence, fall away from both spiritual and material success, and perish like a riven cloud, with no position in any sphere?
