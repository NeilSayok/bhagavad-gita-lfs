Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
suhṛnmitrāryudāsīnamadhyasthadveṣyabandhuṣu .
sādhuṣvapi ca pāpeṣu samabuddhirviśiṣyate

Word Meanings:
सुहृन्मित्रार्युदासीनमध्यस्थद्वेष्यबन्धुषु - among benefactors, friends, enemies, neutrals, arbiters, haters and kin
साधुष्वपि - among the virtuous also
च - and
पापेषु - among sinners
समबुद्धिर्विशिष्यते - one with equal vision excels

Life Application:
Excel in spiritual maturity by maintaining equal goodwill toward well-wishers, friends, enemies, strangers, saints, and sinners.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
He who regards with equal mind well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
He who regards with equal vision well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
He who regards with equal mind well-wishers, companions, foes, neutral observers, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels above all.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
He who regards with equal mind well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
He who regards with equal mind well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
He who regards with equal vision well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
He who regards with equal vision well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "He of equal vision excels"—a seeker who perceives the indwelling Lord Vishnu equally in all living beings stands highest among sages.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The yogi of equal vision excels all because he views every human relationship—whether friend, enemy, saint, or sinner—through the undivided vision of the One Supreme Self.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
He who regards with equal mind well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: A seeker who beholds equal spiritual essence in benefactors, neutral observers, adversaries, saints, and sinners excels supremely.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: He who maintains an equal mind toward well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels supremely.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "He who regards with equal mind well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels"—extols the peak of equal vision (Samabuddhi) which transcends all social and moral dualities in non-dual Self-awareness.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Beholding Vishnu in all living beings is true Samabuddhi; seeing the Lord everywhere free from attachment and aversion is the height of Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The devotee who beholds Lord Krishna"s presence in all beings—whether saint or sinner—receives the Lord"s highest grace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "He of equal mind excels"—one who transcends all external adjuncts and beholds the undivided Consciousness everywhere is the supreme accomplished master.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: He who regards with equal vision well-wishers, friends, foes, neutrals, mediators, the hateful, relatives, saints, and sinners excels.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural analysis of the preeminence of equal vision toward all classes of human relationships.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The sage who realizes the identical spiritual nature in all living entities is the most exalted master.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Emulating Lord Krishna"s equal vision by seeing God in all creatures is true Vaishnava Dharma.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "He of equal mind excels"—beholding the identical Self indwelling within friends and foes alike is the highest knowledge.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
A person is considered still more advanced when he regards honest well-wishers, affectionate benefactors, the neutral, mediators, the envious, friends and enemies, the pious and the sinners all with an equal mind.
