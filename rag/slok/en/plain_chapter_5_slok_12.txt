Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yuktaḥ karmaphalaṃ tyaktvā śāntimāpnoti naiṣṭhikīm .
ayuktaḥ kāmakāreṇa phale sakto nibadhyate

Word Meanings:
युक्तः - the united one
कर्मफलं - fruit of action
त्यक्त्वा - having abandoned
शान्तिमाप्नोति - attains peace
नैष्ठिकीम् - supreme peace (born of steadfastness)
अयुक्तः - the un-united one
कामकारेण - impelled by desire
फले - in the fruit
सक्तो - attached
निबध्यते - is bound

Life Application:
Trade the chronic anxiety of desire-driven work for the lasting peace that comes from surrendering outcome expectations.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The united one (the well-poised), having abandoned the fruit of action, attains everlasting peace; the non-united one, impelled by desire, attached to fruit, is bound.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The united one (the well-poised), having abandoned the fruit of action, attains everlasting peace; the non-united one, impelled by desire, attached to fruit, is bound.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The steadfast Karma-yogi, having abandoned all attachment to the fruits of action, attains absolute everlasting Peace (Liberation); whereas the unintegrated person, impelled by selfish desires and attached to fruits, becomes deeply entangled and bound.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Attains everlasting peace"—a selfless devotee surrendering all actions to Lord Vishnu reaches the eternal peace of Vaikuntha.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The harmonious Karma-yogi, renouncing desire for results, attains steadfast peace (liberation born of Self-knowledge), while the un-integrated desire-driven person becomes bound.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: A practitioner performing God-dedicated work reaches everlasting peace of Self-realization, whereas one attached to fruits falls into bondage.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "The united one, having abandoned the fruit of action, attains everlasting peace; the non-united one, impelled by desire, attached to fruit, is bound"—contrasts the liberating peace of selfless action (*Naisthiki Shanti*) with the enslavement of fruitive desire (*Kamakara*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Attachment to rewards is the root cause of bondage, whereas dedication to God is the sole means to liberating peace.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A devotee acting with dedication to Krishna attains the supreme peace of eternal divine presence.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Attains everlasting peace"—purity of mind born of God-dedicated action bestows absolute Mukti through non-dual knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis contrasting the ultimate peace achieved by selfless practitioners with the bondage suffered by fruit-seeking individuals.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A seeker offering all fruits of work to Vasudeva stays forever free from worldly ties, enjoying supreme peace.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Action performed for Lord Krishna"s pleasure bestows eternal joy and fearless peace upon the seeker.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Attains everlasting peace"—dissolution of ignorance through devotion to Knowledge is the supreme Naisthiki Shanti.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The steadily devoted soul attains unadulterated peace because he offers the result of all activities to Me; whereas a person who is not in union with the Divine, who is greedy for the fruits of his labor, becomes entangled.
