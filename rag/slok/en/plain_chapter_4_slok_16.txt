Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
kiṃ karma kimakarmeti kavayo.apyatra mohitāḥ .
tatte karma pravakṣyāmi yajjñātvā mokṣyase.aśubhāt

Word Meanings:
किं - what
कर्म - action
किमकर्मेति - what is inaction, thus
कवयोऽप्यत्र - even the wise here
मोहिताः - are bewildered
तत्ते - that to you
कर्म - action
प्रवक्ष्यामि - I shall explain
यज्ज्ञात्वा - knowing which
मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् - you will be freed from evil

Life Application:
Study the subtle nature of work and motivation carefully before assuming you fully understand your obligations.
