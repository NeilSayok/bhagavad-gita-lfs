Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
uddharedātmanātmānaṃ nātmānamavasādayet .
ātmaiva hyātmano bandhurātmaiva ripurātmanaḥ

Word Meanings:
उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं - one should elevate oneself by self
नात्मानमवसादयेत् - one should not degrade oneself
आत्मैव - the self alone
ह्यात्मनो - indeed of oneself
बन्धुरात्मैव - self alone is friend
रिपुरात्मनः - enemy of oneself

Life Application:
Uplift yourself through your own mental discipline, refusing to degrade yourself, for you are your own friend or enemy.

Let a man lift himself by his own Self alone, let him not lower himself; for the Self alone is the friend of self, and the Self alone is the enemy of self.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "The Self is the friend of self"—the mind endowed with grace of Lord Vishnu is the true friend; the mind averse to God acts as the fatal enemy causing downfall.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: One must lift oneself from Samsara through one"s own discriminating mind; external entities are neither true friends nor foes, the Self alone is one"s friend or enemy.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Let a man lift himself by his own Self alone, let him not lower himself; for the Self alone is the friend of self, and the Self alone is the enemy of self.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Elevate the self through the mind detached from sense objects, do not degrade the self; for the disciplined mind is a friend and the undisciplined mind an enemy.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Let a man elevate himself by his own Self, let him not degrade himself; for the Self alone is the friend of oneself, and the Self alone is one"s enemy. The Self is the friend of him who has conquered himself by the Self; but for him who has not conquered himself, his own Self acts as an enemy in enmity.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Let a man lift himself by his own Self, let him not lower himself; for the Self alone is the friend of self, and the Self alone is the enemy of self"—stresses personal spiritual agency: the mind turned inward is one"s liberating friend, while the mind chasing sense-desires is one"s degrading enemy.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Personal effort and mental control are essential for liberation; a controlled mind is a friend, an uncontrolled mind a foe.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The mind attached to Krishna-remembrance is the soul"s ultimate friend, raising it from the world to the Lord"s abode.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Lift oneself by the Self"—detaching from non-Self phenomena and realizing one"s inner Self is true self-elevation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Let a man elevate himself by his own Self, let him not degrade himself; for the Self alone is the friend of self, and the Self alone is the enemy of self.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural analysis of personal spiritual freedom and the mind acting as friend or enemy.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Using the intelligence granted by Lord Vasudeva, control the mind to work for the soul"s eternal welfare.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Receiving Lord Krishna"s grace, the soul uses its inner discernment to cross over the ocean of worldly existence.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "The Self is the friend of self"—a pure controlled mind is the friend of Mukti, while an unrefined lustful mind is the enemy.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
One must deliver himself with the help of his mind, and not degrade himself. The mind is the friend of the conditioned soul, and his enemy as well.
