Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yathā dīpo nivātastho neṅgate sopamā smṛtā .
yogino yatacittasya yuñjato yogamātmanaḥ

Word Meanings:
यथा - as
दीपो - a lamp
निवातस्थो - in a windless place
नेङ्गते - does not flicker
सोपमा - that simile
स्मृता - is stated
योगिनो - of the yogi
यतचित्तस्य - of restrained mind
युञ्जतो - practicing
योगमात्मनः - yoga of the Self

Life Application:
Keep your mind steady like a candle flame in a windless place during your daily quiet contemplation.
