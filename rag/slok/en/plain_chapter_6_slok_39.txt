Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
etanme saṃśayaṃ kṛṣṇa chettumarhasyaśeṣataḥ .
tvadanyaḥ saṃśayasyāsya chettā na hyupapadyate

Word Meanings:
एतन्मे - this my
संशयं - doubt
कृष्ण - O Krishna
छेत्तुमर्हस्यशेषतः - You can dispel completely
त्वदन्यः - other than You
संशयस्यास्य - of this doubt
छेत्ता - dispeller
न - not
ह्युपपद्यते - indeed is found

Life Application:
Seek complete clarity from wise spiritual guidance to dissolve your deepest doubts about your life's journey.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Arjuna said: He who possesses faith but lacks self-restraint, whose mind wanders away from Yoga, failing to reach perfection in Yoga—what end does he meet, O Krishna? Fallen from both karma and yoga, unsupported and bewildered on the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a rent cloud, O mighty-armed? You ought to dispel this doubt of mine completely, O Krishna, for there is none other than You who can destroy this doubt.
