Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śucau deśe pratiṣṭhāpya sthiramāsanamātmanaḥ .
nātyucchritaṃ nātinīcaṃ cailājinakuśottaram

Word Meanings:
शुचौ - in a clean
देशे - in a fit place
प्रतिष्ठाप्य - having established
स्थिरमासनमात्मनः - a firm seat for oneself
नात्युच्छ्रितं - neither too high
नातिनीचं - nor too low
चैलाजिनकुशोत्तरम् - covered with cloth, skin and kusha grass

Life Application:
Arrange a clean, stable, and comfortable meditation seat in a quiet place free from unnecessary distractions.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Remaining in solitude, alone, with body and mind controlled, free from desire and possessions, let the Yogi constantly engage his mind in meditation. In a clean spot, establishing a firm seat neither too high nor too low, covered with Kusa grass, a deer skin, and a cloth, making the mind one-pointed and mastering thought and sense, let him practice Yoga for self-purification.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
To practice yoga, one should go to a secluded place and should lay kusa grass on the ground and then cover it with a deerskin and a soft cloth. The seat should be neither too high nor too low and should be situated in a sacred place.
