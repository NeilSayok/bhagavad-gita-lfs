Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yajjñātvā na punarmohamevaṃ yāsyasi pāṇḍava .
yena bhūtānyaśeṣāṇi drakṣyasyātmanyatho mayi

Word Meanings:
यज्ज्ञात्वा - knowing which
न - not
पुनर्मोहमेवं - delusion thus again
यास्यसि - will attain
पाण्डव - O Pandava
येन - by which
भूतान्यशेषेण - all beings without exception
द्रक्ष्यस्यात्मन्यथो - you will see in Self and then
मयि - in Me

Life Application:
See the underlying unity of all living beings in God, overcoming feelings of isolation and division.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Knowing which thou shalt not again fall into delusion, O Pandava; and by which thou shalt see all beings in thy Self, and also in Me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Knowing which thou shalt not again fall into delusion, O Pandava; and by which thou shalt see all beings in thy Self, and also in Me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Knowing that Supreme Wisdom, O descendant of Pandu, thou shalt never again fall into such delusion; for through that Knowledge, thou shalt behold all beings without exception in thy own Self, and consequently in Me.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Knowing which thou shalt not again fall into delusion, O Pandava; and by which thou shalt see all beings in thy Self, and also in Me.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Thou shalt see all beings in the Self and in Me"—purified by wisdom, the seeker directly beholds all living beings subject to and residing within Lord Vishnu, the Inmost Self.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Through Self-knowledge, ignorance-born distinction vanishes, revealing all beings as one in the Atman and Supreme Ishvara.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Knowing which thou shalt not again fall into delusion, O Pandava; and by which thou shalt see all beings in thy Self, and also in Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Upon Self-realization, all pure souls are perceived as equal in essential nature, abiding eternally as attributes (Vibhuti) within Me.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Knowing which thou shalt not again fall into delusion, O Pandava; and by which thou shalt see all beings without exception in thy Self and in Me.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Knowing which thou shalt not again fall into delusion, O Pandava; and by which thou shalt see all beings in thy Self, and also in Me"—reveals the absolute non-dual identity (*Atma-Ishvara-aikya*) that terminates all delusion forever.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Beholding Vishnu in all beings completely uproots all coverings of ignorance-born delusion.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: By the light of Krishna-wisdom, all delusion of duality vanishes, and the seeker experiences the entire cosmos as vibrant with Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Thou shalt see all beings in Self and Me"—with non-dual realization, the entire cause-and-effect universe shines as non-different from Atman and Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Knowing which thou shalt not again fall into delusion, O Pandava; and by which thou shalt see all beings in thy Self, and also in Me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing how non-dual wisdom ends delusion, revealing all creatures as non-different from Atman and Ishvara.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Upon receiving Vasudeva"s wisdom, the essential equality of all souls and their subservience to the Lord is directly intuited.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the practitioner receives Krishna-wisdom, he beholds his beloved Lord Krishna playing within all living beings.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Thou shalt see in Self and Me"—the identity of jiva and Ishvara realized through non-dual wisdom destroys all delusion.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Having obtained real knowledge from a self-realized soul, you will never fall again into such delusion, for by this knowledge you will see that all living beings are but part of the Supreme, or, in other words, that they are Mine.
