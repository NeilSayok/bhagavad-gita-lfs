Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
akṣaraṃ brahma paramaṃ svabhāvo.adhyātmamucyate .
bhūtabhāvodbhavakaro visargaḥ karmasaṃjñitaḥ

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
अक्षरं - the Imperishable
ब्रह्म - Brahman
परमं - the Supreme
स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते - essential nature is called Adhyatma
भूतभावोद्भवकरो - producing the existence of beings
विसर्गः - sacrificial creation
कर्मसंज्ञितः - is termed action

Life Application:
Know Brahman as the supreme imperishable entity, the Self as innate nature, and action as the creative force of life.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Blessed Lord said: Brahman is the Imperishable Supreme; Its essential nature is called Adhyatma; the offering which causes the origin and growth of beings is called Karma.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The Blessed Lord said: Brahman is the Supreme Imperishable; Self-nature is called Adhyatma; the creative force that causes the origin of beings is named Karma.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The Blessed Lord said: The Supreme Imperishable is Brahman; the essential Self in each embodiment is Adhyatma; and the sacrificial offering which brings forth the existence of all beings is known as Karma.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Imperishable Brahman Supreme"—Lord Sri Hari is the supreme indestructible Brahman; the soul"s eternal nature is Adhyatma, and the Lord"s cosmic creation act is Karma.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Imperishable Brahman Supreme"—the eternal all-pervading Brahman is supreme; Its inner Self-aspect is Adhyatma; and sacrificial offerings generating beings constitute Karma.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The Blessed Lord said: Brahman is the Supreme Imperishable; Self-nature is called Adhyatma; the offering causing the origin of beings is named Karma.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The liberated self unconditioned by space and time is Supreme Brahman; the individual soul"s essential nature is Adhyatma; and sacrificial procreative offerings constitute Karma.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: In response to Arjuna"s seven queries (Brahman, Adhyatma, Karma, Adhibhuta, Adhidaiva, Adhiyajna, and divine remembrance at death), the Lord explains that the Supreme Imperishable is Brahman, one"s essential nature is Adhyatma, and the creative offering that originates cosmic existences is Karma.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Brahman is the Supreme Imperishable"—the eternal, partless, indestructible Supreme Brahman (Vasudeva) is Brahman. "Self-nature is called Adhyatma"—that very Brahman manifested as the inner Self (Pratyagatman) in every body is Adhyatma. "The offering causing origin of beings is Karma"—the sacrificial oblations (visarga) prescribed by scriptures that bring forth the generation and growth of all creatures constitute Karma.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Supreme Ruler Lord Vishnu is the Supreme Imperishable Brahman; the soul"s divine spark nature is Adhyatma, and cosmic projection born of divine will is Karma.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Akshara Brahman is the supreme abode and form of Lord Krishna; the soul"s intrinsic divine portion is Adhyatma, and the divine play resolve is Karma.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Supreme Imperishable Brahman"—Lord Vasudeva beyond three gunas is Brahman; unconditioned Consciousness in body is Adhyatma; and sacrificial offering is Karma.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: The Blessed Lord said: Brahman is the Supreme Imperishable; Self-nature is called Adhyatma; the offering causing the origin of beings is known as Karma.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Delineating Brahman, Adhyatma, and Karma; establishing the eternity of Supreme Brahman and sacrificial oblation as the cause of creation.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The nature of the liberated soul is Supreme Brahman, discrimination between body and self is Adhyatma, and sacrificial offering is Karma.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The supreme imperishable essence is Brahman, pure spirit mode is Adhyatma, and the root of cosmic creation is Karma.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Unconditioned Brahman is Akshara Brahman, inner Self in body is Adhyatma, and sacrificial resolve generating creation is Karma.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: The indestructible, transcendental living entity is called Brahman, and his eternal nature is called adhyatma, the self. Action pertaining to the development of the material bodies of the living entities is called karma, or fruitive activities.
