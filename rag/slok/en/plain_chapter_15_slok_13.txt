Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
gāmāviśya ca bhūtāni dhārayāmyahamojasā .
puṣṇāmi cauṣadhīḥ sarvāḥ somo bhūtvā rasātmakaḥ

Life Application:
Entering earth, God sustains all beings by divine energy, and becoming sapful moon, nourishes all plants.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Entering the earth, I support all beings with My energy; and becoming the sapful moon, I nourish all herbs.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Permeating the earth I support all beings by (My) energy; and having become the watery moon I nourish all herbs.

The vast heaven and earth are firmly held by this energy. It permeates the earth to support the world. This energy is destitute of passion and attachment. As the vast earth is supported by the energy of the Lord? it does not fall? it is not broken down to pieces and it does not sink into the nether worlds.The Lord penetrates the earth and supports all movable and immovable objects by His energy.Rasatmakah somah The watery moon The moon is regarded as the repository of all savours or,fluids (Rasas). I? becoming the watery moon? nourish all herbs and plants such as rice? wheat? etc.? by infusing sap into them? and make them savoury. I feed the vegetable kingdom with My vital juice (Ojas) which pervades the soil and generates sweet juice or sap in herbs? plants and trees. The watery or savoury moon nourishes all herbs and plants by infusing sap or savours into them.Moreover --

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I enter this world and animate all My creatures with My vitality; and by My cool moonbeams I nourish the plants.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Permeating the earth I support all beings by My energy; and having become the watery moon I nourish all herbs.

Commentary: Swami Chinmayananda explains the immanence of the Supreme Lord as the cosmic force of support and nourishment. The gravity, thermal energy, and mineral wealth of the earth that support all life are manifestations of the Lord's vital force (ojas). Furthermore, the moon's light, which is scientifically known to influence plant growth and photosynthesis, is described as the sap-giving (rasatmaka) source of nourishment. By declaring Himself to be the earth's gravity and the moon's nourishing sap, the Lord shows that the entire ecosystem, from the grand movements of planets to the growth of a tiny blade of grass, is powered and sustained by the single Reality of Consciousness.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
And penetrating the earth I support [all] beings with [My] energy; being the sapful moon, I nourish all plants.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
And entering the earth I uphold all beings by My strength. I nourish all herbs, becoming the juicy Soma.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
And entering the earth I sustain the beings through (My) power; and nourish all the plants by becoming Soma [According to S. and most other translators, Soma means the moon.-Tr.] which is of the nature of sap.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhvacharya comments that the Lord enters the earth as the supreme controller to support all creatures, and becomes the deity of the moon to nourish the vegetation. Without His immanent power, the earth would disintegrate and no food could grow.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandgiri explains that the vast holding capacity of the earth and the nourishing qualities of the moon are expressions of Brahman's absolute power. This highlights that all ecological processes are driven by the divine rather than mere mechanical nature.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Permeating the earth I support all beings by My energy; and having become the watery moon I nourish all herbs.

Commentary: Swami Ramsukhdas states that the power of gravity and support seen in the earth belongs entirely to the Lord. Without His divine energy (ojas), the earth would crumble or sink. Similarly, the moon holds the nectarous vital juice (rasa) that nourishes all crops, grains, and vegetation, making them healthy and tasty for consumption. When humans eat this nourished food, they receive strength. Therefore, the entire chain of life—from the earth's support to the moon's nourishment—is a direct manifestation of God's love and grace.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Entering the earth I uphold all beings by My strength, namely, by My irresistible power, Likewise, becoming the Soma consisting of the juice of the nectar, I nourish all herbs.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Permeating the earth I support all beings by My energy; and having become the watery moon I nourish all herbs.

Commentary: Sri Abhinavagupta points out that the Lord's power of sustaining the world operates through the elements. By entering the earth, His energy holds everything stable, preventing it from sinking. By becoming the moon, He infuses the plants with sap and sweetness. Thus, the five elements in their macrocosmic and microcosmic roles are but the manifestation of the Lord's absolute sovereignty.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Ca, and; avisya, entering; gam, the earth; aham, I; dharayami, sustain; bhutani, the beings, the world; ojasa, through (My) power, the power that belongs to God and is free from passing and attachment, (and) which has penetrated the earth to support it, and owing to which the heavy earth does not fall and does not crumble. There is a similar mantra:
'By which the heaven is made mighty, and the earth firm' (Tai. Sam. 4.1.8.5), and also,
'He supported the earth' (op.cit., 4.1.8.3), etc.
Hence, it has rightly been said, 'Entering the earth I sustain the moving and non-moving beings.'
Moreover, pusnami, I nourish, I make healthy and full of the sweet flavour of juices; sarvah, all; osadhih, the plants-paddy, barley, etc.; bhutva, by becoming; somah, Soma; rasatmakah, which is of the nature of sap. Soma consists of all the juices; it is the source of all juices. Indeed, it nourishes all plants by infusing its own juice into everything.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha comments that the sustaining power of the earth is not its own inherent property, but rather a direct reflection of the Lord's supreme strength (Ojas) entering it. Similarly, the nourishing qualities of the moon are derived from the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya explains that the power of support in the earth and nourishment in the moon are portions of the Lord's divine attributes. The juicy Soma (moon) is the reservoir of all nectar that sustains the vegetation, which is a manifestation of the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati explains that the Lord, by entering the earth, stabilizes the soil so that it does not scatter like dust or sink into the ocean. By becoming the watery moon, which is the source of all savours, He infuses sap into paddy, barley, and other plants to make them nourishing and sweet.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami explains that the Lord supports all moving and unmoving beings by entering the earth through His strength. He also nourishes all vegetation by becoming the watery moon, which is the source of sweetness and nutrition.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanpati explains that the divine energy (Ojas) of God prevents the heavy earth from falling or breaking apart under the weight of oceans and mountains. Becoming Soma, the repository of all savours, the Lord infuses this sap into plants like paddy and barley, making them delicious and wholesome.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha explains that the Lord supports the universe with His irresistible power (Ojas) without which gravity would fail. By becoming Soma, He infuses plants with nectar-like sap, ensuring they possess nutrition and therapeutic qualities.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji explains that the Lord sustains the physical bodies of all creatures by entering the earth, and then nourishes them by becoming Soma, the source of juice in crops and grains. Thus, both the physical structure and the food that sustains it are manifestations of the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelkanth explains that the holding capacity of the earth and the nourishing, sweet character of the moon are expressions of the Lord's vital power. The sap-giving moon provides nutrients to all herbs, allowing living entities to live comfortably.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I enter into each planet, and by My energy they stay in orbit. I become the moon and thereby supply the juice of life to all vegetables.

 It is understood that all the planets are floating in the air only by the energy of the Lord. The Lord enters into every atom, every planet and every living being. That is discussed in the Brahma-saṁhitā. It is said there that one plenary portion of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Paramātmā, enters into the planets, the universe, the living entity, and even into the atom. So due to His entrance, everything is appropriately manifested. When the spirit soul is there, a living man can float on the water, but when the living spark is out of the body and the body is dead, the body sinks. Of course when it is decomposed it floats just like straw and other things, but as soon as the man is dead, he at once sinks in the water. Similarly, all these planets are floating in space, and this is due to the entrance of the supreme energy of the Supreme Personality of Godhead. His energy is sustaining each planet, just like a handful of dust. If someone holds a handful of dust, there is no possibility of the dust’s falling, but if one throws it in the air it will fall down. Similarly, these planets, which are floating in the air, are actually held in the fist of the universal form of the Supreme Lord. By His strength and energy, all moving and nonmoving things stay in their place. It is said in the Vedic hymns that because of the Supreme Personality of Godhead the sun is shining and the planets are steadily moving. Were it not for Him, all the planets would scatter, like dust in air, and perish. Similarly, it is due to the Supreme Personality of Godhead that the moon nourishes all vegetables. Due to the moon’s influence, the vegetables become delicious. Without the moonshine, the vegetables can neither grow nor taste succulent. Human society is working, living comfortably and enjoying food due to the supply from the Supreme Lord. Otherwise, mankind could not survive. The word rasātmakaḥ is very significant. Everything becomes palatable by the agency of the Supreme Lord through the influence of the moon.
