Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
māmupetya punarjanma duḥkhālayamaśāśvatam .
nāpnuvanti mahātmānaḥ saṃsiddhiṃ paramāṃ gatāḥ

Word Meanings:
मामुपेत्य - having attained Me
पुनर्जन्म - rebirth
दुःखालयमशाश्वतम् - abode of pain and temporary
नाप्नुवन्ति - never attain
महात्मानः - the great souls
संसिद्धिं - ultimate perfection
परमां - the highest
गताः - having reached

Life Application:
Attain the supreme perfection in God so you never again return to this temporary world of sorrow.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Having attained Me, these high-souled ones do not undergo rebirth, which is the home of sorrow and impermanent, for they have reached the Highest Perfection.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Having attained Me, these high-souled ones do not undergo rebirth, which is the home of sorrow and impermanent, for they have reached the Highest Perfection.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Attaining Me, rebirth is no more"—great souls who reach the supreme realm of Lord Sri Hari never fall back into the worldly cycle; this is the ultimate state of Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "They do not undergo rebirth, the home of sorrow"—the reward of God-realization is absolute; ascetics reaching the Lord never return to this transient sorrowful realm.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Having attained Me, these high-souled ones do not undergo rebirth, which is the home of sorrow and impermanent, for they have reached the Highest Perfection.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Having attained Me, they do not undergo rebirth"—great souls who reach Me, the Lord of all, never suffer rebirth in this temporary abode of grief, having gained supreme perfection of likeness to Me.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Having attained Me (the Lord), great souls do not undergo rebirth in this temporary abode of sorrow, for they have reached supreme perfection of liberation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Having attained Me, great souls do not undergo rebirth, the home of sorrow and transient"—upon reaching Me, the Supreme Lord, noble ascetics never suffer rebirth in this impermanent abode of sorrows, for they have attained the highest perfection of Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Non-return to worldly existence upon God-realization is the crown jewel of all perfections; devotees of Lord Vishnu enjoy eternal peace.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Eternal attainment of Lord Krishna"s lotus feet is the supreme perfection; divine devotees never return to this sorrowful world.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Reached the highest perfection"—direct Kaivalya Mukti is the supreme goal, attaining which the soul never re-enters the cycle of ignorance and rebirth.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Having attained Me, these high-souled ones do not undergo rebirth, which is the home of sorrow and impermanent, for they have reached the Highest Perfection.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural proof of non-rebirth upon God-attainment and the transient nature of the world.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Liberated souls attaining the supreme realm of Lord Vasudeva never take birth again in this sorrowful world.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: An exclusive devotee entering the eternal divine realm continuously experiences the blissful form of the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Home of sorrow and transient"—the world is an abode of grief and fleeting; a Brahman-realized sage is freed from it forever.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
After attaining Me, the great souls, who are yogis in devotion, never return to this temporary world, which is full of miseries, because they have attained the highest perfection.
