Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
karmaṇaiva hi saṃsiddhimāsthitā janakādayaḥ .
lokasaṃgrahamevāpi sampaśyankartumarhasi

Word Meanings:
कर्मणैव - by action alone
हि - attained perfection
संसिद्धिमास्थिता - Janaka and others
जनकादयः - welfare of the world also
लोकसंग्रहमेवापि - considering you should perform
सम्पश्यन्कर्तुमर्हसि - considering you should perform

Life Application:
Lead by constructive example in your workplace or family, acting selflessly for the benefit and guidance of all.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Janaka and others indeed attained Perfection by action alone; even with a view to the protection of the world, thou shouldst perform action.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Verily Janaka and other ancient royal sages (Janakadayah) attained Supreme Perfection through action alone (Karmanaiva hi); therefore, even with a view to the guidance and welfare of the world (Lokasangraham), thou shouldst perform thy duty.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Wise ones like King Janaka performed selfless duties to instruct society and maintain cosmic order.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: King Janaka and other enlightened rulers engaged in selfless action solely for Lokasangraha (cosmic and social guidance).

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Janaka and others indeed attained Perfection by action alone; even with a view to the protection of the world, thou shouldst perform action.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Janaka and other wise men performed work for world-welfare; hence you too must execute Kshatriya duty for Lokasangraha.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.20–3.21 jointly): Whatever a great man does, that other men also do; whatever standard he sets up, the world follows.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Janaka and others indeed attained Perfection by action alone; even with a view to the protection of the world, thou shouldst perform action"—presents King Janaka as the exemplar of Lokasangraha (world-welfare through duty).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: An enlightened Sage performs duty exclusively for Lokasangraha, never for personal desire.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Like Janaka and other great devotees, performing duties for Krishna"s delight and public welfare is the seeker"s duty.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Action is required for non-realized seekers for mind-purification, and for Sages for world-welfare.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Janaka and others indeed attained Perfection by action alone; even with a view to the protection of the world, thou shouldst perform action.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing how Janaka attained perfection by action and why Arjuna is obligated to act for Lokasangraha.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Following the ideal of royal sages like Janaka, perform work for world welfare.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a seeker acts for world welfare in Krishna"s divine creation, his work turns into direct devotion.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: An enlightened Sage performs unattached work to protect society from unrighteousness.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Kings such as Janaka attained perfection solely by performance of prescribed duties. Therefore, just for the sake of educating the people in general, you should perform your work.
