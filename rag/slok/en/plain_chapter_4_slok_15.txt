Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
evaṃ jñātvā kṛtaṃ karma pūrvairapi mumukṣubhiḥ .
kuru karmaiva tasmāttvaṃ pūrvaiḥ pūrvataraṃ kṛtam

Word Meanings:
एवं - thus
ज्ञात्वा - having known
कृतं - performed
कर्म - action
पूर्वैरपि - by ancients also
मुमुक्षुभिः - by seekers of liberation
कुरु - perform
कर्मैव - action indeed
तस्मात्त्वं - therefore you
पूर्वैः - by ancients
पूर्वतरं - in ancient times
कृतम् - performed

Life Application:
Follow the example of wise ancestors who fulfilled their worldly duties with inner detachment and devotion.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Having known this, action was performed by ancient seekers after freedom; therefore do thou perform action as was done by ancients in olden time.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Therefore perform action"—realizing God"s non-doership, all ancient seekers performed God-dedicated action; hence Arjuna should engage in desireless action.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Ancient seekers after liberation like Janaka, comprehending God"s non-doership, performed selfless duties for world-welfare; Arjuna must follow this lineage.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Therefore perform action"—ancient liberation-seeking kings like Janaka, possessing Self-knowledge, worked for world-welfare; so shouldst thou perform thy duty.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Having known this non-agency of the Lord, action was performed by ancient seekers after freedom; therefore do thou perform action as was done by ancients in olden time.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Having known this, action was performed by ancient seekers after freedom; therefore do thou perform action as was done by ancients in olden time"—cites the precedent of ancient liberated rulers like Janaka (*Purvaih purvataram kritam*) to urge Arjuna toward selfless action.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The conduct of ancient great sages serves as the authoritative beacon for a spiritual practitioner.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Ancient godly devotees achieved the supreme state by performing work dedicated to Krishna; Arjuna should follow that same loving Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Therefore perform action"—to achieve purity of mind for Knowledge, seeker Arjuna should practice Karma-yoga following the ancients.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing that ancient seekers, knowing God"s non-doership, performed duties, obligating Arjuna to follow suit.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Ancient Kings like Janaka achieved Mukti through selfless work accompanied by Self-knowledge; Arjuna must perform his duties likewise.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Following Krishna"s divine command to act like the ancient saints is the ultimate auspicious path for a devotee.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Therefore perform action"—for mental purification, performing selfless duties is the paramount discipline for a seeker.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
All the liberated souls in ancient times acted with this understanding of My transcendental nature. Therefore you should perform your duty, following in their footsteps.
