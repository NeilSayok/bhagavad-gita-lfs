Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānirbhavati bhārata .
abhyutthānamadharmasya tadātmānaṃ sṛjāmyaham

Word Meanings:
यदा - when
यदा - when
हि - certainly
धर्मस्य - of Dharma
ग्लानिर्भवति - is a decline
भारत - O descendant of Bharata
अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य - rise of unrighteousness
तदात्मानं - then Myself
सृजाम्यहम् - manifest I

Life Application:
Stand up for fairness and integrity when moral standards decline in your surrounding environment.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Whenever there is decay of righteousness, O Bharata, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Whenever there is decay of righteousness, O Bharata, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Whenever and wherever there is a decline in righteousness (Dharma) and a predominant rise of unrighteousness (Adharma), O descendant of Bharata, at that time I manifest Myself in bodily form.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Whenever there is decay of righteousness, O Bharata, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Whenever there is decay of righteousness, O Bharata, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Whenever there is decay of righteousness, O Bharata, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Whenever there is decay of righteousness, O Bharata, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Then I manifest Myself"—Lord Vishnu is eternally complete; "Srijami" denotes His divine manifestation, not material creation.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: For the protection of Dharma and dissolution of Adharma, the Supreme Lord manifests by His divine free will in every era.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Whenever there is decay of righteousness, O Bharata, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Whenever the cosmic order of Varna and Ashrama declines, the Lord descends in His divine auspicious form.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.5–4.9 jointly): Through these slokas the Lord proves that His birth and action are not karmic like ordinary beings, but a divine sport of Pure Consciousness; knowing this in truth, one attains liberation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Whenever there is decay of righteousness, O Bharata, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself"—expounds the cosmic imperative driving Divine Incarnation whenever Dharma declines.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The suppression of unrighteousness and re-establishment of Dharma are the supreme purposes of Divine Descent.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: When devotees" distress and unrighteousness peak, compassionate Krishna Himself appears to nourish Dharma.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Then I manifest Myself"—for the protection of holy souls and restoration of virtue, Ishvara voluntarily manifests His Maya-form.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Whenever there is decay of righteousness, O Bharata, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis verifying how the Supreme Lord manifests whenever Dharma declines and Adharma surges.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Vasudeva"s descent occurs at the decline of Dharma to console and protect the righteous.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Whenever Dharma wanes, Lord Krishna descends out of His boundless compassion to re-establish righteousness.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Then I manifest Myself"—for protecting Dharma, God assumes a divine form through His Yogamaya.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Whenever and wherever there is a decline in religious practice, O descendant of Bharata, and a predominant rise of irreligion – at that time I descend Myself.
