Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
jñānayajñena cāpyanye yajanto māmupāsate .
ekatvena pṛthaktvena bahudhā viśvatomukham

Word Meanings:
ज्ञानयज्ञेन - by sacrifice of knowledge
चाप्यन्ये - and others also
यजन्तो - worshiping
मामुपासते - worship Me
एकत्वेन - in oneness
पृथक्त्वेन - in multiplicity
बहुधा - in many ways
विश्वतोमुखम् - the universal form

Life Application:
Worship God through the sacrifice of knowledge, seeing the divine as one, as distinct, or as multifaceted.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Others also, sacrificing with the wisdom-sacrifice, worship Me as the One, as the Distinct, and as the Manifold, facing everywhere.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Others also sacrificing with the wisdom-sacrifice worship Me as the One, as the Distinct, and as the Manifold, facing everywhere.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Others worship Me through the sacrifice of knowledge, contemplating Me as the One non-dual Reality, as distinct, or as manifested in endless forms everywhere.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Others also sacrificing with the wisdom-sacrifice worship Me as the One, as the Distinct, and as the Manifold facing everywhere.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Others also sacrificing with the wisdom-sacrifice worship Me as the One, as the Distinct, and as the Manifold facing everywhere.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Others also sacrificing with the wisdom-sacrifice worship Me as the One, as the Distinct, and as the Manifold facing everywhere.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Others also sacrificing with the wisdom-sacrifice worship Me as the One, as the Distinct, and as the Manifold facing everywhere.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Seekers performing the sacrifice of knowledge worship the Lord as non-dual Self, as distinct manifestations, and as the manifold cosmic universe.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Others also sacrificing with the wisdom-sacrifice worship Me as the One, as the Distinct, and as the Manifold facing everywhere.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Knowledge-seekers, regarding their own soul as the body of God, worship Him as the One non-dual Supreme Lord, who is distinct yet qualified by all soul and matter, manifesting everywhere.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: 'Others worship Me through knowledge-sacrifice'—some worship through non-dual wisdom 'I am Brahman' (as One); others through distinct perspectives like Sun and Moon (as Distinct); and others as the cosmic embodiment facing all sides.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Realizing that Lord Narayana alone abides everywhere, enlightened seekers free from illusory monism worship the infinite all-pervading forms of Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: In the divine sacrifice of wisdom, seekers worship the Lord as the Supreme One, as distinct playful manifestations, and as the cosmic All-Facing Being.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Non-dual Self-knowledge is the supreme sacrifice of wisdom; seekers worship God as the One Self, through distinct manifestations, and as the All-Pervading Cosmic Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Others also, sacrificing with the wisdom-sacrifice, worship Me as the One, as the Distinct, and as the Manifold, facing everywhere.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Wisdom-sacrifice is threefold: non-dual vision, distinct worship, and cosmic form; through all three, the Supreme Lord alone is worshipped.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The sacrifice of knowledge is the mature stage of devotion, wherein seekers contemplate the Lord as having all creation as His body and as the internal Ruler facing all.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Devotees devoted to knowledge-sacrifice worship the Lord with love as One, as distinct, and in manifold forms.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Wisdom-sacrifice has diverse modes: unity through non-dual vision, distinction through symbol worship, and universal meditation on the Cosmic Lord.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Others, who engage in sacrifice by the cultivation of knowledge, worship the Supreme Lord as the one without a second, as diverse in many, and in the universal form.
