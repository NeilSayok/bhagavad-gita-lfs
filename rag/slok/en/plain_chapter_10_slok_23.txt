Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
rudrāṇāṃ śaṅkaraścāsmi vitteśo yakṣarakṣasām .
vasūnāṃ pāvakaścāsmi meruḥ śikhariṇāmaham

Word Meanings:
रुद्राणां - among Rudras
शङ्करश्चास्मि - I am Shankara and
वित्तेशो - Kubera (lord of wealth)
यक्षरक्षसाम् - among Yakshas and Rakshasas
वसूनां - among Vasus
पावकश्चास्मि - I am fire and
मेरुः - Mount Meru
शिखरिणामहम् - among mountains I am

Life Application:
Recognize God as Shiva among Rudras, Kubera among wealth-keepers, Agni among elements, and Meru among mountains.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
And among the Rudras I am Shankara; among the Yakshas and Rakshasas I am Kubera (Lord of wealth); among the Vasus I am Agni (Fire); among mountains I am Meru.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
And, among the Rudras I am Sankara; among the Yakshas and Rakshasas, Kubera; among the Vasus I am Pavaka (Fire); and among mountains I am Meru.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of all the Rudras I am Lord Shiva, of the Yakshas and Rakshasas I am Kubera, the Lord of wealth; of the Vasus I am Agni, and of mountains I am Meru.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among the eleven Rudras, the auspicious Shankara; among Yakshas and Rakshasas, Kubera; among eight Vasus, Agni (Fire); and among mountain peaks, Meru symbolizes the Lord's divine presence.

Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.
