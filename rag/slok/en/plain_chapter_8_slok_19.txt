Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
bhūtagrāmaḥ sa evāyaṃ bhūtvā bhūtvā pralīyate .
rātryāgame.avaśaḥ pārtha prabhavatyaharāgame

Word Meanings:
भूतग्रामः - host of beings
स - that
एवायं - very this
भूत्वा - being born
भूत्वा - repeatedly
प्रलीयते - dissolves
रात्र्यागमेऽवशः - at nightfall helplessly
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
प्रभवत्यहरागमे - comes into being at dawn

Life Application:
See how the multitude of beings repeatedly comes into existence and dissolves helplessly at the cosmic night.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
This same multitude of beings, arising again and again, dissolves helplessly at the coming of night, O Partha, and comes forth at the coming of day.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
This same multitude of beings, arising again and again, dissolves helplessly at the coming of night, O Partha, and comes forth at the coming of day.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
This very multitude of beings, helplessness subject to their own past karmas, is born again and again, dissolving at the arrival of night, O Partha, and re-emerging at the dawn of day.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
This same multitude of beings, arising again and again, dissolves helplessly at the coming of night, O Partha, and comes forth at the coming of day.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
This same multitude of beings, arising again and again, dissolves helplessly at the coming of night, O Partha, and comes forth at the coming of day.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
This same multitude of beings, arising again and again, dissolves helplessly at the coming of night, O Partha, and comes forth at the coming of day.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
This same multitude of beings, arising again and again, dissolves helplessly at the coming of night, O Partha, and comes forth at the coming of day.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: A single day of Brahma spans a thousand mahayugas, and his night is of equal duration. At cosmic dissolution, all worlds merge into Brahma"s unmanifest state and emerge again; yet Lord Vishnu"s supreme realm remains eternal beyond this cycle.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "This same multitude of beings repeatedly merges and emerges"—souls, bound helplessly by past actions and ignorance, dissolve and re-emerge to reap unspent karma.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
This same multitude of beings, arising again and again, dissolves helplessly at the coming of night, O Partha, and comes forth at the coming of day.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "This same multitude of beings repeatedly takes birth and dissolves"—souls helplessly bound by past karmic impressions emerge and dissolve again and again; hence worldly existence is filled with misery.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those who know Brahma"s day lasting a thousand yugas and night lasting a thousand yugas are truly knowers of cosmic time. At daybreak, all beings manifest from the unmanifest (Brahma"s subtle state); at nightfall, they dissolve back into it. Thus the multitude of beings, helpless under karma, repeatedly emerge and dissolve.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "This same multitude of beings, arising again and again, dissolves helplessly at nightfall and re-emerges at daybreak, O Partha"—to avoid unmerited experience, the very same souls bound helplessly by ignorance and karma repeatedly undergo creation and dissolution.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The duration of Brahma"s day and night reveals the limit of cosmic time; the cycle of creation and dissolution demonstrates the helplessness of souls, whereas the Lord"s realm is eternal and non-returning.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Souls bound by ignorance rotate helplessly in creation and dissolution; devotion to the Lord alone bestows liberation.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Helplessly re-emerges, O Partha"—bound by ignorance and karma, the soul repeatedly takes birth; without unselfish devotion, escape from this wheel is impossible.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: This same multitude of beings, arising again and again, dissolves helplessly at the coming of night, O Partha, and comes forth at the coming of day.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural explanation of the ceaseless birth-death chain of living beings under the bondage of ignorance.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Being helplessly driven by karma to emerge and dissolve repeatedly is the primary grief of worldly cycle.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Due to ignorance souls rotate in creation and dissolution cycles; liberation is possible only through divine grace.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "This same multitude repeatedly arising"—without exhaustion of karmic fruits, the soul is not eradicated but assumes new bodies.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Again and again, when Brahma"s day arrives, all living entities come into being, and with the arrival of Brahma"s night they are helplessly annihilated.
