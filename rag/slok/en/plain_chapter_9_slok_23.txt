Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ye.apyanyadevatābhaktā yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ .
te.api māmeva kaunteya yajantyavidhipūrvakam

Word Meanings:
येऽप्यन्यदेवता - who even other deities
भक्ता - devotees
यजन्ते - worship
श्रद्धयान्विताः - endowed with faith
तेऽपि - they also
मामेव - Me alone
कौन्तेय - O son of Kunti
यजन्त्यविधिपूर्वकम् - worship against rules

Life Application:
Understand that even those who worship other deities with faith are indirectly worshipping the one divine source.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Even those devotees who, endowed with faith, worship other gods, worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, by the wrong method.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Even those devotees who, endowed with faith, worship other gods, worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, by the wrong method.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Even those devotees who worship other demigods with faith worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, though they do so improperly out of ignorance.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Even those devotees who, endowed with faith, worship other gods, worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, by the wrong method.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Even those devotees who, endowed with faith, worship other gods, worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, by the wrong method.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Even those devotees who, endowed with faith, worship other gods, worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, by the wrong method.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Even those devotees who, endowed with faith, worship other gods, worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, by the wrong method.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Improperly"—worship performed without realizing Lord Vishnu who indwells demigods as their Inner Ruler is termed improper, yielding only transient realms.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Worship of demigods like Indra ultimately reaches the Supreme Lord, yet due to ignorance of the Lord within all gods, such worship is deemed improper.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Even those devotees who, endowed with faith, worship other gods, worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, by the wrong method.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: All demigods constitute the divine body of the Supreme Lord; worshipping them without knowing them as God"s body is improper worship.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Even those who worship demigods with faith worship Me alone ignorantly; failing to realize My supreme reality through improper knowledge, they go to demigods, ancestors, and spirits, whereas My exclusive devotees attain Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Even devotees of other gods worshipping with faith"—O son of Kunti, those who worship demigods with faith actually worship Me alone, but "improperly" (without knowledge that I am the sole Supreme Lord and dispenser of salvation).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Worshippers of demigods act "improperly" due to ignorance of Vishnu"s supreme supremacy; knowledge of the Inner Lord Hari alone makes worship proper.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Devotees of demigods, out of ignorance, worship limbs of the Divine without comprehending Lord Krishna"s supreme personal Form.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Improperly"—worship of other gods performed in ignorance of the Supreme Lord who is the Self of all deities leads to repeated births.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Even those devotees who, endowed with faith, worship other gods, worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, by the wrong method.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the divine essence within all gods: worship without non-dual realization remains improper and yields minor fruits.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: All deities are the body of God; hence worshipping deities independently is improper and fails to grant salvation.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Ignorant souls unknowingly worship Me even when worshipping other gods, yet fail to gain full fruits due to improper method.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Ignorance of the Supreme Lord who abides in all demigods constitutes "improper" worship, keeping the seeker bound to celestial realms.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those who are devotees of other gods and who worship them with faith actually worship Me alone, O son of Kunti, but they do so in a wrong way.
