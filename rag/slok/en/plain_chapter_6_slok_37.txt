Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
arjuna uvāca .
ayatiḥ śraddhayopeto yogāccalitamānasaḥ .
aprāpya yogasaṃsiddhiṃ kāṃ gatiṃ kṛṣṇa gacchati

Word Meanings:
अर्जुन - O Arjuna
उवाच - said
अयतिः - the unpersevering
श्रद्धयोपेतो - possessed of faith
योगाच्चलितमानसः - whose mind strayed from yoga
अप्राप्य - not attaining
योगसंसिद्धिं - perfection in yoga
कां - which
गतिं - destination
कृष्ण - O Krishna
गच्छति - attains

Life Application:
Ask with trust about the fate of an earnest seeker who strays from practice due to an un-mastered mind.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Arjuna said: He who, endowed with faith, but unable to control himself, whose mind wanders away from Yoga, failing to attain perfection in Yoga, what end does he meet, O Krishna?

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Arjuna said: He who is endowed with faith, but unable to control himself, whose mind wanders away from Yoga, failing to attain perfection in Yoga, what end does he meet, O Krishna?

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Arjuna said: O Krishna, he who possesses genuine faith in the scriptures and the path of Yoga, but owing to lack of self-restraint lets his mind wander away from Yoga at the time of death, failing to reach the perfection of Self-realization—what destiny does such a practitioner meet?

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "What end does he meet, O Krishna"—does the seeker who is endowed with devotion but fails to complete Yoga owing to mind"s restlessness face ruin?

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Arjuna inquires into the future realm and destiny of a fallen yogi who began meditation with faith but failed to reach perfection due to mental restlessness.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Arjuna said: He who, endowed with faith, but unable to control himself, whose mind wanders away from Yoga, failing to attain perfection in Yoga, what end does he meet, O Krishna?

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: He who has faith in Yoga, but owing to lack of sufficient effort or mental flickering strays from Yoga and misses perfection—what becomes of him?

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Arjuna said: He who possesses faith but lacks self-restraint, whose mind wanders away from Yoga, failing to reach perfection in Yoga—what end does he meet, O Krishna? Fallen from both karma and yoga, unsupported and bewildered on the path of Brahman, does he not perish like a rent cloud, O mighty-armed? You ought to dispel this doubt of mine completely, O Krishna, for there is none other than You who can destroy this doubt.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Arjuna said: He who is endowed with faith, but unable to control himself, whose mind wanders away from Yoga, failing to attain perfection in Yoga, what end does he meet, O Krishna?"—introduces the fundamental question of *Yoga-bhrashta* (one who stumbles on the path of Yoga).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Arjuna"s inquiry aims to dispel forever the fear and doubt lurking in the minds of spiritual aspirants.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Arjuna asks about the fate of one who has faith in Krishna"s feet but fails to bring his practice to completion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Failing to attain Yoga perfection"—does the faithful fallen yogi get deprived of both ritual rewards and Mukti?

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of faith-endowed aspirants who stray from meditation before attaining ultimate perfection.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Arjuna"s inquiry regarding the ultimate destination of a faithful but restless practitioner of Vasudeva-meditation.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Arjuna asks whether the spiritual efforts of a Krishna devotee whose mind faltered at death go in vain.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Failing to attain perfection in Yoga"—Arjuna presents the apprehension whether any harm comes to a faithful fallen yogi.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Arjuna said: O Krishna, what is the fate of the unsuccessful transcendentalist, who begins the process of self-realization with faith but who later desists due to worldly-mindedness and thus does not attain perfection in mysticism?
