Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
tasmāttvamindriyāṇyādau niyamya bharatarṣabha .
pāpmānaṃ prajahi hyenaṃ jñānavijñānanāśanam

Word Meanings:
तस्मात्त्वमिन्द्रियाण्यादौ - therefore you senses first
नियम्य - controlling
भरतर्षभ - O best of Bharatas
पाप्मानं - the sinful
प्रजहि - destroy
ह्येनं - this indeed
ज्ञानविज्ञाननाशनम् - destroyer of wisdom and realization

Life Application:
Practice sensory discipline first when temptation strikes, protecting your clarity and capacity for deeper self-understanding.
