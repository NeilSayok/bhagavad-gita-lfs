Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī niyatamānasaḥ .
śāntiṃ nirvāṇaparamāṃ matsaṃsthāmadhigacchati

Word Meanings:
युञ्जन्नेवं - thus practicing yoga
सदात्मानं - constantly the self
योगी - yogi
नियतमानसः - with controlled mind
शान्तिं - peace
निर्वाणपरमां - culminating in liberation
मत्संस्थामधिगच्छति - attains peace abiding in Me

Life Application:
Keep your mind steadily disciplined through regular practice to experience supreme peace and spiritual liberation.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Thus constantly keeping the mind absorbed in the Self, the Yogi of controlled mind attains the peace that culminates in Liberation, which abides in Me.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Thus constantly keeping the mind absorbed in the Supreme Self, the Yogi of controlled mind attains the supreme peace (Liberation) that abides in Me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Thus constantly keeping his mind controlled and absorbed in divine contemplation of Me, the Yogi attains that supreme peace of Liberation which abides in Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Attains peace abiding in Me"—the yogi whose mind is constantly absorbed in Lord Vishnu attains Vishnu"s supreme eternal Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Through continuous divine practice, the self-controlled yogi attains the supreme peace of Mukti rooted in Lord Vasudeva"s eternal essence.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Thus constantly keeping the mind absorbed in the Self, the Yogi of controlled mind attains the peace that culminates in Liberation, which abides in Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Thus practicing constant meditation on the Self, the self-controlled yogi reaches the supreme peace of Mukti abiding in Me.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Thus constantly concentrating his mind, the self-controlled yogi attains the supreme peace of Nirvana abiding in Me.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Thus constantly keeping the mind absorbed in the Self, the Yogi of controlled mind attains the peace that culminates in Liberation, which abides in Me"—summarizes the fruit of Dhyana-Yoga as ultimate Mukti (Nirvana-parama Shanti) inherent in Brahman Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Peace culminating in Nirvana means absolute destruction of ignorance and reaching the supreme state of Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Steadying the mind at Krishna"s feet, the devotee enters the Lord"s eternal supernatural sports and attains supreme peace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Peace culminating in Nirvana"—through unbroken meditation, ignorance is negated, yielding eternal non-dual Kaivalya.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of the self-governed yogi attaining supreme peace and abiding in the Lord.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Through constant meditation on Lord Vasudeva, the yogi is released from worldly bondage and reaches the Eternal Abode.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the mind merges into Lord Krishna, the soul attains eternal divine peace and enters the Lord"s abode.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Abiding in Me"—abiding in unbroken Brahman Consciousness is the ultimate peace of Nirvana, perfected by meditation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Thus practicing constant control of the body, mind and activities, the mystic transcendentalist, his mind regulated, attains to the kingdom of God [or the abode of Krishna] by cessation of material existence.
