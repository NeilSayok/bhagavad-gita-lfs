Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
evaṃ paramparāprāptamimaṃ rājarṣayo viduḥ .
sa kāleneha mahatā yogo naṣṭaḥ parantapa

Word Meanings:
एवं - thus
परम्पराप्राप्तमिमं - received in succession this
राजर्षयो - royal sages
विदुः - knew
स - he
कालेनेह - by long lapse of time here
महता - by great
योगो - Yoga
नष्टः - lost
परन्तप - O scorcher of foes

Life Application:
Preserve essential values and spiritual insights in your community before neglect causes them to fade away.

This handed down thus in regular succession, the royal sages knew. This Yoga by long lapse of time has been lost here, O Burner of foes (Arjuna).

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
This Yoga, thus handed down in regular disciplic succession, was known to the ancient royal sages (Rajarshis); but by the long lapse of time, O Arjuna, that great wisdom was lost to the world.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "By long lapse of time this Yoga was lost"—with the rise of unrighteousness and ignorance, this path had faded, which the Supreme Lord illuminates anew.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Over time, owing to the dominance of desire-driven people, this Yoga became hidden, prompting the Supreme Lord to incarnate and restore it.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
This handed down thus in regular succession, the royal sages knew. This Yoga by long lapse of time has been lost here, O Burner of foes (Arjuna).

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Royal sages (rulers protecting the people) preserved this path; over long ages, due to dull-witted individuals, this supreme Yoga had vanished.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.2–4.3 jointly): Through these slokas the Lord shows that this selfless Yoga is ancient and eternal; because Arjuna is His devotee and friend, this supreme secret is declared today.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "This handed down thus in regular succession, the royal sages knew; this Yoga by long lapse of time has been lost here, O Burner of foes"—explains that unbridled desires break the traditional chain of wisdom (*Sampradaya*), causing spiritual decay.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: When line of disciplic succession breaks, spiritual knowledge perishes; hence the Supreme Lord Himself revives the authentic line.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: When devotional Yoga becomes obscured by the defects of time, Lord Krishna re-manifests it for His beloved friend Arjuna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "The Yoga was lost, O Parantapa"—due to sense-bound uncontrolled people breaking the parampara, this liberating Yoga was lost.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
This handed down thus in regular succession, the royal sages knew. This Yoga by long lapse of time has been lost here, O Burner of foes.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing how the parampara-led Yoga was lost due to the rise of uncontrolled individuals over long ages.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: When this ancient path revealed by Vasudeva faded from the world, the Lord re-instructed it to Arjuna.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Out of causeless mercy for His friend Arjuna, Lord Krishna revived this lost science of Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "The Yoga was lost, O Parantapa"—due to absence of qualified recipients, this secret Yoga-path became obscured.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
This supreme science was thus received through the chain of disciplic succession, and the saintly kings understood it in that way. But in course of time the succession was broken, and the science as it is appears to be lost.
