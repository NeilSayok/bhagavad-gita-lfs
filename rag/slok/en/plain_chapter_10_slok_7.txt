Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
etāṃ vibhūtiṃ yogaṃ ca mama yo vetti tattvataḥ .
so.avikampena yogena yujyate nātra saṃśayaḥ

Word Meanings:
एतां - this
विभूतिं - divine glory
योगं - yoga path
च - and
मम - my
यो - he who
वेत्ति - knows
तत्त्वतः - in truth
सोऽविकम्पेन - he with unwavering
योगेन - by Yoga
युज्यते - is used.
नात्र - not here
संशयः - doubt.

Life Application:
He who knows in truth this divine glory and power becomes established in unwavering yoga without a doubt.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
He who in truth knows this Vibhuti (manifested opulence) and Yoga (power) of Mine becomes established in unwavering Yoga; of this there is no doubt.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
He who in truth knows these manifold manifestations of My Being and (this) Yoga-power of Mine becomes established in unwavering Yoga; there is no doubt about it.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
He who rightly understands My manifested glory and My Creative Power, beyond doubt attains perfect peace.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
One who in truth knows My Vibhuti (extending opulences) and Yoga (creative power) becomes established in unwavering Yoga. To know 'in truth' (tattvatah) means not mere intellectual conviction, but direct subjective realization.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
He, who knows correctly this extensively manifesting power and the Yogic power of Mine—he is endowed with the unwavering Yoga. There is no doubt here.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
He who in truth knows this supernal manifestation and splendour of auspicious attributes of Mine, becomes united with unwavering Yoga; of this there is no doubt.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
One who knows truly this majesty and yoga of Mine, he becomes imbued with unwavering Yoga. There is no doubt about this.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Explaining the fruit of realizing the Lord's manifestations. One who fully comprehends the Lord's Vibhuti (expressed in the twenty mental traits and sages) and His divine Yoga power achieves the unwavering Yoga of true knowledge.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The person who knows in truth (firmly accepts) My Vibhuti (cosmic glory) and My Yoga (divine power) becomes endowed with unwavering unalloyed devotion; of this there is no doubt.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Vibhuti" signifies divine sovereignty (everything being subject to God) and "Yoga" denotes His realm of auspicious attributes. Whoever knows in truth this divine sovereignty and His freedom from all flaws attains unwavering Bhakti-yoga.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The great sages, Kumaras, and Manus govern the cosmic order solely through the will and power of the Supreme Lord. Enlightening one another regarding the Lord's divine glory, these enlightened souls abide in supreme bliss.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Vibhuti" means cosmic extension, and "Yoga" means the Lord's omniscience and power. He who knows this Vibhuti and Yoga of Mine in truth becomes endowed with unwavering Yoga, characterized by steadfast right knowledge. There is no doubt in this.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: He who understands in truth the Lord's infinite divine sovereignty and yoga-majesty becomes firmly united with the Yoga of unwavering devotion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: One who truly realizes the Lord's Vibhuti (manifested in intellect, sages, etc.) and His Yoga (omniscience and power) becomes blessed with "unwavering" Yoga, characterized by firm right vision free from doubt and error.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: He who knows in truth My Vibhuti (manifested in Bhrigu, etc.) and My Yoga of divine sovereignty becomes endowed with unwavering right knowledge; of this there is no doubt.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: True knowledge of the Lord's extensive opulences and Yogic power firmly establishes the seeker in unwavering, doubt-free Self-realization.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Realizing the Lord's Vibhuti (all entities being subject to Him) and His Yoga of auspicious attributes acts as the primary catalyst for Bhakti-yoga, leading the seeker to steadfast devotion.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: One who in truth knows My Vibhuti (manifested in Bhrigu, etc.) and My divine sport (Yoga) becomes endowed with unwavering devotional service.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: He who knows in truth the Lord's all-pervading Vibhuti and the Yoga of mental concentration becomes established in unwavering meditation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
One who is factually convinced of this opulence and mystic power of Mine engages in unalloyed devotional service; of this there is no doubt.
