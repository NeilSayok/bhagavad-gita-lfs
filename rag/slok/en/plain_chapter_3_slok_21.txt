Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yadyadācarati śreṣṭhastattadevetaro janaḥ .
sa yatpramāṇaṃ kurute lokastadanuvartate

Word Meanings:
यद्यदाचरति - whatever he practices
श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो - the great man, that alone the other
जनः - people
स - he
यत्प्रमाणं - whatever standard
कुरुते - sets
लोकस्तदनुवर्तते - the world follows that

Life Application:
Set a high moral standard in your daily behavior, knowing that those around you look to your example.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Whatever a great man does, that other men also do; whatever standard he sets up, that the world follows.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Whatever a great man does, that other men also do; whatever standard he sets up, that the world follows.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Whatever a great man (Shreshthah) does, that very thing other ordinary men also imitate; whatever standard or ideal (Pramanam) he establishes, the world follows after that (Lokas tad anuvartate).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The conduct of exalted devotees serves as the sole authoritative ideal of virtue and devotion for society.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The conduct of a noble leader serves as the ideal for common people; hence the enlightened must perform righteous duties unattached.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Society accepts the conduct of leaders as authoritative standard; hence exemplary selfless action by great men is essential.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Whatever a great man does, that other men also do; whatever standard he sets up, the world follows.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Whatever a great man does, that other men also do; whatever standard he sets up, that the world follows"—stresses the social responsibility of leaders and Sages to guide mankind through exemplary conduct.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The conduct of great souls forms the touchstone of virtue and duty for ordinary people.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The exemplary devotional service of exalted Krishna-acharyas presents the liberating path for all society.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: It is the duty of societal leaders and enlightened Sages to protect Dharma through exemplary personal action.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Whatever a great man does, that other men also do; whatever standard he sets up, that the world follows.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis substantiating why enlightened leaders must act to set an authentic standard for general society.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The selfless conduct of exalted souls devoted to Vasudeva is the supreme guiding light for all mankind.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When exalted devotees act for Krishna"s joy, the entire world is inspired to tread the path of divine love.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Whatever standard he sets up"—the living conduct of great souls reveals the practical essence of scriptural truth.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Whatever action a great man performs, common men follow. And whatever standards he sets by exemplary acts, all the world pursues.
