Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
avyakto.akṣara ityuktastamāhuḥ paramāṃ gatim .
yaṃ prāpya na nivartante taddhāma paramaṃ mama

Word Meanings:
अव्यक्तोऽक्षर - unmanifest and imperishable
इत्युक्तस्तमाहुः - thus said Him they call
परमां - the supreme
गतिम् - destination
यं - which
प्राप्य - having attained
न - not
निवर्तन्ते - do they return
तद्धाम - that abode
परमं - supreme
मम - my

Life Application:
Make that imperishable unmanifest reality your supreme goal, from which those who reach it never return.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
That which is called the Unmanifested and the Imperishable is declared to be the Supreme Goal; reaching which they return not—that is My Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
That which is called the Unmanifested and the Imperishable is declared to be the Supreme Goal; reaching which they return not—that is My Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
That Unmanifested Reality which is termed the Imperishable is declared by the scriptures to be the Supreme Goal; having attained which souls never return—that is My Supreme Divine Abode.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
That which is called the Unmanifested and the Imperishable is declared to be the Supreme Goal; reaching which they return not—that is My Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
That which is called the Unmanifested and the Imperishable is declared to be the Supreme Goal; reaching which they return not—that is My Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Superior even to Prakriti and Brahma is Eternal Unmanifest Lord Vasudeva; even during universal dissolution, His supreme abode remains imperishable, which is the ultimate goal of all souls.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Unmanifest is called Imperishable"—the Eternal Unmanifest is designated as Akshara in Vedanta, attaining which is the supreme Mukti and Lord Vasudeva"s true nature.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
That which is called the Unmanifested and the Imperishable is declared to be the Supreme Goal; reaching which they return not—that is My Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The eternal unmanifest spirit beyond matter called Akshara is the Supreme Goal of seekers; souls reaching that supreme realm never return to samsara.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Superior to that unmanifest is the Eternal Unmanifest Supreme Lord, who perishes not even when all beings perish. That is declared as the Imperishable and Supreme Goal, attaining which souls return not; that Supreme Person is reachable through exclusive devotion.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "That Unmanifest is called Imperishable; It is declared as Supreme Goal; reaching It they return not—that is My Supreme Abode"—the Eternal Unmanifest denoted by Akshara is the highest goal, attaining which none returns to samsara; that is Lord Vishnu"s supreme nature.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Distinct from that is another eternal state"—Lord Vishnu, distinct from cosmic unmanifest cause, is called Unmanifest and Imperishable, whose supreme abode is eternal Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Akshara Brahman is Lord Krishna"s supreme abode; attaining It is the all-in-all of an exclusive soul.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "That is My Supreme Abode"—Akshara Brahman is Lord Vasudeva"s absolute realm, merging into which the enlightened devotee reaches eternal Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: That which is called the Unmanifested and the Imperishable is declared to be the Supreme Goal; reaching which they return not—that is My Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural delineation of Akshara Brahman as the Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The supreme Akshara state is the Lord"s abode, attaining which the soul never re-enters the sorrowful world.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Akshara Brahman is Lord Krishna"s supreme abode; attaining It is the all-in-all of an exclusive soul.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Reaching which they return not"—attaining Lord Vasudeva is absolute Mukti from which return is impossible.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
That which the Vedantists describe as unmanifest and infallible, that which is known as the supreme destination, that place from which, having attained it, one never returns—that is My supreme abode.
