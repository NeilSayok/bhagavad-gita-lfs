Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yuktāhāravihārasya yuktaceṣṭasya karmasu .
yuktasvapnāvabodhasya yogo bhavati duḥkhahā

Word Meanings:
युक्ताहारविहारस्य - moderate in food and recreation
युक्तचेष्टस्य - moderate in effort
कर्मसु - in actions
युक्तस्वप्नावबोधस्य - regulated in sleep and waking
योगो - Yoga
भवति - accrues
दुःखहा - destroyer of sorrow

Life Application:
Regulate your food, recreation, effort in work, and sleep schedule to eliminate physical and mental suffering.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Yoga becomes the destroyer of pain for him who is moderate in eating and recreation, who is moderate in exertion in actions, and who is moderate in sleep and wakefulness.
