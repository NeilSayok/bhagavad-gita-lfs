Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
idaṃ tu te guhyatamaṃ pravakṣyāmyanasūyave .
jñānaṃ vijñānasahitaṃ yajjñātvā mokṣyase.aśubhāt

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
इदं - this
तु - indeed
ते - to you
गुह्यतमं - most secret
प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे - I shall declare to the non-envious
ज्ञानं - knowledge
विज्ञानसहितं - with realization
यज्ज्ञात्वा - knowing which
मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् - you will be freed from evil

Life Application:
Receive this profound secret knowledge with a non-carping mind, releasing yourself from all worldly evil.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Supreme Lord said: To you who are free from envy, I shall now reveal this most profound secret knowledge along with direct realization, knowing which you shall be liberated from all inauspiciousness and bondage.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The Blessed Lord said: To thee who dost not cavil, I shall now declare this greatest secret, knowledge combined with realization, by knowing which thou shalt be freed from evil.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The Lord said: To you who are free from malice and envy, I shall impart this supreme secret of spiritual wisdom with direct experience, by which you will be freed from all worldly distress.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Lord Krishna states: O Arjuna, since you are a non-envious seeker, I reveal to you this most secret wisdom together with intuitive realization, which will free you from worldly bondage.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
The Blessed Lord said: To you who are non-envious I shall declare this most secret knowledge along with realization, knowing which you will be liberated from evil.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
The Lord said: To you who do not carp, I shall declare this most secret knowledge with realization, knowing which you shall be freed from evil.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
The Blessed Lord said: To you who are non-envious, I shall declare this most secret knowledge along with realization, knowing which you will be freed from evil.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: To clarify the divine science revealed in Chapter 7, the Lord imparts this supreme secret knowledge and wisdom of devotion to the non-envious Arjuna, releasing him from worldly bondage.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: To maintain the continuity of Brahma-vidya taught previously, the Lord indicates that a student free from malice and envy alone is qualified for this direct spiritual realization.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The Lord said: To you who are free from fault-finding, I shall declare this most secret knowledge along with realization, knowing which you shall be freed from all worldly suffering.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: 'This most secret knowledge' refers to devotion (Bhakti-yoga). The Lord reveals the mystery of His supreme sovereignty and loving devotion to Arjuna.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Freedom from envy is the principal prerequisite for receiving supreme knowledge. The Lord reveals the all-pervading and flawless knowledge of the Self.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: 'This most secret knowledge' refers to Brahma-vidya comprised of indirect comprehension and direct intuitive realization (Vijnana). Taught to a non-envious seeker, it bestows total liberation from evil (Samsara).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Elucidating the divine majesty set forth in Chapter 7 through devotional meditation, Lord Vasudeva declares Himself to be the Supreme Ruler and ultimate refuge.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: In the ninth chapter, the Lord expounds His imperishable divine majesty embedded with pure devotion, destroying all ignorance of the soul.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Supreme Brahman Lord Krishna imparts the essence of all Upanishads—non-dual wisdom and realization—to the non-envious Arjuna to eradicate all misery.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Having shown the glory of pure devotion in Chapter 8, the Lord now expounds the Sovereign Science imbued with devotion in Chapter 9.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Only a seeker free from envy is qualified for supreme wisdom; the Lord's teaching serves as the unfailing means for freedom from rebirth.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Revealing His unique compassion and absolute lordship, Lord Krishna explains the most profound secrecy of Bhakti-yoga.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Showering grace upon the non-envious Arjuna, the Lord initiates the Sovereign Science leading to God-realization.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Resolving earlier inquiries, the Lord now illuminates the most secret science of direct spiritual experience.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: My dear Arjuna, because you are never envious of Me, I shall impart to you this most confidential knowledge and realization, knowing which you shall be relieved of all miseries of material existence.
