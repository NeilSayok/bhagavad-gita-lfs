Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
etānyapi tu karmāṇi saṅgaṃ tyaktvā phalāni ca .
kartavyānīti me pārtha niścitaṃ matamuttamam

Word Meanings:
एतान्यपि - even these
तु - indeed
कर्माणि - actions
सङ्गं - attachment
त्यक्त्वा - having abandoned
फलानि - fruits
च - and
कर्तव्यानीति - must be performed
मे - my
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
निश्चितं - certain
मतमुत्तमम् - highest opinion

Life Application:
These actions should be performed abandoning attachment and fruit; this is My definitive best conviction.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
But even these actions should be performed abandoning attachment and fruits, O Partha; this is My definite and best conviction.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
But even these actions should be performed abandoning attachment and fruits, O Partha; this is My definite and best conviction.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
But even these actions must be performed renouncing attachment and the desire for fruits, O Partha; this is My definitive and supreme conviction.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
But even these actions should be performed, leaving aside attachment and the fruits, O Partha; this is My definite and best conviction.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
But even these actions should be performed, leaving aside attachment and the fruits, O Partha; this is My definite and best conviction.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
But even these actions should be performed abandoning attachment and fruits, O Partha; this is My definite and best conviction.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
But even these actions should be performed abandoning attachment and fruits, O Partha; this is My definite and best conviction.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Performing all prescribed duties for Lord Vishnu"s pleasure without desire for personal rewards is true renunciation.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: For mental purification, a seeker must perform sacrificial acts abandoning sense of agency and desire for results.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
But even these actions should be performed abandoning attachment and fruits, O Partha; this is My definite and best conviction.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Performing obligatory duties as worship of God, free from possessiveness and desire for fruits, is the true secret of Sattvika renunciation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Etani api etc. Actions performed abandoning attachment and fruits establish the seeker in supreme wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "But even these actions should be performed, leaving aside attachment and the fruits, O Partha; this is My definite and best conviction"—establishes that work done without attachment or desire for rewards purifies the mind for Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Unattached execution of duty is the direct doorway to intuitive knowledge of God via mental purity.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Performing all duties unattached as service to Lord Krishna is the devotee"s supreme dharma.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Unattached performance of duty for inner purity and wisdom is mandatory as the Lord"s supreme injunction.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
But even these actions should be performed abandoning attachment and fruits, O Partha; this is My definite and best conviction.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis establishing the obligatory execution of sacrificial acts devoid of attachment and desire for fruits.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Performing duties dedicated to Vasudeva while abandoning possessiveness and fruit-desire is the seeker"s true duty.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Action performed unattached for Krishna"s delight frees the soul from worldly bondage.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Mental purification achieved via selfless Karma-yoga forms the essential prerequisite for liberating Knowledge.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
All these activities should be performed without any expectation of result. They should be performed as a matter of duty, O son of Prtha. That is My final opinion.
