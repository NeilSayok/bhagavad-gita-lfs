Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sargāṇāmādirantaśca madhyaṃ caivāhamarjuna .
adhyātmavidyā vidyānāṃ vādaḥ pravadatāmaham

Word Meanings:
सर्गाणामादिरन्तश्च - beginning, end of creations and
मध्यं - middle
चैवाहमर्जुन - and indeed I am, O Arjuna
अध्यात्मविद्या - spiritual science
विद्यानां - among sciences
वादः - conclusive debate (vada)
प्रवदतामहम् - among debaters I am

Life Application:
Perceive God as the beginning, middle, and end of creations, self-knowledge among sciences, and logic in debate.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Of creations I am the beginning, the end, and also the middle, O Arjuna; of sciences I am the Science of the Self (Adhyatma-vidya); among debaters I am Vada (logical debate for truth).

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among creations I am the beginning, the end, and also the middle, O Arjuna; of sciences I am the Science of the Self; among debaters I am Vada.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of creations I am the beginning and the end and also the middle, O Arjuna. Of sciences I am the Science of the Soul, and of debaters I am the Vada, truth-seeking logic.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
The origin, sustenance, and end of all creation; the liberating Adhyatma-vidya (Spiritual Knowledge) among all sciences; and Vada (truth-seeking logic) among forms of debate represents the Divine.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The source of origin, maintenance, and dissolution of all creation; Adhyatma-vidya (Self-knowledge) bringing liberation among sciences; and unbiased Vada seeking truth among debaters are the Lord's chief Vibhutis.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Of creations I am the beginning, the end, and also the middle, O Arjuna; of sciences I am the Science of the Self; among debaters I am Vada.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: I am the origin, dissolution, and maintenance of created entities. Among all sciences, the liberating Adhyatma-vidya; and among forms of discussion, Vada aimed at determining truth is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Of creations I am the beginning, the end, and also the middle, O Arjuna. Of sciences I am the Science of the Self (Adhyatma-vidya) leading to liberation; and among debaters I am Vada (discourse aimed at discovering truth, distinct from Jalpa and Vitanda).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The ultimate cause of creation, maintenance, and dissolution; liberating Adhyatma-vidya; and truth-establishing Vada manifest the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: The substratum of creation, sustenance, and dissolution; Brahma-vidya (Spiritual Science) among sciences; and truth-discovering Vada among the three modes of debate (Vada, Jalpa, Vitanda) is My divine opulence.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: The pervasive presence in creation, liberating Self-knowledge, and truth-seeking Vada devoid of pride express God's divine nature.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Universal causality of creation, Adhyatma-vidya supreme among sciences, and truth-determining Vada belong to the Lord's principal Vibhutis.
