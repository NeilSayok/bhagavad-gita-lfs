Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yatra kāle tvanāvṛttimāvṛttiṃ caiva yoginaḥ .
prayātā yānti taṃ kālaṃ vakṣyāmi bharatarṣabha

Word Meanings:
यत्र - wherein
काले - in the time
त्वनावृत्तिमावृत्तिं - non-return and return
चैव - and indeed
योगिनः - the yogis
प्रयाता - departing
यान्ति - attain
तं - that
कालं - time
वक्ष्यामि - I will declare
भरतर्षभ - O best of Bharatas

Life Application:
Learn of the cosmic times and paths along which yogis depart, either to return or not to return.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Now I shall tell you the time (path) at which departing yogis do not return, and also that at which departing they return, O bull of Bharatas.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Now I shall tell you the time at which yogis departing do not return, and also when departing they return, O best of Bharatas.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O best of the Bharatas, I shall now declare unto you that time (path or presiding deities) departing at which yogis do not return, and also that departing at which they do return.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Now I shall tell you the time at which yogis departing do not return, and also when departing they return, O bull of Bharatas.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Now I shall tell you the time at which yogis departing do not return, and also when departing they return, O bull of Bharatas.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "At which time"—here "time" denotes the presiding deities of time (Agni, Surya, Chandra, etc.) who guide the soul on its departure.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "The time at which departing yogis return not"—here "time" signifies the northern and southern paths indicated by their presiding deities (Devayana and Pitriyana).

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Now I shall tell you the time at which yogis departing do not return, and also when departing they return, O bull of Bharatas.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Expounding the two paths—Devayana led by light-deities from which souls return not, and Pitriyana from which they return.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Now I shall tell you the paths (indicated by time-deities) departing by which yogis do or do not return.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Now I shall tell you the time/path departing at which yogis do not return, and departing at which they return, O best of Bharatas"—here "time" refers to the northern (Devayana) and southern (Pitriyana) paths indicated by presiding time-deities.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The presiding deities of Devayana and Pitriyana are designated by the term "time", leading souls according to their eligibility to Brahmaloka or Chandraloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Expounding the time measures of Devayana and Pitriyana paths ordained by the Lord"s supreme will.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I shall describe that time/path"—scriptural delineation of non-return via Northern Path and return via Southern Path.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Now I shall tell you the time at which yogis departing do not return, and also when departing they return, O best of Bharatas.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural explanation of the path-deities of Devayana and Pitriyana implied by the word "time".

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Explaining non-return for seekers departing via Archiradi Devayana path versus return via Dhumradi path.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Two paths are created by divine will for the soul"s departure, the arrangement of which is described here.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "At which time"—delineating the presiding time-deities of the Northern (Archiradi) and Southern (Dhumradi) departure paths.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O best of the Bharatas, I shall now explain to you the different times at which, passing away from this world, the yogi does or does not come back.
