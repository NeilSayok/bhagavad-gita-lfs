Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
daṇḍo damayatāmasmi nītirasmi jigīṣatām .
maunaṃ caivāsmi guhyānāṃ jñānaṃ jñānavatāmaham

Word Meanings:
दण्डो - scepter of punishment (danda)
दमयतामस्मि - among disciplinarians I am
नीतिरस्मि - I am statesmanship
जिगीषताम् - of those seeking victory
मौनं - silence
चैवास्मि - and also I am
गुह्यानां - among secrets
ज्ञानं - wisdom
ज्ञानवतामहम् - of the wise I am

Life Application:
See God as the rod of punishers, statesmanship of victors, silence of secrets, and wisdom of the wise.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am the scepter (punishment) of rulers/subduers; I am statesmanship of those seeking victory; among secrets I am Silence, and I am the Wisdom of the wise.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I am the scepter of rulers; I am the statesmanship of those who seek victory; and of secret things I am silence, and the wisdom of the wise am I.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of rulers I am the scepter; of those who seek victory I am statesmanship. Of secrets I am silence, and of the wise I am wisdom.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
The Scepter of justice; Statesmanship of those seeking victory; Silence among secrets; and True Wisdom among the wise represent the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among subduers I am the scepter; among those seeking victory I am statesmanship; of secrets I am silence, and I am the wisdom of the wise.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Among subduers I am the scepter; among those seeking victory I am statesmanship; of secrets I am silence, and I am the wisdom of the wise.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
I am the scepter of subduers and the policy of those seeking victory; among secret things I am silence, and the wisdom of the wise am I.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The royal Scepter disciplining the lawless, righteous Policy for victory-seekers, Silence preserving secrets, and Direct Knowledge of the Self among the wise are the Lord's chief Vibhutis.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am the scepter of subduers and the statesmanship of victory-seekers; of secret things I am silence, and the wisdom of the wise am I.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The scepter governing offenders; statesmanship for victory-seekers; silence regarding secrets; and spiritual wisdom among the wise is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among subduers I am the Scepter; among seekers of victory I am Statesmanship; of secrets I am Silence; and of the wise I am Wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Righteous scepter, just policy, ultimate silence, and spiritual wisdom reflect the Lord's supreme glory.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: The scepter of law enforcement, righteous policy for victory, silence of secrets, and direct realization of the wise is My divine opulence.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am the scepter of subduers, the policy of victory-seekers; of secrets I am silence, and the wisdom of the wise am I.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: The scepter's justice, policy's triumph, silence's gravity, and wisdom's light reveal God.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Scepter, policy, silence, and wisdom serve as the Lord's chief means and opulences for protecting Dharma.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Among subduers I am the scepter; among victory-seekers I am policy; of secrets I am silence; and of the wise I am wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The scepter, statesmanship, silence, and spiritual wisdom represent the Lord's divine governance and light.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Among all means of suppressing lawlessness I am punishment, and of those who seek victory I am morality. Of secret things I am silence, and of the wise I am the wisdom.
