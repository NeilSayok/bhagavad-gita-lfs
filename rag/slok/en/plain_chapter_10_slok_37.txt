Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
vṛṣṇīnāṃ vāsudevo.asmi pāṇḍavānāṃ dhanañjayaḥ .
munīnāmapyahaṃ vyāsaḥ kavīnāmuśanā kaviḥ

Word Meanings:
वृष्णीनां - among the Vrishnis
वासुदेवोऽस्मि - I am Vasudeva (Krishna)
पाण्डवानां - among the Pandavas
धनञ्जयः - Arjuna
मुनीनामप्यहं - among sages also I am
व्यासः - Vyasa
कवीनामुशना - among poets Ushanas (Shukracharya)
कविः - the seer/poet

Life Application:
Recognize God as Krishna among Vrishnis, Arjuna among Pandavas, Vyasa among sages, and Ushana among poets.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva (Krishna); among the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya (Arjuna); among sages I am Vyasa, and among seers/poets I am Ushana (Shukracharya).

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva; among the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya; among sages I am Vyasa; among poets I am Ushanas the poet.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva; of the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya. Of sages I am Vyasa, and of poets I am Ushana, Shukracharya.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among Vrishnis, Lord Vasudeva; among Pandavas, Arjuna (Dhananjaya); among sages, Vyasa; and among visionary poets, Sage Ushanas (Shukracharya) represents the Divine.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva; among the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya; among sages I am Vyasa; among poets I am Ushanas.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva; among the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya; among sages I am Vyasa; among poets I am Ushanas.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva; among the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya; among sages I am Vyasa; among poets I am Ushanas.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Among the Vrishnis, the name 'Vasudeva' signifies Lord Vishnu who envelops all creation, abides as the supreme refuge of all beings, and dwells everywhere. The name 'Vyasa' signifies the Supreme Lord who is pre-eminent above all and all-pervading.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Lord Vasudeva among Yadavas, Arjuna among Pandavas, Sage Vyasa composer of Scriptures among sages, and Shukracharya among visionary seers are the Lord's principal opulences.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva, among the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya; among sages I am Vyasa, and among poets I am Ushanas.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among Vrishnis, Vasudeva; among Pandavas, Dhananjaya (Arjuna); among sages, Vyasa; and among visionary poets, Shukracharya is My divine Vibhuti.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among Vrishnis (Yadavas) I am Vasudeva (Lord Krishna Himself); among Pandavas I am Dhananjaya (you Arjuna); among sages I am Vyasa; and among seers I am Ushanas (Shukracharya).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Etymologically, 'Vasudeva' combines 'Vasu' (the all-pervading refuge that covers and abides in all creation) with 'Deva', highlighting Lord Vishnu's universal presence. 'Vyasa' denotes the Supreme Lord who transcends all and pervades every dimension.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Vasudeva Krishna, Dhananjaya Arjuna, Sage Vyasa, and Poet Shukracharya illuminate the Lord's full and partial powers.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Among Vrishnis, Lord Vasudeva Himself; among Pandavas, Dhananjaya; among sages, Vyasa; and among omniscient poets, Shukracharya is My divine opulence.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva; among the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya; among sages I am Vyasa; among poets I am Ushanas.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Vasudeva's supremacy, Arjuna's heroism, Vyasa's wisdom, and Shukracharya's poetic statesmanship express God's grandeur.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Vasudeva, Dhananjaya, Vyasa, and Poet Ushanas are universally revered as the Lord's extraordinary forms and manifestations.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Among Vrishnis I am Vasudeva; among Pandavas I am Dhananjaya; among sages I am Vyasa; among poets I am Ushanas.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Vasudeva among Vrishnis, Dhananjaya among Pandavas, Vyasa among sages, and Shukracharya among poets represent the Lord.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Of the descendants of Vṛṣṇi I am Vāsudeva, and of the Pāṇḍavas I am Arjuna. Of the sages I am Vyāsa, and among great thinkers I am Uśanā.
