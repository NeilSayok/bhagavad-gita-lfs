Speaker: Arjuna

Slok:
vistareṇātmano yogaṃ vibhūtiṃ ca janārdana .
bhūyaḥ kathaya tṛptirhi śṛṇvato nāsti me.amṛtam

Word Meanings:
विस्तरेणात्मनो - in detail Your own
योगं - yoga path
विभूतिं - glory
च - and
जनार्दन - O Krishna
भूयः - again
कथय - tell
तृप्तिर्हि - satiety indeed
शृण्वतो - hearing
नास्ति - there is not
मेऽमृतम् - my nectar-like

Life Application:
Request to hear again in detail of divine glory and power, for there is never satiety in drinking this nectar.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Tell me again in detail, O Janardana, of Your Yogic power and opulence; for I am never sated with listening to Your nectar-like words.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Tell me again in detail, O Janardana, of Thy Yogic power and glory; for I am not satiated with listening to Thy nectar-like speech.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O Janardana, again please describe in detail the mystic power of Your opulences. I am never sated with hearing Your nectar-like words.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
When an earnest seeker receives spiritual wisdom from an enlightened Master, his inquiry knows no end. Arjuna entreats Lord Krishna to describe again in detail His divine Yoga power and opulences, for listening to the Lord's words is like partaking of pure nectar.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Declare again in detail Your Yogic power and manifestation, O Janardana! For there is no satiety for me listening to Your nectar-like speech.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The name 'Janardana' signifies Him who vanquishes the wicked and fulfills the prayers of the righteous. Arjuna begs the Lord to describe His divine manifestations again in complete detail.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Lord's speech is nectar itself. Arjuna experiences supreme eagerness to hear the Lord's opulences and Yogic power, for listening to divine discourses never causes satiation in a seeker, but only deepens devotion.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
O Janardana, please describe again in detail Your Yogic power and opulences; for I am not sated with listening to Your nectar-like speech.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: You previously stated in brief that 'I am the origin of all and everything proceeds from Me.' O Janardana, please declare again in full detail Your divine Yoga and Vibhuti—Your creation, control, and auspicious qualities; for I am not sated drinking the nectar of Your speech.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Abhinavagupta did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Tell me again in detail, O Janardana, of Your Yogic power (omniscience and capacity) and Vibhuti (extending objects of contemplation); for I experience no satiation while listening to the nectar of Your divine speech.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Arjuna declares the Lord's words to be 'nectar'. For a devotee drinking the nectar of divine knowledge, satiation never arises; rather, the thirst for listening grows continuously.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Arjuna requests the Supreme Lord, stating that even while constantly hearing of the Lord's infinite Yogic power and opulences, his soul feels no satiation; hence he begs the Lord to describe them again in detail.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
