Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ye me matamidaṃ nityamanutiṣṭhanti mānavāḥ .
śraddhāvanto.anasūyanto mucyante te.api karmabhiḥ

Word Meanings:
ये - who
मे - my
मतमिदं - this teaching of Mine
नित्यमनुतिष्ठन्ति - constantly practice
मानवाः - men
श्रद्धावन्तोऽनसूयन्तो - with faith and free from envy
मुच्यन्ते - are freed
तेऽपि - they also
कर्मभिः - from actions

Life Application:
Put constructive spiritual principles into daily practice with trust, freeing your mind from chronic doubt and cynicism.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Those men who constantly practice this teaching of Mine, full of faith and without cavil, they also are freed from actions.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Those men who constantly practice this teaching of Mine, full of faith and without cavil, they also are freed from actions.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Those men who constantly practice (Nityam anutishthanti) this teaching of Mine (Me matam idam), full of faith (Shraddhavantah) and without cavil or jealousy (Anasuyantah), they also are liberated from the bondage of actions (Mucyante te api karmabhih).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Souls practicing this doctrine of Supreme Lord Vishnu as authoritative cross the ocean of samsara.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Devotees accepting the Lord"s doctrine with faith and executing duties without cavil are liberated from all karmic bondage.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.31–3.32 jointly): Ye me etc. and Ye tv etat etc. Those who carp at My doctrine and do not practice it, deluded in all knowledge, know them to be ruined; whereas the faithful are freed.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Those men who constantly practice this teaching of Mine, full of faith and without cavil, they also are freed from actions"—assures that faithful adherence to Krishna"s doctrine neutralizes karmic bondage.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Faith and non-cavil form the essential qualification for practicing the divine doctrine.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Devotees placing full trust in Krishna"s instruction and performing selfless service attain the supreme Realm.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: A faithful seeker, while engaging in actions, remains untainted by karma and attains the Supreme State.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving why faithful and non-jealous practitioners of the Lord"s teaching gain freedom from karmic ties.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Faithful adherence to Vasudeva"s selfless duty doctrine qualifies the soul for Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Souls who reverently practice Krishna"s doctrine receive the Lord"s eternal grace continuously.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Performing prescribed duties with God-dedicated intellect destroys all karmic fetters completely.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those persons who execute their duties according to My injunctions and who follow this teaching faithfully, without envy, become free from the bondage of fruitive actions.
