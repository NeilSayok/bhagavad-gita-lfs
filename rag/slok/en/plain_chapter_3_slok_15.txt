Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
karma brahmodbhavaṃ viddhi brahmākṣarasamudbhavam .
tasmātsarvagataṃ brahma nityaṃ yajñe pratiṣṭhitam

Word Meanings:
कर्म - originated from Vedas
ब्रह्मोद्भवं - know
विद्धि - Vedas originated from the Imperishable
ब्रह्माक्षरसमुद्भवम् - therefore all-pervading
तस्मात्सर्वगतं - the Supreme
ब्रह्म - always
नित्यं - in sacrifice
यज्ञे - established
प्रतिष्ठितम् - is established

Life Application:
See your daily selfless actions as expressions of a universal divine order that connects all living beings.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Know that action originates from the Veda, and the Veda originates from the Imperishable; therefore the all-pervading Brahman is ever established in sacrifice.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Know that action originates from the Veda, and the Veda originates from the Imperishable; therefore the all-pervading Brahman is ever established in sacrifice.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Know that action originates from the Veda (Brahmodbhavam), and the Veda originates from the Imperishable Supreme Self (Aksharasamudbhavam); therefore the All-pervading Brahman (Sarvagatam Brahma) is ever established in Sacrifice (Nityam Yagne pratishthitam).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The all-pervading Lord Vishnu is the supreme subject of the Vedas and is eternally present in Yajna.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Veda originates as the breath of the Imperishable Brahman, and the all-pervading Lord is ever manifest in sacrifice.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Know that action originates from the Veda, and the Veda originates from the Imperishable; therefore the all-pervading Brahman is ever established in sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Sacrificial action stems from nature (Brahman) and the imperishable soul; hence the all-pervading Supreme Lord is ever present in Yajna.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Action is born of the Veda, and the Veda from the Imperishable; therefore the all-pervading Brahman is ever established in sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Know that action originates from the Veda, and the Veda originates from the Imperishable; therefore the all-pervading Brahman is ever established in sacrifice"—traces action back to the Veda and the Veda to Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Veda-based performance of duty is the supreme discipline for realizing the all-pervading Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Vedas emanating from Krishna"s lotus mouth inspire all duties, and the Lord Himself presides as Yajna-purusha.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Supreme Brahman is the source of the Vedas and is eternally present in sacrificial acts.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Know that action originates from the Veda, and the Veda originates from the Imperishable; therefore the all-pervading Brahman is ever established in sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis substantiating why the all-pervading Brahman originating the Veda is ever established in Yajna.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Duty stemming from the Vedas (breath of Vasudeva) is the direct worship of the all-pervading Lord.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When scriptural duties are done with Krishna-dedication, the all-pervading Supreme manifests directly in Yajna.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Sacrificial execution is the direct symbol and portal for realizing the all-pervading Brahman.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Regulated activities are prescribed in the Vedas, and the Vedas are directly manifested from the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Consequently the all-pervading Transcendence is eternally situated in acts of sacrifice.
