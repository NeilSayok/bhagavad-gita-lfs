Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
mahātmānastu māṃ pārtha daivīṃ prakṛtimāśritāḥ .
bhajantyananyamanaso jñātvā bhūtādimavyayam

Word Meanings:
महात्मानस्तु - great souls, but
मां - Me
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
दैवीं - divine
प्रकृतिमाश्रिताः - sheltered in nature
भजन्त्यनन्यमनसो - worship with single mind
ज्ञात्वा - knowing
भूतादिमव्ययम् - imperishable origin of beings

Life Application:
Embrace a noble nature, worshipping God with single-pointed devotion as the imperishable origin of all.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
But the great souls, O Partha, possessed of the divine nature, worship Me with undivided mind, knowing Me to be the imperishable source of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
But the great souls, O Partha, possessed of the divine nature, worship Me with a single mind, knowing Me as the immutable source of all beings.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
But the great souls, O Partha, endowed with the divine nature, worship Me with devotion, knowing Me as the imperishable source of all creation.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
But the mahatmas, O Partha, possessed of the Divine nature, worship Me with undivided mind, knowing Me to be the imperishable origin of all beings.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
But the great souls, O Partha, possessed of the divine nature, worship Me with undivided mind, knowing Me to be the imperishable source of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
But the great souls, O Partha, possessed of the divine nature, worship Me with single-minded devotion, knowing Me as the immutable source of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
But the great souls, O Partha, possessed of the divine nature, worship Me with single-minded devotion, knowing Me as the immutable source of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Great souls endowed with divine (sattvic) virtues know the Supreme Lord as the imperishable source of all and worship Him with unswerving devotion.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
But the great souls, O Partha, possessed of the divine nature, worship Me with undivided mind, knowing Me to be the imperishable source of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Great souls possessed of divine (Sattvic) disposition, recognizing Me as the Lord of all endowed with divine names, forms, and attributes, worship Me with exclusive devotion.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Great souls endowed with divine nature, knowing the Supreme Lord to be the imperishable source of all beings, worship Him with undivided mind through perpetual chanting, steadfast endeavor, and devotion.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: 'Great souls, O Partha, endowed with divine nature'—those of noble minds resort to divine (Sattvic) virtues like control of mind/senses and compassion, and knowing Me as the imperishable source of all, worship Me with undivided devotion.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Devotees endowed with divine virtues recognize Lord Krishna as the Supreme Lord Narayana Vasudeva and engage in His unswerving devotion.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Great souls blessed by God resort to Sattvic disposition, comprehend His eternal blissful Form, and serve Him with exclusive devotion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Great souls equipped with divine virtues like tranquility and restraint know the Lord as the material and efficient cause of the universe, surrendering with undivided mind.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: But the great souls, O Partha, possessed of the divine nature, worship Me with undivided mind, knowing Me to be the imperishable source of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Divine nature is the mark of a true seeker; Sattvic devotees knowing the Lord as the cause of all remain absorbed in exclusive remembrance.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Qualification for devotion springs from divine nature; great souls take exclusive refuge in the Lord's names, forms, and incarnations.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Devotees of divine disposition, holding the Lord as everything and the cause of all, serve and remember Him with exclusive love.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Sattvic seekers resting in divine nature comprehend the All-Indwelling Lord as the eternal substratum and practice unswerving devotion.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O son of Kunti, those who are not deluded, the great souls, are under the protection of the divine nature. They are fully engaged in devotional service because they know Me as the Supreme Personality of Godhead, original and inexhaustible.
