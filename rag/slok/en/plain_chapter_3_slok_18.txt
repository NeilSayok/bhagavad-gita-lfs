Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
naiva tasya kṛtenārtho nākṛteneha kaścana .
na cāsya sarvabhūteṣu kaścidarthavyapāśrayaḥ

Word Meanings:
नैव - not indeed
तस्य - purpose in action
कृतेनार्थो - nor by non-action here
नाकृतेनेह - any whatever
कश्चन - and of him
न - among all beings
चास्य - any dependence for self-interest
सर्वभूतेषु - among all beings
कश्चिदर्थव्यपाश्रयः - any dependence of self-interest

Life Application:
Perform your work with complete inner independence, free from needy dependence on other people's approval or assistance.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
For him there is no interest whatever in what is done or what is not done here, nor does he depend on any being for any object.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
For such a self-realized soul, there is no interest whatsoever in what is done (Kritena) nor in what is left undone (Akritena); nor does he depend upon any living being for any purpose (Arthavyapashrayah).

Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A Sage relishing Vasudeva is completely free from desire for karmic fruits and dependence on creatures.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a seeker finds exclusive refuge in Krishna, he has no self-serving interest with any creature.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Transcending all means and ends, the Sage of Supreme Reality abides in his own glory.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
A self-realized man has no purpose to serve in the discharge of his prescribed duties, nor has he any reason not to perform such work. Nor has he any need to depend on any other living being.
