Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śreyānsvadharmo viguṇaḥ paradharmātsvanuṣṭhitāt .
svadharme nidhanaṃ śreyaḥ paradharmo bhayāvahaḥ

Word Meanings:
श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो - better is one's own duty
विगुणः - even imperfect
परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् - than another's duty well-performed
स्वधर्मे - in one's own duty
निधनं - death
श्रेयः - better
परधर्मो - another's duty
भयावहः - fraught with fear

Life Application:
Focus on fulfilling your own authentic life calling, avoiding the temptation to copy someone else's path.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Better is one"s own duty, though devoid of merit, than the duty of another well performed. Death in one"s own duty is preferable; the duty of another is fraught with fear.

Sri Shankaracharya states: "Better is one"s own duty, though devoid of merit, than the duty of another well performed. Death in one"s own duty is preferable; the duty of another is fraught with fear"—solemnly commands Arjuna to embrace Kshatriya Swadharma over monastic renunciation.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Performance of Swadharma is the infallible portal to Moksha via mental purity, whereas Paradharma causes downfall.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Swadharma ordained by Lord Krishna for a soul is the true form of divine service; Paradharma must be eschewed.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Practicing duty according to qualification brings mental purity and Moksha; another"s duty brings ruin.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Selfless performance of Varna/Ashrama Swadharma under Vasudeva"s command is the gateway to all bliss.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Executing Krishna-given Swadharma is supreme service; adopting Paradharma is disrespecting the Lord"s decree.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Performing duty suited to one"s qualification and nature brings mental purity and ultimate liberation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
It is far better to discharge one"s own prescribed duties, even though faultily, than another"s duties perfectly. Destruction in the course of performing one"s own duty is better than engaging in another"s duties, for to follow another"s path is dangerous.
