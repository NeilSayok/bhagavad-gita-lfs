Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
devānbhāvayatānena te devā bhāvayantu vaḥ .
parasparaṃ bhāvayantaḥ śreyaḥ paramavāpsyatha

Word Meanings:
देवान्भावयतानेन - nourish the gods by this
ते - the gods
देवा - may nourish
भावयन्तु - you
वः - one another
परस्परं - nourishing
भावयन्तः - the supreme good
श्रेयः - you shall attain
परमवाप्स्यथ - you shall attain the highest

Life Application:
Nurture the natural and social systems around you through responsible habits, receiving their ongoing support in return.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Gratifying demigods who are limbs of Lord Vishnu via Yajna causes world welfare and ultimate Moksha for the seeker.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Nourishing the gods with sacrificial offerings causes them to nourish humanity with rain and food, leading sequentially to Mukti.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
With this do ye nourish the gods and may those gods nourish you; thus nourishing one another, ye shall attain the highest good.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Worshipping demigods as limbs of Supreme Lord Vishnu with offerings pleases them, bestowing highest spiritual welfare upon seekers.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: "Sahayajnah etc. and Devan etc."—the Creator established co-relationship between Yajna and mankind for attaining supreme good through mutual nourishment.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "With this do ye nourish the gods and may those gods nourish you; thus nourishing one another, ye shall attain the highest good"—explains mutual cosmic interdependence leading to ultimate Liberation (*Param Sreyah*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The cycle of worshipping demigods and sustaining living beings forms the core foundation of Dharma.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Yajna performed for Krishna"s delight satisfies all demigods and carries the practitioner to the supreme divine Realm.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Mutual nourishment and practice of virtue purify the mind, culminating in the supreme good of liberation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis showing how mutual nourishment between gods and humans leads to the supreme good of Moksha.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Satisfying demigods as body-limbs of Vasudeva via Yajna bestows Sattvika prosperity and Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When souls serve demigods through Yajna, divine grace causes all creation to abide in mutual love.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Ye shall attain the highest good"—mental purification via mutual sustenance leading to absolute Mukti is the conclusion.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The demigods, being satisfied by performances of sacrifice, will also satisfy you; and thus, by cooperation between men and demigods, prosperity will reign for all.
