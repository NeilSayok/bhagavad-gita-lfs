Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
bahūni me vyatītāni janmāni tava cārjuna .
tānyahaṃ veda sarvāṇi na tvaṃ vettha parantapa

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
बहूनि - many
मे - my
व्यतीतानि - have passed
जन्मानि - births
तव - your
चार्जुन - and, O Arjuna
तान्यहं - them I
वेद - know
सर्वाणि - all
न - not
त्वं - you
वेत्थ - knowest
परन्तप - O scorcher of foes

Life Application:
Maintain humility regarding your limited memory and view, honoring the broader divine vision behind life's events.

The Blessed Lord said: Many births of Mine have passed, as well as thine, O Arjuna; I know them all, but thou knowest not, O Burner of foes.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "I know them all"—Lord Vishnu, being omniscient, directly knows all past, present, and future births of Himself and all living entities.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Lord reincarnates via His Maya-power, possessing absolute omniscience of all past births, whereas the jiva, obscured by Avidya, forgets past births.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The Blessed Lord said: Many births of Mine have passed, as well as thine, O Arjuna; I know them all, but thou knowest not, O Burner of foes.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The Lord"s incarnations are real and divine, assumed by His free will to uphold Dharma; whereas jivas" births are driven helplessly by karma and ignorance.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.5–4.9 jointly): Through these slokas the Lord proves that His birth and action are not karmic like ordinary beings, but a divine sport of Pure Consciousness; knowing this in truth, one attains liberation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Many births of Mine have passed, as well as thine, O Arjuna; I know them all, but thou knowest not, O Burner of foes"—contrasts the eternal omniscience (*Sarvajnata*) of Ishvara with the ignorant forgetfulness (*Avidya*) of the bound soul.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Omniscience and omnipotence are eternal characteristics of God, distinguishing Him completely from Avidya-bound souls.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna, in His omniscient Sat-Chit-Ananda form, retains unbroken memory of all His lila-incarnations, while the jiva forgets due to Maya.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I know them all"—because Ishvara"s adjunct is pure Sattva (Maya), His omniscience remains intact, whereas the jiva"s adjunct of Avidya renders it forgetful.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
The Blessed Lord said: Many births of Mine have passed, as well as thine, O Arjuna; I know them all, but thou knowest not, O Burner of foes.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving Ishvara"s omniscience in remembering all past incarnations versus the jiva"s forgetfulness caused by nescience.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Vasudeva"s incarnation is real and assumed by His supreme free will; He is the direct Knower of all His descents.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna manifests repeatedly out of infinite grace to deliver His devotees, knowing all His divine lilas through eternal omniscience.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "I know them all"—because Ishvara"s adjunct is pure Sattva, His omniscience remains forever intact.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Personality of Godhead said: Many, many births both you and I have passed. I can remember all of them, but you cannot, O subduer of the enemy!
