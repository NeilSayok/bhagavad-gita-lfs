Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
maharṣayaḥ sapta pūrve catvāro manavastathā .
madbhāvā mānasā jātā yeṣāṃ loka imāḥ prajāḥ

Word Meanings:
महर्षयः - great sages
सप्त - seven
पूर्वे - ancient
चत्वारो - four
मनवस्तथा - Manus also
मद्भावा - born of My nature
मानसा - from the mind
जाता - born
येषां - whose
लोक - world
इमाः - these
प्रजाः - mankind

Life Application:
Know that the ancient progenitors and patriarchs of humanity were born from divine mind and power.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The seven great sages (Bhrigu and others), the ancient four (Sanaka and others), and the Manus were born of My mind, endowed with My nature, and from them all these creatures in the world have descended.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The seven great sages, the ancient four and also the Manus, possessed of powers like Me (on account of their minds being fixed on Me), were born of My mind; from them are these creatures in the world.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The seven Great Seers, the Progenitors of mankind, the Ancient Four, and the Lawgivers were born of My Will and mind, and from them all the inhabitants of the world have sprung.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
The Lord is both the material and efficient cause of the universe. The seven great sages (Bhrigu, etc.), the four Kumaras (Sanaka, etc.), and the fourteen Manus were all mind-born sons of the Supreme Lord. Through them the population of the world has descended; hence all creation originates from the Lord alone.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
The ancient Seven Great-Seers and also the Four Manus, of whom these creatures in this world are offsprings—they have been born of My mind, possessing My nature.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
The seven great seers of yore and similarly the four Manus, all possessing My mental disposition, were born of My mind; from them these creatures in the world have come forth.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
The seven great sages as also the four Manus of ancient days, of whom are these creatures in the world, had their thoughts fixed on Me, and were born of My mind.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The seven great sages (Marichi, Atri, Angiras, Pulastya, Pulaha, Kratu, Vasishtha), the ancient four Kumaras (Sanaka, etc.), and the Manus were born of the mind of Lord Vishnu. Possessing powers granted by the Lord, they project and maintain the world's population.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Lord is not merely the material cause of the world, but also the omniscient Supreme Ruler. The seven sages (Bhrigu, etc.), the four Kumaras, and the Manus were projected by the Lord's divine resolve to generate creatures in the universe.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The seven great sages, the four ancient ones (Sanaka and others) born prior to them, and the fourteen Manus were all born of My mind and possess My divine attributes. All the inhabitants of this world are their progeny.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The seven great sages of the past manvantara (Bhrigu and others) and the four Manus were born from the mind of Brahma according to the Supreme Lord's divine will. All current inhabitants of the world descend in their lineage.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The great sages, Kumaras, and Manus govern the cosmic order solely through the will and power of the Supreme Lord. Enlightening one another regarding the Lord's divine glory, these enlightened souls abide in supreme bliss.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: The seven great sages (Bhrigu and others) and the four Manus of ancient times were possessed of My nature (madbhavah) and born of My mind. All these living beings in the world are their offspring.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The seven great sages and the Manus, appearing from the Lord's mind, execute the creation and governance of the world. They are completely dependent on the Supreme Lord and function by His power alone.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The Supreme Lord is the ultimate cause of all creatures and sages. The mind-born sons of Brahma, the great seers and Manus, manifest by the Lord's divine resolve and expand the manifestation of His divine Being.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: The seven great sages (Bhrigu, etc.), the ancient four Kumaras, and the Manus are the originators of Vedic knowledge and spiritual lineages. Born of the Lord's mind and endowed with His power, they generate all creatures.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: The seven great sages (Bhrigu and others), the four Kumaras, and the Manus were born of My mind and possessed My divine power. From them all these creatures in the world have multiplied.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: When even Bhrigu and the other seers, endowed with the Lord's power, are revered as lords of the world, what need be said of the Supreme Lord Himself! They are all His mind-born manifestations.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The seven sages and Manus, though celebrated as causes of creation and preservation, are not independent; they originate from the Lord's divine resolve and function in total subordination to Him.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The Supreme Lord alone is the primal producer of all living beings, their progenitor sages, and the Manus. Everything manifests solely by the Lord's mind and divine will.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Illustrating the order of cosmic lineage and tradition, the Lord declares that the seven sages, the four Kumaras, and the Manus are all His mind-born offspring, from whom all these inhabitants have sprung.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The seven great sages and before them the four other great sages and the Manus [progenitors of mankind] come from Me, born of My mind, and all the living beings populating the various planets descend from them.
