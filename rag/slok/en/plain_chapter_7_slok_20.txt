Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
kāmaistaistairhṛtajñānāḥ prapadyante.anyadevatāḥ .
taṃ taṃ niyamamāsthāya prakṛtyā niyatāḥ svayā

Word Meanings:
कामैस्तैस्तैर्हृतज्ञानाः - wisdom stolen by desires
प्रपद्यन्तेऽन्यदेवताः - surrender to other deities
तं - that
तं - respective
नियममास्थाय - following the rules
प्रकृत्या - by nature
नियताः - governed / led
स्वया - by their own

Life Application:
Recognize how personal desires can lead people to worship temporary forces according to their own nature.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Those whose wisdom has been stolen by various desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature and following diverse rules.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Those whose wisdom is stolen by diverse desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature and following different rules.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
But those whose wisdom is clouded by worldly desires for wealth and pleasure resort to other deities, driven by their past habitual nature and bound by diverse rituals.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Those whose wisdom has been stolen by various desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature and following diverse rules.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Those whose wisdom has been stolen by various desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature and following diverse rules.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Those whose wisdom is stolen by diverse desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature and following different rules.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Those whose wisdom is stolen by diverse desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature and following different rules.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Resort to other deities"—ignorant souls, unaware of Lord Vishnu"s supreme majesty, surrender to limited demigods for petty temporary rewards.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Wisdom stolen by diverse desires, they resort to other deities"—souls whose minds are clouded by desire for sons, wealth, and fame abandon Lord Vasudeva to worship lesser demigods.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Those whose wisdom has been stolen by various desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature and following diverse rules.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Those whose discrimination is destroyed by worldly desires born of past Karmas resort to other demigods (like Indra, Surya) for temporary material gains.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those whose wisdom is carried away by diverse desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature. Whichever form of a deity a devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that faith steady. Endowed with that faith, they obtain their desires, which are in truth ordained by Me alone. But finite is the fruit of those of small intellect; worshippers of deities go to deities, but My devotees come unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Those whose wisdom is stolen by diverse desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature"—explains that desire-bound individuals, blinded by material ambitions, turn to limited demigods rather than seeking the Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Worship of other demigods is the result of ignorance from past Karmas; Lord Vishnu alone is the inner indweller and bestower of all rewards.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Driven by fruitive motives, the soul forgets Lord Krishna"s supreme sovereignty and strays into the worship of limited deities.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Wisdom stolen by desires"—men whose intellect is veiled by lust born of ignorance worship finite demigods instead of Supreme Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Those whose wisdom is stolen by diverse desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature and following different rules.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural exposition of the causes of worshipping other deities and the influence of past nature.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Worshippers of other demigods due to petty desires obtain finite results because they fail to realize Lord Vasudeva as the Indweller of all.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: As long as the soul remains covered by material desires, it cannot take unswerving refuge in Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Wisdom stolen by desires"—the mind obscured by desires born of Maya remains entangled in worshipping finite demigods.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those whose intelligence has been stolen by material desires surrender unto demigods and follow the particular rules and regulations of worship according to their own natures.
