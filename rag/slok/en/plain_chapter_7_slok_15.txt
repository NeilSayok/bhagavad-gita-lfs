Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
na māṃ duṣkṛtino mūḍhāḥ prapadyante narādhamāḥ .
māyayāpahṛtajñānā āsuraṃ bhāvamāśritāḥ

Word Meanings:
न - not
मां - unto Me
दुष्कृतिनो - evil-doers
मूढाः - fools
प्रपद्यन्ते - surrender
नराधमाः - lowest among men (vile ones)
माययापहृतज्ञाना - knowledge stolen by illusion
आसुरं - demonic
भावमाश्रिताः - accepting the demonic nature

Life Application:
Avoid the trap of evil conduct and ignorance that robs people of wisdom and leads them into destructive mindsets.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The evil-doers, the deluded, the lowest of men, do not seek Me; their wisdom being stolen by Maya, they partake of the demonic nature.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The evil-doers, the deluded, the lowest of men, do not seek Me; their wisdom being stolen by Maya, they resort to the demonic nature.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
But those foolish evil-doers, the lowest among men, whose wisdom has been stolen by Maya and who embrace the demonic, atheistic nature, never surrender unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Resorting to demonic nature"—atheistic souls of demonic disposition, controlled by ignorance and pride, remain averse to Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Evil-doers, deluded, lowest among men surrender not unto Me"—foolish, atheistic, and demonic men trapped in ignorance do not seek refuge in Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The evil-doers, the deluded, the lowest of men, do not seek Me; their wisdom being stolen by Maya, they partake of the demonic nature.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Four classes of evil-doers—the foolish, the degraded, those whose knowledge is stolen by Maya, and those of demonic nature—never take refuge in Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Four classes of evil-doers whose wisdom is stolen by Maya fail to surrender unto Supreme Lord due to demonic disposition.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Evil-doers, the foolish, the lowest among men, whose wisdom is stolen by Maya and who follow demonic nature, do not surrender unto Me"—explains four categories of non-believers who, blinded by ignorance and demonic tendencies, fail to seek refuge in Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Sinful deeds and atheistic thoughts breed demonic nature in the heart, depriving the soul of devotion to Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Demonic souls averse to the Lord, trapped in ignorance, fail to perceive the divine Leela and glory of Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Wisdom stolen by Maya"—those whose discernment is veiled by unrighteousness and demonic traits cannot become true seekers.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
The evil-doers, the deluded, the lowest of men, do not seek Me; their wisdom being stolen by Maya, they resort to the demonic nature.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural and psychological analysis of why ignorant and sinful men fail to surrender unto God.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Four classes of sinful souls remain deprived of eternal relationship with Lord Vasudeva due to Maya.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Demonic nature drives the soul away from Krishna-devotion and binds it in the wheel of worldly rebirths.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Wisdom stolen by Maya"—deluded souls infected by perverse knowledge and arrogance fail to worship the Lord.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those miscreants who are grossly foolish, who are lowest among mankind, whose knowledge is stolen by illusion, and who partake of the atheistic nature of demons do not surrender unto Me.
