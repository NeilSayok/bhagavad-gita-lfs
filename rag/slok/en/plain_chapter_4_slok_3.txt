Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sa evāyaṃ mayā te.adya yogaḥ proktaḥ purātanaḥ .
bhakto.asi me sakhā ceti rahasyaṃ hyetaduttamam

Word Meanings:
स - he
एवायं - even this
मया - by Me
तेऽद्य - to you today
योगः - Yoga
प्रोक्तः - taught
पुरातनः - ancient
भक्तोऽसि - you are My devotee
मे - my
सखा - friend
चेति - and thus
रहस्यं - secret
ह्येतदुत्तमम् - indeed this supreme

Life Application:
Approach spiritual learning with trust and genuine friendship, opening your heart to receive deeper life truths.
