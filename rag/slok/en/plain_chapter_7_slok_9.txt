Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
puṇyo gandhaḥ pṛthivyāṃ ca tejaścāsmi vibhāvasau .
jīvanaṃ sarvabhūteṣu tapaścāsmi tapasviṣu

Word Meanings:
पुण्यो - sacred / pure
गन्धः - fragrance
पृथिव्यां - in the earth
च - and
तेजश्चास्मि - and heat I am
विभावसौ - in fire
जीवनं - the life-force
सर्वभूतेषु - in all living beings
तपश्चास्मि - and penance I am
तपस्विषु - in ascetics

Life Application:
Sense God as the sweet fragrance in earth, brilliance in fire, life in all beings, and austerity in spiritual seekers.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am the sweet fragrance in earth and the brilliance in fire; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I am the pure fragrance in earth and the brilliance in fire; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I am the pure untainted fragrance in the earth; I am the brilliant heat in fire; I am the vital life-principle in all living beings, and the holy austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
I am the sweet fragrance in earth and the brilliance in fire; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
I am the sweet fragrance in earth and the brilliance in fire; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
I am the pure fragrance in earth and the brilliance in fire; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
I am the pure fragrance in earth and the brilliance in fire; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The pure fragrance of earth, brilliance of fire, and life of all creatures represent the glory and eternal presence of Supreme Lord Sri Hari.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Pure natural fragrance in earth, heat in fire, vital life-force in living beings, and endurance in ascetics demonstrate the Lord"s immanence.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am the sweet fragrance in earth and the brilliance in fire; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The untainted scent of earth, burning power of fire, life-sustaining potency of all creatures, and austerity of ascetics form My cosmic body.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: O Kaunteya, I am the taste in waters, light in sun and moon, sacred syllable Om in all Vedas, sound in ether, and manhood in men. I am the pure fragrance in earth, brilliance in fire, life in all beings, and austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I am the pure fragrance in earth, brilliance in fire, life in all beings, and austerity in ascetics"—the inherent pure essence of all elements and the vital life-force of creatures are sustained by Lord Vasudeva as their Substratum.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The life-force of all living beings and the austerity of ascetics operate solely by the supreme will and essence of Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The fragrance of earth and the life-art of creatures are the direct supernatural beauty of Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Life in all beings"—the sustenance and life-force of all creatures is impossible without Substratum Brahman, hence Brahman is all.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: I am the pure fragrance in earth and the brilliance in fire; I am the life in all beings and the austerity in ascetics.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural explanation of the divine nature of earth"s fragrance and the life-principle of all creatures.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The existence, life, and penance of all beings become meaningful solely through Lord Vasudeva"s continuous indwelling.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The ascetic devotion of sages and the life of all beings are the direct grace-potency of Lord Krishna.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Life in all beings"—the Supreme Self is the life-breath of creatures and the inner purity of ascetics.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am the original fragrance of the earth, and I am the heat in fire. I am the life of all that lives, and I am the penances of all ascetics.
