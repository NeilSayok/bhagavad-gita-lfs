Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
kṣipraṃ bhavati dharmātmā śaśvacchāntiṃ nigacchati .
kaunteya pratijānīhi na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśyati

Word Meanings:
क्षिप्रं - quickly
भवति - becomes
धर्मात्मा - righteous soul
शश्वच्छान्तिं - eternal peace
निगच्छति - attains
कौन्तेय - O son of Kunti
प्रतिजानीहि - proclaim / know for certain
न - not
मे - my
भक्तः - devotee
प्रणश्यति - perishes

Life Application:
Trust that genuine devotion quickly transforms a person into a righteous soul; God's devotee never perishes.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Soon he becomes righteous and attains eternal peace; O son of Kunti, know you for certain that My devotee never perishes.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Soon he becomes righteous"—by divine grace, evil intellect is destroyed in a moment; it is an absolute truth that Vishnu"s devotee never perishes.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Unswerving devotion burns all mental impurities instantly, rendering the seeker supremely righteous and peacefully liberated; a devotee of God never suffers ruin.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Soon he becomes righteous and attains eternal peace; O Kaunteya, know for certain that My devotee never perishes.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: By the power of devotion, all sins are expelled and the soul swiftly becomes righteous, attaining eternal peace. O Kaunteya, declare boldly: My devotee never perishes!

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord is equal to all beings, without hatred or partiality; yet those who worship Him with devotion abide in Him, and He in them. Even a deeply wicked person worshipping with undivided devotion swiftly becomes virtuous and attains eternal peace, and the Lord"s devotee never perishes.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Soon he becomes righteous and attains eternal peace"—through the power of devotion, even a sinner quickly becomes righteous and gains Mukti. "O son of Kunti, proclaim that My devotee never perishes"—go into the assembly of scholars and boldly declare that a true devotee of Lord Vasudeva is never destroyed.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The potency of Hari-devotion is infallible; an exclusive devotee swiftly gains firm righteousness and eternal peace, never suffering downfall.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: By the Lord"s grace, even a sinner becomes a supreme pure devotee; Lord Krishna Himself causes Arjuna to proclaim His pledge of protecting devotees.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "My devotee never perishes"—an exclusive devotee of Lord Vasudeva never suffers destruction in any situation; devotion purifies his mind quickly to grant eternal salvation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Soon he becomes righteous and attains eternal peace; O Kaunteya, know for certain that My devotee never perishes.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the infallible power of devotion; remembrance of God destroys wickedness, and the seeker attains eternal peace of Mukti.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord"s solemn pledge is that His surrendered devotee never suffers ruin, but attains the eternal state of salvation.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: A devotee of the Lord never perishes; God Himself holds His devotee"s hand and leads him to ultimate peace.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Manifestation of devotion dissolves all ignorance-born sins; "My devotee never perishes" is the eternal truth of God.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
He quickly becomes righteous and attains lasting peace. O son of Kunti, declare it boldly that My devotee never perishes.
