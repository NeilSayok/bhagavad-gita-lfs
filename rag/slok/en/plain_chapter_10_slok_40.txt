Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
nānto.asti mama divyānāṃ vibhūtīnāṃ parantapa .
eṣa tūddeśataḥ prokto vibhūtervistaro mayā

Word Meanings:
नान्तोऽस्ति - there is no end
मम - my
दिव्यानां - of divine
विभूतीनां - manifestations
परन्तप - O scorcher of foes
एष - this
तूद्देशतः - briefly indeed
प्रोक्तो - has been declared
विभूतेर्विस्तरो - extent of glory
मया - by Me

Life Application:
Understand that there is no end to divine manifestations; what has been declared is merely a brief illustration.

There is no end to My divine opulences, O conqueror of foes (Arjuna); what has been stated by Me is merely a brief illustration of My infinite glory.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: O scorcher of foes, there is no limit to My divine opulences, for the manifestations of the Omnipotent Lord are boundless. What has been declared by Me regarding the extent of My opulences is only a brief indication.
