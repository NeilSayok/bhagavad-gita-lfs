Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
aśraddadhānāḥ puruṣā dharmasyāsya parantapa .
aprāpya māṃ nivartante mṛtyusaṃsāravartmani

Word Meanings:
अश्रद्दधानाः - without faith
पुरुषा - persons
धर्मस्यास्य - of this dharma
परन्तप - O scorcher of foes
अप्राप्य - without attaining
मां - Me
निवर्तन्ते - they return
मृत्युसंसारवर्त्मनि - in path of death and rebirth

Life Application:
Cultivate faith in spiritual truth, lest lacking faith you fail to reach God and remain trapped in mortality.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Persons lacking faith in this Dharma, O oppressor of foes, fail to attain Me and return to the path of the mortal world.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Persons lacking faith in this Dharma, O oppressor of foes, return to the path of the mortal world without attaining Me.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Those who lack faith in this sacred Dharma, O scorcher of foes, fail to reach Me and remain trapped in the cycle of birth and death.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Persons lacking faith in this Dharma, O oppressor of foes, return to the path of the mortal world without attaining Me.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Persons lacking faith in this Dharma, O oppressor of foes, return to the path of the mortal world without attaining Me.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Persons lacking faith in this Dharma, O oppressor of foes, return to the path of the mortal world without attaining Me.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Persons lacking faith in this Dharma, O oppressor of foes, return to the path of the mortal world without attaining Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Persons lacking faith in the supreme Dharma of self-knowledge drown in the world cycle; faith is the mother of all spiritual disciplines.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Persons lacking faith in this Dharma, O oppressor of foes, return to the path of the mortal world without attaining Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Those who lack faith in this most excellent and accessible worship of God remain unfit for liberation and wander endlessly in the ocean of samsara.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those who lack faith in this supreme Dharma of Self-realization and exclusive devotion fail to attain the Supreme Lord and wander endlessly in the cycle of birth and death.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Persons devoid of faith in this Dharma"—those who lack faith in the right vision of Self-knowledge, fail to attain Me, the Supreme Lord, and return repeatedly to the mortal pathway of Samsara.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Faith is the root of God-realization; the faithless soul, deprived of the Lord's intrinsic bliss, wanders in the world of rebirth.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Devoid of faith"—faith is the threshold to right knowledge; those lacking faith, even upon gaining the means of Mukti, fail to reach the Supreme Self.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Persons lacking faith in this Dharma, O oppressor of foes, return to the path of the mortal world without attaining Me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Demonstrating that lack of faith is the primary obstacle to liberation; without faith, neither knowledge nor devotion can mature.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Faith is the core qualification in divine worship; faithless persons, devoid of all spiritual means, fall into samsara.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Unwavering trust in God is the foundation of devotion; those devoid of faith remain deprived of divine nectar.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The light of wisdom does not dawn in a faithless heart; hence the faithless soul falls back into the well of worldly existence.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those who are not faithful in this devotional service cannot attain Me, O conqueror of enemies. Therefore they return to the path of birth and death in this material world.
