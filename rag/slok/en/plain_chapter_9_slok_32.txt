Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
māṃ hi pārtha vyapāśritya ye.api syuḥ pāpayonayaḥ .
striyo vaiśyāstathā śūdrāste.api yānti parāṃ gatim

Word Meanings:
मां - Me
हि - certainly
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
व्यपाश्रित्य - taking refuge in
येऽपि - whoever even
स्युः - may be
पापयोनयः - of sinful birth
स्त्रियो - women
वैश्यास्तथा - merchants as well
शूद्रास्तेऽपि - laborers also
यान्ति - attain
परां - the supreme
गतिम् - destination

Life Application:
Take refuge in God regardless of your social background or past, for all who surrender reach the supreme destination.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
For taking refuge in Me, O Partha, even those who may be of sinful birth—women, Vaishyas, as well as Shudras—attain the Supreme Goal.

O Partha, even those who are born of sinful origins, women, Vaishyas, or Shudras—by surrendering unto Me, they all attain the Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Divine refuge is open to all; birth, gender, or social status are no barriers to God-realization; surrender alone opens the door to Mukti.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
For taking refuge in Me, O Partha, even those who may be of sinful birth—women, Vaishyas as well as Shudras—attain the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: In divine surrender there is no distinction of caste or birth; women, Vaishyas, Shudras, or those of low origin, taking refuge in Me, attain supreme liberation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: O Arjuna! Taking exclusive refuge in Me, even women, Vaishyas, Shudras, and those of low origin attain the supreme goal; how much more then pious Brahmanas and royal sages! Therefore, having come into this impermanent joyless world, fix your mind on Me, become My exclusive devotee, and surrender unto Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Taking refuge in Me, even those of sinful birth"—O Partha, even those lacking scriptural study qualification, including women, Vaishyas, and Shudras, attain the Supreme Goal of liberation by taking unswerving refuge in Me.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: In Pushti devotion, all souls are equally eligible for grace; transcending gender and birth distinctions, exclusive divine refuge yields the supreme goal.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Those of sinful birth"—boundless is the glory of devotion; even souls lacking formal Vedic eligibility achieve direct liberation through total surrender to God.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: For taking refuge in Me, O Partha, even those who may be of sinful birth—women, Vaishyas as well as Shudras—attain the Supreme Goal.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining universal accessibility of divine refuge; defects of birth or social rank are totally wiped out by God"s grace, bestowing supreme salvation.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: No lack of worldly qualification hinders exclusive surrender to the Lord; even souls of humble birth reach Vaikuntha through divine refuge.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: The door of devotion to God is open to all; whoever comes under the Lord"s shelter surely attains supreme mukti.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Taking refuge in devotion renders external birth distinctions meaningless; all souls become capable of attaining the Supreme Self.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O son of Pritha, those who take shelter in Me, though they be of lower birth—women, vaishyas [merchants] and shudras [workers]—can attain the supreme destination.
