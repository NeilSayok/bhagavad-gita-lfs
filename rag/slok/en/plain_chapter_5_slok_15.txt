Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
nādatte kasyacitpāpaṃ na caiva sukṛtaṃ vibhuḥ .
ajñānenāvṛtaṃ jñānaṃ tena muhyanti jantavaḥ

Word Meanings:
नादत्ते - does not accept
कस्यचित्पापं - anyone's sin
न - not
चैव - and also
सुकृतं - virtuous deed
विभुः - the all-pervading Lord
अज्ञानेनावृतं - shrouded by ignorance
ज्ञानं - wisdom
तेन - by that
मुह्यन्ति - are deluded
जन्तवः - living beings

Life Application:
Dispel personal delusion by recognizing that divine presence remains untainted by human errors or passing claims.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Lord takes neither the evil nor the good deed of any; wisdom is enveloped by ignorance; thereby people are deluded.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The Lord takes neither the self-reproach nor the merit of any; wisdom is enveloped by ignorance; thereby people are deluded.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The All-pervading Supreme Being accepts neither the sins nor the merits of anyone; pure Wisdom remains covered by ignorance, and thereby all living beings are deluded into egoism and attachment.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Receives neither sin nor virtue"—Lord Vishnu is untainted by the sins or merits of living entities, acting as the just dispenser of karma.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The All-pervading Supreme Self, being unattached and immutable, receives neither sin nor merit of anyone; veiled by ignorance, souls falsely delude themselves as actors.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The Supreme Lord accepts neither sin nor virtue for Himself; obscured by ignorance, souls fall into delusion owing to latent karmic tendencies.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "The Lord takes neither the evil nor the good deed of any; wisdom is enveloped by ignorance; thereby people are deluded"—reveals that the Supreme Lord is untainted by human karma, while Avidya causes the illusion of agency and enjoyership.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Ignorance is the soul"s bondage, obstructing it from apprehending God"s non-doing and spotless nature.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna is beyond the sins and merits of living beings, embodying Existence-Consciousness-Bliss; without devotional knowledge, the soul remains deluded by Maya.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Knowledge is veiled by ignorance, deluding creatures"—because fundamental Avidya veils Self-knowledge, the soul falsely assumes agency and transmigrates.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving that the All-pervading Lord is detached from human sin and virtue, while Maya creates worldly delusion.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva is merely the witness of individual karma; it is the soul"s own ignorance that subjects it to bondage.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the veil of ignorance is removed by Krishna"s grace, the soul experiences its pure Sat-Chit-Ananda nature.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Knowledge is enveloped by ignorance"—veiled by Avidya, the soul falsely identifies with the body, undergoing worldly transmigration.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Nor does the Supreme Lord assume anyone"s sinful or pious activities. Embodied beings, however, are bewildered because of the ignorance which covers their real knowledge.
