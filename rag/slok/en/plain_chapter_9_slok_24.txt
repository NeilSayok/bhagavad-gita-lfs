Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ahaṃ hi sarvayajñānāṃ bhoktā ca prabhureva ca .
na tu māmabhijānanti tattvenātaścyavanti te

Word Meanings:
अहं - I
हि - certainly
सर्वयज्ञानां - of all sacrifices
भोक्ता - enjoyer
च - and
प्रभुरेव - Lord indeed
च - and
न - not
तु - indeed
मामभिजानन्ति - know Me
तत्त्वेनातश्च्यवन्ति - in truth, hence they fall
ते - they

Life Application:
Remember that God is the sole enjoyer and Lord of all sacrifices; failing to recognize this leads to falling back.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
For I alone am the enjoyer and also the Lord of all sacrifices; but they do not know Me in reality, hence they fall.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "For I am the enjoyer of all sacrifices"—Lord Vishnu alone is the supreme enjoyer and master; worshippers of demigods who fail to know Vishnu"s supremacy remain bound to worldly birth.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Lord Vasudeva alone is the enjoyer and lord of all rites; seekers who fail to recognize Him abiding within demigods fall back to mortal world after enjoying fruits.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
For I alone am the Enjoyer and also the Lord of all sacrifices; but they do not know Me in reality, hence they fall.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: I am the primary enjoyer and Lord of all sacrifices; those who fail to recognize Me as the Inner Ruler of demigods receive finite rewards and fall into rebirth.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Even those who worship demigods with faith worship Me alone ignorantly; failing to realize My supreme reality through improper knowledge, they go to demigods, ancestors, and spirits, whereas My exclusive devotees attain Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "For I am the enjoyer and Lord of all sacrifices"—I am the true enjoyer and fruit-dispenser of all rituals; but failing to know My divine Reality in truth, they fall from heaven back into Samsara.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Ignorance of Lord Vishnu, Master of all sacrifices, is the sole cause of downfall; Hari"s devotees attain mukti, while the ignorant inevitably fall.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna Himself is the supreme enjoyer and Lord of all sacrifices; those who fail to recognize Him as Universal Guru fall from their position.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Supreme Brahman alone is the enjoyer and rewarder of all rites; due to ignorance of Divine Reality, desire-seekers fall back to rebirth after heavenly delights.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: For I alone am the enjoyer and also the Lord of all sacrifices; but they do not know Me in reality, hence they fall.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the Lord"s role as enjoyer of all sacrifices; those ignorant of God"s true nature fall back to mortal earth after consuming transient celestial fruits.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord is the primary object of worship and enjoyer in all sacrifices; those who do not recognize Him as Supreme Lord suffer downfall.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: I am the enjoyer of all sacrifices; failing to recognize Me, souls enjoy minor pleasures and fall back into rebirth.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Ignorance of the Supreme Lord, Master of all sacrifices, causes desire-driven worshippers to fall from heaven back into the mortal world.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am the only enjoyer and master of all sacrifices. Therefore, those who do not recognize My true transcendental nature fall down.
