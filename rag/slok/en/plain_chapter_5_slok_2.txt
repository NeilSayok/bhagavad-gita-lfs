Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
saṃnyāsaḥ karmayogaśca niḥśreyasakarāvubhau .
tayostu karmasaṃnyāsātkarmayogo viśiṣyate

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
संन्यासः - renunciation
कर्मयोगश्च - and karma yoga
निःश्रेयसकरावुभौ - both lead to liberation
तयोस्तु - but of the two
कर्मसंन्यासात्कर्मयोगो - karma-yoga than renunciation
विशिष्यते - is superior

Life Application:
Choose active, unselfish service over passive withdrawal, knowing that dedicated work is easier and brings higher fulfillment.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Blessed Lord said: Renunciation and the Yoga of action both lead to the highest bliss; but of the two, the Yoga of action is superior to the renunciation of action.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The Blessed Lord said: Renunciation and the Yoga of action both lead to the highest bliss; but of the two, the Yoga of action is superior to the renunciation of action.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The Blessed Lord said: Both the renunciation of actions (Jnana Yoga) and selfless action (Karma Yoga) lead to the ultimate liberation; yet of these two, Karma Yoga is superior to pure physical renunciation because it is easier to practice and purifies the mind rapidly.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Both grant highest bliss"—renunciation and Karma-Yoga both grant Mukti, but Karma-Yoga performed with dedication to the Lord is superior to mere withdrawal.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Both paths ultimately lead to liberation, but for one who has not yet attained inner purity, Karma-Yoga is far easier and superior to Sannyasa.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Karma-Yoga is the easy and obstacle-free means to Self-realization; hence it is superior to isolated knowledge-renunciation for an aspirant.

Sri Shankaracharya states: "The Blessed Lord said: Renunciation and the Yoga of action both lead to the highest bliss; but of the two, the Yoga of action is superior to the renunciation of action"—clarifies that for one bound by ego, Karma-Yoga is far superior and practical.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Karma-Yoga performed as service to Lord Krishna automatically bestows the fruit of supreme renunciation.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Karma-Yoga is superior"—without mental purity, true Sannyasa is impossible; hence Karma-Yoga is specifically superior for an aspirant.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving the supreme practicality and purity-granting capacity of Karma-Yoga over formal Sannyasa.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Karma-Yoga offered to Lord Vasudeva carries no risk of spiritual downfall, making it superior to Sannyasa.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna clarifies that selfless Karma-Yoga easily grants the practitioner the ultimate perfection of Sannyasa.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Karma-Yoga is superior"—as the primary cause of mind purification leading to Mukti, Karma-Yoga is the primary duty of an aspirant.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Personality of Godhead replied: The renunciation of work and work in devotion are both good for liberation. But, of the two, work in devotional service is better than renunciation of work.
