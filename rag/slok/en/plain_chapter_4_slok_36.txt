Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
api cedasi pāpebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ pāpakṛttamaḥ .
sarvaṃ jñānaplavenaiva vṛjinaṃ santariṣyasi

Word Meanings:
अपि - even
चेदसि - even if you are
पापेभ्यः - than sinners
सर्वेभ्यः - all
पापकृत्तमः - greatest sinner
सर्वं - all
ज्ञानप्लवेनैव - by boat of wisdom alone
वृजिनं - ocean of sin
सन्तरिष्यसि - will cross over

Life Application:
Never give up hope over past mistakes, trusting that spiritual wisdom has the power to transform any life.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "By the raft of Knowledge thou shalt cross over all sin"—the authentic wisdom of Lord Vishnu is the infallible raft carrying souls across all sins and worldly miseries.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Boarding the sturdy raft of Self-knowledge, the practitioner effortlessly crosses the vast ocean of sins accumulated over millions of lives.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Even if thou art the most sinful of all sinners, yet by the raft of Knowledge alone shalt thou cross over all sin.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The glory of Self-knowledge is boundless; it enables the practitioner to cross over all accumulated past sins in a single moment.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.36–4.37 jointly): "Api cet etc. and Yathaidhamsi etc."—just as a blazing fire reduces wood to ashes, so does the fire of Knowledge consume all karmas, liberating the practitioner.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Even if thou art the most sinful of all sinners, yet by the raft of Knowledge alone shalt thou cross over all sin"—glorifies Self-knowledge as the supreme raft that ferries even the worst sinner across the ocean of samsara.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Crossing the ocean of worldly transmigrations is impossible without the raft of Self-knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Boarding the raft of Krishna-knowledge enables even the most sinful soul to reach the supreme divine Abode.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "By the raft of Knowledge alone"—the sturdy boat of non-dual Self-realization sublates all accumulated and current sins in a moment.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis substantiating how even the worst sinner crosses all sins via the raft of non-dual Knowledge.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The raft of Vasudeva-knowledge is the sole capable means to cross the boundless ocean of all sins.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Taking refuge in Krishna"s wisdom instantly burns away all sins and imperfections of the seeker.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Shalt cross all sin by the raft of Knowledge"—all nescience-born sins vanish immediately through the boat of Self-awareness.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Even if you are considered to be the most sinful of all sinners, when you are situated in the boat of transcendental knowledge you will be able to cross over the ocean of miseries.
