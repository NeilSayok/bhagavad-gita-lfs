Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
caturvidhā bhajante māṃ janāḥ sukṛtino.arjuna .
ārto jijñāsurarthārthī jñānī ca bharatarṣabha

Word Meanings:
चतुर्विधा - four kinds of virtuous people
भजन्ते - worship
मां - Me
जनाः - people
सुकृतिनोऽर्जुन - pious persons, O Arjuna
आर्तो - the distressed (suffering one)
जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी - the inquisitive and seeker of wealth
ज्ञानी - the man of wisdom
च - and
भरतर्षभ - O best of the Bharatas

Life Application:
Turn to God whether you are in distress, seeking prosperity, asking deep questions, or searching for pure wisdom.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Four kinds of virtuous men worship Me, O Arjuna: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Four kinds of virtuous men worship Me, O Arjuna: the afflicted, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O best of the Bharatas, four kinds of virtuous men of pious deeds worship Me: the distressed, the seeker of wisdom, the seeker of wealth, and the realized man of true wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Four classes of pious devotees—Arta, Jijnasu, Artharthi, and Jnani—seek refuge at the holy feet of Supreme Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Devotion to God sprouts from accumulated virtuous deeds of past lives; four classes of devotees—distressed, seeker, wealth-seeker, and wise—seek Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Four kinds of virtuous men worship Me, O Arjuna: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Four classes of pious souls worship Me: Arta (the fallen seeking lost wealth), Artharthi (seeking new wealth), Jijnasu (seeking Self-realization), and Jnani (seeking God alone).

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Four types of virtuous men worship Me: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise. Of these, the wise one who is ever steadfast in exclusive devotion excels; for I am exceedingly dear to the wise, and he is dear to Me. Noble indeed are all these, but the wise man I consider My very Self. At the end of many births, the wise man surrenders unto Me, realizing "Vasudeva is all"; such a great soul is exceedingly rare.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Four kinds of virtuous men worship Me, O Arjuna: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise"—elucidates four distinct classes of pious devotees who seek Lord Vasudeva based on their spiritual orientation and inner maturity.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Both selfless wisdom and desire-driven prayer, when directed exclusively unto Lord Vishnu, bring exalted merit to the soul.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: All four categories of virtuous devotees surrendering to Lord Krishna experience His grace according to their respective inner feelings.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Virtuous men, O Arjuna"—pious deeds of previous births alone ripen the mind to engage in the worship of Lord Vasudeva.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Four kinds of virtuous men worship Me, O Arjuna: the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the wise.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural analysis of the four categories of virtuous devotees and the dawn of divine devotion.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Four classes of pious devotees seek refuge in Lord Vasudeva according to their spiritual maturity.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna bestows His compassionate grace upon all four classes of virtuous devotees according to their inner attitude.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Four kinds of men worship Me"—due to past merits, distress, inquiry, desire for wealth, or devotion to wisdom dawns in the seeker.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O best among the Bharatas, four kinds of pious men begin to render devotional service unto Me—the distressed, the desirer of wealth, the inquisitive, and he who is searching for knowledge of the Absolute.
