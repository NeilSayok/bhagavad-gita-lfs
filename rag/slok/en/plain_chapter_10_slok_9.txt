Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
maccittā madgataprāṇā bodhayantaḥ parasparam .
kathayantaśca māṃ nityaṃ tuṣyanti ca ramanti ca

Word Meanings:
मच्चित्ता - with minds on Me
मद्गतप्राणा - lives surrendered to Me
बोधयन्तः - enlightening
परस्परम् - one another
कथयन्तश्च - and speaking of My glories
मां - Me
नित्यं - in sacrifice
तुष्यन्ति - they are contented
च - and
रमन्ति - they rejoice
च - and

Life Application:
With thoughts fixed on God and lives surrendered, wise seekers enlighten one another and converse with delight.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
With their minds fixed on Me and their vital forces (lives) surrendered to Me, enlightening one another and ever conversing about Me, My devotees remain ever contented and delighted.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
With their mind and their life wholly absorbed in Me, enlightening each other and ever speaking of Me, they are contented and delighted.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The thoughts of My pure devotees dwell in Me, their lives are fully devoted to My service, and they derive great satisfaction and bliss from enlightening one another and conversing about Me.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
When the mind and vital energies are fully integrated in the Lord, devotees derive supreme contentment and spiritual joy in enlightening each other and constantly discoursing upon the Divine.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
With their minds fixed on Me, their vital functions dedicated to Me, clarifying to each other and narrating of Me always, they are satisfied and delighted.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
With their minds fixed on Me, with their lives dedicated to Me, enlightening one another and speaking of Me always, they are contented and rejoiced.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
With their minds fixed on Me, with their life-forces surrendered to Me, enlightening one another and speaking of Me always, they derive contentment and joy.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The Lord declares that He alone is the creator (prabhava) of this universe, and by His command alone everything moves. Realizing this truth, selfless devotees meditate on and worship Him with pure love.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Describing the discipline of true devotees. Those whose minds are absorbed in the Lord and who cannot sustain their life without Him experience supreme contentment and spiritual bliss by enlightening one another on divine glories.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
With their minds absorbed in Me and their very lives dedicated to Me, My devotees remain ever contented and joyful, enlightening one another and constantly conversing on My names, virtues, and divine sports.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Mac-chittah" means minds fully fixed on God; "Mad-gata-pranah" means those who cannot sustain life without Him. Conversing amongst themselves regarding the Lord's marvelous auspicious attributes, they experience immense contentment and joy.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The great sages, Kumaras, and Manus govern the cosmic order solely through the will and power of the Supreme Lord. Enlightening one another regarding the Lord's divine glory, these enlightened souls abide in supreme bliss.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Mac-chittah" means those whose minds are absorbed in Me, Vasudeva; "Mad-gata-pranah" means those whose senses and vital forces are offered unto Me. Enlightening each other with divine knowledge and conversing on My attributes, they feel ever contented and spiritually delighted.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The Supreme Lord alone is the creator, maintainer, and dissolver of all worlds. Knowing this, wise devotees continuously worship and remember Him with unalloyed love.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The entire cosmic manifestation is the Lord's own divine Form, and all divine play proceeds from Him. Realizing this, great souls endowed with devotional love delight in singing His glories.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Devotees whose inner minds are absorbed in the Lord and who cannot sustain their existence for even a moment without Him experience supreme contentment and ecstatic bliss while imparting spiritual illumination to one another and singing My divine glories.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Devotees whose minds and life-forces are surrendered unto Me experience great satisfaction and joy in illuminating one another with divine knowledge and constantly glorifying My attributes.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Wise seekers whose minds are absorbed in the Supreme Lord constantly discuss divine truths amongst themselves, and by singing the Lord's glorious manifestations, they abide ever contented and immersed in divine love.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Devotees who find separation from God unbearable sustain their very lives solely by mutually listening to and singing the Lord's wondrous auspicious attributes, thereby attaining supreme fulfillment.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Devotees who surrender their minds and lives to the Lord continuously sing His glories and awaken divine love in each other, delighting in supreme satisfaction.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Seekers whose minds are dedicated to God experience supreme tranquility of mind and spiritual bliss by mutually instructing one another in Self-knowledge and chanting the divine names.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The thoughts of My pure devotees dwell in Me, their lives are fully devoted to My service, and they derive great satisfaction and bliss from enlightening one another and conversing about Me.
