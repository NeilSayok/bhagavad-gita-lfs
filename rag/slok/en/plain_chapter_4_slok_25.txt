Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
daivamevāpare yajñaṃ yoginaḥ paryupāsate .
brahmāgnāvapare yajñaṃ yajñenaivopajuhvati

Word Meanings:
दैवमेवापरे - to gods some
यज्ञं - sacrifice
योगिनः - yogis
पर्युपासते - perform / worship
ब्रह्माग्नावपरे - in fire of Brahman others
यज्ञं - sacrifice
यज्ञेनैवोपजुह्वति - offer sacrifice by sacrifice itself

Life Application:
Offer your small daily sacrifices with high intention, dedicating your efforts to universal well-being.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Some Yogis perform sacrifice to the gods alone; while others offer sacrifice by the sacrifice itself into the fire of Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Some Yogis perform sacrifice to the gods alone; while others offer sacrifice by the sacrifice itself into the fire of Brahman.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Some Karma-yogis perform sacrifice offered solely unto demigods; while other Jnana-yogis offer the sacrifice of their individual self into the fire of the Supreme Brahman through the sacrifice of non-dual Knowledge itself.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "In the fire of Brahman others offer"—Lord Vishnu Himself is the true enjoyer and bestower of results for all sacrificial fires and demigod worship.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Karma-yogis perform ritualistic offerings to deities, while Jnana-yogis offer the conditioned self into the fire of non-dual Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Some Yogis perform sacrifice to the gods alone; while others offer sacrifice by the sacrifice itself into the fire of Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Karma-yogis perform sacrifices directed to demigods, whereas Jnana-yogis offer all activities directly into the Supreme Person.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Daivam etc. Some Yogis perform sacrifice to demigods, while others offer the Self into the fire of Brahman through knowledge itself.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Some Yogis perform sacrifice to the gods alone; while others offer sacrifice by the sacrifice itself into the fire of Brahman"—contrasts ritualistic demigod-worship (*Daiva-yajna*) with the supreme Vedantic offering of Self-realization (*Brahma-yajna*).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Discerning demigod worship from Brahman-Yajna is the stepping stone moving from ritualism to wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Loving devotees carry out all sacrifices solely for Krishna"s delight, wherein the fire and oblations are Lord-forms.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Offer sacrifice by sacrifice itself"—sacrificing individual ego into the fire of Brahman via Mahavakya-inquiry is the supreme Sannyasa-yajna.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Some Yogis perform sacrifice to the gods alone; while others offer sacrifice by the sacrifice itself into the fire of Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis highlighting the distinction between ritualistic Daiva-yajna and non-dual Brahma-yajna.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Whether demigod sacrifice or wisdom sacrifice, Vasudeva alone is the Supreme Object of Worship and Yajna-Purusha.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Surrendering one"s very self at Lord Krishna"s lotus feet is the supreme wisdom-sacrifice.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "In the fire of Brahman others offer"—true renunciates dissolve the adjunct of nescience in Brahman through non-dual realization.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Some yogis perfectly worship the demigods by offering different sacrifices to them, and some offer sacrifices in the fire of the Supreme Brahman.
