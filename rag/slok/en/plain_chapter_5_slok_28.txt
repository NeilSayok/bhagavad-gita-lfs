Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yatendriyamanobuddhirmunirmokṣaparāyaṇaḥ .
vigatecchābhayakrodho yaḥ sadā mukta eva saḥ

Word Meanings:
यतेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिर्मुनिर्मोक्षपरायणः - senses, mind and intellect controlled, sage intent on liberation
विगतेच्छाभयक्रोधो - free from desire, fear and anger
यः - who
सदा - always
मुक्त - liberated
एव - also
सः - he

Life Application:
Govern your thoughts and emotions deliberately, releasing fear, desire, and resentment to abide in permanent freedom.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
With senses, mind, and intellect controlled, the sage intent on liberation, free from desire, fear, and anger, is indeed ever liberated.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
With senses, mind, and intellect controlled, the sage intent on liberation, free from desire, fear, and anger, is indeed ever liberated.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
With senses, mind, and intellect fully controlled, the meditative sage (Muni), intent solely upon absolute Liberation (Moksha) and completely freed from desire, fear, and anger, is verily ever liberated (Jivanmukta).

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
With senses, mind, and intellect controlled, the sage intent on liberation, free from desire, fear, and anger, is indeed ever liberated.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "He is indeed ever liberated"—controlling senses and mind to meditate on Lord Vishnu, the sage becomes a Jivanmukta enjoying eternal liberation.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Mastering senses, mind, and intellect, freed from attraction, repulsion, and fear, the meditating sage abides as ever-liberated in this life.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
With senses, mind, and intellect controlled, the sage intent on liberation, free from desire, fear, and anger, is indeed ever liberated.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "He is indeed ever liberated"—the self-controlled sage free from desire, fear, and anger who seeks Moksha alone is liberated while in the body.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Withdrawing external contacts, fixing gaze between eyebrows, balancing Prana-Apana, the sage with controlled senses, mind, and intellect, free from desire, fear, and anger, is ever liberated.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "With senses, mind, and intellect controlled, the sage intent on liberation, free from desire, fear, and anger, is indeed ever liberated"—proclaims that such a practitioner is a *Sada-mukta* (ever-liberated soul).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Complete cessation of desire, fear, and anger constitutes the mind"s true freedom and the state of Jivanmukti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A devotee seeking Krishna"s sole refuge, freed from all desires, abides as ever-liberated, absorbed in the Lord"s supreme joy.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "He is indeed ever liberated"—the seeker remains liberated not only during Samadhi but at all times, even in normal waking state.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
With senses, mind, and intellect controlled, the sage intent on liberation, free from desire, fear, and anger, is indeed ever liberated.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving that the sage who subdues senses, mind, and intellect while abandoning desire, fear, and anger is eternally free.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: A sage devoted to Vasudeva, liberated from all mental afflictions, directly experiences Jivanmukti.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the seeker"s whole mind is dedicated to Krishna, fear and anger completely vanish.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "He is indeed ever liberated"—absolute cessation of desire, fear, and anger is the essential hallmark of eternal liberation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
And who forms the goal of all sacrifices and austerities, the Supreme Lord of all planets and demigods, and the benefactor and well-wisher of all living entities, attains peace from the pangs of material miseries.
