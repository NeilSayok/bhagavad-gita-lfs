Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ye tvetadabhyasūyanto nānutiṣṭhanti me matam .
sarvajñānavimūḍhāṃstānviddhi naṣṭānacetasaḥ

Word Meanings:
ये - who
त्वेतदभ्यसूयन्तो - however finding fault with this
नानुतिष्ठन्ति - do not practice
मे - my
मतम् - teaching
सर्वज्ञानविमूढांस्तान्विद्धि - know them deluded in all knowledge
नष्टानचेतसः - ruined and senseless

Life Application:
Beware of mocking or ignoring wise guidance, as persistent cynicism destroys your capacity for personal growth.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
But those who carp at My teaching and do not practice it, deluded in all knowledge and devoid of discrimination, know them to be ruined.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
But those who carp at My teaching (Abhyasuyantah) and do not practice it (Na anutishthanti me matam), deluded in all knowledge (Sarvajnanavimudhan) and devoid of discrimination (Achetasah), know them to be utterly ruined and lost (Viddhi nashtan).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Ignorant souls who disregard the teachings of Lord Vishnu attain perpetual downfall and ruin.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Atheistic people who carp at the Lord"s teaching and disregard Karma-yoga fall from all spiritual goals and perish.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Foolish people who mock the Lord"s doctrine and abandon Swadharma are permanently deprived (ruined) of Self-realization.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Ye me etc. and Ye tv etat etc. Those who carp at My doctrine and do not practice it, deluded in all knowledge, know them to be ruined; whereas the faithful are freed.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "But those who carp at My teaching and do not practice it, deluded in all knowledge and devoid of discrimination, know them to be ruined"—severely censures those who scoff at divine wisdom and neglect duty.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Fault-finding and envy toward divine wisdom is the ultimate cause of all ignorance and spiritual ruin.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Souls finding fault in Krishna"s divine message turn away from His grace and fall into complete ruin.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: A envious ignorant person falls away completely from all four goals of life—Dharma, Artha, Kama, and Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis substantiating how carping at the Lord"s doctrine leads to total delusion and spiritual destruction.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Demonic-natured people who revile Vasudeva"s scriptures are ruined in respect of all life goals.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a soul disregards the Lord"s doctrine, his inner being sinks into deep darkness and perishes.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Lack of faith in God"s scriptures is the sole cause of the soul"s absolute downfall.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
But those who, out of envy, do not regularly follow these teachings and disregard them are to be considered bereft of all knowledge, befooled, and ruined in their endeavors for perfection.
