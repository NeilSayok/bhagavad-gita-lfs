Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
āsurīṃ yonimāpannā mūḍhā janmani janmani .
māmaprāpyaiva kaunteya tato yāntyadhamāṃ gatim

Life Application:
Entering demonic wombs, deluded birth after birth, failing to reach Me, they sink into lowest degradation.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Entering into demoniacal wombs and deluded, birth after birth, not attaining Me, they thus fall, O Arjuna, into a condition still lower than that.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Entering into demoniacal wombs and deluded, birth after birth, not attaining Me, they thus fall, O Arjuna, into a condition still lower than that.

They are reduced to the very lowest condition or the most appalling state. They are cast into utter darkness. They fall into lower and yet lower wombs in response to their own satanic desires and actions and their extreme Tamasic nature. From the birth of a tiger? they will get the birth of a serpent from a serpents they will get the birth of a wormfrom that they will get the birth of a tree? etc. In Sanskrit the suffix Tara denotes comparative degree and Tama is superlative degree. These people get Nikrishtatama Yonis (the lowest wombs).They do not reach Me as their minds are filled with impurities? as they do not tread the path of righteousness in accordance with the injunctions of the scriptures. The diabolical nature is inimical to spiritual progress. Therefore? you will have to annihilate the evil tendencies by cultivating divine alities and the practice of regular meditation. Then and only then will you attain liberation.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
So reborn, they spend life after life, enveloped in delusion. And they never reach Me, O Prince, but degenerate into still lower forms of life.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Attaining repeated birth amongst the species of demoniac life, O son of Kuntī, such persons can never approach Me. Gradually they sink down to the most abominable type of existence.

Commentary: Swami Chinmayananda explains that as long as a person remains a slave to his lower animalistic impulses, he continues to take such degraded births. He cannot experience the supreme bliss of the Self. However, Hinduism does not support the idea of eternal damnation. Even these fallen souls, after exhausting their sins in lower births, will eventually get human birth again to purify themselves. The Lord shows Arjuna the path of self-improvement so that anyone can escape this hellish cycle by renouncing lust, anger, and greed.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Having come to the demoniac womb, birth after birth, and not attaining Me at all, these deluded persons, therefore, pass to the lowest state, O son of  Kunti !

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Fallen into demoniac wombs in birth after birth, these deluded men, not attaining Me, further sink down to the lowest level, O Arjuna.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Being born among the demoniacal species in births after births, the foods, without ever reaching Me, O son of Kunti, attain conditions lower than that.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhvacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandgiri explains that there is no hope of their spiritual rise as long as they are in subhuman species. The word 'without attaining Me' means they cannot even reach the virtuous path shown by the Lord, which leads to God-realization.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Entering into demoniacal wombs and deluded, birth after birth, not attaining Me, they thus fall, O Arjuna, into a condition still lower than that.

Commentary: Swami Ramsukhdas explains that human life is the only place where a soul can change its nature and attain God. If one wastes this opportunity and lives an Asuri life of lust and pride, he goes to lower animal births. In those animal births, there is no chance to practice spiritual disciplines. They simply exhaust their bad deeds, and if they still maintain their cruel nature, they fall into terrifying hells like Kumbhipaka. The Lord feels deep compassion and sorrow for these souls who reject His open invitation to liberation.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
'These deluded men,' viz., those entertaining perverse knowledge about Me, attain repeatedly births that tend them to be antagonistic to Me. Never 'attaining Me,' viz., never arriving at the knowledge that Vasudeva, the Lord and the ruler of all, truly exists, they go farther and farther down, from that birth, to the lowest level.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Apannah, being born, having acired; (births) asurim, among the demoniacal; yonim, species; janmani janmani, in births after births; the mudhah, fools, non-discriminating ones; being born in every birth into species in which tamas prevails, and going downwards, aprapya eva, without ever reaching, approaching; mam, Me, who am God; O son of Kunti, yanti, they attain; gatim, conditions; tatah adhamam, lower even than that.
Since there is not the least possibility of attaining Me, what is implied by saying, 'without ever reachin Me', is, 'by not attaining the virtuous path enjoined by Me.'
This is being stated as a summary of all the demoniacal alities. The triplet-under which are comprehended all the different demoniacal alities though they are infinite in number, (and) by the avoidance of which (three) they (all the demaniacal alities) become rejected, and which is the root of all evils- is being stated:

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya explains that by never attaining even a connection with the Lord, they sink into the deepest darkness (andhantamas), which is their final lower state.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati explains that one who enters the demoniac womb takes birth in animal and plant kingdoms where it is impossible to perform spiritual practices. The word 'O Kaunteya' is used by Krishna to remind Arjuna of his pure lineage, showing that he is free from such a downfall. Since these fools can never practice the Vedic path, they keep going downwards.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami explains that they never reach Me. Not even attaining the good path that leads to Me, they sink further into the state of worms and insects.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanpati explains that by addressing Arjuna as 'Kaunteya', the Lord reassures him of his safety from this terrible state due to his birth in a noble family. Since Asuri souls cannot access the righteous path of scriptures, they sink forever into lower species.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha explains that since even pure-minded seekers take many births to attain Brahman, there is no chance for these dark-minded people. They go down to the lowest level because they never acquire the scriptural knowledge that 'Vasudeva is all'.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji explains that having entered births hostile to righteous action, they cannot utilize any means to reach God. The word 'O Kaunteya' suggests that because Arjuna is born to a devotee mother, he is fit for God-realization.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelkanth explains that they fall into hellish conditions, or into animal and immovable (plant) forms, where there is no consciousness for self-realization.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Attaining repeated birth amongst the species of demoniac life, O son of Kuntī, such persons can never approach Me. Gradually they sink down to the most abominable type of existence.

 It is known that God is all-merciful, but here we find that God is never merciful to the demoniac. It is clearly stated that the demoniac people, life after life, are put into the wombs of similar demons, and, not achieving the mercy of the Supreme Lord, they go down and down, so that at last they achieve bodies like those of cats, dogs and hogs. It is clearly stated that such demons have practically no chance of receiving the mercy of God at any stage of later life. In the Vedas also it is stated that such persons gradually sink to become dogs and hogs. It may be then argued in this connection that God should not be advertised as all-merciful if He is not merciful to such demons. In answer to this question, in the Vedānta-sūtra we find that the Supreme Lord has no hatred for anyone. The placing of the asuras, the demons, in the lowest status of life is simply another feature of His mercy. Sometimes the asuras are killed by the Supreme Lord, but this killing is also good for them, for in Vedic literature we find that anyone who is killed by the Supreme Lord becomes liberated. There are instances in history of many asuras – Rāvaṇa, Kaṁsa, Hiraṇyakaśipu – to whom the Lord appeared in various incarnations just to kill them. Therefore God’s mercy is shown to the asuras if they are fortunate enough to be killed by Him.
