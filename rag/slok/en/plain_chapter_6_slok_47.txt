Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yogināmapi sarveṣāṃ madgatenāntarātmanā .
śraddhāvānbhajate yo māṃ sa me yuktatamo mataḥ

Word Meanings:
योगिनामपि - even among yogis
सर्वेषां - of all
मद्गतेनान्तरात्मना - with inner mind fixed on Me
श्रद्धावान्भजते - endowed with faith worships
यो - he who
मां - Me
स - he
मे - my
युक्ततमो - most united in yoga
मतः - is regarded.

Life Application:
Offer your deepest faith and love to the divine presence, becoming the most devoted and centered of all practitioners.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
And among all Yogis, he who worships Me with faith, with his inmost self absorbed in Me, he is considered by Me to be the most devout.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
And among all Yogis, he who worships Me with faith, with his inmost self absorbed in Me, he is considered by Me to be the most devout.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
And among all types of Yogis—Karma-yogis, Jnana-yogis, and Hatha-yogis—he who, endowed with supreme unswerving faith, merges his inmost self and mind completely into Me, Lord Vasudeva, worshipping Me with unceasing divine love, is considered by Me to be the most devout and highest Yogi of all.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "He is considered by Me the most devout"—the devotee merging his mind in unswerving faith at Lord Vishnu"s feet is supreme among all yogis.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Among all yogis worshipping various deities, he who fixes his inmost mind in Lord Vasudeva alone with unshakeable faith is considered the most devout (Yuktatama).

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
And among all Yogis, he who worships Me with faith, with his inmost self absorbed in Me, he is considered by Me to be the most devout.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Compared to yogis meditating on lesser powers, he who surrenders his inmost soul to Me Vasudeva with divine love is the highest of all yogis (Yuktatama).

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Yogi is thought to be superior to ascetics, superior even to men of scriptural knowledge, and superior to ritualists; therefore be a Yogi, O Arjuna. And among all Yogis, he who worships Me with faith, with his inmost self absorbed in Me, is regarded by Me as the most devout and highest Yogi.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "And among all Yogis, he who worships Me with faith, with his inmost self absorbed in Me, he is considered by Me to be the most devout"—concludes Chapter 6 by establishing devotion to Supreme Brahman Vasudeva (*Mad-gatenantaratmana*) as the ultimate pinnacle of all Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Inmost self absorbed in Me" means absolute surrender into Vasudeva"s divine form; this devotion is the zenith of Ashtanga-Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The devotee absorbing his mind in Krishna"s form and engaging in exclusive devotion is the crown jewel "Yuktatama" among all yogis.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "He is considered by Me the highest yogi"—the devotee absorbed with unswerving faith in Lord Vasudeva is supreme among all yogis.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
And among all Yogis, he who worships Me with faith, with his inmost self absorbed in Me, he is considered by Me to be the most devout.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis concluding Chapter 6 by establishing the supreme standing of the devout worshipper of the Lord.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The king of yogis possessing unswerving devotion at Lord Vasudeva"s feet is the most devout of all.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna proclaims His unswerving loving devotee as the crown jewel among all yogis in existence.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "He is considered by Me the most devout"—absorption of the inner self into Sat-Chit-Ananda Vasudeva is the ultimate fruit of all Yoga.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
And of all yogis, the one with great faith who always abides in Me, thinks of Me within himself, and renders transcendental loving service to Me—he is the most intimately united with Me in yoga and is the highest of all. That is My opinion.
