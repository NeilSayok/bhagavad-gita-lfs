Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
bṛhatsāma tathā sāmnāṃ gāyatrī chandasāmaham .
māsānāṃ mārgaśīrṣo.ahamṛtūnāṃ kusumākaraḥ

Word Meanings:
बृहत्साम - Brihat-sama hymn
तथा - so
साम्नां - among Sama hymns
गायत्री - Gayatri mantra
छन्दसामहम् - among meters I am
मासानां - among months
मार्गशीर्षोऽहमृतूनां - Margashirsha I am, of seasons
कुसुमाकरः - spring (season of flowers)

Life Application:
Know God as Brihat-sama among hymns, Gayatri among meters, Margashirsha among months, and spring among seasons.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among Sama-veda hymns I am the Brihat-sama; among poetic meters I am Gayatri; among months I am Margashirsha (Nov-Dec); among seasons I am Spring (the season of flowers).

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among hymns also I am the Brihat-sama; among meters I am Gayatri; among months I am Margasirsha; among seasons I am the flowery season (spring).

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of the hymns of Sama-Veda I am the Brihat-sama; of poetic meters I am Gayatri. Of months I am Margashirsha, and of seasons I am Spring, the bearer of flowers.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among Sama hymns, Brihat-sama; among meters, the sacred Gayatri; among months, Margashirsha (season of harvest); and among seasons, flower-filled Spring (Kusumakara) represents the Divine.
