Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yo.antaḥsukho.antarārāmastathāntarjyotireva yaḥ .
sa yogī brahmanirvāṇaṃ brahmabhūto.adhigacchati

Word Meanings:
योऽन्तःसुखोऽन्तरारामस्तथान्तर्ज्योतिरेव - happy within, rejoicing within, illumined within
यः - who
स - that
योगी - yogi
ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं - liberation in Brahman (Moksha)
ब्रह्मभूतोऽधिगच्छति - becoming Brahman, attains

Life Application:
Discover the joy of your own quiet inner presence, letting self-illumination guide your daily choices and interactions.
