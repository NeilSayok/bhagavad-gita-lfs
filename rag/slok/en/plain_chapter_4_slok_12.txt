Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
kāṅkṣantaḥ karmaṇāṃ siddhiṃ yajanta iha devatāḥ .
kṣipraṃ hi mānuṣe loke siddhirbhavati karmajā

Word Meanings:
काङ्क्षन्तः - longing for
कर्मणां - of actions
सिद्धिं - success
यजन्त - worship
इह - here
देवताः - the gods
क्षिप्रं - quickly
हि - certainly
मानुषे - in human world
लोके - in the world
सिद्धिर्भवति - success arises
कर्मजा - born of action

Life Application:
Recognize that short-term material rewards come quickly, but invest your deepest energy in lasting spiritual growth.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Quick is success born of action"—rewards obtained by worshipping other deities are fleeting, whereas selfless worship of Lord Vishnu bestows eternal Vaikuntha.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Fruitive individuals worship lesser deities seeking swift temporal rewards, whereas the wise seek only the Supreme Lord selflessly.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Because temporal rewards manifest quickly in this mortal realm, small-minded people worship lesser gods, whereas the salvation-seeker worships Vasudeva alone.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: In whatever manner men approach Me, I reward them accordingly; those seeking immediate success in actions worship lesser deities here, obtaining temporary rewards.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Longing for success in action, men sacrifice to the gods here; for quick in this human world is success born of action"—explains why most human beings pursue fruitive rituals (*Karmaja siddhi*) rather than selfless liberation.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Karmic fruits are transitory; hence the prudent seeker shuns impermanent deity-worship to embrace eternal Vishnu-bhakti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Fruitive seekers worship other demigods out of longing for swift rewards, but the desireless devotee delights solely in Krishna"s service.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Success born of action comes quickly"—because fruitive actions yield fast results in the human realm, most ignorant souls worship demigods.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving why fruitive-minded individuals worship demigods due to the swift attainment of worldly desires.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Worship of Vasudeva alone bestows eternal results; those seeking petty rewards take refuge in lesser deities.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When the practitioner"s mind turns desireless, he abandons fleeting demigod worship to choose Krishna"s unswerving love.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Success born of action"—karmic fruits ultimately end in sorrow, hence the seeker of liberation must embrace Jnana and non-action.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Men in this world desire success in fruitive activities, and therefore they worship the demigods. Quickly, of course, men get results from fruitive work in this world.
