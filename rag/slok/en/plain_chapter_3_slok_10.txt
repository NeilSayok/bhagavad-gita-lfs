Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sahayajñāḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā purovāca prajāpatiḥ .
anena prasaviṣyadhvameṣa vo.astviṣṭakāmadhuk

Word Meanings:
सहयज्ञाः - along with sacrifice
प्रजाः - mankind
सृष्ट्वा - having created
पुरोवाच - said of old
प्रजापतिः - the Creator
अनेन - by this
प्रसविष्यध्वमेष - may you multiply, this
वोऽस्त्विष्टकामधुक् - be your milker of desires

Life Application:
Contribute your share to the world generously, trusting that mutual service and cooperation sustain all life.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Prajapati (the Creator), having in the beginning created mankind together with Yajna, said: "By this shall ye propagate; let this be the milch-cow of your desires."

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Sacrificial institution ordained by Supreme Lord Vishnu serves as the wish-fulfilling tree for righteous desires.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: At creation"s dawn, Brahma created mankind alongside Yajna, ordaining Yajna as the universal fulfiller of righteous desires.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Prajapati created souls equipped with the duty of Yajna, commanding them to attain material prosperity and liberation through Yajna.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 3.10–3.11 jointly): "Sahayajnah etc. and Devan etc."—the Creator established co-relationship between Yajna and mankind for attaining supreme good through mutual nourishment.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "The Prajapati, having in the beginning created mankind together with Yajna, said: By this shall ye propagate; let this be the milch-cow of your desires"—highlights the cosmic design of Yajna as the link between humanity and prosperity.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Creation of Yajna by Prajapati proves that selfless duty is the eternal obligation of all living beings.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: In Sri Hari"s divine creation, Yajna forms the harmonious medium of joy between souls and the cosmos.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: The eternal sacrificial tradition serves as the ladder to Dharma, Artha, Kama, and ultimately Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving Prajapati"s creation of Yajna as the wish-fulfilling means for societal stability.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Yajna ordained by Prajapati for Vasudeva"s satisfaction is the foundation of all welfare.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When mankind engages in Krishna-yajna, all cosmic forces fulfill their righteous desires.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Practice of Yajna is the fundamental rule for prosperity and initial mental purification.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
In the beginning of creation, the Lord of all creatures sent forth generations of men and demigods, along with sacrifices for Visnu, and blessed them by saying, "Be thou happy by this yajna [sacrifice] because its performance will bestow upon you everything desirable for living happily and achieving liberation."
