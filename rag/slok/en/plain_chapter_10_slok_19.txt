Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi divyā hyātmavibhūtayaḥ .
prādhānyataḥ kuruśreṣṭha nāstyanto vistarasya me

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
हन्त - now indeed
ते - they
कथयिष्यामि - I shall tell
दिव्या - divine
ह्यात्मविभूतयः - My divine manifestations
प्राधान्यतः - prominent ones
कुरुश्रेष्ठ - O best of Kurus
नास्त्यन्तो - there is no end
विस्तरस्य - to the extent
मे - my

Life Application:
Listen as God describes His prominent divine manifestations, for there is no end to divine expanse.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Blessed Lord said: Very well, O best of the Kurus! I will declare to you My divine opulences in their prominent aspects; for there is no end to My detailed manifestations.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The Blessed Lord said: Very well! Now I will declare to thee My divine glories in their prominence, O best of the Kurus; for there is no end to My detailed extent.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: Yes, I will tell you of My splendorous manifestations, but only of those that are prominent, O best of the Kurus, for there is no end to My extent.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Pleased with Arjuna's earnest devotion, Lord Krishna agrees to reveal His divine manifestations. Since an exhaustive enumeration of the infinite Lord is impossible, He chooses to highlight only the most prominent manifestations.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
The Lord said: Ah! I shall declare to you My divine manifestations in their order of prominence, O best of the Kurus; for there is no end to My detailed manifestations.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
The Lord said: Yes, I shall declare to you My divine manifestations in their order of prominence, O best of the Kurus; for there is no end to My extent.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Highly pleased with Arjuna's selfless devotion, the Lord declares that since His manifestations are limitless, He will reveal only the main opulences that serve as accessible focal points for meditation.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The Supreme Lord said: O best of the Kurus, I shall now declare to you My divine opulences in their prominence; for there is no limit to My detailed manifestations.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The Supreme Lord declares: O best of the Kurus, My auspicious manifestations and Yogic powers are infinite. Hence I shall expound only those that are prominent, for there is no end to My detailed expansion.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Graciously responding to Arjuna's prayer, the Supreme Lord promises to reveal the chief divine manifestations from His infinite Being.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: The Lord says—O best of the Kurus, there is no boundary to the expansion of My Vibhutis; therefore I shall state only those prominent divine manifestations suitable for meditation.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Out of grace for Arjuna, the Lord enumerates the chief manifestations from His infinite opulences so that the practitioner may perceive the Supreme Ruler in every noble form.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Due to the infinite nature of His manifestations, the Lord reveals them 'in prominence' alone, enabling the devotee to easily experience the Divine Presence everywhere.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna declares that since His opulences are endless, He will describe only the most prominent divine manifestations.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The Lord assures Arjuna that although the expanse of His manifestations is boundless, He will highlight the principal ones for the purpose of contemplation.
