Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
jñānena tu tadajñānaṃ yeṣāṃ nāśitamātmanaḥ .
teṣāmādityavajjñānaṃ prakāśayati tatparam

Word Meanings:
ज्ञानेन - by knowledge
तु - indeed
तदज्ञानं - that ignorance
येषां - whose
नाशितमात्मनः - destroyed of self
तेषामादित्यवज्ज्ञानं - their knowledge, sun-like
प्रकाशयति - illumines
तत्परम् - that Supreme Reality

Life Application:
Let the light of self-knowledge illuminate your mind today, dispelling doubts and fears like the morning sun.
