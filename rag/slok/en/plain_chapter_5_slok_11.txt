Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
kāyena manasā buddhyā kevalairindriyairapi .
yoginaḥ karma kurvanti saṅgaṃ tyaktvātmaśuddhaye

Word Meanings:
कायेन - with the body
मनसा - by the mind
बुद्ध्या - by intellect
केवलैरिन्द्रियैरपि - even by mere senses
योगिनः - the yogis
कर्म - action
कुर्वन्ति - perform
सङ्गं - attachment
त्यक्त्वात्मशुद्धये - abandoning, for purification

Life Application:
Use your daily work as an instrument for mental purification, acting with care while releasing selfish attachment.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Yogis, having abandoned attachment, perform actions only with the body, mind, intellect, and even by the senses, for the purification of the self.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Yogis, having abandoned attachment, perform actions only with the body, mind, intellect, and even by the senses, for the purification of the self.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The Karma-yogis, completely relinquishing attachment and egoism, perform duties using their body, mind, intellect, and purified senses, solely for the purification of the mind.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Yogis, having abandoned attachment, perform actions only with the body, mind, intellect, and even by the senses, for the purification of the self.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "For self-purification"—actions performed for the pleasure of Lord Vishnu wash away all mental impurities of the jiva.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Selfless Karma-yogis devoid of possessiveness perform actions using body, mind, intellect, and senses purely for mental purification and eligibility for Knowledge.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Yogis, having abandoned attachment, perform actions only with the body, mind, intellect, and even by the senses, for the purification of the self.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "For the purification of the self"—Karma-yogis perform duties using body and senses, abandoning reward-desire, solely for mental purity.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: He who is devoted to Yoga, pure in self, master of his body and senses, who realizes his own Self as the Self of all beings, is not tainted though acting. The knower of Truth, centered in Yoga, while seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, walking, sleeping, breathing, speaking, releasing, grasping, opening and closing eyes, thinks: "I do nothing at all; the senses merely move among their objects." He who performs actions offering them to Brahman, abandoning attachment, is not stained by sin, even as a lotus leaf is untouched by water. Yogis perform actions without attachment, with body, mind, intellect, and senses alone, solely for the purification of the self.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Yogis, having abandoned attachment, perform actions only with the body, mind, intellect, and even by the senses, for the purification of the self"—clarifies that *Kevalaih* means devoid of possessiveness (*Mamata-rahitah*), acting solely for *Atma-vishuddhi* (purity of heart).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Kevalaih" means devoid of selfish desire and possessiveness, transforming action into pure devotion.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Body, mind, intellect, and senses employed in Krishna"s service naturally become pure, opening the door to divine communion.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Abandoning attachment for self-purification"—action performed after renouncing egoism and possessiveness is the primary means of purifying the mind.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Yogis, having abandoned attachment, perform actions only with the body, mind, intellect, and even by the senses, for the purification of the self.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of performing actions with body, mind, intellect, and senses without attachment for self-purification.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Sensory functions offered to Vasudeva turn into Karma-Yoga, bestowing ultimate purification upon the soul.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When all senses of the practitioner are engaged in Lord Krishna"s service, all inner impurities are washed away naturally.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "For self-purification"—acting free from egoism removes mental impurities, opening the highway to Self-realization.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The yogis, abandoning attachment, act with body, mind, intellect and even with the senses, only for the purpose of purification.
