Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
bhūmirāpo.analo vāyuḥ khaṃ mano buddhireva ca .
ahaṃkāra itīyaṃ me bhinnā prakṛtiraṣṭadhā

Word Meanings:
भूमिरापोऽनलो - earth, water, fire
वायुः - air
खं - ether
मनो - mind
बुद्धिरेव - intellect indeed
च - and
अहंकार - ego
इतीयं - thus this
मे - my
भिन्ना - divided
प्रकृतिरष्टधा - eightfold nature

Life Application:
Recognize the eightfold elements of physical nature—earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and ego.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My nature divided eightfold.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My nature divided eightfold.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus My material nature of Maya is divided into eight categories.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My nature divided eightfold.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My nature divided eightfold.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My nature divided eightfold.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My nature divided eightfold.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The five elements, mind, intellect, and egoism are the insentient divisions of Prakriti, all functioning under the absolute control of Lord Sri Hari.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Five gross elements and three internal faculties (mind, intellect, egoism) constitute the Lord"s eightfold lower nature (Apara Prakriti), the material cause of the world.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My nature divided eightfold.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: The five elements (with their subtle tanmatras), mind, intellect, and egoism form My eightfold insentient material nature (Apara Prakriti) meant for enjoyment.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—this is My eightfold lower unmanifest nature (Apara Prakriti). O mighty-armed, know My other higher consciousness-nature (Para Prakriti), the life-force, by which this entire universe is sustained.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My nature divided eightfold"—these eight categories represent Lord Vasudeva"s lower material nature (Apara Prakriti / Maya) which forms the cosmos.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The eightfold insentient Prakriti is the external potency (Maya) of Lord Vishnu, serving as the material cause of cosmic creation.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The eightfold universe is Lord Krishna"s lower manifestation (Apara Shakti) created for the experience of living entities.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "My nature divided eightfold"—the eight evolutes of fundamental ignorance form the foundation of the phenomenal world, called Apara Prakriti.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intellect, and egoism—thus is My nature divided eightfold.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural delineation of the eightfold lower nature according to Samkhya and Vedanta and its role as material cause.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva"s eightfold insentient nature becomes the cause of physical bodies and sense objects.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna"s lower nature constructs the outer form of the world, which is a potency of the Lord Himself.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Eightfold nature"—five gross elements and threefold internal instrument together constitute Apara Prakriti born of Maya.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intelligence and false ego—all together these eight constitute My separated material energies.
