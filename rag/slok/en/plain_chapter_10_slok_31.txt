Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
pavanaḥ pavatāmasmi rāmaḥ śastrabhṛtāmaham .
jhaṣāṇāṃ makaraścāsmi srotasāmasmi jāhnavī

Word Meanings:
पवनः - wind
पवतामस्मि - among purifiers I am
रामः - Rama
शस्त्रभृतामहम् - among weapon-bearers I am
झषाणां - among fishes
मकरश्चास्मि - crocodile (Makara) I am
स्रोतसामस्मि - among rivers I am
जाह्नवी - Ganges (Jahnavi)

Life Application:
Know God as the wind among purifiers, Rama among warriors, Makara among fish, and Ganges among rivers.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among purifiers I am the wind; among weapon-bearers I am Rama; among aquatic creatures I am the crocodile/alligator; among rivers I am the holy Ganges.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among purifiers I am the wind; Rama among warriors am I; among fishes I am the shark (alligator); among streams I am the Ganges.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of purifiers I am the wind; of warriors I am Rama. Of aquatic creatures I am the alligator, and of rivers I am the Ganges.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Among purifiers, the Wind; among warriors, Lord Rama; among aquatic species, the mighty Alligator; and among flowing rivers, the holy Ganges (Jahnavi) represents the Supreme Divine.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Among weapon-bearers, the name 'Rama' signifies the Supreme Lord Vishnu who is of the nature of infinite bliss, complete in all perfections, and delights all worlds. As declared in the Shandilya Samhita, 'Rama' is derived from 'Ra' (Bliss) and 'Ama' (Imperishable Perfection).

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The purifying Wind, Lord Sri Rama son of Dasharatha among warriors, the Alligator among aquatics, and the holy Ganges among rivers are the Lord's principal opulences.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among purifiers I am the wind; among weapon-bearers I am Rama; among aquatic creatures I am the alligator, and among rivers I am the Ganges.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among purifying agents, the Wind; among weapon-bearers, Lord Sri Rama son of Dasharatha; among aquatic creatures, the Alligator; and among rivers, the celestial Ganges is My divine Vibhuti.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among purifiers I am the Wind; among weapon-bearers I am Rama, son of Dasharatha; among aquatic creatures I am the Alligator; and among flowing rivers I am Jahnavi (the Ganges).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Etymologically, 'Rama' combines 'Ra' (Nature of Supreme Bliss) and 'Ama' (Infinite/Unbounded). Among weapon-bearers, Lord Rama is the crown jewel of all warriors and the supreme delight of all creation.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The purifying Wind, Lord Sri Rama among warriors, the Alligator, and the celestial Ganges embody the Lord's blissful opulence.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Among purifiers, the world-cleansing Wind; among warriors, Dasharathi Rama who vanquished Ravana; among aquatic beings, the Alligator; and among rivers, the holy Ganges is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Among purifiers I am the wind; among weapon-bearers I am Rama; among aquatic creatures I am the alligator; among rivers I am the Ganges.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: The wind's movement among purifiers, Sri Rama's righteous strength among archers, the alligator's vigor in waters, and the Ganges' purity reflect the Divine.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Wind, Sri Rama among warriors, the Alligator, and the holy Ganges are revered as supreme in their domains solely through the Lord's unique glory.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The Wind, Sri Rama among warriors, the Alligator among aquatic creatures, and the Ganges among rivers embody the Lord's auspicious glory.
