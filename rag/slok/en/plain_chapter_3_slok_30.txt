Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
mayi sarvāṇi karmāṇi saṃnyasyādhyātmacetasā .
nirāśīrnirmamo bhūtvā yudhyasva vigatajvaraḥ

Word Meanings:
मयि - in Me
सर्वाणि - all
कर्माणि - actions
संन्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा - renouncing with mind fixed on Self
निराशीर्निर्ममो - free from desire and possessiveness
भूत्वा - having become
युध्यस्व - fight
विगतज्वरः - free from anguish

Life Application:
Dedicate all your daily tasks to a higher divine purpose, working calmly without anxiety, possessiveness, or self-importance.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Renouncing all actions in Me, with mind fixed on the Self, free from hope and egoism, fight without mental fever!

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Renouncing all actions in Me, with mind fixed on the Self, free from hope and egoism, fight without mental fever!

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Surrendering all actions unto Me (Mayi sarvani karmani sannyasya), with thy mind intent on the Supreme Self (Adhyatmacetasa), free from desire (Nirashih), free from ego-possession (Nirmamah), and free from mental fever or grief (Vigatajvarah), do thou fight (Yudhyasva).

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a seeker dedicates all actions to Krishna, his life becomes completely fearless and blissful.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Surrendering all actions to Me"—God-dedicated action is the supreme remedy for release from karmic snares.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Therefore, O Arjuna, surrendering all your works unto Me, with full knowledge of Me, without desires for profit, with no claims to proprietorship, and free from lethargy, fight.
