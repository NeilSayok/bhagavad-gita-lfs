Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yogasaṃnyastakarmāṇaṃ jñānasañchinnasaṃśayam .
ātmavantaṃ na karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya

Word Meanings:
योगसंन्यस्तकर्माणं - whose actions are renounced by yoga
ज्ञानसञ्छिन्नसंशयम् - whose doubts are severed by knowledge
आत्मवन्तं - poised in the Self
न - not
कर्माणि - actions
निबध्नन्ति - bind
धनञ्जय - O Dhananjaya

Life Application:
Dispel your doubts through study and practice, performing your daily work with complete inner freedom.
