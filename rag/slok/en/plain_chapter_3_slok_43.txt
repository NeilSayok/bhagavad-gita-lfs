Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
evaṃ buddheḥ paraṃ buddhvā saṃstabhyātmānamātmanā .
jahi śatruṃ mahābāho kāmarūpaṃ durāsadam

Word Meanings:
एवं - thus
बुद्धेः - than intellect
परं - superior
बुद्ध्वा - knowing
संस्तभ्यात्मानमात्मना - restraining self by Self
जहि - slay
शत्रुं - the enemy
महाबाहो - O mighty-armed one
कामरूपं - in form of desire
दुरासदम् - formidable

Life Application:
Anchor your identity in your divine inner self to conquer destructive cravings and gain lasting emotional freedom.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Thus knowing Him to be superior to the intellect, restraining the self by the Self, slay thou, O mighty-armed, the enemy in the form of desire, hard to conquer.
