Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
vedāhaṃ samatītāni vartamānāni cārjuna .
bhaviṣyāṇi ca bhūtāni māṃ tu veda na kaścana

Word Meanings:
वेदाहं - know I
समतीतानि - the past (beings who have passed)
वर्तमानानि - the present
चार्जुन - and O Arjuna
भविष्याणि - the future
च - and
भूतानि - living beings
मां - Me
तु - indeed
वेद - knows
न - not
कश्चन - anyone

Life Application:
Trust in the omniscient divine presence that knows all past, present, and future beings, even when we fail to know It.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I know, O Arjuna, the beings of the past, present, and future; but Me no one knows.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I know, O Arjuna, the beings of the past, present, and future; but Me no one knows.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
O Arjuna, I, the Omniscient Lord, know all beings of the past, present, and future; but Me, veiled by Maya, no ordinary living entity comprehends in truth.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
I know, O Arjuna, the beings of the past, present, and future; but Me no one knows.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
I know, O Arjuna, the beings of the past, present, and future; but Me no one knows.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
I know, O Arjuna, the beings of the past, present, and future; but Me no one knows.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
I know, O Arjuna, the beings of the past, present, and future; but Me no one knows.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Me no one knows"—Lord Vishnu is Omniscient and All-seeing, but souls bound by Maya cannot know His divine essence without His grace.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The Omniscient Supreme Lord knows the Karmas and destinies of all beings in all three times, yet souls bound by Maya fail to know Him.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I know, O Arjuna, the beings of the past, present, and future; but Me no one knows.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: As the Omniscient Lord, I know all beings past, present, and future; but failing to surrender, ignorant souls remain ignorant of Me.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: O Arjuna, I know the beings of the past, present, and future, but Me no one knows. O Bharata, scorcher of foes, by the delusion of opposites arising from desire and aversion, all beings fall into deep delusion at the time of creation.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I know the beings of the past, present, and future, O Arjuna, but Me no one knows"—shows that while the Omniscient Supreme Ishvara knows all creation, His Maya deludes ordinary beings from recognizing Him.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Omniscience is ever-established in Lord Vishnu; Vishnu knows all times, whereas the soul covered by ignorance fails to know Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Omniscient Lord Krishna knows the past and future of all souls; only a devotee blessed with His grace recognizes the Lord.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Me no one knows"—ordinary souls bound by ignorance fail to comprehend Lord Vasudeva"s eternal omniscience.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
I know, O Arjuna, the beings of the past, present, and future; but Me no one knows.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural demonstration of the Lord"s eternal omniscience contrasted with the soul"s veil of ignorance.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva"s knowledge is unconditioned by time, witnessing all beings; but the ignorant soul remains averse to Him.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Omniscient Lord Krishna alone knows all creation; without surrendering to the Lord, no soul can attain divine truth.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "I know the past, present, and future"—unconditioned Supreme Consciousness alone illumines all entities across time.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O Arjuna, as the Supreme Personality of Godhead, I know everything that has happened in the past, all that is happening in the present, and all things that are yet to come. I also know all living entities; but Me no one knows.
