Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yo yo yāṃ yāṃ tanuṃ bhaktaḥ śraddhayārcitumicchati .
tasya tasyācalāṃ śraddhāṃ tāmeva vidadhāmyaham

Word Meanings:
यो - whoever
यो - whichever person
यां - which
यां - whichever deity
तनुं - form (of deity)
भक्तः - devotee
श्रद्धयार्चितुमिच्छति - desires to worship with faith
तस्य - his
तस्याचलां - his unwavering
श्रद्धां - faith
तामेव - that very
विदधाम्यहम् - make firm I

Life Application:
Appreciate how God strengthens the faith of every devotee, whichever form of the divine they choose to worship.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Whatever celestial form any devotee seeks to worship with faith, I make that faith of his unflinching.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Whichever form of a deity any devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his steady.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Whatever celestial form of any deity a devotee desires to worship with faith, I, the Indwelling Lord, make that very faith of his steady and unshakeable.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Whatever form of a deity any devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his steady.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Whatever form of a deity any devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his steady.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Whichever form of a deity any devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his steady.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Whichever form of a deity any devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his steady.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: As the Inner Guide of all deities, Lord Vishnu infuses faith into the hearts of worshippers and dispenses fruits through demigods.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The cosmic forms of demigods are also manifestations of Lord Vasudeva; the Indwelling Lord Himself strengthens the faith of worshippers in those deities.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Whatever celestial form any devotee seeks to worship with faith, I make that faith of his unflinching.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Demigods like Indra are also My divine cosmic bodies; whichever deity an ignorant worshipper approaches, I as the Indweller render his faith steady.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those whose wisdom is carried away by diverse desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature. Whichever form of a deity a devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that faith steady. Endowed with that faith, they obtain their desires, which are in truth ordained by Me alone. But finite is the fruit of those of small intellect; worshippers of deities go to deities, but My devotees come unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Whichever form of a deity any devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his steady"—the Indwelling Lord (Ishvara) grants steadfastness of faith even to demigod-worshippers according to their inner orientation.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Even faith directed toward other deities is prompted and sustained by Lord Vishnu"s power; Vishnu is the ultimate source of all faith.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Lord Krishna Himself is the inspirer of faith in all living beings, steadying each according to their inner disposition.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I make that faith steady"—the Indwelling Supreme Self confirms and steadies the faith of devotees according to their mental tendencies.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Whichever form of a deity any devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that very faith of his steady.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural demonstration of the Indwelling Lord confirming the steadfast faith of devotees.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Lord Vasudeva, the Inner Soul of all deities, is worshipped through them and Himself establishes the devotee"s faith.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Faith dawns in the soul solely through Lord Krishna"s grace, regardless of the divine form worshipped.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "I make that faith steady"—the Supreme Self seated in all hearts solidifies their faith in accordance with past Karma.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am in everyone"s heart as the Supersoul. As soon as one desires to worship some demigod, I make his faith steady so that he can devote himself to that particular deity.
