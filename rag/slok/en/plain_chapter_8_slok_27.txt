Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
naite sṛtī pārtha jānanyogī muhyati kaścana .
tasmātsarveṣu kāleṣu yogayukto bhavārjuna

Word Meanings:
नैते - neither of these
सृती - two paths
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
जानन्योगी - knowing, a yogi
मुह्यति - is bewildered
कश्चन - anyone
तस्मात्सर्वेषु - therefore at all
कालेषु - times
योगयुक्तो - steadfast in yoga
भवार्जुन - be you, O Arjuna

Life Application:
Understand these two paths clearly so you are never deluded, remaining steadfast in yoga at all times.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Knowing these two paths"—the wise devotee who understands the difference in outcomes remains ever intent on unswerving devotion to Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Knowing these two paths, no yogi is deluded"—one who understands the nature and outcomes of these paths is never confused; hence one must remain ever-steadfast in Yoga.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: A practitioner who understands both paths rejects the temporary rewards of the southern path and remains ever-steadfast in unselfish Yoga to reach God via the northern path.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Knowing the secret of both paths, a yogi is never deluded; therefore, O Arjuna, remain ever steadfast in yogic discipline.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Knowing these two paths, O Partha, no yogi is deluded; therefore at all times be steadfast in Yoga, O Arjuna"—one who understands the reality of both paths never strays onto the inferior fruitive path; hence remain ever-focused in spiritual Yoga.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Upon attaining wisdom, the seeker abandons desire for the southern path and takes sole refuge in the Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A devotee of the Lord abides eternally in Krishna-consciousness, untroubled by path distinctions.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Therefore at all times be steadfast in Yoga"—discernment of both paths enables the seeker to abide continuously in samadhi-yoga to reach Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural proof of the necessity of remaining steadfast in Yoga through discernment of the two paths.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Recognizing the excellence of the northern path, the practitioner abides ever-steadfast in divine worship.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: An exclusive devotee of Krishna, fearless through knowledge of both paths, remains continuously engaged in Krishna-remembrance.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Therefore at all times be steadfast in Yoga"—unswerving devotion in Jnana-Yoga is the easy path to non-return.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Although the devotees know these two paths, O Arjuna, they are never bewildered. Therefore be always steadfast in devotion.
