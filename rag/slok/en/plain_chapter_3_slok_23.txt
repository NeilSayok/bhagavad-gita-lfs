Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yadi hyahaṃ na varteyaṃ jātu karmaṇyatandritaḥ .
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ

Word Meanings:
यदि - if
ह्यहं - indeed I
न - should engage
वर्तेयं - ever
जातु - in action unwearied
कर्मण्यतन्द्रितः - in action unwearied
मम - follow the path
वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते - men
मनुष्याः - in every way
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
सर्वशः - in every way

Life Application:
Stay active and reliable in your role, realizing that your laziness can encourage slackness in those who rely on you.
