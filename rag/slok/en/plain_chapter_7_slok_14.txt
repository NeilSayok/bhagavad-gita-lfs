Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
daivī hyeṣā guṇamayī mama māyā duratyayā .
māmeva ye prapadyante māyāmetāṃ taranti te

Word Meanings:
दैवी - divine (supernatural)
ह्येषा - indeed this
गुणमयी - consisting of three modes
मम - my
माया - illusory energy
दुरत्यया - difficult to overcome
मामेव - unto Me alone
ये - who
प्रपद्यन्ते - surrender / take refuge
मायामेतां - this Maya
तरन्ति - cross over
ते - they

Life Application:
Surrender to divine grace to cross over the formidable illusion of material nature that is otherwise hard to overcome.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Verily, this divine Maya of Mine, consisting of the three Gunas, is hard to overcome. Those who surrender themselves unto Me alone cross beyond this Maya.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Surrendering unto Me alone"—taking total refuge in Lord Vishnu is the sole path to freedom from Maya; without Vishnu"s grace, Maya"s veil is never dissolved.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "This divine Maya of Mine is hard to overcome"—crossing the Lord"s supernatural illusion through self-effort is impossible; total surrender (Bhakti) alone dissolves Maya.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Those who surrender unto Me alone cross this Maya"—unswerving self-surrender (Prapatti) unto Lord Vasudeva is the sole effective means to transcend the cosmic Maya.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: My divine three-fold Maya is exceedingly hard to overcome; those alone who take unswerving refuge in Me, the Supreme Lord, cross beyond It.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Verily, this divine Maya of Mine consisting of three Gunas is hard to cross over; those who surrender unto Me alone cross beyond this Maya"—exclusive surrender to Supreme Lord Vasudeva breaks the veil of illusion, bestowing immediate Liberation.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Unswerving surrender is the climax of devotion to Lord Vishnu, delivering the soul forever from the three-fold afflictions of Maya.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: "Pushti Marga"—surrendering all unto Lord Krishna leads to divine grace by which the Lord Himself lifts the devotee across Maya.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Those who surrender to Me alone"—unswerving wisdom and surrender unto Brahman Vasudeva alone effects total destruction of ignorance.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Authoritative scriptural analysis of Maya"s invincible nature and its transcendence through absolute surrender to God.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Absolute surrender at the lotus feet of Lord Vasudeva is the infallible weapon to sever the bond of Maya.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna"s glance of compassion easily carries the surrendered devotee across the ocean of Maya.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Those who surrender to Me alone"—dissolution of ignorance-Maya is possible solely through unswerving surrender to Guru and Lord.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
This divine energy of Mine, consisting of the three modes of material nature, is difficult to overcome. But those who have surrendered unto Me can easily cross beyond it.
