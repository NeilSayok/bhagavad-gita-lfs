Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
tatra taṃ buddhisaṃyogaṃ labhate paurvadehikam .
yatate ca tato bhūyaḥ saṃsiddhau kurunandana

Word Meanings:
तत्र - there
तं - that
बुद्धिसंयोगं - connection with wisdom
लभते - obtains
पौर्वदेहिकम् - acquired in previous body
यतते - he strives
च - and
ततो - thereafter
भूयः - again
संसिद्धौ - for perfection
कुरुनन्दन - O descendant of Kuru

Life Application:
Reawaken your accumulated wisdom from past efforts, striving with renewed energy toward complete spiritual fulfillment.

There he comes into touch with the knowledge acquired in his former body, and strives more than before for perfection, O son of the Kurus.

In that new holy birth, he spontaneously regains the spiritual wisdom and meditative impressions acquired in his previous body; and impelled by their inner momentum, O son of the Kurus, he strives with far greater intensity than before for ultimate perfection.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Wisdom of former body"—impressions of Vishnu-remembrance performed in past life are never destroyed; they manifest spontaneously to guide toward Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The accumulated Self-knowledge of the former body awakens as dormant impressions in the new body, impelling the seeker to strive intensely for liberation.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
There he comes into touch with the knowledge acquired in his former body, and strives more than before for perfection, O son of the Kurus.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Impressions of Self-knowledge from the former body re-awaken spontaneously in the new birth, driving him to strive harder for Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: There in that new body he naturally regains the intellectual impressions acquired in his former body, and he strives more than before for perfection, O son of Kurus. By that very former practice he is borne on helplessly, for even a mere seeker of Yoga transcends the realm of ritualistic Vedic sound.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "There he comes into touch with the wisdom acquired in his former body, and strives more than before for perfection, O son of the Kurus"—explains how spiritual Samskaras carry over uninterruptedly across births.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The mental impression inherited from the past life turns the seeker toward God from childhood even without fresh instruction.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Impressions of Krishna"s service accompany the soul into future lives and unfold spontaneously in the new body.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Strives more than before"—upon awakening of past impressions, the practitioner strives for Asamprajnata Samadhi with double enthusiasm.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
There he comes into touch with the wisdom acquired in his former body, and strives more than before for perfection, O son of the Kurus.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of regaining past-life spiritual intellect and intensifying efforts toward final liberation.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: By Vasudeva"s grace, past-life spiritual wisdom awakens spontaneously to propel the seeker toward the Supreme Realm.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Impressions of remembering Lord Krishna never wane; they re-emerge in the new birth to carry the devotee to perfection.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Acquired in former body"—the subtle impression bundle of previous life awakens to advance the seeker on Mukti"s path.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
On taking such a birth, he revives the divine consciousness of his previous life, and he tries to make further progress in order to achieve complete success, O son of Kuru.
