Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
karmaṇo hyapi boddhavyaṃ boddhavyaṃ ca vikarmaṇaḥ .
akarmaṇaśca boddhavyaṃ gahanā karmaṇo gatiḥ

Word Meanings:
कर्मणो - of action
ह्यपि - indeed also
बोद्धव्यं - should be known
बोद्धव्यं - should be known
च - and
विकर्मणः - of forbidden action
अकर्मणश्च - and of inaction
बोद्धव्यं - should be known
गहना - deep / unfathomable
कर्मणो - of action
गतिः - course

Life Application:
Reflect deeply on what actions to take, what to avoid, and when to remain still during complex situations.

For one ought to understand the true nature of prescribed Action (Karma), one ought to understand the nature of forbidden Action (Vikarma), and one ought to understand the nature of Non-action (Akarma); indeed, mysterious and profound is the path of Action.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Deep is the path of action"—subtle comprehension of Karma (offering to God), Vikarma (sinful action), and Akarma (Wisdom) liberates the seeker.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The threefold dynamics of Karma (prescribed), Vikarma (prohibited), and Akarma (knowledge/non-agency) is exceedingly subtle, necessary for a seeker to comprehend.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Deep is the path of action"—without understanding the subtle secrets of Karma (practice), Vikarma (varied work), and Akarma (Self-knowledge), the path to liberation cannot be mastered.
