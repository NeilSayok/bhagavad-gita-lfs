Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ahamātmā guḍākeśa sarvabhūtāśayasthitaḥ .
ahamādiśca madhyaṃ ca bhūtānāmanta eva ca

Word Meanings:
अहमात्मा - I am the Self
गुडाकेश - O Gudakesa
सर्वभूताशयस्थितः - seated in hearts of all beings
अहमादिश्च - I am beginning and
मध्यं - middle
च - and
भूतानामन्त - end of beings
एव - also
च - and

Life Application:
Know God as the Self seated in the heart of all beings, the beginning, middle, and end of all existence.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
I am the Self, O Gudakesha, seated in the hearts of all beings. I am the beginning, the middle, and also the end of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
I am the Self, O Gudakesa, seated in the hearts of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle, and also the end of all beings.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
I am the Self, O Arjuna, seated in the hearts of all living entities. I am the beginning, the middle, and the end of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Lord Krishna first reveals His ultimate manifestation: He is the innermost Self (Pratyagatman) seated in the hearts of all beings. The Supreme Divine is the origin (beginning), sustenance (middle), and dissolution (end) of all creation.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
I am the Self, O Gudakesa, seated in the inner heart of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle, and also the end of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
I am the Self, O Gudakesa, seated in the hearts of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle and also the end of all beings.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
I am the Self, O Gudakesa, seated in the hearts of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle and the end of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: 'Gudakesha' means Arjuna who has mastered sleep and lethargy. The Lord declares that He resides as the innermost Self of all beings and is the primal cause of their creation, maintenance, and dissolution.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
I am the Self, O Gudakesha, seated in the heart of all beings; and I am the beginning, the middle, and also the end of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: 'I am the Self seated in the hearts of all beings'—I am the Inner Controller (Antaryamin) of all conscious and unconscious entities which form My cosmic body. The Self is that which sustains, rules, and owns the body. I am the origin, maintenance, and dissolution of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: O Gudakesha (conqueror of sleep), I am the Inward Self (Pratyagatman) seated in the heart (ashaya) of all beings, to be meditated upon constantly. Also, I am the origin (beginning), sustenance (middle), and dissolution (end) of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Defining the foremost Vibhuti-yoga, the Lord declares Himself to be the Indwelling Self residing in the hearts of all beings. The Supreme Lord alone is the sole Reality before creation, during its existence, and at its dissolution.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: O Gudakesha, I am the Inward Self residing in the intellect of all beings. For those unable to meditate directly on the Self, I am to be contemplated as the origin, sustenance, and dissolution of all beings.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: O Gudakesha, I am the Innermost Self seated in the hearts of all beings; and I am the origin, maintenance, and dissolution of all creatures.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: As the chief among all divine manifestations, the Lord first reveals His Form as the Inward Self of all beings, the Witness and ultimate Substratum of everything.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: By 'I am the Self', the Lord establishes His all-pervading Indwelling Reality and Universal Body-Soul relation, whereby every entity in creation constitutes the divine body of God.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: I alone am the Indwelling Lord seated in the lotus heart of all living entities, and the ultimate cause of creation, sustenance, and destruction.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The Lord reveals His foremost manifestation: I am the Innermost Self of all creatures. As the Self alone, God is the beginning, middle, and end of all.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
I am the Supersoul, O Arjuna, seated in the hearts of all living entities. I am the beginning, the middle, and the end of all beings.
