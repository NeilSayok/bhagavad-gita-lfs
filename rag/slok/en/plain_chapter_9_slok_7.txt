Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sarvabhūtāni kaunteya prakṛtiṃ yānti māmikām .
kalpakṣaye punastāni kalpādau visṛjāmyaham

Word Meanings:
सर्वभूतानि - all beings
कौन्तेय - O son of Kunti
प्रकृतिं - into nature
यान्ति - go / enter
मामिकाम् - My
कल्पक्षये - at cycle end
पुनस्तानि - them again
कल्पादौ - at beginning of cycle
विसृजाम्यहम् - I send forth

Life Application:
Recognize how all beings enter physical nature at the end of a cosmic cycle and are created anew at the start.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
O son of Kunti, all beings go into My Prakriti (nature) at the end of a Kalpa (cycle); I send them forth again at the beginning of a Kalpa.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
All beings, O son of Kunti, go into My Prakriti at the end of a Kalpa; I send them forth again at the beginning of a Kalpa.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
At the end of a cosmic cycle, O son of Kunti, all beings merge into My material nature; at the beginning of the next cycle, I bring them forth once more.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
All beings, O son of Kunti, go into My nature at the end of a Kalpa; I send them forth again at the beginning of a Kalpa.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
All beings, O son of Kunti, go into My Prakriti at the end of a Kalpa; I send them forth again at the beginning of a Kalpa.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
All beings, O son of Kunti, go into My Prakriti at the end of a Kalpa; I send them forth again at the beginning of a Kalpa.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
All beings, O son of Kunti, go into My Prakriti at the end of a Kalpa; I send them forth again at the beginning of a Kalpa.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: At the time of dissolution, the entire universe of names and forms merges into the Lord's unmanifest nature, and is emitted again at the dawn of creation.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
O son of Kunti, all beings go into My Prakriti at the end of a Kalpa; I send them forth again at the beginning of a Kalpa.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: At the end of Brahma's Kalpa, all conscious and unconscious entities dissolve into My subtle Tamas-natured Prakriti; at the dawn of creation, I manifest them afresh with names and forms.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: At the dissolution of a cosmic cycle, all beings dissolve into the Lord's unmanifest nature; at the dawn of a new cycle, the Lord projects them again by His divine sovereign will.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Explaining the cycle of creation and dissolution: O son of Kunti, at the end of a Kalpa all beings merge into My nature (Prakriti characterised by Maya/ignorance); at the commencement of a new Kalpa I project them again.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: During dissolution, all beings lie dormant in the Lord's subtle nature; at the time of creation, inspired by Lord Vasudeva, they are manifested again as the gross cosmos.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: At the end of a Kalpa, all creation withdraws into the Lord's nature; at the start of a new Kalpa, it manifests again through His joyful divine play.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: At cosmic dissolution, all effects merge into their cause, Maya (Prakriti); at the beginning of a Kalpa, they are projected again under God's supreme oversight.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: O son of Kunti, at the great dissolution all beings merge into My Maya-Prakriti, and at the beginning of a Kalpa I create them again.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: The Supreme Lord is the efficient and material cause of origin and dissolution; during dissolution all beings merge into causal nature and re-emerge at creation.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: In dissolution, the universe remains merged in the Lord qualified by subtle matter and souls; at creation, He projects it afresh in gross form.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: At the end of a cycle, all souls merge into the Lord's divine nature; at the start of a new cycle, He manifests them again by His will.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: At the end of a cosmic cycle, all creation dissolves into its primordial nature, and is recreated at the dawn of a new cycle by divine impulse.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
O son of Kunti, at the end of the millennium all material manifestations enter into My nature, and at the beginning of another millennium, by My potency, I create them again.
