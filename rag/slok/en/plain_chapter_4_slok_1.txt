Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
imaṃ vivasvate yogaṃ proktavānahamavyayam .
vivasvānmanave prāha manurikṣvākave.abravīt

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
इमं - this
विवस्वते - to the Sun-god
योगं - Yoga
प्रोक्तवानहमव्ययम् - I taught this imperishable
विवस्वान्मनवे - Vivasvan to Manu
प्राह - said
मनुरिक्ष्वाकवेऽब्रवीत् - Manu told Ikshvaku

Life Application:
Appreciate timeless wisdom traditions, learning from proven historical principles when navigating modern life challenges.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Blessed Lord said: I taught this imperishable Yoga to Vivasvan (the Sun-god); Vivasvan taught it to Manu; and Manu spoke it to Ikshvaku.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The Blessed Lord said: I taught this imperishable Yoga to Vivasvan; Vivasvan taught it to Manu; Manu declared it to Ikshvaku.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The Blessed Lord Krishna said: I imparted this imperishable and eternal Yoga of selfless action to Vivasvan (the Sun-god) at the beginning of creation; Vivasvan instructed it to Manu, and Manu imparted it to Ikshvaku.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "I taught this Yoga to Vivasvan"—Lord Vishnu is the primordial Acharya of eternal wisdom, bestowing this divine knowledge upon the Sun at creation.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Lord Krishna describes imparting this eternal Yoga first to Vivasvan to establish the timeless authenticity and ancient lineage of liberation-yielding wisdom.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The Blessed Lord said: I taught this imperishable Yoga to Vivasvan; Vivasvan taught it to Manu; and Manu spoke it to Ikshvaku.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: This Karma-yoga is no ordinary practice but a direct path to Mukti; I taught it at the cosmic dawn to Vivasvan, progenitor of Manu.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta does not provide a direct commentary on this verse. In his Gītārtha-saṅgraha, he takes up the commentary from verse 4.2 onwards.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "I taught this imperishable Yoga to Vivasvan; Vivasvan taught it to Manu; Manu declared it to Ikshvaku"—praises Karma-yoga by revealing its primordial divine lineage (*Sampradaya-parampara*) extending from the Sun-god.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The imperishability of Yoga and the lineage of authentic teachers establish the supreme value of selfless action.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: This selfless divine dedication is the inner wisdom of Purushottama Krishna Himself, taught to the Sun-god at creation"s origin.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "I taught this imperishable Yoga"—to glorify and validate the lineage of Karma-yoga expounded in Chapter 3, the Lord cites His instruction to Vivasvan.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
The Blessed Lord said: I taught this imperishable Yoga to Vivasvan; Vivasvan taught it to Manu; Manu declared it to Ikshvaku.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis proving the imperishable nature and authoritative parampara of Yoga through Vivasvan, Manu, and Ikshvaku.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: This Yoga taught by Vasudeva to Vivasvan is the eternal, supreme path to liberation.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Out of Lord Krishna"s loving inspiration, this line of Yoga flowed through the Sun-god for the world"s spiritual welfare.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "I taught this imperishable Yoga"—because it bestows the undecaying fruit of liberation, this Karma-yoga is termed Avyaya.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Personality of Godhead, Lord Sri Krishna, said: I instructed this imperishable science of yoga to the sun-god, Vivasvan, and Vivasvan instructed it to Manu, the father of mankind, and Manu in his turn instructed it to Iksvaku.
