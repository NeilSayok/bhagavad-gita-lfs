Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
parastasmāttu bhāvo.anyo.avyakto.avyaktātsanātanaḥ .
yaḥ sa sarveṣu bhūteṣu naśyatsu na vinaśyati

Word Meanings:
परस्तस्मात्तु - but transcendent beyond that
भावोऽन्योऽव्यक्तोऽव्यक्तात्सनातनः - another eternal unmanifest existence
यः - which
स - that
सर्वेषु - in all
भूतेषु - beings
नश्यत्सु - being annihilated
न - not
विनश्यति - is destroyed

Life Application:
Seek that unmanifest eternal state which exists beyond physical creation and is never destroyed when all perishes.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
But higher than that unmanifested, there is another unmanifested Eternal Being, who perishes not when all beings perish.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
But higher than that unmanifested, there is another unmanifested Eternal Being, who perishes not when all beings perish.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Yet beyond that unmanifest cause, there exists another Unmanifest Eternal Reality, which is never destroyed even when all created beings perish.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
But higher than that unmanifested, there is another unmanifested Eternal Being, who perishes not when all beings perish.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
But higher than that unmanifested, there is another unmanifested Eternal Being, who perishes not when all beings perish.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
But higher than that unmanifested, there is another unmanifested Eternal Being, who perishes not when all beings perish.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
But higher than that unmanifested, there is another unmanifested Eternal Being, who perishes not when all beings perish.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Superior even to Prakriti and Brahma is Eternal Unmanifest Lord Vasudeva; even during universal dissolution, His supreme abode remains imperishable, which is the ultimate goal of all souls.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Beyond the unmanifest cause of Brahma lies the Supreme Eternal Unmanifest Reality (Brahman), which remains imperishable even during universal annihilation.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
But higher than that unmanifested, there is another unmanifested Eternal Being, who perishes not when all beings perish.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Superior and distinct from unmanifest matter is the eternal spiritual nature (liberated soul / Supreme Self), which is never destroyed when all material entities perish.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Superior to that unmanifest is the Eternal Unmanifest Supreme Lord, who perishes not even when all beings perish. That is declared as the Imperishable and Supreme Goal, attaining which souls return not; that Supreme Person is reachable through exclusive devotion.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Yet beyond that unmanifest there is another Unmanifest Eternal Reality, which is not destroyed when all beings perish"—distinct from the ignorant unmanifest cause of creation lies the Supreme Eternal Unmanifest Consciousness (Brahman Lord Vasudeva), which remains completely untouched by cosmic dissolution.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Distinct from that is another eternal state"—Lord Vishnu, distinct from cosmic unmanifest cause, is called Unmanifest and Imperishable, whose supreme abode is eternal Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The Eternal Unmanifest that perishes not during universal dissolution is Lord Krishna"s supreme abode.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Eternal beyond Unmanifest"—distinct from Maya cause is the Supreme Truth Brahman, eternally pure, conscious and free, unaffected by cosmic dissolution.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: But higher than that unmanifested, there is another unmanifested Eternal Being, who perishes not when all beings perish.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural distinction between the unmanifest of ignorance and the Supreme Unmanifest Brahman.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Beyond material unmanifest lies the eternally pure immutable Supreme Brahman Lord Vasudeva, the ultimate goal of seekers.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Lord Krishna"s form and abode are the eternal unmanifest reality that remains imperishable through all cosmic dissolutions.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Distinct from that is another state"—the Kutastha Inner Self beyond causal ignorance is the Eternal Unmanifest Goal.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Yet there is another unmanifest nature, which is eternal and is transcendental to this manifested and unmanifested matter. It is supreme and is never annihilated. When all in this world is annihilated, that part remains as it is.
