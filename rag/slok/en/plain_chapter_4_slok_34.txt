Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
tadviddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā .
upadekṣyanti te jñānaṃ jñāninastattvadarśinaḥ

Word Meanings:
तद्विद्धि - know that
प्रणिपातेन - by prostration
परिप्रश्नेन - by inquiry
सेवया - by service
उपदेक्ष्यन्ति - will instruct
ते - they
ज्ञानं - knowledge
ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः - the wise seers of truth

Life Application:
Approach wise teachers with genuine humility, respectful questions, and willing service to gain deep life insight.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Know that by long prostration, by inquiry and by service, the wise who have realized the Truth will instruct thee in Knowledge.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Know that by long prostration, by inquiry and by service, the wise who have realized the Truth will instruct thee in Knowledge.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Learn that Supreme Knowledge by humble prostration (Pranipatena), by devout inquiry and sincere questioning (Pariprasnena), and by selfless service unto the Guru (Sevaya); those Seers of Truth (Tattvadarsinah) who have directly realized Brahman will impart that Divine Wisdom unto thee.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Seers of Truth will instruct thee in Knowledge"—an enlightened Guru who directly perceives Lord Vishnu is alone competent to instruct the disciple in authentic wisdom.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Relinquishing pride, humble prostration, sincere inquiry, and devoted service to the Guru form the scriptural method to receive liberating Self-knowledge.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Know that by long prostration, by inquiry and by service, the wise who have realized the Truth will instruct thee in Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: "Know it by prostration"—that supreme Self-knowledge is attained solely by prostrating before Seer-Acharyas, modest questioning, and selfless service.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.34–4.35 jointly): Knowing which thou shalt not again fall into delusion, O Pandava; and by which thou shalt see all beings without exception in thy Self and in Me.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Know that by long prostration, by inquiry and by service, the wise who have realized the Truth will instruct thee in Knowledge"—prescribes Guru-upasadana (approaching an enlightened Master) as the sole means for receiving Atma-Jnana.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Prostration, earnest inquiry, and service are the three indispensable qualifications for receiving Brahma-vidya from the Guru.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Through selfless service and humble approach to Krishna"s dedicated Seer-devotees, supreme divine wisdom illuminates the heart.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Seers of Truth"—service to a true Guru who is learned in scriptures and established in Brahman (*Srotriya-Brahmanishtha*) is the paramount means for Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Know that by long prostration, by inquiry and by service, the wise who have realized the Truth will instruct thee in Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis substantiating the mandatory approach to an enlightened Guru via reverence, inquiry, and service.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Service and prostration to Vasudeva"s Seer-Acharyas ignite the lamp of wisdom in the seeker"s heart.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a practitioner serves the Guru with pure sincerity, Lord Krishna Himself in Guru-form unveils wisdom in his heart.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Seers of Truth"—a Guru endowed with both scriptural learning and direct Self-realization is the true Teacher.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Just try to learn the truth by approaching a spiritual master. Inquire from him submissively and render service unto him. The self-realized souls can impart knowledge unto you because they have seen the truth.
