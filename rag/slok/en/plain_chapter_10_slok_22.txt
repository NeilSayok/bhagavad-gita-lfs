Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
vedānāṃ sāmavedo.asmi devānāmasmi vāsavaḥ .
indriyāṇāṃ manaścāsmi bhūtānāmasmi cetanā

Word Meanings:
वेदानां - among the Vedas
सामवेदोऽस्मि - I am Sama Veda
देवानामस्मि - among gods I am
वासवः - Indra
इन्द्रियाणां - among senses
मनश्चास्मि - I am the mind and
भूतानामस्मि - in living beings I am
चेतना - intelligence

Life Application:
Perceive God as the Sama Veda among scriptures, Indra among gods, mind among senses, and consciousness in beings.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Among the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; I am Indra among the gods; among the senses I am the mind, and I am consciousness in living beings.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Among the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; I am Vasava (Indra) among the gods; among the senses I am the mind; and I am consciousness in living beings.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; of the demigods I am Indra, king of heaven; of the senses I am the mind, and in living beings I am consciousness.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
The Rig-Vedic hymns set to music become the Sama-Veda; hence it is the divine glory among scriptures. Among demigods, Indra; among senses, the Mind; and in living beings, 'Consciousness' (the spark of Life) represents the Lord.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Among the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; I am Vasava among gods; among senses I am the mind, and I am consciousness in living beings.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Among the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; among the gods I am Vasava; among the senses I am the mind; and in living beings I am consciousness.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Among the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; I am Vasava among the gods; among the senses I am the mind; and I am consciousness in living beings.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Among the four Vedas, the musical and sweet Sama-Veda is foremost. Among gods, Indra; among senses, the mind; and among living beings, the manifestation of intelligence as 'Consciousness' is the Lord's own glory.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Among the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; I am Indra among the gods; among the senses I am the mind, and in living beings I am consciousness.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Among the Vedas (Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva), I am the pre-eminent Sama-Veda; among gods, I am Indra; among senses, I am the Mind; and in living beings, the cognitive function 'Consciousness' is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: The Lord describes His infinite divine opulences in prominence. The Supreme Self alone abides in the hearts of all living beings; whatever is glorious, beautiful, or mighty in the universe manifests from a fragment of the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Among the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; among gods (Rudras, Adityas, etc.) I am Vasava (Indra); among senses I am the mind; and in living beings I am consciousness (the light of awareness in body and mind).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Owing to its musical richness in sacrifices, the Sama-Veda is the Lord's Form. Among gods, King Indra; among senses, the master Mind; and in living beings, Consciousness is the manifestation of His Being.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Among Vedas, the pre-eminent Sama-Veda; among demigods, the majestic Indra; among senses, the governing Mind; and in all living beings, the expressed cognitive power (Consciousness) is My divine manifestation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Among the Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; I am Indra among gods; among the senses I am the mind, and I am consciousness in living beings.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: The musical chanting of Sama-Veda among scriptures, Indra's majesty among gods, the mind's resolve among senses, and living consciousness among beings are the Lord's supreme Vibhutis.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord's Vibhuti is that entity which is supreme and foremost in its class, such as Sama-Veda among scriptures and Mind among senses.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Among Vedas I am the Sama-Veda; among demigods I am Indra; among senses I am the mind; and in living beings I am consciousness.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Sama-Veda among scriptures, Indra among gods, Mind among senses, and knowledge-oriented Consciousness in beings represent the Lord's divine splendor.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Of the Vedas I am the Sāma Veda; of the demigods I am Indra, the king of heaven; of the senses I am the mind; and in living beings I am consciousness.
