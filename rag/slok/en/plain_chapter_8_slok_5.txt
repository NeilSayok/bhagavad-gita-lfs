Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
antakāle ca māmeva smaranmuktvā kalevaram .
yaḥ prayāti sa madbhāvaṃ yāti nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ

Word Meanings:
अन्तकाले - at the time of death
च - and
मामेव - Me alone
स्मरन्मुक्त्वा - remembering and abandoning
कलेवरम् - the body
यः - whoever
प्रयाति - departs
स - he
मद्भावं - My divine state
याति - attains
नास्त्यत्र - there is no here
संशयः - doubt

Life Application:
Remember God at the time of leaving your body to attain the divine state without any doubt.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
And whoever at the time of death leaves the body remembering Me alone, he attains My being; there is no doubt about this.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
And whoever at the time of death leaves the body remembering Me alone, he attains My being; there is no doubt about this.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
And whoever at the moment of death leaves the physical body remembering Me alone, undoubtedly attains My divine state.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Remembering Me alone at death"—unswerving remembrance of Lord Hari at the moment of death opens the door to eternal Mukti; lifelong spiritual practice enables divine remembrance at the end.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Remembering Me alone at the end"—exclusive remembrance of the Supreme Lord at the moment of death grants direct Brahman-realization (Mukti); there is no doubt in this.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
And whoever at the time of death leaves the body remembering Me alone, he attains My being; there is no doubt about this.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: One who by lifelong practice remembers Me alone, the Supreme Lord, at the moment of death, departs to attain My supreme likeness (Vaikuntha abode).

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Perishable object is Adhibhuta, cosmic Purusha is Adhidaiva, and the Lord in the body is Adhiyajna. One who leaves the body at death meditating on the Lord attains His divine state without doubt.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: Answering Arjuna"s final question: Whoever at the time of death leaves the physical body remembering Lord Vasudeva alone, undoubtedly attains My divine state (Vishnutva/Mukti); there exists not the slightest doubt in this regard.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Remembrance of the Lord at death is infallible; only one who is constantly devoted to Vishnu achieves Hari-remembrance at the final departure to gain Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Divine refuge at the final moment grants the soul direct entry into Lord Krishna"s eternal form.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "He attains My divine state"—unbroken contemplation on God at death burns all accumulated karma to bestow Sayujya Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: And whoever at the time of death leaves the body remembering Me alone, he attains My being; there is no doubt about this.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Demonstrating the supreme glory of final remembrance; remembrance of God destroys ignorance, enabling the seeker to attain divine being.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Divine meditation with exclusive love at death is the direct cause of attaining the Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: One who remembers the Lord at the final departure attains the Lord"s realm of pure divine love.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The final mental mode determines post-mortem destination; only one constantly practicing divine remembrance can remember the Lord at the end.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
And whoever, at the end of his life, quits his body remembering Me alone at once attains My nature. Of this there is no doubt.
