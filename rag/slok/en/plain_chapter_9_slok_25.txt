Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yānti devavratā devānpitṝnyānti pitṛvratāḥ .
bhūtāni yānti bhūtejyā yānti madyājino.api mām

Word Meanings:
यान्ति - go to
देवव्रता - worshippers of deities
देवान्पितॄन्यान्ति - to gods; to ancestors go
पितृव्रताः - worshippers of ancestors
भूतानि - to spirits
यान्ति - go
भूतेज्या - worshippers of spirits
यान्ति - go
मद्याजिनोऽपि - My worshippers also
माम् - unto Me

Life Application:
Know that worshippers of gods go to gods, ancestor worshippers to ancestors, but God's devotees come directly to God.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Devotees of the Devas go to the Devas; worshippers of ancestors go to ancestors; worshippers of spirits go to spirits; and My worshippers come to Me.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Devotees of gods go to gods"—destinations vary according to intention; worshipping demigods independently yields minor delights, but Vishnu"s unselfish devotees attain supreme bliss.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Fruits accord with intention; worshippers of demigods, ancestors, and spirits reach their respective realms, whereas exclusive worshippers of the Lord attain imperishable Supreme Brahman.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Devotees of the Devas go to the Devas; worshippers of ancestors go to ancestors; worshippers of spirits go to spirits; and My worshippers come to Me.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: As one"s vow and resolve are, so is the reward; worshippers of gods, ancestors, and spirits attain perishable realms, whereas My exclusive worshippers attain My eternal Realm.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Even those who worship demigods with faith worship Me alone ignorantly; failing to realize My supreme reality through improper knowledge, they go to demigods, ancestors, and spirits, whereas My exclusive devotees attain Me alone.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Worshippers of demigods go to demigods, ancestor-worshippers to ancestors"—those devoted to demigods, ancestors, or spirits attain those finite realms; but My devotees come to Me, Supreme Lord Vasudeva, and never return.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "My worshippers come to Me"—the fruit of Vishnu"s worship alone is eternal salvation; rewards of other worshippers are transient, proving the supreme glory of devotion to Lord Hari.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: As one"s inner feeling and refuge are, so is one"s reward; pure devotees of the Lord attain Lord Krishna"s eternal abode.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Rewards correspond to the worshipper"s vision; seekers of gods, ancestors, and spirits gain finite realms, but worshippers of Lord Vasudeva attain infinite liberation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Devotees of the Devas go to the Devas; worshippers of ancestors go to ancestors; worshippers of spirits go to spirits; and My worshippers come to Me.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Explaining the diversity of rewards according to worship; unselfish devotees of the Supreme Lord alone attain the eternal immortal realm of mukti.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The destination varies with the object of worship; exclusive worshippers of Supreme Lord Vishnu alone attain the highest Realm, Vaikuntha.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Worshippers of demigods, ancestors, and spirits go to those transient realms, but My exclusive devotee attains Me directly and enjoys supreme bliss.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Destination is reached according to worship; those who worship the all-pervading Supreme Self attain the immortal realm of liberation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those who worship the demigods will take birth among the demigods; those who worship the ancestors go to the ancestors; those who worship ghosts and spirits will take birth among such beings; and those who worship Me will live with Me.
