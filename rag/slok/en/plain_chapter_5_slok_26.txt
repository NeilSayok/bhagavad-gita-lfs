Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
kāmakrodhaviyuktānāṃ yatīnāṃ yatacetasām .
abhito brahmanirvāṇaṃ vartate viditātmanām

Word Meanings:
कामक्रोधवियुक्तानां - free from desire and anger
यतीनां - of the ascetics
यतचेतसाम् - of controlled minds
अभितो - all around
ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं - liberation in Brahman (Moksha)
वर्तते - exists
विदितात्मनाम् - of the self-realized

Life Application:
Free your mind from craving and hostility, experiencing peaceful freedom right here in your current surroundings.
