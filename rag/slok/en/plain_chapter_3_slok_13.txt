Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
yajñaśiṣṭāśinaḥ santo mucyante sarvakilbiṣaiḥ .
bhuñjate te tvaghaṃ pāpā ye pacantyātmakāraṇāt

Word Meanings:
यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः - eaters of sacrifice remnants
सन्तो - the virtuous
मुच्यन्ते - are released
सर्वकिल्बिषैः - from all sins
भुञ्जते - eat
ते - sin indeed
त्वघं - the sinful
पापा - who
ये - cook for their own sake
पचन्त्यात्मकारणात् - cook for their own sake

Life Application:
Share your meals and resources with others before satisfying your own needs, experiencing the joy of shared abundance.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The righteous who eat the remnants of sacrifice are freed from all sins; but those sinful ones who cook food for their own sakes eat sin.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The virtuous who eat the remnants of sacrifice (Yajnasishtasinah) are freed from all sins; but those sinful people who cook food merely for their own self-nourishment (Pachantyatmakaranat) eat verily sin itself (Bhunjate te tvagham).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: Eating food unoffered to Lord Vishnu is consuming sin; only sacred Maha-prasada destroys all sins.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Those partaking of sacrificial remnants are delivered from household sins (Pancha-suna), whereas selfish cooks consume sin.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: Partaking of God-dedicated food burns away household sins, rendering the mind completely pure.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Consuming sacred Prasada dedicated to Vasudeva purifies the seeker"s heart, leading to God-realization.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When food is eaten after offering to Krishna, it is no longer ordinary food but divine grace-infused nectar.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Cooking solely for self-nourishment causes nescience and sin, whereas sacrificial remnants are sanctifying.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The devotees of the Lord are released from all kinds of sins because they eat food which is offered first for sacrifice. Others, who prepare food for personal sense enjoyment, verily eat only sin.
