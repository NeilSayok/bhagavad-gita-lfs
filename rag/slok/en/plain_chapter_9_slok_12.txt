Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
moghāśā moghakarmāṇo moghajñānā vicetasaḥ .
rākṣasīmāsurīṃ caiva prakṛtiṃ mohinīṃ śritāḥ

Word Meanings:
मोघाशा - of vain hopes
मोघकर्माणो - of futile actions
मोघज्ञाना - of empty knowledge
विचेतसः - deluded / senseless
राक्षसीमासुरीं - demonic and fiendish
चैव - and certainly
प्रकृतिं - nature
मोहिनीं - deluding
श्रिताः - resorted to

Life Application:
Avoid vain hopes, vain actions, and vain knowledge that spring from a deluded, materialistic, and arrogant mindset.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Of vain hopes, of vain actions, of vain knowledge, and senseless, they verily are possessed of the delusive nature of Rakshasas and Asuras.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
Of vain hopes, of vain actions, of vain knowledge and senseless, they verily are possessed of the delusive nature of demons and monsters.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
Of vain hopes, vain endeavors, and empty knowledge, such senseless people adopt the delusive nature of demons and evil spirits.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
Of vain hopes, of vain actions, of vain knowledge, and senseless, they verily are possessed of the delusive nature of Rakshasas and Asuras.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
Of vain hopes, vain actions, vain knowledge, and senseless, they adopt the delusive nature of demons and monsters.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
Of vain hopes, of vain actions, of vain knowledge and senseless, they verily are possessed of the delusive nature of demons and monsters.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
Of vain hopes, of vain actions, of vain knowledge and senseless, they verily are possessed of the delusive nature of demons and monsters.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya did not comment on this sloka.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: For souls turned away from God, all desires, rites, and scriptural learning become completely futile, leading them to adopt demonic and tamasic natures.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
Of vain hopes, vain actions, and vain knowledge, such senseless persons verily adopt the delusive nature of demons and monsters.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Cherishing vain hopes of attaining God through wrong means, performing futile sacrifices, and possessing erroneous knowledge, ignorant souls remain steeped in cruel (Rakshasi) and passionate (Asuri) delusion.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: For those averse to the Lord's true divine nature, all expectations, actions, and knowledge are rendered completely void; trapped in tamasic and rajasic delusion, they suffer spiritual destruction.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: 'Vain hopes, vain actions, vain knowledge, senseless'—their desires are fruitless, their Vedic rites yield no eternal merit, and their learning is meaningless. Devoid of discrimination (vicetas), they adopt demonic (cruel tamasic) and aggressive (arrogant rajasic) delusive nature leading to downward ruin.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: All rituals and knowledge of those opposed to Lord Vasudeva are rendered utterly futile (mogha); attaining demonic and ferocious dispositions, they suffer endlessly in Samsara.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Hopes, actions, and knowledge of those hostile to God are completely futile; under the sway of demonic delusion, they remain entirely deprived of divine grace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: For fools who fail to perceive God's divine Reality, all desires, rites, and knowledge are rendered null and void, thrusting them into demonic and delusive natures.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: Of vain hopes, vain actions, and vain knowledge, senseless people verily adopt the delusive nature of demons and monsters.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: The fruit of blasphemy and ignorance is total futility; senseless men absorbed in demonic tamasic tendencies fall away from their ultimate spiritual good.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Ignorance of God's majesty renders all fruits null and void; souls endowed with demonic nature attain only darkness and downfall.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Hopes, works, and wisdom of persons averse to God are entirely vain; trapped in demonic delusion, they suffer ruin.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: Ignorance of the Lord's supreme nature is the root of all futility (moghasha), driving the soul into demonic inclinations.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Those who are thus bewildered are attracted by demonic and atheistic views. In that deluded condition, their hopes for liberation, their fruitive activities, and their culture of knowledge are all defeated.
