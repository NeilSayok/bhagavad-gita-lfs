Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
asaṃyatātmanā yogo duṣprāpa iti me matiḥ .
vaśyātmanā tu yatatā śakyo.avāptumupāyataḥ

Word Meanings:
असंयतात्मना - by one uncontrolled in mind
योगो - Yoga
दुष्प्राप - hard to attain
इति - thus
मे - my
मतिः - conviction
वश्यात्मना - by the self-controlled
तु - indeed
यतता - by the striving
शक्योऽवाप्तुमुपायतः - can be attained by proper means

Life Application:
Understand that self-mastery is hard for the undisciplined, but achievable for one who strives using proper methods.
