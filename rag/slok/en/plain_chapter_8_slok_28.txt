Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
vedeṣu yajñeṣu tapaḥsu caiva dāneṣu yatpuṇyaphalaṃ pradiṣṭam .
atyeti tatsarvamidaṃ viditvā yogī paraṃ sthānamupaiti cādyam

Word Meanings:
वेदेषु - in the Vedas
यज्ञेषु - in sacrifices
तपःसु - in austerities
चैव - and certainly
दानेषु - in gifts
यत्पुण्यफलं - whatever fruit of merit
प्रदिष्टम् - declared
अत्येति - transcends
तत्सर्वमिदं - all that
विदित्वा - having understood
योगी - yogi
परं - supreme
स्थानमुपैति - attains the abode
चाद्यम् - and primal

Life Application:
Rise above merit gained from rituals, charity, and austerities by gaining spiritual wisdom, reaching the primeval abode.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
Whatever fruit of merit is declared for the study of Vedas, sacrifices, austerities, and gifts—the yogi, having known this, transcends all that and attains the Supreme Primeval Abode.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
A person who accepts the path of devotional service is not bereft of the results derived from studying the Vedas, performing sacrifices, undergoing austerities, giving charity or pursuing philosophical and fruitive activities. Simply by performing devotional service, he attains all these, and at the end he reaches the supreme eternal abode.
