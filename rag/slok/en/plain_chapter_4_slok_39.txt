Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śraddhāvā.Nllabhate jñānaṃ tatparaḥ saṃyatendriyaḥ .
jñānaṃ labdhvā parāṃ śāntimacireṇādhigacchati

Word Meanings:
श्रद्धावाँल्लभते - the faithful attains
ज्ञानं - knowledge
तत्परः - devoted
संयतेन्द्रियः - with senses controlled
ज्ञानं - knowledge
लब्ध्वा - having attained
परां - supreme
शान्तिमचिरेणाधिगच्छति - attains peace without delay

Life Application:
Combine steady faith, focused devotion, and sensory control to experience deep, lasting inner peace.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
He who has faith, who is devoted, and who has controlled his senses attains Knowledge; and having attained Knowledge, he immediately goes to Supreme Peace.

The man of faith (Sraddhavan), who is deeply devoted and intent on Truth (Tatparah), and who has mastered his senses (Samyatendriyah), attains Wisdom; and having attained Wisdom, he swiftly reaches Supreme Peace (Param Santim).

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "A man of faith attains Knowledge"—a self-controlled devotee possessing firm faith in Guru and Lord Vishnu receives true wisdom and supreme peace.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Faith, intentness on Guru"s service, and sensory restraint form the three direct qualifications for receiving Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Supreme faith in Guru"s words, dedication to God, and control of senses form the infallible doorway to Knowledge.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta (commenting on verses 4.39–4.40 jointly): "Sraddhavan etc. and Ajnasca etc."—a faithful, devoted, and self-controlled seeker attains Knowledge and supreme peace; whereas the ignorant, faithless, and doubting soul perishes, inheriting neither this world nor the next.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "He who has faith, who is devoted, and who has controlled his senses attains Knowledge; and having attained Knowledge, he immediately goes to Supreme Peace"—defines the prerequisite triad (*Sraddha, Tatparata, Samyatendriyata*) for Knowledge and its instant fruit of Moksha.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Faith and sensory restraint are the direct gates to Knowledge, bestowing ultimate peace of liberation.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: A self-controlled devotee having unswerving faith in Krishna"s divine Form and Lilas swiftly attains supreme peace.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Having attained Knowledge he immediately reaches Supreme Peace"—upon the dawn of non-dual realization, supreme peace of liberation is attained without any interval.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Supreme faith in Vasudeva coupled with sense-restraint rapidly grants the seeker ultimate peace.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a seeker"s heart is filled with faith in Krishna, Knowledge manifests, bestowing supreme peace.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Swiftly reaches Supreme Peace"—there is zero time-gap between the attainment of Knowledge and final liberation.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
A faithful man who is dedicated to transcendental knowledge and who subdues his senses is eligible to achieve such knowledge, and having achieved it he quickly attains the supreme spiritual peace.
