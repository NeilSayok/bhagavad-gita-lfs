Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
niyataṃ kuru karma tvaṃ karma jyāyo hyakarmaṇaḥ .
śarīrayātrāpi ca te na prasiddhyedakarmaṇaḥ

Word Meanings:
नियतं - prescribed duty
कुरु - perform
कर्म - you
त्वं - you
कर्म - superior
ज्यायो - than inaction indeed
ह्यकर्मणः - maintenance of body also
शरीरयात्रापि - maintenance of body also
च - and
ते - they
न - not
प्रसिद्ध्येदकर्मणः - would not succeed without action

Life Application:
Fulfill your essential daily duties conscientiously, knowing that basic living and health require steady, active effort.
