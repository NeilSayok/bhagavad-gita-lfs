Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
sarvabhūtasthitaṃ yo māṃ bhajatyekatvamāsthitaḥ .
sarvathā vartamāno.api sa yogī mayi vartate

Word Meanings:
सर्वभूतस्थितं - situated in all beings
यो - he who
मां - Me
भजत्येकत्वमास्थितः - worships established in oneness
सर्वथा - in all ways
वर्तमानोऽपि - even while acting
स - he
योगी - yogi
मयि - in Me
वर्तते - acts

Life Application:
Worship the divine presence dwelling in all beings, living in spiritual unity no matter your daily circumstances.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
He who, established in unity, worships Me dwelling in all beings, that Yogi lives in Me, whatever be his mode of living.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
He who, established in non-dual unity, worships Me abiding in all beings, that Yogi lives in Me, whatever be his mode of life.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
That enlightened Yogi who, firmly established in non-dual, undivided Consciousness, worships Me, the Supreme Lord Vasudeva indwelling the heart of all beings, ever abides and moves in Me alone, whatever be his outward activities in the world.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
He who, established in unity, worships Me dwelling in all beings, that Yogi lives in Me, whatever be his mode of living.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "That yogi lives in Me"—the yogi who meditates with unbroken devotion on Lord Vishnu indwelling all beings abides eternally in Vishnu"s supreme refuge.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: The enlightened sage who worships the one Lord Vasudeva indwelling all beings in non-difference abides eternally in Brahman even while living.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
He who, established in unity, worships Me dwelling in all beings, that Yogi lives in Me, whatever be his mode of living.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: He who, perceiving all souls as equal to his own pure conscious nature, worships Me Vasudeva, abides in Me under all conditions.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: He whose heart is harmonized by Yoga sees the Self abiding in all beings and all beings abiding in the Self, beholding the same everywhere. He who sees Me everywhere and sees all in Me, to him I am never lost, nor is he ever lost to Me. The Yogi who, established in unity, worships Me dwelling in all beings, lives in Me whatever be his mode of life. O Arjuna, he who sees equality in all beings by analogy with himself, whether in pleasure or in pain, is regarded as the highest Yogi.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "He who, established in non-dual unity, worships Me abiding in all beings, that Yogi lives in Me, whatever be his mode of life"—asserts that the Jivanmukta sage established in non-dual consciousness abides eternally in Brahman regardless of outward actions.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: "Whatever be his mode of life" means regardless of the enlightened sage"s outward behavior, his mind abides immovably in God.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: The devotee worshipping Krishna in all beings dwells eternally in Krishna"s divine form while performing all actions.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "That yogi lives in Me"—all actions of a Jivanmukta sage endowed with unbroken non-dual knowledge are inherently Brahman.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
He who, established in non-dual unity, worships Me abiding in all beings, that Yogi lives in Me, whatever be his mode of life.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural analysis of non-dual devotion to the indwelling Lord and abiding in Brahman amidst all worldly conditions.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The seeker worshipping Lord Vasudeva as the Antaryamin of all beings abides eternally in God"s presence.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: When a devotee experiences Krishna"s presence everywhere, every act of his becomes an offering of worship.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "Whatever be his mode of life"—the sage"s body functions by Prarabdha Karma, but his awareness remains absorbed in unbroken Brahman.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Such a yogi, who engages in the worshipful service of the Supersoul, knowing that I and the Supersoul are one, remains always in Me in all circumstances.
