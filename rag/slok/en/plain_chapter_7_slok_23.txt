Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
antavattu phalaṃ teṣāṃ tadbhavatyalpamedhasām .
devāndevayajo yānti madbhaktā yānti māmapi

Word Meanings:
अन्तवत्तु - perishable however
फलं - the fruit
तेषां - of them
तद्भवत्यल्पमेधसाम् - that is of men of small wit
देवान्देवयजो - worshipers of demigods to demigods
यान्ति - go / attain
मद्भक्ता - My devotees
यान्ति - attain
मामपि - Me alone

Life Application:
Seek eternal spiritual fulfillment rather than temporary rewards that fade away with time.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "My devotees come unto Me"—small-minded souls reach perishable realms of demigods, whereas devotees of Lord Vishnu reach His eternal supreme abode.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: "Worshippers of demigods go to demigods, My devotees come to Me"—those of small intellect gain perishable worlds, whereas unswerving devotees of Vasudeva attain eternal Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: Perishable is the fruit obtained by worshipping demigods; but My devotees reach Me, the infinite and eternal Supreme Lord.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: Those whose wisdom is carried away by diverse desires resort to other deities, impelled by their own nature. Whichever form of a deity a devotee desires to worship with faith, I make that faith steady. Endowed with that faith, they obtain their desires, which are in truth ordained by Me alone. But finite is the fruit of those of small intellect; worshippers of deities go to deities, but My devotees come unto Me.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "Finite indeed is the fruit of those of small intellect; worshippers of deities go to deities, but My devotees come unto Me"—highlights the tragedy of equal exertion resulting in finite perishability for demigod-worshippers versus infinite eternal Mukti for Vasudeva"s devotees.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: For equal exertion, devotion to Vishnu yields Mukti while worshipping demigods yields rebirth; hence the wise should worship Vishnu alone.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Demigod-worshippers receive transient rewards, while Krishna-devotees gain entry into the Lord"s eternal divine sports.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "My devotees reach Me"—worshippers of non-Self obtain finite rewards, whereas devotees of the Lord realize eternal non-dual Liberation.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Scriptural contrast between the finite fruits of demigod worship and the eternal Liberation of God"s devotees.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Worshipping finite demigods grants temporary worlds, but exclusive devotees of Vasudeva attain the eternal Supreme Abode.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Devotion to Lord Krishna"s lotus feet alone grants eternal reward, delivering the devotee forever from Samsara.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "My devotees come unto Me"—the devotee of the imperishable Lord attains the Supreme Self and achieves ultimate fulfillment.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
Men of small intelligence worship the demigods, and their fruits are limited and temporary. Those who worship the demigods go to the planets of the demigods, but My devotees ultimately reach My supreme planet.
