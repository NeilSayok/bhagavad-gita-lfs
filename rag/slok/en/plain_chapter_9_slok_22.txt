Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
ananyāścintayanto māṃ ye janāḥ paryupāsate .
teṣāṃ nityābhiyuktānāṃ yogakṣemaṃ vahāmyaham

Word Meanings:
अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो - meditating exclusively
मां - Me
ये - who
जनाः - people
पर्युपासते - worship devoutly
तेषां - of them
नित्याभियुक्तानां - of the ever-steadfast
योगक्षेमं - needs and security
वहाम्यहम् - I provide

Life Application:
Think of God constantly with undivided devotion; for those ever-united, God carries what they lack and preserves what they have.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
To those men who worship Me alone, thinking of no other, to those ever-harmonized, I bring full security (Yoga and Kshema).

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
To those men who worship Me alone, thinking of no other, to those ever harmonious, I bring full security.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
But for those who worship Me with single-minded devotion, meditating on Me alone, to those ever-steadfast souls, I personally secure what they lack and preserve what they have.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
To those men who worship Me alone, thinking of no other, to those ever-harmonized, I bring full security.

Commentary by Dr.S.Sankaranarayan:
To those men who worship Me alone, thinking of no other, to those ever-harmonized, I bring full security.

Commentary by Swami Adidevananda:
To those men who worship Me alone, thinking of no other, to those ever-harmonized, I bring full security.

Commentary by Swami Gambirananda:
To those men who worship Me alone, thinking of no other, to those ever-harmonized, I bring full security.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: "Unswerving"—those pure devotees who deem no other independent of Lord Vishnu and worship Him everywhere, have their spiritual union (Yoga) and protection of devotion (Kshema) directly sustained by God.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: For devotees meditating on the non-different Self, the Lord Himself personally carries the entire responsibility of their spiritual attainment and worldly sustenance.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
To those men who worship Me alone, thinking of no other, to those ever-harmonized, I bring full security.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: For devotees who hold Me as their everything and worship Me with undivided mind, I personally grant their ultimate union (Yoga) and preserve their divine experience (Kshema).

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: For those unswerving devotees who constantly meditate upon the Supreme Lord absorbed in His divine nature, the All-Facing Supreme Lord Himself personally carries their spiritual attainment and security.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "To those who worship Me, thinking of no other"—to those renunciates who contemplate non-duality "I am Brahman", I personally carry their Yoga (acquisition of unobtained spiritual liberation) and Kshema (preservation of realized wisdom and bodily maintenance).

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: Exclusive worship signifies firm conviction in Lord Vishnu"s supremacy, not monistic oneness; the Lord Himself assumes total responsibility for the mukti and welfare of such unselfish devotees.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: For Pushti devotees who take exclusive refuge, the Lord Himself carries their Yoga (God-realization) and Kshema (preservation of divine bliss) like a loving father.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: For non-dual enlightened souls who constantly abide in the Supreme Lord, Lord Vasudeva Himself fulfills and preserves both their spiritual and worldly necessities.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: To those men who worship Me alone, thinking of no other, to those ever-harmonized, I bring full security.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Extolling the immense glory of exclusive worship: the All-Facing Supreme Lord Himself manages all responsibilities (acquisition and preservation) for His devoted seeker.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: The Lord Himself through His grace grants union (attainment of God) and preservation (eternal service) for devotees of exclusive surrender.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: For those who worship holding the Lord alone as their all, God personally shoulders the entire burden of their maintenance and protection.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: The Supreme Lord Himself carries the burden of spiritual liberation (Yoga) and bodily sustenance (Kshema) for the wise who worship with non-dual vision.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
But those who always worship Me with exclusive devotion, meditating on My transcendental form—to them I carry what they lack, and I preserve what they have.
