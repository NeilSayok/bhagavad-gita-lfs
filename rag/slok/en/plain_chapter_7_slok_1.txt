Speaker: Sri Bhagavan

Slok:
śrībhagavānuvāca .
mayyāsaktamanāḥ pārtha yogaṃ yuñjanmadāśrayaḥ .
asaṃśayaṃ samagraṃ māṃ yathā jñāsyasi tacchṛṇu

Word Meanings:
श्रीभगवानुवाच - the Supreme Lord said
मय्यासक्तमनाः - with mind attached to Me
पार्थ - O son of Pritha
योगं - practice of yoga
युञ्जन्मदाश्रयः - practicing with Me as refuge
असंशयं - without doubt
समग्रं - completely
मां - Me
यथा - as
ज्ञास्यसि - you shall know
तच्छृणु - hear that

Life Application:
Listen attentively to divine wisdom with your mind attached to God, taking refuge in practice to know truth completely.

Commentary by Swami Tejomayananda:
The Blessed Lord said: With mind intent on Me, O Partha, practicing yoga and relying on Me as your sole refuge, hear how you shall know Me fully without doubt.

Commentary by Swami Sivananda:
The Blessed Lord said: With mind fixed on Me, taking refuge in Me and practicing yoga, hear how you shall know Me in full without any doubt, O Partha.

Commentary by Shri Purohit Swami:
The Blessed Lord said: With mind deeply attached to Me, O Partha, taking Me alone as your supreme refuge while practicing Yoga, hear how you shall come to know My complete Divine Self beyond all doubt.

Commentary by Swami Chinmayananda:
The Blessed Lord said: With mind intent on Me, O Partha, practicing yoga and taking refuge in Me, hear how you shall know Me fully without doubt.

Commentary by Sri Madhavacharya:
Sri Madhavacharya states: The middle six chapters (7 to 12) primarily expound the supreme glory, unswerving devotion, and comprehensive divine nature of Lord Vishnu.

Commentary by Sri Anandgiri:
Sri Anandagiri states: Expanding on the supreme yogi-devotee mentioned at the close of Chapter 6, the Lord begins revealing His comprehensive Nature (Samagra Brahman) in this 7th Chapter.

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas:
The Blessed Lord said: With mind intent on Me, O Partha, practicing yoga and relying on Me as your sole refuge, hear how you shall know Me fully without doubt.

Commentary by Sri Ramanuja:
Sri Ramanuja states: With mind intensely attached to Me out of supreme love, regarding Me as your all-in-all and practicing Bhakti-Yoga, hear how you shall know My divine nature completely beyond all doubt.

Commentary by Sri Abhinav Gupta:
Sri Abhinavagupta states: With mind intently attached to Me, taking Me as your sole refuge and practicing Yoga, hear how you shall know Me, the Supreme Lord, completely without doubt. I shall impart to you in full this wisdom combined with realization, knowing which nothing further remains to be known in this world.

Commentary by Sri Shankaracharya:
Sri Shankaracharya states: "With mind attached to Me, O Partha, practicing Yoga with Me as refuge, hear how you shall know Me completely without doubt"—Lord Vasudeva invites Arjuna to listen to the ultimate instruction revealing His divine glory, power, and absolute essence in full.

Commentary by Sri Jayatritha:
Sri Jayatirtha states: The primary subject of Chapter 7 is the exposition of Lord Vishnu"s unconditioned divine majesty along with the fruits of knowledge and meditation.

Commentary by Sri Vallabhacharya:
Sri Vallabhacharya states: Out of supreme grace, Lord Krishna bestows upon His exclusive devotee the complete wisdom of His personal and impersonal divine essence.

Commentary by Sri Madhusudan Saraswati:
Sri Madhusudan Saraswati states: "Me in full without doubt"—undoubted and complete realization of Lord Vasudeva"s majesty, form, wisdom, and power is the ultimate means to Mukti.

Commentary by Sri Sridhara Swami:
Sri Sridhara Swami states: With mind fixed on Me, taking refuge in Me and practicing yoga, hear how you shall know Me in full without any doubt, O Partha.

Commentary by Sri Dhanpati:
Sri Dhanapati states: Commencement of the middle hexad; scriptural delineation of the Lord"s divine nature, glories, and absolute surrender.

Commentary by Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha:
Vedantadeshikacharya Venkatanatha states: Supreme Lord Krishna pledges to impart the comprehensive knowledge of His divine self to Arjuna.

Commentary by Sri Purushottamji:
Sri Purushottamji states: Unswerving love for Lord Krishna is the divine eye through which His complete nature is realized.

Commentary by Sri Neelkanth:
Sri Neelakantha states: "With mind attached to Me"—unswerving attention of consciousness upon the Supreme Self opens the gateway to Brahman-knowledge.

Commentary by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada:
The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: Now hear, O son of Pritha, how by practicing yoga in full consciousness of Me, with mind attached to Me, you can know Me in full, free from doubt.
